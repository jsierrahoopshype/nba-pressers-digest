**3. Jordan Ott — "he just has to know when to take that chance" — Jordan Ott on Rashir's defensive instincts and decision-making** [02:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spn4qEd-V-k&t=172s)

**4. Jordan Ott — "there are very few movement shooters in the NBA. It feels like they are going away" — Jordan Ott on Luka Doncic's unique movement and playmaking abilities** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spn4qEd-V-k&t=352s)

Jordan Ott: "There are very few movement shooters in the NBA. It feels like they are going away and he can do it. And I think he has more playmaking ability. We saw it there at the end of the last season, the playmaking ability of him. He is another guy defensively we are going to ask to probably do something he has not done, try to stretch him there to take the ball, like we talk about a lot. Just knowing him and how he approaches it, he is one of those routine, consistent, and the shooting piece is as real as I have ever seen."

**5. Jordan Ott — "we will keep trying to make that a play after play to where we stay out of neutral" — Jordan Ott on the team's offensive spacing and screening partnerships** [07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spn4qEd-V-k&t=465s)

Jordan Ott: "We will keep trying to make that a play after play to where we stay out of neutral. We talked about our next advantage. We worked a lot on it, especially with the younger guys in the offseason. It has not come out quite as much because we are playing so fast. I think some of these games will be good as the game slows down. Can we do one more action to try to create an advantage before we end up getting to ISO? So I think **Miles** is part of it. **Miles** has got to use his size, he can use some of the small, small screening. I know he is not small, but to have that roll or punish this with the smalls."
