**9. LeBron James — "We could be a top 10, if not top 5 defensive team if we commit every night." — LeBron James on the team's defensive potential and rebounding** [05:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v9ZLHEpW_80&t=348s)

LeBron James: "I think the potential of our team defensively can be very high. If we really commit ourselves to the defensive end, we could be a top 10, if not top 5 defensive team if we commit every night. It starts at the point of attack, picking guys up from the point of position, and whoever is picking the ball up we have to have four guys behind them with eyes and ears, just communicating constantly throughout the whole possession. Even after that, if we make teams miss, a lot of teams now in the NBA are crashing three or four guys at times and we have to put bodies on bodies and we have to rebound. It does not matter how much firepower we have offensively, if we are not cleaning glass and we are not rebounding, we are never going to get out in transition. We have the length, we have the athleticism, we have the minds, we have the schemes as we are still putting them in, but if we can do that consistently, we could be really good and it is only going to help our offense."
