**6. Craig Porter Jr. — "it'll give them the same type of understanding of how the NBA is played" — Craig Porter Jr. on the potential benefits of NBA Europe** [05:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VE1omWTMOdw&t=321s)

Craig Porter Jr.: "I feel like it'll be beneficial for guys. It'll give them the same type of understanding of how the NBA is played, but they'll be able to be over here and just experience a lot of the different culture and just how the game's played in a different way. Sometimes in your career you'll have to fill a role or you'll have to be that main guy, and sometimes guys could go overseas and be the **Donovan Mitchell** and they can figure out how they can score, how they can be a better player, or they can also come over here and be a **Sam Merrill**, they can be a catch-and-shoot guy who can affect the game in so many different ways."

**7. Craig Porter Jr. — "the only way you're going to get on the court is defense" — Craig Porter Jr. on finding his path through defensive effort** [07:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VE1omWTMOdw&t=437s)

Craig Porter Jr.: "When I came out of college and I was doing a lot of my pre-draft workouts, coaches told me straight up, everybody in the NBA is getting looked at because they can score or their offense. So the only way you're going to get on the court is defense. I understood that a lot of the things that would get me on the court are the little things: hustling, playing defense, and rebounding too. That's one of the things I'm good at at my size, and just understanding that plays a big role into it and it'll allow you to pick your spots."
