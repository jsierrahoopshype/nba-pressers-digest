# Tuomas Iisalo Press Conference | Grizzlies vs. Wizards — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50w6Xp5HMds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50w6Xp5HMds)

_Speakers identified: Tuomas Iisalo_

**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "Davis is one of the best rim protectors in recent NBA history" — Tuomas Iisalo on the team's defensive strategy against the Wizards** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50w6Xp5HMds&t=15s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "I think it's a common misconception that when people say offense is action and defense is reaction. They started the game off by sagging off of him, so they had **Davis** guarding him and he was all the way in the paint. **Davis** is one of the best rim protectors in recent NBA history and just having him there on the backside, there's not a lot on the rim and he made them pay for that choice and after that everything else then opened up."

**2. Tuomas Iisalo — "they basically sent three defenders to guard two players against Zach and one of the handlers" — Tuomas Iisalo on the defensive attention given to Zach Edey** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50w6Xp5HMds&t=75s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "He's been playing great for us. I don't think he always gets the credit he deserves. Actually, I know he doesn't get the credit he deserves. That's another example of we faced a lot of teams that have put a lot of resources on the pick and roll, so they basically sent three defenders to guard two players against Zach and one of the handlers and that means a lot of the wings are going to be open in that situation. That's the right read and Zach's been anchoring our defense and doing his job."

**3. Tuomas Iisalo — "he was a very promising young coach and in the beginning he mostly set up the video" — Tuomas Iisalo on the development of Julius Thomas** [03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50w6Xp5HMds&t=212s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "I got to know him when he was a very young coach for Dragons Rhöndorf in the German third league and was our farm team head coach and spent a couple of days with him when I took over the Bonn program and thought he would be very promising. He was a very promising young coach and in the beginning he mostly set up the video and mopped on the baseline and now he's an assistant coach in the NBA. That says everything you need to know about what I think about him and his development. He works mostly on the offensive side, he has very high standards and a very fast learner and a really well-rounded coach already at a young age."

**4. Tuomas Iisalo — "I didn't come to a head coaching role, I came to be an assistant coach" — Tuomas Iisalo on the challenges of transitioning from Europe to the NBA** [05:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50w6Xp5HMds&t=322s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "I never felt that way like coming over because I didn't come to a head coaching role, I came to be an assistant coach and wanted to do the best in that job and helping the head coach in that situation, **Taylor Jenkins**, have the best possible success. So that's what I felt and didn't feel a lot of difficulties with the environment, more so just learning about new things, learning about the lack of practice time, whether it relates to the summer or the preseason or in-season. You have really tight schedules and how you can do better in that scenario and you learn more about the other teams also."

**5. Tuomas Iisalo — "I don't think there's any question that the best players in the world are in the NBA" — Tuomas Iisalo on the competitive level of European basketball versus the NBA** [07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50w6Xp5HMds&t=465s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "It's very difficult to compare because the court is different, the rules are different, the game time is different. Also the amount of games that we played. Now if you would put NBA teams on schedules that play let's say two to three times a week instead of four times a week, I think it would also look a lot different and that discussion is also ongoing in the NBA about the amount of games. But I don't think there's any question that the best players in the world are in the NBA, that's very clear, but at the same time I'm very proud of the European basketball."
