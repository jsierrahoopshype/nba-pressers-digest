**5. Ime Udoka — "off ball and on ball movement, we didn't set screens to slip out" — Ime Udoka on necessary adjustments to avoid future defensive lapses** [03:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdZTD0KyR6M&t=205s)

Ime Udoka: "Off ball and on ball movement, we didn't set screens to slip out, set screens to get hit unders, played to our off ball actions like we had been. Then it becomes a one-on-one game, which is fine, you have some favorable matchups, but then they're attacking the those matchups and if our passing is not as crisp or we're not spaced properly, leads to some of those turnovers. So we just got a little bit too stagnant from the start. To **Cooper**, continue to get to his right hand, we want to kind of shade him left and so a few tendencies and short close outs on shooters, hard close outs on non-shooters and a lot of them attack the basket, so those were more so that we gave it kind of like old-world defensive look."
