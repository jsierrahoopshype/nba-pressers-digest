# Joe Mazzulla PRAISES Jordan Walsh After Strong Outing | Celtics vs Mavericks Postgame — *Boston Celtics*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc)

_Speakers identified: Joe Mazzulla_

**1. Joe Mazzulla — "Jordan's continuing to develop the mindset that we need him to have versus great players." — Joe Mazzulla on Jordan Walsh's development and team mindset** [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc&t=89s)

Joe Mazzulla: "To me, I think Jordan's continuing to develop the mindset that we need him to have versus great players. A great team has to have a group of guys that they can rely on to make it difficult for the other team's best player every single night. It's got to be by committee. Jordan's on that list, he's in that committee. He's taking ownership of that and continuing to get better in those moments. It's not just one person's job, but at the same time, we want to be who we want to be, we got to have guys that can go out there and make things happen and Jordan's taking ownership of that."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc&t=89s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc&t=89s)

**2. Joe Mazzulla — "They don't care about who starts, who finishes, 10 minutes, 24 minutes, they just impact winning." — Joe Mazzulla on the collective growth of young Celtics players** [02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc&t=147s)

Joe Mazzulla: "They don't care about who starts, who finishes, 10 minutes, 24 minutes, they just impact winning. They're not distracted by any nonsense and they understand that any night they could do something different that gives us a chance to win. Whether it's guarding the team's best player, whether it's rebounding, whether it's making shots, whether it's crashing on the offensive glass, those guys take pride in having a role of our team that is necessary for us to get to where we want to get to."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc&t=147s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc&t=147s)

**3. Joe Mazzulla — "He's important in the locker room, he's important on the offensive end." — Joe Mazzulla on Neemias Queta's growth and versatility** [04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpJsNzAhXc&t=254s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I'm glad you asked about Neemias because I feel like his growth and his role has gone under the radar. He's more important than just our defense, he's important in the locker room, he's important on the offensive end. He just continues to get better and better and I think he's gotten, it's easy to overlook it because of how easy he kind of makes it sometimes. The offensive end, his ability to create two-on-ones for others, his defensive IQ that we can change coverages throughout the game and he can change pick-and-roll coverages and change matchups and his ability to protect the rim, he's just a guy that I have a lot of joy watching because of who he was when he got here and who he is now."
