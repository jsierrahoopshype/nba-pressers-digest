# Jordan Walsh: I'm The BEST Defender | Celtics vs Mavericks Postgame — *Boston Celtics*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk)

_Speakers identified: Jordan Walsh_

**1. Jordan Walsh — "I was more tasked with creating havoc with guys bringing the ball up the court." — Jordan Walsh on his defensive role against the Mavericks** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=11s)

Jordan Walsh: "I wasn't even guarding him. It was kind of **PG** who took on that responsibility and that personal challenge. He did a great job. I was more tasked with creating havoc with guys bringing the ball up the court. So his job was to contain him, and then obviously we shifted around him and tried to shrink the floor against him, and it turned out good for us."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=11s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=11s)

**2. Jordan Walsh — "Their cutting was a big thing. They got to a lot of slide cut, pin-ins." — Jordan Walsh on defensive adjustments against Dallas's cutting** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=50s)

Jordan Walsh: "The biggest things we had to watch for is their cutting was a big thing. They got to a lot of slide cut, pin-ins on the back side whenever they would come off pick-and-rolls, which gave us a problem for a couple possessions. So just taking that out of the game, how do we want to guard it, how are we going to shift off of guys like **Marshall**, how are we going to shift off of guys like **Martin**, and activate our shifts and try to deter **Cooper Flagg** or whoever it is from driving."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=50s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=50s)

**3. Jordan Walsh — "I don't get a lot of shots, so when I do catch the ball, I'm looking for an opportunity." — Jordan Walsh on finding offensive opportunities as a role player** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=96s)

Jordan Walsh: "I'm just trying to find pockets to be aggressive. I don't get a lot of shots, so when I do catch the ball, I'm looking for an opportunity to score instead of trying to pass it. I'm just trying to create a real threat in the offense. With the help of **JT** throwing me that one same-side corner pass, making them respect that corner three and not make it, now he's in the game, that corner guy doesn't want to shift as much, now **JT** has a driving lane."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=96s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=96s)

**4. Jordan Walsh — "My game is never predicated on what's on the box score." — Jordan Walsh on his defensive identity and team value** [02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=138s)

Jordan Walsh: "For me, I know that my game is never predicated on what's on the box score. I'm never really judged by that, maybe the plus-minus, but the things that I do, not letting the point guard bring the ball up the court and making the secondary ball-handler bring the ball up the court every time, stuff like that which is valued, that doesn't show up on a box score is what I'm graded by. It's just about what can I create for the team. Can I create situations where they can't help off me? Can I create a situation where **Cooper Flagg** doesn't bring the ball up the court and it's somebody else?"

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=138s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=138s)

**5. Jordan Walsh — "I think I'm the best defender." — Jordan Walsh on his confidence as an elite defender** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_nM1478tEk&t=194s)

Jordan Walsh: "I think I'm the best defender. For sure. Every game is guard the best player, and obviously the organization believes that I can do that every night, so that's why they put me on that guy, whoever it may be. Taking that in and of itself gives me confidence to go out there and try to do the best that I can."
