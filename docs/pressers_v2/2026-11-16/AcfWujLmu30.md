**1. Mitch Johnson — "when he's in attack mode and switched on defensively, he is at his young age one of the better players" — Mitch Johnson on Dylan Harper's talent and defensive growth** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AcfWujLmu30&t=13s)

Mitch Johnson: "**Dylan Harper** has a lot of talent. I would like to say that I was drawing up a bunch of great plays, but he is very, very talented. It took him a while to get going, and I thought he was a little lethargic in the first half. But when he's in attack mode and switched on defensively, he is at his young age one of the better players in this league. The more he can make it more consistent in the 48 minutes, he is going to be really tough and he is going to make us that much better."

**4. Mitch Johnson — "you got to win some ugly games if you're going to win a few in a row" — Mitch Johnson on the difficulty of winning consecutive games** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AcfWujLmu30&t=114s)

Mitch Johnson: "Games in this league are hard to come by to win. Games when you play the same team multiple times in short stretches are hard to win. Games when you've won a few in a row or they've lost a few in a row are hard to win because typically percentages equal out and every team in this league is damn good and can beat you on that night. So you got to win some ugly games if you're going to win a few in a row, and it's been good to see our guys find ways to play ugly and still learn some things and then also find a way to be on top at the end of the game."
