# Houston Rockets Postgame: Ime Udoka talks win and Kevin Durant — *Houston Rockets*

_Speakers identified: Ime Udoka_

**1. Ime Udoka — "it's going to take some time to get his wind back, but those four minute stints" — Ime Udoka on Aaron Holiday's return to the lineup** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=32s)

Ime Udoka: "**Aaron Holiday** is going to take some time to get his wind back, but those four minute stints, even in those, you can see what he does for us and he was dominant in one of them, I think the third stretch, a bunch of offensive rebounds, freeing guys up, making some nice passes, so good to see the rhythm getting back and the IQ and experience that he brings and what he brings to the table is still there."

**2. Ime Udoka — "I do feel our small ball or traditional one big was much quicker" — Ime Udoka on playing small ball versus big lineups** [00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=54s)

Ime Udoka: "They play a lot of big, but I do feel our small ball or traditional one big was much quicker and with the pace and that's what kind of blew it open."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=54s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=54s)

**3. Ime Udoka — "I like how he played off the ball, got to his spots in the corner" — Ime Udoka on Amen Thompson's offensive performance** [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=73s)

Ime Udoka: "**Amen Thompson** had the ball in his hands, obviously with Fred out, but I like how he played off the ball, got to his spots in the corner, slashed behind it, got out in transition, anytime we run, he's going to be a big recipient of that and so just a well all-around efficient game, nine for 11 from the field and obviously knocking down the threes as well, but just as important as that was his defense on Young, holding him to zero for four."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=73s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=73s)

**4. Ime Udoka — "when the team doubles him like they did tonight, take care of the ball" — Ime Udoka on Kevin Durant's playmaking and passing** [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=100s)

Ime Udoka: "**Kevin Durant** is going to have the ball in his hands quite a bit and when the team doubles him like they did tonight, take care of the ball and get it to the guys and they'll make shots and so just like we went after Young quite a bit and they made some shots early, they did the same thing with Kevin from the start and as we were able to get those stops and run, they can't double as well so he got some easy baskets, but in the half court there we were going after him and he made the right play."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=100s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=100s)

**5. Ime Udoka — "he has to learn to not just go one speed" — Ime Udoka on Amen Thompson's athleticism and decision-making** [02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=146s)

Ime Udoka: "I think **Amen Thompson** has to learn to not just go one speed, just put his head down the whole time and he's so explosive that if he does that, he still can plant and finish and so felt like he was more balanced as far as that tonight, not just playing at that 100 mile an hour pace and we got some favorable matchups that he liked and kind of broke some plays off and went ISO and got to the basket or made some nice passes and so he's a freak of nature athletically and can do those things, it's just a matter of mixing it in and not just doing the same thing every time."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=146s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=146s)

**6. Ime Udoka — "you want him to take the shots when you're ready to take them" — Ime Udoka on Amen Thompson's shot selection and confidence** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=215s)

Ime Udoka: "It's a mix, you want him to take the shots when you're ready to take them and not just settle for what the defense is doing. A few times he was in the corners, he slashed behind and got dunks, got to the basket and a few times he stayed out and got threes and so he's reading the defense and how they're guarding, if he can get behind he got some of those lobs and so you want him to take them but also do the things that he does really well, which is slash and finish at the basket, but the fact that he's taking them is a great thing, being confident as far as that and he's putting in the work to improve as a shooter and so time will come that he takes them more at a higher clip."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=215s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIjCQwJh_FM&t=215s)
