**6. Doug Christie — "we're looking at ourselves, trying to make sure that we're not turning the ball over" — Doug Christie on the Kings' internal focus despite opponent injuries** [06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVMHTGvE-Ug&t=406s)

Doug Christie: "As we continue to develop, you're not really looking at the opponent that way. We're looking at ourselves, trying to make sure that we're not turning the ball over, we're making sure that we're crashing, we're making sure that we're making second and third and multiple efforts on the defensive end, keeping a tight shell. All those little things that we need to focus on, the opponent is the opponent at this time."
