# Doug Christie reveals Keegan Murray was sick in Kings’ loss | NBC Sports California — *Sacramento Kings*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I)

_Speakers identified: Doug Christie_

**1. Doug Christie — "I was proud of the guys stepping up. It was a lot going on, and I thought they stepped up and they competed." — Doug Christie praises Kings' competitive effort despite loss** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=21s)

Doug Christie: "I was proud of the guys stepping up. It was a lot going on, and I thought they stepped up and they competed. You are asking a lot out of guys, and in those moments, execution is big. We got to execute better. Some of them guys were put in those positions, and I thought they did a marvelous job of getting down a little bit, coming back, and competing. But at the end of quarters and the end of halves, that is where games are won, especially against an incredible player in Shai and a really, really good sound team defense."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=21s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=21s)

**2. Doug Christie — "I think the biggest takeaway probably is the execution piece." — Doug Christie emphasizes execution and defensive discipline** [01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=80s)

Doug Christie: "I think more than anything, it is like you are capable of doing a lot, because a lot was thrown at them right before the game, and nobody blinked. Everybody said, 'Let's go,' as a unit, as a team. I think the biggest takeaway probably is the execution piece. Part of it was you got to make sure that because their shell is so tight on the defensive end, when you come off of a pick and roll, when you gain an advantage and you see a player in front of you, you got to get off of it right away because they are going to collapse and not allow you to drive those lanes."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=80s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=80s)

**3. Doug Christie — "This is big boy basketball and that is the nature of the beast." — Doug Christie discusses the challenge of playing without starters** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=143s)

Doug Christie: "You learn early that we walked through, had a plan, everything, and then all that just happens and you just got to scrap and throw it out the window. But this is big boy basketball and that is the nature of the beast. Nobody is going to check for you, no one cares. You got to step up, and that is where I was proud of them. I thought that they came back. I threw a lot at them tonight. We were changing defenses on the fly multiple times, trying to show Shai different looks, their team different looks, and I thought they executed it."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=143s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=143s)

**4. Doug Christie — "Keegan did not feel well, and he gave us everything that he had." — Doug Christie confirms Keegan Murray was playing while sick** [04:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=245s)

Doug Christie: "Keegan did not feel well, and he gave us everything that he had, and that is a massive amount of respect for the kid because he is like, 'I am just going to figure it out.' I am seeing him hydrating, and I am looking at him, and I am like, 'You need one?' No. But the way he said no was like, 'Get in there and get him, get him over a break, put him back in.' But the guys who played, they played their hearts out."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=245s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=245s)

**5. Doug Christie — "I thought the energy, the physicality of the game, I thought they met both of those head on." — Doug Christie commends Lyles and Davion Mitchell's physicality** [05:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20nmDEzOn8I&t=316s)

Doug Christie: "I thought the energy, the physicality of the game, I thought they met both of those head on. You are asking both of those guys to guard the MVP, and you are trying to keep your hand out of the cookie jar. The physicality, there were some rebounds, some tips, some running the floor, shooting the basketball. A little while ago it was like, 'Hey, you are in. No problem, here I go.' That just speaks to their preparation, their professionalism, and their willingness to do whatever is asked of them. I have a massive amount of respect for how they go about their business."
