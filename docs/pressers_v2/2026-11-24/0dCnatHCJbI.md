**2. Steve Kerr — "We probably have to start teaching our defensive players to flop on screens because everybody around the league is doing it." — Warriors need to teach players to stop flopping** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0dCnatHCJbI&t=180s)

Steve Kerr: "We probably have to start teaching our defensive players to flop on screens because everybody around the league is doing it. You set a screen and guys chase around the screen and act like they've been hit by a car and the whole bench jumps up and those are automatic fouls, so we have to start teaching that."

**3. Steve Kerr — "We've gotten a lot of illegal screens because the ball handler has gone too early in the pick and roll." — Warriors' league-leading illegal screen count is a coaching issue** [04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0dCnatHCJbI&t=274s)

Steve Kerr: "We've gotten a lot of illegal screens because the ball handler has gone too early in the pick and roll. We've also gotten some where we've just extended our arms out or extended an elbow out or a hip and so we have to be better. We're number one in the league in illegal screens and it's not the officiating, it's us. We have to adapt to how the game is called and to why we're getting these illegal screens."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0dCnatHCJbI&t=274s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0dCnatHCJbI&t=274s)

**4. Steve Kerr — "On the road we got bothered by ball pressure. You can't do that with Brandon, he'll just blow right by you." — Brandon Williams' return will help with ball pressure issues** [08:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0dCnatHCJbI&t=513s)

Steve Kerr: "On the road we got bothered by ball pressure by a couple of the teams. They took us out in San Antonio, Dallas, Memphis in particular. Those last few games, ball pressure really hurt us, kind of put us on our heels and you can't do that with Brandon, he'll just blow right by you. He'll be a big help and definitely him going out at the same time as Steph was a big blow."
