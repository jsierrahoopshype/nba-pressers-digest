**3. Gui Santos — "we winning the game, we cannot give up a two right now. So whatever it takes" — Gui Santos on his defensive mindset during crunch time** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3cwC19-AoA&t=144s)

Gui Santos: "I was not expecting them to call foul on that play, but I was like, okay, we're winning, we're winning the game, we cannot give up a two right now. So whatever it takes to put the body in the middle of the way to him to not score that one. Even let's say if they call the foul before, even if I wasn't there, that could be an and-one and that put them in an even better possession. So my thoughts was just like, he cannot score the easy two points after the foul. So just be there, be present, and trying to make him miss."

**4. Gui Santos — "when you play with Steph so much easier, everything. The space, you always open" — Gui Santos on the challenge of playing without Stephen Curry** [03:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3cwC19-AoA&t=197s)

Gui Santos: "When you play with Steph so much easier, everything. The space, you always open, he always got two guys guarding him and that's so easy. And when you don't have him, it's just kind of weird because you got to create your own stuff. Everybody got to create their own shots or create for everybody else. When we don't have him out there, it's just a change of mindset because just because he's in a corner, we're playing four-on-four, because they're not going to leave him. And when he's not on the floor, the guy that is in the corner now is in the help. So even if you drive by somebody, there is somebody helping and we start moving the ball."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3cwC19-AoA&t=197s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3cwC19-AoA&t=197s)

**5. Gui Santos — "Yaxel has been phenomenal this year for us, you know. He's been playing really well" — Gui Santos on Yaxel Lendeborg's role and team trust** [04:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3cwC19-AoA&t=282s)

Gui Santos: "Yaxel has been phenomenal this year for us, you know. He's been playing really well and the team trusts him, the staff trusts him, and we know that he's going to do the right decision when he got the ball. So whenever we can, we can give the ball to him in the middle of the paint or either playing pick-and-roll, he's going to have the best decision. But sometimes I feel like the teams are helping and over-helping on him and that's put us in a situation to have the ball doing kind of like the Jimmy role, and he's been doing amazing, but we also got to be there to help him and make his life easier."
