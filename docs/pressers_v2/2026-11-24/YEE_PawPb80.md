# Ty Jerome Walk-Off Interview | Grizzlies vs. Hornets — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YEE_PawPb80](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YEE_PawPb80)

_Speakers identified: Rob Fischer, Ty Jerome_

**1. Ty Jerome — "they stepped it up in that third and fourth quarter. That's what they do, they were big tonight." — Ty Jerome on Grizzlies' late-game rebounding and performance** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YEE_PawPb80&t=3s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "End of the third quarter, fourth quarter, what did you guys turn around to distance yourself?"

**Ty Jerome:** "Finally grabbed the rebounds. Moses is a hell of an offensive rebounder. We had our hands full, but still, and **Zach**, they stepped it up in that third and fourth quarter. That's what they do, they were big tonight."

**2. Ty Jerome — "I don't know what I'm going to do till I do it." — Ty Jerome on his improvisational playstyle and lateral movement** [00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YEE_PawPb80&t=24s)

**3. Ty Jerome — "they showed the full arsenal tonight, so it was cool to watch." — Ty Jerome on the impact of Omax and Chris Murray** [00:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YEE_PawPb80&t=45s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Two guys tonight that maybe didn't show up in the box score too much, **Omax** and **Chris Murray**. What do they mean for your team as far as the energy they bring?"

**Ty Jerome:** "Everything. With Booz and Jeremy down, the way they can compete tonight, the way they guarded, the way Chris guarded **Brandon Miller**, the way **Omax** competed. And they were huge on offense too. A few big plays, Euro steps and all that. They showed the full arsenal tonight, so it was cool to watch."

**4. Ty Jerome — "We just got to play with our brand of basketball for 48 minutes." — Ty Jerome on maintaining Grizzlies' brand of basketball for 48 minutes** [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YEE_PawPb80&t=65s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "You head back home to take on the Clippers in a cup game. What do you need to take from this one into that one?"

**Ty Jerome:** "We just got to play with our brand of basketball for 48 minutes. I think it's we get away from it sometimes and, you know, the rebounding battle, the possession game, the intensity on defense. We got to do that for 48 minutes."
