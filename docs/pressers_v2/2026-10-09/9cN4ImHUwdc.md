**7. Taylor Jenkins — "It's a consistent principle of ours. It's a concept within our play." — Taylor Jenkins on the team's conceptual approach to off-ball movement** [06:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9cN4ImHUwdc&t=372s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I would say it's a consistent principle of ours. It's a concept within our play. I talk about how we create advantages, our movement off the ball is how we maintain the advantages, you know, whether it's off a pick-and-roll, it's off of a drive. So that should happen regardless of what the defense is doing or not. You know, we want to compromise the defense with our initial spacing, get a great what we call trigger, an advantage creator, try to get two-on-one, create that advantage, more often than not it's probably going to be a pick-and-roll, maybe it's a drive into space, read early in possessions. We got to get better in that area, but as soon as that next advantage is created, we got to get everyone moving off the ball and disorient the defense."
