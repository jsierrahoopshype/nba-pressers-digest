**1. Jamahl Mosley — "selfish play, which led to them running out and get easy transition baskets." — Jamahl Mosley on the Pelicans' selfish play in the first half** [07:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3XcXmsqPDvs&t=423s)

Jamahl Mosley: "Selfish play, which led to them running out and get easy transition baskets. I think we have to continue to understand as a unit and as a group that we have to do it together. That's the style of play we need to have on both sides of the ball. And I think when we got down, each one of us wanted to chase it, to think that we could get it on our own. But in situations like that, you have to have a level of poise about you to know that that ball has got to move from side to side and find the right matchup, and then share it and trust the pass."
