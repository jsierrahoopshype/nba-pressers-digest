# Hawks vs. Heat Postgame Press Conference: Head Coach Quin Snyder — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ok7uf2GAy8s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ok7uf2GAy8s)

_Speakers identified: Quin Snyder_

**1. Quin Snyder — "we've got to move the ball better as much as anything, particularly when teams are aggressive" — Quin Snyder on the need for better ball movement and offensive flow** [00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ok7uf2GAy8s&t=23s)

Quin Snyder: "We've got to be better. We've got to move the ball better as much as anything, particularly when teams are aggressive on us on the ball. Sometimes it's not about, we have willing passers, but we have to pass. Sometimes it's about when you pass, and our ability to get off the ball quicker and get more movement, I think will help us offensively."

**6. Quin Snyder — "we were trying to make the scoring pass and they were getting tipped" — Quin Snyder on the team's commitment to spacing and ball movement** [05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ok7uf2GAy8s&t=309s)
