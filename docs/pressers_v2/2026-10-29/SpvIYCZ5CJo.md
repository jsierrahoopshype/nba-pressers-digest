**1. J.B. Bickerstaff — "defensively I thought we were elite that quarter which translated to some easy opportunities for us" — Pistons' defensive intensity in the third quarter** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SpvIYCZ5CJo&t=4s)

J.B. Bickerstaff: "We got familiar with ourselves again, I think. Defensively, I thought we were elite that quarter, which translated to some easy opportunities for us on the offensive end. That's where we get our mojo. We get our mojo when it is gritty, when it is nasty. That's where our guys thrive. Early in this season, coming back off all summer vacation, it takes a little bit of time to find that again because things are so nice in the summer. But that was Pistons basketball to me, to be able to win ugly is something that we've been able to do, and tonight that's what it took."
