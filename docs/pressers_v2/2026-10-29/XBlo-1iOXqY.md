**1. Muggsy Bogues — "I just wanted to play basketball and just so happened that folks had their own belief" — Muggsy Bogues on overcoming size barriers in the NBA** [00:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XBlo-1iOXqY&t=44s)

Muggsy Bogues: "I just wanted to play basketball and just so happened that folks had their own belief of what a person should look like and how they should be to pursue that game. Basketball with which folks think that it should be only played by bigger and taller players and the position that I chose to play or it chose me in terms of position wise. I was 14 years old when I first heard about a **Magic Johnson**. Being compared to someone that that size, saying that you shouldn't and you can't, don't even waste your time, just motivated me just to go out there and just want to be accepted. The way I approach the game was play against the best, you have success against the best, you need to be included with the best."

**2. Muggsy Bogues — "That's a special bond right there with he and I because we came here in 1988" — Muggsy Bogues on his special bond with Dell Curry** [02:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XBlo-1iOXqY&t=127s)

Muggsy Bogues: "That's a special bond right there with he and I because we came here in 1988. I was 19 years old, he was 20 years old. We were still pursuing our dream. We both got drafted highly from an organization that decided to let us go. We came here and understood what the task was and what we were trying to accomplish. For some reason, he and I really clicked. We knew each other from college days, but we didn't know each other that well, but we just gravitated toward each other. We both had similar situations. I had a kid, he had a kid, and we were understanding that the NBA was something that we wanted to continue and not just have a short stay."

**5. Muggsy Bogues — "Even though we didn't win a championship, we had some opportunities" — Muggsy Bogues on the Hornets' early years and team injuries** [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XBlo-1iOXqY&t=671s)

Muggsy Bogues: "We are very proud and even though we didn't win a championship, we had some opportunities. The teams that we had, possibly making it to the Eastern Conference Finals, but it seemed like we always were derailed with some sort of injury. It's something that always came up with one of our core players and we weren't able to keep that core team together for a very long time, and they gave up on us I believe a little too, too early. But the teams that we were able to, the teams that I was able to play with, from the very first day that we played in the Charlotte Coliseum, playing against the Cleveland Cavaliers where we lost by 40 points, the very first day, we knew how special it was when the entire stands stood up and gave us a standing ovation."
