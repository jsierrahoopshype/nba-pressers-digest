**3. Kingston Flemings — "The game is still super fast. I think it's definitely slowing down for me." — Kingston Flemings on the speed of the NBA game for a rookie** [00:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-r0fEUdsIY&t=51s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Kingston, this might be a little early to ask you, but with only five games into your NBA career, the game slows down for different guys at different times. How much is the game starting to slow down for you as you matriculate through your NBA career, your rookie year?"

**Kingston Flemings:** "The game is still super fast. I think it's definitely slowing down for me. For me, it's just being aggressive. I see a lot of the plays throughout the court, nothing really changed. It's just when the defense knows I'm going to pass, my passes aren't going to be there. So coaches talked to me about that, everyone's talking about that. So putting the ball on the rim, I'm going to continue to do that. Just put the ball up, make some shots, and when you play like that, it makes the defense second-guess."

**4. Kingston Flemings — "Him and Nikhil, I work out with them almost every single day, taking bits and pieces." — Kingston Flemings on CJ's veteran mentorship and support** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-r0fEUdsIY&t=96s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Coach mentioned how supportive **CJ** has been of you in your early career. How important has his veteran presence been to you transitioning to the NBA?"

**Kingston Flemings:** "Otherworldly, to be honest. Him and **Nikhil**, I work out with them almost every single day, taking bits and pieces. For **CJ** to be playing this long at this high of a level, it's not easy to do. You don't even see a lot of players doing it. But just **CJ**, the things he's done, he's in my ear all the time. Every timeout when he's on the bench, just telling me what he sees, offense, defense, helping me in those ways. So just invaluable having players like him on the team, I'm grateful for it."

**5. Kingston Flemings — "As long as I'm on the court, just do what I do to help the team win." — Kingston Flemings on staying in the game despite rotation changes** [02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-r0fEUdsIY&t=134s)

**Kingston Flemings:** "To be honest, I don't really try to look too much at the rotation. As long as I'm on the court, just do what I do to help the team win.
