## Shaq and Charles Barkley on the Heat — *HoopsHype*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0G9_f46X60](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0G9_f46X60)

_Speakers identified: Shaquille O'Neal, Charles Barkley_

**1. Shaquille O'Neal — "When times get hard they just give the ball to Giannis and everybody just waiting around." — Shaquille O'Neal on Giannis Antetokounmpo needing more selfless play** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0G9_f46X60&t=0s)

Shaquille O'Neal: "When times get hard they just give the ball to Giannis and everybody just waiting around. They have to figure out a way to have continuous ball movement, everybody touching the ball. Giannis is going to have to sometime be a little bit more unselfish."

**2. Charles Barkley — "I'm not going to play like I played in Milwaukee. I'm going to trust Bam." — Charles Barkley on Giannis Antetokounmpo needing to trust his teammates** [00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0G9_f46X60&t=24s)

**Shaquille O'Neal:** "It has nothing to do with Spo."

**Charles Barkley:** "It has nothing to do with the head coach. The coaching matters, but the player has to like accept it like, you know what, I'm not going to play like I played in Milwaukee. I'm going to trust Bam. I'm going to trust Davion. When you're a great player, you have to say I know I can score or get a shot every time, but if I get these other guys involved, that's going to free me up. And Giannis is going to have to do like I told you, I use Pat Riley's word, voluntary cooperation. Coach Spo is a great coach, probably the best coach in NBA today, but Giannis can't be stubborn. He's got to get off the ball."
