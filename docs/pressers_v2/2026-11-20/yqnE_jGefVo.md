# Jaylen Wells Walk-Off Interview | Grizzlies vs. Warriors — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yqnE_jGefVo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yqnE_jGefVo)

**1. Jaylen Wells — "It just wasn't our identity. We just had to get back to what we were doing." — Jaylen Wells on the Grizzlies' second-half turnaround against the Warriors** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yqnE_jGefVo&t=0s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Jaylen, the first half, they started creeping back into the game a little bit before halftime. What changed in that second half? Where were you guys clicking?"

**Jaylen Wells:** "I think a lot of it started with rebounding. They were getting a lot of offensive rebounds. It just wasn't our identity. We just had to get back to what we were doing."
