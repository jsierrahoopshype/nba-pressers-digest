# Cedric Coward Walk-Off Interview | Grizzlies vs. Warriors — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xotTS1p0YIo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xotTS1p0YIo)

_Speakers identified: Jessica Benson, Cedric Coward_

**1. Cedric Coward — "defense was the main thing and rebounding. I think we kept giving them extra possessions" — Cedric Coward on Grizzlies' defensive adjustments and rebounding against Warriors** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xotTS1p0YIo&t=0s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "That a big third quarter allowed you guys to really open this one up. What were the biggest adjustments you made coming out of the half?"

**Cedric Coward:** "Defense was the main thing and rebounding. I think we kept giving them extra possessions. They had like 10 more shots than us at halftime. We were making shots, but we can't rely on our shot making. We got to lock in on the defensive end."

**2. Cedric Coward — "we're sharing the ball great. The ball's popping, the ball's moving, guys are taking shots" — Cedric Coward on Grizzlies' offensive success and ball movement** [00:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xotTS1p0YIo&t=18s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "When you make shots at the rate you all are, you can rely on it a little bit. What has led to such offensive success over this last stretch of games?"

**Cedric Coward:** "We're sharing the ball great. The ball's popping, the ball's moving, guys are taking shots like we essentially take in shoot-arounds in practice. You shoot them a thousand times, you're going to eventually make them. Honestly, I think we're executing really well. We're making sure we're honed in on the details."

**3. Cedric Coward — "it shows just the grit and grind we have for not only our team, but our culture" — Cedric Coward on Grizzlies' team culture and grit** [00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xotTS1p0YIo&t=40s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "This was a Warriors team that you faced early on in the season. Obviously they're a little different tonight, down some big players. But when you look at your team, where have you seen the biggest improvements for this group since that first matchup?"

**Cedric Coward:** "The first time we played the Warriors, I didn't play, but still, the guys fought like hell to get back in that game and we ended up winning. It shows just the grit and grind we have for not only our team, but our culture of what the Memphis Grizzlies are. Today, obviously, they're different, but we had some guys out too. It doesn't matter, once you're in between these four lines, it doesn't matter who's in, who's out, you got to play."
