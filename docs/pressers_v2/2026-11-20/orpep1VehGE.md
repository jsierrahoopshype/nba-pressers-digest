# Tuomas Iisalo on Taylor Hendricks’ growth in Memphis — *HoopsHype*

**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "Taylor who's stepping up in a major way who came to us from Utah as an unknown" — Taylor Hendricks' rapid development and increased role for the Grizzlies** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orpep1VehGE&t=5s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "There is obviously Taylor who's stepping up in a major way who came to us from Utah as an unknown. We didn't know what he could do when we had him in some of our first games as a five because we didn't have a lot of big guys. He said I've never been really a screener, I've never done this, but you know what he said about that? He said I feel like this will add a dimension to my game and I'm happy that I just want to play. That attitude has carried him very far."
