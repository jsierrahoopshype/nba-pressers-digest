**1. Damian Lillard — "it was a game that we had to fight for" — Damian Lillard on the team's resilience and physical play** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUP8JDDWCEE&t=0s)

Damian Lillard: "I liked that it was a game that we had to fight for. We came out and started the game and it seemed like we couldn't make a shot. We had balls rattling in and out, got good looks and we fought a lot. They were able to get out to a lead on us but we just talked about not allowing them to pick us apart for threes. We knew we would be able to get ourselves back in the game if we stayed physical and continued to play good offense and I think overtime that paid off for us."

**2. Damian Lillard — "there is no excuses, we are fouling" — Damian Lillard on reducing fouls and maintaining discipline** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUP8JDDWCEE&t=41s)

Damian Lillard: "A lot of the plays in the first half, they probably had a quick whistle a little bit, but a lot of them I think we just made undisciplined plays. **3. Damian Lillard — "I told him, I want you to be the horse" — Damian Lillard on the team's chemistry and shared offensive responsibility** [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUP8JDDWCEE&t=101s)

Damian Lillard: "I think he has been himself. Coming in, I told him, I want you to be the horse. We said the same thing. The type of physicality he plays with offensively, how he can get in the paint, you almost have to foul him because he is that big and that fast and that forceful. That is good offense. Guys are going to have to help. You want to be able to get into that paint to get defenses to collapse and be able to play off of that on the perimeter, spray out passes. Once he gets stopped, there was a play down the stretch tonight, he attacked and they pulled all over and I was able to catch on the run and make a shot coming off that secondary action. Him knowing for sure that we are encouraging that, you be the horse, you be on go, you get downhill, you attack. I think that is good for our team."

**4. Damian Lillard — "how invested everybody is from top to bottom" — Damian Lillard on the team's collective investment and work ethic** [03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUP8JDDWCEE&t=213s)

Damian Lillard: "I would say what impresses me the most is how invested everybody is from top to bottom. Usually you get on a team and it will be some guys that might not get as many minutes or feel like they should get more minutes. You look at **Chris Youngblood** tonight, this dude probably didn't even know he was going to be on this trip until the day we left.
