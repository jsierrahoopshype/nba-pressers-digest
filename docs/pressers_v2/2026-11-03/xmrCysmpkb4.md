**7. Zion Williamson — "I got to thank Sham and RG for that. Every day, after every practice, with every shooter around, we're always putting in work." — Zion Williamson on the impact of extra work with coaches** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xmrCysmpkb4&t=180s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "How much of the work that you've been putting in on the shot, but to go to that at that point in the overtime, just how key was that for you to be able to know and trust all that work you've been putting in?"

**Zion Williamson:** "I got to thank Sham and RG for that. Every day, after every practice, with every shooter around, we're always putting in work. Those exact situations, like work on taking a dribble, hesitation, pull.
