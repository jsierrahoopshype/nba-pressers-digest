# Cameron Boozer Press Conference | Grizzlies vs. Pelicans — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GIDZ04-GiV8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GIDZ04-GiV8)

_Speakers identified: Cameron Boozer, Unidentified speaker_

**1. Cameron Boozer — "It's special just what Ty's doing this year and what he's been doing since he's got here." — Cameron Boozer on playing alongside Ty Jerome** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GIDZ04-GiV8&t=12s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Maybe I'm wrong, but I'm assuming you've been the best player on your team for most of your life. Have you ever played alongside a late-game player like **Ty Jerome** and what he was able to do? What is that experience like being on the court with him in those late moments?"

**Cameron Boozer:** "I definitely have. I think it's special just what Ty's doing this year and what he's been doing since he's got here. For me, it's definitely different, but it's exciting because he's a great guy to play off of and get a lot of easy looks just because he draws so much attention. It's been great so far."

**2. Cameron Boozer — "We got to start off stronger. I think we came out with a little bit of a slow start." — Cameron Boozer on defensive breakdowns and rebounding** [00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GIDZ04-GiV8&t=48s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Coach talked about it too, there were some late-game defensive breakdowns there as well, and also the rebounding. They had a lot of second-chance points. What can you guys take away from this game to learn and sort of work on to not really be in that position like that again?"

**Cameron Boozer:** "I think first of all we got to start off stronger. I think we came out with a little bit of a slow start and let them get comfortable. I don't want any team to get comfortable, they become tough to stop. Like you said, we got to come with more rebounds, more 50-50 balls, and just out-tough the other team."
