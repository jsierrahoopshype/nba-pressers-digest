# Ayo Dosunmu Postgame Interview With Sean Dorcellus | 11.02.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8JQQTSC0oE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8JQQTSC0oE)

_Speakers identified: Sean Dorcellus, Ayo Dosunmu_

**1. Ayo Dosunmu — "I'm just living in the moment trying to take each opportunity, each game for what it's worth" — Ayo Dosunmu on maintaining an aggressive mindset for the Timberwolves** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8JQQTSC0oE&t=6s)

**Sean Dorcellus:** "19 points, 12 rebounds, 8 assists. What was your mindset coming into this one?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "Just being aggressive. I'm trying to get the win. We're short on guys right now, but I'm just living in the moment trying to take each opportunity, each game for what it's worth, and trying to get a win, trying to compete."

**2. Ayo Dosunmu — "We weren't fouling. I think in the first half we had a pretty good job defensively" — Ayo Dosunmu on the importance of defensive consistency** [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8JQQTSC0oE&t=20s)

**Sean Dorcellus:** "What made the difference in the second half for you guys to be able to pull away here?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "We weren't fouling. I think in the first half we had a pretty good job defensively, I think we only gave up 47 points. So just doing that more in the second half and then just not getting discouraged. Sometimes you look at a team record and the body can naturally just think that you're entitled to a win, but in the NBA, there's great teams, great players, and nothing is easy. So just going out there and just chopping wood each and every possession."

**3. Ayo Dosunmu — "Taking it one game at a time. Now we focus on Houston." — Ayo Dosunmu on taking the season one game at a time** [00:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8JQQTSC0oE&t=45s)

**Sean Dorcellus:** "How do you keep chopping wood the rest of the week and turn this one win into a streak?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "Taking it one game at a time. Now we focus on Houston. Enjoy this win, get treatment, get proper rest, and then get ready for Houston, take each game at a time, and just keep getting better."
