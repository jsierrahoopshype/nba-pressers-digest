# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 10.11.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jVRg4bSWDQY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jVRg4bSWDQY)

_Speakers identified: Taylor Jenkins_

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "we got to get better there, and then the three-point line, they put a lot of pressure" — Taylor Jenkins on the importance of defending second-chance opportunities** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jVRg4bSWDQY&t=13s)

Taylor Jenkins: "That was a huge key coming into the game. Last year they were top maybe two or three in the league in offensive rebounding. That was one of our top keys, early actions, defending the three-point line, and their second-chance opportunities, knowing that obviously their bigs do a great job of it. **Diabate** was six, five, but they had guys off the bench that had multiple ones. I don't think we executed our game plan, first-time scouting a team, trying to apply that. There were good moments for sure, but definitely struggled guarding the early actions. We got to get better there, and then the three-point line, they put a lot of pressure on the paint, so that got some rotations a little step late. Something good to build off of, but we got to be more locked in, especially when it comes to the rebounding."

**2. Taylor Jenkins — "the block shots are awesome, but can he just be a presence that deters?" — Taylor Jenkins praises Miles Bridges' defensive impact and rim protection** [01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jVRg4bSWDQY&t=68s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I love what I am seeing out of **Miles**. His care factor on the defensive side, he is being vocal, his presence, he is playing with a level of physicality. The block shots are awesome, but can he just be a presence that deters? And then if that forces a pass-out, can we scramble to get to the three-point line? We got to do a better job of our point-of-attack defense. We will keep working on that, that is a daily emphasis of ours right now, but knowing that we have him in a good place and he is only going to get better, just unlocking that elite rim-protecting big that he has been for so long in his career, playing physical on the offensive side, finding a rhythm. Great performance by him defensively, and I know he is going to continue to elevate us and himself moving forward."
