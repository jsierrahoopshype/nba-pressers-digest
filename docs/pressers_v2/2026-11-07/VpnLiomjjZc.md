**2. Taylor Jenkins — "We made them shoot threes, they missed them. We didn't get hits. We did a great job." — Taylor Jenkins on the team's offensive struggles and missed shots** [00:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VpnLiomjjZc&t=28s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Well, I guess as the game went on, on offense, beyond just second chances, in terms of execution and getting looks, you were trying to create points out of sets or within the flow of offense. What were you seeing there that maybe did not click as what you would have liked?"

**Taylor Jenkins:** "We were getting stops in the first half, we were playing fast, we didn't have to call sets. But then we just gave up second-chance opportunity, fouled them way too much, and they're taking the ball out of the net so we couldn't really play as fast as we needed to. So then I'm calling sets to try to get them open and all that stuff, create advantages against a physical defense, and then we didn't tag up. We didn't stick to what we do."

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "You should never defer. Should be assertive when you're open, let it fly." — Taylor Jenkins on Miles Turner's shooting struggles and coaching philosophy** [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VpnLiomjjZc&t=76s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "When someone as important as **Miles Turner** goes over for seven, do you want him to keep shooting through that? What's your coaching advice to him, or are you okay with like deferring and letting him figure it out?"

**Taylor Jenkins:** "You should never defer. Should be assertive when you're open, let it fly. He owned it after the game, he told his teammates I didn't have it. It was a rough night for me. I got to continue to be better and still impact the game on the defensive side, but I'm never going to tell a guy when you're missing shots to not shoot it."

**4. Taylor Jenkins — "He earned it. I thought his playmaking, his ability to get downhill, he did a great job." — Taylor Jenkins on the performance of the rookies** [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VpnLiomjjZc&t=101s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "**Braden** also has 38 minutes. How do you feel about that? Rookies are getting minutes in the 30s in the NBA right now. It's hard to sit a guy like that who's playing well."

**Taylor Jenkins:** "Yeah, he played great. He earned it. I thought his playmaking, his ability to get downhill, he did a great job offensive rebounding, defensive rebounding, was making some shots, getting to the free-throw line. I thought he did a solid job on defense. There's some things he can do better, but he was playing great tonight. I mean **Tyler** had a great night, **Braden** had a great night. They were kind of our best at getting into the paint throughout the game, so reward the kid, he earned it."
