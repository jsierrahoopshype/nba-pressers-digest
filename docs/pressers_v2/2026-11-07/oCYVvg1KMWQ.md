# Jamahl Mosley | Pelicans vs. Timberwolves Postgame Interview 11/6/26 — *New Orleans Pelicans*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCYVvg1KMWQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCYVvg1KMWQ)

_Speakers identified: Jamahl Mosley_

**1. Jamahl Mosley — "17 offensive rebounds, 37 free throws. That's enough for a little bit of frustration." — Jamahl Mosley on the team's frustration with free throw disparity** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCYVvg1KMWQ&t=16s)

Jamahl Mosley: "17 offensive rebounds, 37 free throws. That's enough for a little bit of frustration. Part of it is that, it's a close game going into the fourth. Our ability to get downhill, we attacked the basket, a couple times it didn't get the call. Not the reason, but again, big fellas going down there trying to attack the basket the right way, he's got to get the same kind of bumps that we're giving away on the other end. We've got to be better at finishing and making sure we punish them at the rim and not look for the bailout."

**2. Jamahl Mosley — "the frustration level is some of the screening angles, trying to get over screens, you're getting hit." — Jamahl Mosley on defensive screening and officiating challenges** [01:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCYVvg1KMWQ&t=99s)

**3. Jamahl Mosley — "Rudy was guarding Carlo and Carlo was getting all the shots. So that's part of it." — Jamahl Mosley explains the defensive rotation involving Rudy Gobert and Karl-Anthony Towns** [02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCYVvg1KMWQ&t=142s)

Jamahl Mosley: "Part of that first half or that first quarter, when he was in there, Rudy was guarding Carlo and Carlo was getting all the shots. So that's part of it. If you force-feed it, there's a way you can do it, but Carlo got hot, he kept us in that first quarter. And then as he moved into the second, Z gets in a little bit later, you go to him and he gets some shots there. But again, that's part of the downhill attacking and then getting bumped, getting hit, not necessarily being able to get the call there."

**4. Jamahl Mosley — "I thought he was great in the first half. I thought he was really good." — Jamahl Mosley on Karl-Anthony Towns' energy and impact** [03:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCYVvg1KMWQ&t=184s)

Jamahl Mosley: "I thought he was great in the first half. I thought he was really good and then picking up in the second half, got a couple rim runs, got downhill a little bit. I think just continue to keep that same energy is big. Him being able to stretch the floor with Rudy being out there, I thought that was good for us. Obviously, they made the adjustment to get him out of there as fast as they can going against Carlo and then bringing him back when we made our adjustment. So I think those are all just the chess match within a game, but just continuing to keep Carlo going because he brings the right energy, the right spirit to the game."

**5. Jamahl Mosley — "Herb is one of our best defenders, and his frustration level came because he's a dog fighter over screens." — Jamahl Mosley defends Herb Jones' physical play and frustration** [04:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCYVvg1KMWQ&t=241s)

Jamahl Mosley: "Herb is one of our best defenders, and his frustration level came because he's a dog fighter over screens. And if I'm outside of my body and outside of my base and I'm trying to get over that screen and I get clipped in my knee and I get clipped in my back and I've got my elbows out and I get hit, I would be frustrated just the same. So that's part of what came from where Herb was in that moment and so we'll have to look at it, we'll have to talk about it, and then we go from there."
