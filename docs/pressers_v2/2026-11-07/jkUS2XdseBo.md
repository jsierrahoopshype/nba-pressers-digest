**1. Mark Daigneault — "we really married the two critical aspects of your defense, the paint protection and the disruption" — Thunder's defensive rhythm and paint protection improvements** [00:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jkUS2XdseBo&t=2s)

Mark Daigneault: "I thought we had a pretty good understanding of how we needed to defend them tonight. Obviously, they didn't make shots tonight, so the variance contributes to the final score, but I thought we did a good job protecting the paint. To your point, we filed too much, which I think the aggression and the disruption, that's a fine line to walk, but we had way too many plays that we made it easy on the officials to put them on the line. So those defensive free throws are on us. We need to improve those fundamentals, but in general, I thought the execution of the plan and the fundamentals otherwise were really good."
