**5. Dyson Daniels — "the offense that Quin's built into us is we move the ball side to side" — Dyson Daniels on the Hawks' ball movement and offensive system** [05:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NYxPxPKUNSI&t=338s)

Dyson Daniels: "The offense that Quin's built into us is we move the ball side to side. Everyone gets touches, and when you play that way you're going to get assists. We want to get out and run. We're undersized as a team, so when we play a team like Washington who's really big, we want to run up and down as much as we can. Our offense is just moving the ball side to side, breaking the paint, eyes out. Playing that way is going to get you a lot of assists and everyone gets touches."
