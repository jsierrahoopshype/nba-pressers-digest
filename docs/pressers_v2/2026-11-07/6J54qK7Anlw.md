# Hawks vs. Wizards Postgame Press Conference: Luguentz Dor — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6J54qK7Anlw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6J54qK7Anlw)

_Speakers identified: Unidentified speaker, Luguentz Dort_

**1. Luguentz Dort — "It is big when rookies come in and are ready to go right away." — Luguentz Dort on the development of Cason Wallace and Jalen Williams** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6J54qK7Anlw&t=6s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Luguentz, you had a front-row seat to it for several years in Oklahoma City. A lot of young guys getting drafted and developing there, yourself, **Jalen Williams**, **Cason Wallace** in recent years. What have you seen from this rookie class in Cason and Jalen specifically and their ability to contribute at this rate this early on in the season?"

**Luguentz Dort:** "Really talented, obviously. It did not take too long for them to get going. These are two guys that are really confident. It is big when rookies come in and are ready to go right away. I give a lot of respect to Cason and Jalen, just the way that they want to learn and also whenever they have an opportunity to go out there, they really show what they are capable of. I am glad to see how hard they work."

**2. Luguentz Dort — "I was the one that was open to make those shots." — Luguentz Dort on hitting clutch shots and team ball movement** [00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6J54qK7Anlw&t=58s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Luguentz, you hit a couple of big buckets down the stretch, that three and then the mid-range jumper. When you guys are in those close game situations, how does maybe the net open up a little bit more for you in terms of being able to hit those big shots to meet the moment?"

**Luguentz Dort:** "I think as a team, we are just trying our best to find the right shots in those situations. When the game comes down to that, you have to try your best to get stops and take the right shots. Obviously, we just had to read the game. We got the ball moving a little bit and I was the one that was open to make those shots. But as a team, whenever we get to that type of stretch, we just have to keep playing together and turn the ball."

**3. Luguentz Dort — "I worked on my whole game in general." — Luguentz Dort on his role change and off-season preparation** [01:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6J54qK7Anlw&t=104s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Luguentz, you have seen a pretty drastic change in your role from what you did in OKC. Career-high driving frequency, you are playing power forward at the highest rate of your career, and some of that is because **Jock Landale** is hurt, but I think you have done a fantastic job. I am curious, when you were coming into this season for Atlanta, was there anything in particular you worked on in terms of your on-ball game or your driving game?"

**Luguentz Dort:** "I would say in the off-season, I worked on my whole game in general. It is not really something that I was doing specific. Obviously, I had a conversation with coach about position-wise and situation as well and everything. I am just adjusting, getting used to everything, trying my best to fit in. I love the group of guys that we have, so the way that coach wants to use me, I will be ready to go."

**4. Luguentz Dort — "It does not matter what time or if he just checked in, he will be ready." — Luguentz Dort on Andrew Wiggins' consistency and confidence** [02:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6J54qK7Anlw&t=158s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Luguentz, I am curious, because you know **Andrew Wiggins** a lot better than we do from all the time you spent with him in OKC. Have you seen something different from him since he got here, or is this just the same thing he was always doing in OKC and there are just more guys there?"

**Luguentz Dort:** "To me, I would say it is the same thing. Obviously, the opportunity here is different, but the type of player he is, how confident he is out there, it does not matter what time or if he just checked in, he will be ready to go right away and that is big about Wiggins. Off the court, he is just a great guy as well. I think Wiggins has been like that for a long time and now the opportunity is just in front of him and he gets to show it."
