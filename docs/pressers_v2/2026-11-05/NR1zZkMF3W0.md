# Ayo Dosunmu Postgame On Court Interview After Big Win Vs The Rockets | 11.4.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NR1zZkMF3W0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NR1zZkMF3W0)

_Speakers identified: Meghan McPeak, Ayo Dosunmu_

**1. Ayo Dosunmu — "We want to just put ourselves in a position to win. I think we did that." — Ayo Dosunmu on the Bulls' mindset during the final minutes** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NR1zZkMF3W0&t=0s)

**Meghan McPeak:** "Down the stretch in the final five minutes, in some capacity, every play on both ends of the floor included you, Melo, and JK. What was your mindset to finish this one out?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "We want to just put ourselves in a position to win. I think we did that. It's hard to win this league, so anytime you have a chance to win down the stretch, I try to just preach to the guys that keep going. We don't want to have no regrets. We don't want to after the game be thinking like what we could have done. So it was all about us giving our all."

**2. Ayo Dosunmu — "It was very important that we limit them to one shot. They're a great offensive rebounding team." — Ayo Dosunmu on the Bulls' defensive strategy against the Rockets** [00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NR1zZkMF3W0&t=29s)

**Meghan McPeak:** "They are a very high-powered offensive team. What did it take on the defensive end in the final three minutes to try to slow them down and wear them out and really just get the stops necessary?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "It was very important that we limit them to one shot. They're a great offensive rebounding team, one of the best in the league, if not the best. So we just wanted to limit them to one shot, make it hard for them, make their catches hard. We were able to do that, we were able to get good stops, and we came out with the win."
