# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 11.4.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ol8z8XbrbSM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ol8z8XbrbSM)

_Speakers identified: Taylor Jenkins_

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "we cannot overlook the fact that we were down 22 to the Celtics and came back" — Taylor Jenkins on the team's resilience after trailing by 22 points** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ol8z8XbrbSM&t=9s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I will comment quickly. I mean, I would say we made some plays, but we cannot overlook the fact that we were down 22 to the Celtics and came back and took the lead. I am way more focused on that. We put ourselves in a position to win the game. The resilience, the fight that our guys had in the second half, the defensive intensity, the ball movement, obviously we started making some shots, but just the energy, the life was there. Our guys responded to halftime down 20 plus in a very tough environment against a team that was playing extremely well. A lot of growth, a lot of progress here, and we will figure out how to close out a game."

**2. Taylor Jenkins — "I just kept telling the team all game, stay connected. We are playing collective basketball together." — Taylor Jenkins on the importance of collective ball movement and sharing the rock** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ol8z8XbrbSM&t=93s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I just kept telling the team all game, stay connected. We are playing collective basketball together. What you are doing on offense, we need to share the rock. We talked about it at halftime. We had I think only 14 made shots, 11 of them were assisted, and we had plenty of other potential assists. But when we were not playing to their hands, they were doing a great job. When we were moving, shifting on one side, when we started getting to the second side in the second half, we were getting open looks, we were able to penetrate the defense a little bit more, had a little bit more rhythm. So there is something to it. 28 assists when we only had 11 at halftime, but then we backed it up with some defensive intensity."

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "It was great to see Ryan have a great game there, a lot of different guys knocking shots." — Taylor Jenkins on the offensive contributions of Ryan, Jaime, Tyler, and Miles** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ol8z8XbrbSM&t=157s)

Taylor Jenkins: "The ball always is going to find energy. Hopefully it leads to a spark for us. It was great to see **Ryan** have a great game there, a lot of different guys knocking shots. **Jaime** has been shooting the ball well, **Tyler** with big shots, **Miles** knocking down his shots as well. But we were playing connected. We were finding our guys open. When they were in drop coverage, we were picking and popping, we were touching the paint, slowing down, letting the defense be aggressive and finding the open windows. Hopefully this is a springboard for us. I just kept telling the guys just continue to trust. Continue to trust the work that you are putting in."

**4. Taylor Jenkins — "Miles had six assists. So when you talk about being a hub, he plays on selfish." — Taylor Jenkins on the playmaking and chemistry between Miles and Tyler** [03:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ol8z8XbrbSM&t=224s)

Taylor Jenkins: "They are great. You look at it, **Miles** had six assists. So when you talk about being a hub, I know he could have had even more. He plays on selfish when he needs to be aggressive and he is open. We tell him to let it fly. He and I talked about that the other day. And then obviously when **Tyler** is moving off the ball and cutting, they are going to put their best defender on him every single night. How do we get him into aggressive spots, scoring spots for him? But he can just find a way. And when he knows he has teammates around him, he has **Miles** out there, guys he can trust and space off of, that is something hopefully that we can continue to build and just click with."
