# Joe Mazzulla TAKES BLAME For Loss vs Hawks | Celtics vs Hawks Postgame — *Boston Celtics*

_Speakers identified: Joe Mazzulla_

**1. Joe Mazzulla — "our play calling wasn't right. We didn't put them in position and spacing to make the right reads." — Joe Mazzulla takes responsibility for late-game play calling and spacing** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=6s)

Joe Mazzulla: "It was a bang-bang play. I thought we had come out of the timeout and I was like if we have a breakaway we're going to go for it. It was one of those things where you don't know exactly what was going to happen. I thought there was going to be a foul there or there was going to be a breakaway and obviously it wasn't. So, it was just one of those bang-bang moments. Those guys, they do that, that's something Atlanta does very well. Our play calling wasn't right. We didn't put them in position and spacing to make the right reads, so I think that one's on me."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=6s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=6s)

**2. Joe Mazzulla — "we can't have a 35 point second half. I have to call better plays, put us in better spacing." — Mazzulla attributes turnovers to stylistic changes and lack of offensive efficiency** [01:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=61s)

Joe Mazzulla: "Against Atlanta it's been an issue for us. I remember a game last year we had 20. It's something that they're really good at. A little bit of it is a stylistic change to the way that we're playing, just with some off-ball and moving a lot more and a little bit of unpredictability. But tonight, and especially in the second half, we can't have a 35 point second half. I have to call better plays, put us in better spacing, get better opportunities for us to get clean catches, clean advantages and just didn't do that tonight and they were able to take advantage of that."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=61s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=61s)

**3. Joe Mazzulla — "we just can't have two quarters where we have 18 points and 17 points." — Mazzulla emphasizes the need for consistent offensive efficiency across all four quarters** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=95s)

Joe Mazzulla: "31 seems like a lot, but it's really the 18. We just can't have two quarters where we have 18 points and 17 points. Obviously in Washington the 40 point quarter is our defense, but tonight I thought it was our offense. Our defense is putting us in position to win. 87 points, 17 point fourth quarter, it's just our lack of offensive efficiency at times is putting too much pressure on our defense and it's something I have to figure out how I could just put us in better positions in the second half."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=95s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=95s)

**4. Joe Mazzulla — "I have to find better opportunities to either get him in different spots on the floor." — Mazzulla discusses Jayson Tatum's struggles and the need for better positioning** [02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=167s)

Joe Mazzulla: "It starts with me finding better opportunities to either get him in different spots on the floor where it's easier that he can make plays for us. Obviously teams are adjusting to him in the second half because of what he's having to do. So yeah, I have to, whether it's different locations, different play calling, I have to put him in better positions for sure to be able to take advantage of stuff like that, especially in the fourth quarter."

**5. Joe Mazzulla — "it comes down to three things: free throws, turnovers and rebounds." — Mazzulla identifies turnovers, free throws, and rebounding as key areas for improvement** [03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=227s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I still think it comes down to three things: free throws, turnovers and rebounds. You take a look at the Sacramento game, it was rebounding. You take a look at tonight, it was turnovers. So I just think in those moments, your turnovers, your free throws, your rebounding, those things are just increased a lot more and you just have to be better at those."

**6. Joe Mazzulla — "it's really just a byproduct of our offense. It's five to ten points here and there." — Mazzulla explains defensive struggles as a byproduct of offensive inefficiency** [05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gejItFJTdEY&t=330s)

Joe Mazzulla: "Three out of the four quarters we've had elite defensive games and I think it just weighs on you. You can't have an 18 point quarter and a 17 point quarter and that just puts too much pressure on your defense. So I think it's really just a byproduct of our offense. It's five to ten points here and there that we have to get better at, which will take a lot of pressure off our defense if we can just play more efficient offense."
