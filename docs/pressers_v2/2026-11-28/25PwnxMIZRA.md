# “We Lost The Battle Of The Boards Which Lost Us The Game.” | Chris Finch Postgame Sound | 11.27.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

**1. Chris Finch — "everyone tried to do too much offensively. We put our head down, went to the crowd" — Chris Finch on Timberwolves' offensive struggles without Karl-Anthony Towns** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=25PwnxMIZRA&t=11s)

Chris Finch: "I think it was a case where everyone tried to do too much offensively. We put our head down, went to the crowd and we missed a lot of easy baskets. We are not going to have a centralized playmaker with **Karl-Anthony Towns** on the floor, so we have to make plays that are there. There were a lot of them at halftime I thought that we should have made, and we didn't. We paid for that. It led to turnovers and offensive rebounds that killed us. They had 18 more shots than us. Regardless of whether we were dealing with an injury or a mindset reset, you cannot have a team take 18 more shots than you."
