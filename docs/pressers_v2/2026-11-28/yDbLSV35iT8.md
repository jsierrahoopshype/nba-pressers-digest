# Dusty May Postgame Press Conference | Mavericks at Suns | Nov. 27, 2026 — *Dallas Mavericks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8)

_Speakers identified: Dusty May_

**1. Dusty May — "It gives us confidence and belief. Obviously, we lean on Kai for a lot." — Dusty May on Mavericks' ability to win without Luka Doncic and Kyrie Irving** [00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=48s)

Dusty May: "Even without Cooper's minutes, we were able to hold it down. It gives us confidence and belief. Obviously, we lean on Kai for a lot, and for us to simply stay with it and Max come in, stay ready, and get those minutes that would usually be going to Kai and really take advantage of it. We need to be able to win in numerous ways with different lineups, and so tonight was a step forward where things weren't necessarily going well, and we just stayed with it and then we found our run late."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=48s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=48s)

**2. Dusty May — "Because Max made a few, they started top blocking him." — Dusty May on contributions from Naji Marshall and Max Christie** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=95s)

Dusty May: "We were struggling breaking down their defense, and because Max made a few, they started top blocking him. And because they had someone else that they weren't able to muck off of, it gave Naji and Cooper a little bit more space. Obviously, the shots, and I thought Max was poised, his finishing in transition was big. He just played well. I thought he defended. They tried to isolate us down the stretch, and other than one great shot by Gillespie, I felt like we defended well on an island."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=95s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=95s)

**3. Dusty May — "The NBA game, no lead is ever safe." — Dusty May on the team's growth during their recent road trip** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=131s)

Dusty May: "That's what we're learning, that the NBA game, no lead is ever safe. If you can just stay the course, you're always in position to come back if things fall your way a little bit. You can force the opposition to miss a few shots. I thought our zone got them out of rhythm. Even though they had a good plan and made some shots, it seemed like that took them out of their offensive rhythm a little bit, and then we were able to go back and forth and late in the game, our gap activity and individual defense was excellent."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=131s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=131s)

**4. Dusty May — "It's his big shot making, first and foremost, his late game, last couple minutes." — Dusty May on Cooper Flagg's late-game shot making** [02:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=178s)

Dusty May: "It's his big shot making, first and foremost, his late game, last couple minutes, he gets to his spots and makes big shot after big shot. I also thought this wasn't a night where we shot, I guess, probably worse from the three than we typically would for the value of our shots. I think he would have had well over double-digit assists. I thought he made great reads, great decisions, and that's probably the most rewarding part, the night where we're not making shots, our threes aren't going in, we were able to not let that impact our defense and still rebound and find a way."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=178s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=178s)

**5. Dusty May — "At the end of the day, if we don't turn it over, it allows you to miss shots." — Dusty May on the team's improved possession battle** [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=276s)

Dusty May: "We were disappointed at half that we had given up five offensive rebounds, and in the second half, we only gave up three, so that was solid. At the end of the day, if we don't turn it over, and I don't know if it's four, five, six games now where we've managed to control the turnover battle, it allows you to miss shots. It allows you to not be at your best, but I do think, off memory, we were able to get out in the passing lanes late and turn their turnovers into baskets at a time when we were struggling to generate great looks in the half court."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=276s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=276s)

**6. Dusty May — "We were much more intentional slipping some screens, putting some pressure on the rim." — Dusty May on offensive spacing and movement improvements** [05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDbLSV35iT8&t=330s)

Dusty May: "We were able to find some different spacing and our cutting improved. I thought in the first half, even when we got in the paint, we didn't have any second cuts, we didn't have any movement, and we just kind of left our teammates on an island at times. I thought in the second half, we were much more intentional slipping some screens, putting some pressure on the rim. Those are the plays that I just think we weren't, we were letting our inability to make shots in the first half affect how we were playing the game. In the second half, I thought we just, it was almost like the scoreboard was off and we were just playing."
