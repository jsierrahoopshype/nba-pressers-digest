**10. Jamahl Mosley — "There is a balance for when you command the floor and have everybody in their places." — Jamahl Mosley on the balance between scoring and playmaking** [06:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MVE5ZmJIx7Q&t=381s)

Jamahl Mosley: "That's the conversation, that's the film we watch. He did a great job studying at the summer, knowing exactly what he needs to do, watching great players, and there is a balance. There is a balance for when you command the floor and have everybody in their places, and then there's when you get downhill and you can attack and find and make the right read. So he's done a heck of a job of finding that balance. There's a lull for a little bit, he comes in, gets it off, gets a rebound, pushes the break, gets downhill, makes a layup. Next time down, calls a play, gets everybody set, and then there's a lull for a moment, then he comes down the court, takes a midrange pull-up that gets the momentum going back. So he's starting to understand that balance is what we're going to continue to need from him."

**11. Jamahl Mosley — "You continue to preach it. What we're building, you want it to be sustainable." — Jamahl Mosley on maintaining the process throughout the season** [07:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MVE5ZmJIx7Q&t=446s)

Jamahl Mosley: "You continue to preach it. I understand it's the preseason and guys look at it maybe a little bit differently, but what we're building, you want it to be sustainable. And that's why we talked from the beginning, it's not going to be about skipping steps. The same conversations we're having now, we can have in January because it's the process. Did we crash the boards the right way? Were we right on rotation? Did we make the one more pass? Did we touch the paint? Did we communicate with our teammate the right thing? Did we execute down the stretch? Those are all process things, they're not result things, and so we have to continue to focus on the process of what we're doing and how we can continue to make it sustainable and consistent for each one of these guys. So the conversations won't stop from now until the end of the year."
