**2. Micah Nori — "we're getting Ja Morant at the right time" — Micah Nori on Ja Morant's role and fit with the team** [01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SQL_8DZs2I0&t=83s)

Micah Nori: "I think that we're getting **Ja Morant** at the right time. And what I mean by that is he's coming to a situation where he feels like he has an opportunity to win basketball games again. And I think that the group that we have around him is very good. As far as I'm talking about the leadership people that are on the team, the shooting that's around him, the bigs that are around him. So I think that he's going to be in a position where he's going to be successful. And we know what he's capable of. And I think the one good thing when you talk to **Denny**, last year **Denny** as an All-Star had to do a lot of things by himself because of all the injuries that the Trail Blazers had. And now what we're able to do and adding willing passers like Ja allows him to basically get off the ball a little bit more. And I think that you've seen this preseason, maybe not as many shots for **Denny**, but he's been able to be much more efficient."

**3. Micah Nori — "there's really no loss of his, not to say explosiveness, but his quickness in that" — Micah Nori on Damian Lillard's return and role** [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SQL_8DZs2I0&t=148s)

Micah Nori: "He's getting to the free throw line, so that means he's getting himself downhill. Gets 13 free throw attempts, so there's really no loss of his, not to say explosiveness, but his quickness in that. But the other thing is, like I tell people, you know he hurt his lower body, not his upper body. He can still shoot the thing. So his game is not reliant on all that quickness. It's just more of him getting shots and us finding space for him. And I think the one difference that you'll see with Dame now is he'll be off the ball a little bit more than what he was earlier in his career. We can still play him obviously in pick and roll, we'd be idiots not to, but he doesn't have to do all that and we're able to find him open shots more so in the flow. And you see him competing at the defensive end as well."

**4. Micah Nori — "Hansson needs to play high-level basketball" — Micah Nori on Hansson's performance and development** [03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SQL_8DZs2I0&t=212s)

Micah Nori: "He goes three for four and he gets himself seven rebounds, three of them offensive, so we like that. And I think his first run, we put him in a little bit of a tough spot because it was **Horford** and then **Horford** was able to make I think three of his threes against him, but it was just because he was down the floor. But that's on us, that's not on him. So I was very happy with **Hansson**'s play. I think the one thing that we want to make sure, as we got to him 20 minutes tonight, **Hansson** needs to play high-level basketball and just as the more he gets used to the speed, the length, and the activity of the NBA game, the better he's going to be. Because the one thing that his skill level is being a hub, being a passer, that type of guy and the more he sees and feels it and plays, the better off he's going to be. So **Hansson** was great."
