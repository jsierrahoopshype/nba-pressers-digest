# De'Anthony Melton Previews Warriors 2026-27 Season &#124; Media Day — *Golden State Warriors*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ck6tYJSZ81Y](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ck6tYJSZ81Y)

_Speakers identified: De'Anthony Melton_

**1. De'Anthony Melton (Golden State Warriors) — "we want to go for a top three defense in this league" — De'Anthony Melton says the Warriors are aiming for a top three defense this season.** [05:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ck6tYJSZ81Y&t=324s)

De'Anthony Melton: "Frank, I think he is going to add an interesting piece for us. I think his expertise in terms of the defensive scheme and just his experience throughout the league is important. I think he is going to provide what we need to really take our defense to the next level. And I think for us, and other players probably can speak for it, we want to go for a top three defense in this league. So some would say we want to go for the best defense in the league, but I think ultimately just starting trying to get top three week after week and I think it is just weeks will start to add up and as long as we aim for that, I think we will be in a good position."
