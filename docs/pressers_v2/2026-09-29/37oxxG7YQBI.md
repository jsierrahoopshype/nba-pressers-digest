**1. Unidentified speaker — Unidentified speaker emphasizes the importance of increased athleticism and physicality from the team's bench.** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37oxxG7YQBI&t=12s)

Unidentified speaker: "It has been years since we have had I would say right now the back end of our team be this athletic, which really helps our starters. So there was a lot of physicality picking up full. We talked about that yesterday. There is a lot of questions about it. I brought it up a lot. It is what the NBA is now. **3. Unidentified speaker — Unidentified speaker explains the plan to transition DeRon Holmes II to the power forward position.** [08:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37oxxG7YQBI&t=522s)

Unidentified speaker: "I think for DeRon it is just to try to find a way to be a four because we know what he can do as a five. I started him last year because I liked him against traditional fives during that winter month where a lot of guys were out, and that allowed him to pick and pop, which is his special gift. The guy can really shoot it, but I think in our league he is going to have to be interchangeable and to play the four he is going to have to learn to guard smaller guys."
