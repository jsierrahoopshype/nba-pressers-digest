**1. Coach Adelman (Denver Nuggets) — Coach Adelman emphasizes the importance of increased athleticism and physicality from the team's bench.** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37oxxG7YQBI&t=12s)

Coach Adelman: "It has been years since we have had I would say right now the back end of our team be this athletic, which really helps our starters. So there was a lot of physicality picking up full. We talked about that yesterday. There is a lot of questions about it. I brought it up a lot. It is what the NBA is now. So to have guys like Bryce, Bryce Hopkins picking up Jamal, picking up Aaron and pushing them is what we need because you can be in shape, but you have to be in game shape and that includes physicality."

**3. Coach Adelman (Denver Nuggets) — Coach Adelman explains the plan to transition DeRon Holmes II to the power forward position.** [08:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37oxxG7YQBI&t=522s)

Coach Adelman: "I think for DeRon it is just to try to find a way to be a four because we know what he can do as a five. I started him last year because I liked him against traditional fives during that winter month where a lot of guys were out, and that allowed him to pick and pop, which is his special gift. The guy can really shoot it, but I think in our league he is going to have to be interchangeable and to play the four he is going to have to learn to guard smaller guys."

_Clip: 08:42-09:14 (32s)_

Social: Coach Adelman says the goal for DeRon Holmes II is to transition to the four position, noting that he needs to learn to guard smaller players to be more interchangeable.

**4. Aaron Gordon (Denver Nuggets) — Aaron Gordon explains the team's decision to hold training camp in Puerto Rico.** [15:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37oxxG7YQBI&t=903s)

Aaron Gordon: "We were going to go to LA, but I figured let us do something out of the norm, you know. Everybody goes to LA. Some of the other teams were overseas. I think a couple of the teams were in Paris and the team was in Slovenia. So I thought all right cool, let us travel too. So we went to Puerto Rico. It was nice, nice facilities over there and we got some work in and hung out on the beach and it was a good time, man. Just traveling does something for the mind, you know, and it was good for the young guys to be expansive."

_Clip: 15:03-15:41 (38s)_

Social: Aaron Gordon says the team chose to hold training camp in Puerto Rico to do something out of the norm, noting that the travel was good for the team's mindset and the young players.
