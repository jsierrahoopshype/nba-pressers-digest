**1. Gui Santos — "I am going to be the same Gui that brings in energy every night." — Gui Santos aims to reduce turnovers and improve his fit within the Warriors system.** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mx73pr0DVn0&t=144s)

Gui Santos: "I was turning the ball over more than I should and this year I am just trying to fit in the system and the things that he wanted me to do. Doesn't matter what is that paper, I will do it the best way that I can. I am going to be the same Gui that brings in energy every night. So I probably just take care of the ball and make sure that we got the same good things from last year and the bad things we just move forward and keep doing it better."

**2. Gui Santos — "if you help your team to win that's what really matters." — Gui Santos says he is comfortable coming off the bench as long as he helps the team win.** [03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mx73pr0DVn0&t=206s)

Gui Santos: "For me, it doesn't really make any difference if you start a game or just start in the bench because by the end of the day, if you help your team to win that's what really matters. So for me, that's a good thing. Now we got **Yax**, like a big forward, a guy that can help us a lot and he is ready to help right now. He won a championship last year, he came for the Summer League, we won the Summer League championship. So of course I am just happy to have more guys that can help us like **George**, we got **George**, we got **Bill**, there is a guy that you know, really good too that can help us. So for me, about the expectations or just starting the game or don't start the game, for me that's not going to change nothing. As soon as **Steve** call my name and I step on the court, I will bring the best that I can."

**3. Gui Santos — "if I play under control, every time I step into the paint, use the two feet" — Gui Santos credits watching Jimmy for his improved focus on playing under control.** [04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mx73pr0DVn0&t=271s)

Gui Santos: "I think play more under control. A lot of times I was trying to rush it a little bit last year and that's when I was turning the ball over, when I was committing a lot of mistakes. And this year, after being watching **Jimmy** for almost two years, I know that for me, if I play under control, every time I step into the paint, use the two feet to take the next decision. So for me, I think it's just play under control, watching **Jimmy**, watching these guys, how they do it, it's really what takes you to the next level."
