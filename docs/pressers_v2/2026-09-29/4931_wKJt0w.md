**1. John Collins — "One thing that I continually understand more and more is to just not take it personal." — John Collins discusses the importance of not taking business decisions personally in the NBA.** [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4931_wKJt0w&t=89s)

John Collins: "One thing that I continually understand more and more is to just not take it personal. You know it is a business at the end of the day and trying to just separate what is business and what is real. And you know that is also what I try to lead to our young guys, is to just try to separate the two. But when you come into the gym man, you lock all the way in and you work and you try to just like I said, use that to build a stronger mind. There is going to be a lot of stuff that you cannot control in this league and you know it starts early if you can block out the noise."
