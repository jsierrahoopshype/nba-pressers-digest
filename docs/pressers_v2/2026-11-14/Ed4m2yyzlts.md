**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "we committed to the things that we didn't do so well in the Denver game" — Tuomas Iisalo on team commitment and applying lessons from Denver loss** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ed4m2yyzlts&t=0s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "Super proud of our performance today. Had a tough one against Denver the other night and to come in here and have this type of performance, a full team performance. To me, we committed to the things that we didn't do so well in the Denver game and took the lessons and applied it today. Overall, the biggest difference from possession to possession is when we stick to the things we've practiced, the basketball looks really good in spurts already, which is very encouraging for early season."
