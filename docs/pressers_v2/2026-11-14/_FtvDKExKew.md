# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 11.13.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_FtvDKExKew](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_FtvDKExKew)

_Speakers identified: Taylor Jenkins_

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "we've got to play a perfect game for 48 minutes." — Taylor Jenkins on the need for a perfect 48-minute performance** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_FtvDKExKew&t=16s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I think it really starts with we've got to play a perfect game for 48 minutes. It's hard when you are double digits behind. We had a little resilience and response. Give the Magic credit, they played well. Wagner was great tonight, Banchero was great. Their defense, obviously limiting us in the paint, and our ability to try to knock down shots there. We've got to play with more force early in the game, not let them get going. We talked about that before the game, but we've got to play perfect for 48 minutes."

**2. Taylor Jenkins — "I thought Ryan was great, he was disruptive." — Taylor Jenkins praises Ryan Rollins' disruptive play and impact** [00:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_FtvDKExKew&t=51s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I thought Ryan was great, he was disruptive. I ended up playing him a super long stretch there in the second half just because of his impact. He was rebounding the ball well, he was impacting the drives, taking hits in one-on-one situations. I thought he was making some winning plays there, getting his hands on balls, and then I thought he did a good job on the offensive side, getting into the paint, finishing, distributing. Obviously, wish we could knock down a few more shots, but I loved the force that he was playing with on both sides."

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "he did a great job creating second-chance opportunities for us." — Taylor Jenkins on Miles Bridges' offensive performance and playmaking** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_FtvDKExKew&t=77s)

Taylor Jenkins: "Him knocking down shots and when teams switch on him and he's rolling, I thought he did a great job creating second-chance opportunities for us. We've got to continue to find ways to get him free, open looks. When he's spaced on the perimeter, knowing that team switch, can we do other actions to get him in those spots? I thought he played well. He was really great with the rebounding, 11 rebounds tonight, four offensive. I thought the three assists too, when he's at the top of the key and he's driving and kicking, he's just continuing to add to his game and it was good to see him have a good offensive performance tonight."

**4. Taylor Jenkins — "he's a stud of a player, plays with force, physicality." — Taylor Jenkins on Franz Wagner's triple-double and defensive challenges** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_FtvDKExKew&t=117s)

Taylor Jenkins: "That was a problem. We let it be too comfortable for him. He's a stud of a player, plays with force, physicality. He had a triple-double. He gets in, he can finish at the rim, hit floaters, was hitting threes tonight, finding his teammates, assists with his playmaking, kick-out for threes and lob passes. We just didn't play with enough force, we didn't execute our coverages on him. When we adjusted, we still made too many mistakes. He made us pay, he's a heck of a player."
