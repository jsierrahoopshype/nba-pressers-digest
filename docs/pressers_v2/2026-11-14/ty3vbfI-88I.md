# Hawks vs. Bulls Postgame Press Conference: Head Coach Quin Snyder — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty3vbfI-88I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty3vbfI-88I)

_Speakers identified: Quin Snyder_

**1. Quin Snyder — "We didn't play with force at the beginning of the game, and that impacts our defense." — Quin Snyder on the Hawks' lack of force and defensive intensity** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty3vbfI-88I&t=21s)

Quin Snyder: "We didn't play with force at the beginning of the game, and that impacts our defense. It's the complementary basketball with how your offense impacts your defense. When we're not forceful defensively or active and we're not running, we don't play well. We know the things we need to have in the front of our minds to focus on, and when we don't do that, we don't play well."
