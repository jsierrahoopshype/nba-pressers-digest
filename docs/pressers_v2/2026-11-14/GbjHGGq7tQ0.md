# Postgame Media Availability with Coach Daigneault, JMac, and Shai | OKC Thunder | November 13, 2026 — *Oklahoma City Thunder*

_Speakers identified: Mark Daigneault, Jalen Williams, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander_

**1. Mark Daigneault — "I think the challenge in that situation is can you just stay in character and double down" — Mark Daigneault on the Thunder's first-half struggles and second-half recovery** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GbjHGGq7tQ0&t=3s)

Mark Daigneault: "I thought the first half we missed some free throws. We were two for 17 from three. We had those passes out of bounds that it just felt like we were off rhythm. I think the challenge in that situation is can you just stay in character and double down on your identity and your stuff. And I thought we did that and let the game turn in the second half. Didn't take us long to get back in it and then to take a lead and get control of it for much of the second half. So I thought just the poise and the maturity in us coming out of a first half that was uncharacteristic and didn't feel great was a positive tonight."
