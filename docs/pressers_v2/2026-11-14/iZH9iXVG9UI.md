**5. Kenny Atkinson — "They were killing us in the short roll, I felt like." — Kenny Atkinson on defensive struggles against the Heat** [02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iZH9iXVG9UI&t=177s)

Kenny Atkinson: "They were killing us in the short roll, I felt like. **Bam Adebayo** had that stretch there in the third quarter where they're getting it to him in the pocket and he's hitting those eight-footers. They're really good at those long paint twos. We made an adjustment, we tried to bring our big up higher to take away the pocket, I thought that was a little better, you know, cut it to six. But again, we got to stop it, we got to finish the play with rebounds."
