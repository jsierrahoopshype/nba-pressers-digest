**1. Jock Landale — "the chemistry pace has been really good. It's always a process just day by day, week by week." — Jock Landale on building team chemistry with younger Hawks players** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y8eTjlzUS6M&t=13s)

Jock Landale: "The chemistry pace has been really good. It's always a process just day by day, week by week. As we go, our offense being so free-moving and read-based, things will slow down and become easier because right now we are trying to instill structure into a chaotic environment. Tonight was our best showing and guys like CJ and stuff were saying it looked like we were finally playing some good, real basketball."

**2. Jock Landale — "naturally that's what I am is someone who will talk a lot" — Jock Landale on his role in improving team communication** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y8eTjlzUS6M&t=86s)

Jock Landale: "From day one when I got here midway through last season, they were really talking a lot about how this group lacks communication in offense, defense, off the court, on the court. I have just really tried to, or not tried to, but I think naturally that's what I am is someone who will talk a lot. Whether it's in the locker room or on the court, guys tend to tell me I don't shut up. I really try and bring that to this environment and help instill confidence into other people that being loud is actually a good thing in this kind of environment."

**3. Jock Landale — "having the confidence to press up on that, knowing that's what they were trying to get to" — Jock Landale on defensive aggressiveness and reading the opponent** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y8eTjlzUS6M&t=157s)

Jock Landale: "They continued to run that pitch action to the big in the middle of the court, so having the confidence to press up on that, knowing that's what they were trying to get to, made it easy to read what they were doing. A couple of those steals just fell into my lap, so I don't want to take too much credit for those, but it's a fine line in preseason because things are called differently, they are a lot stricter."

**4. Jock Landale — "we're so versatile in how we can defend teams and we're strong, we're big, we're fast" — Jock Landale on the Hawks' high-octane defensive identity** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y8eTjlzUS6M&t=287s)

Jock Landale: "It's a really high-octane up-the-court defense where a lot of teams throughout the course of the NBA season fall back into preserving. We have now structures where we pick up full court, three-quarter court, and half court. We tend to air on the side of always being a full-court pick-up team just because we're so versatile in how we can defend teams and we're strong, we're big, we're fast. That will be our identity. We've had some growing pains there initially, but I think the desire is there to be really good."
