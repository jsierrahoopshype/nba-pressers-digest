## What Doug Christie wants Kings’ Darius Acuff Jr. to learn Friday | NBC Sports California — *Sacramento Kings*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0)

_Speakers identified: Doug Christie, Unidentified speaker_

**1. Doug Christie — "Pace means how quickly you're moving in the half court. That's a huge one." — Doug Christie on the importance of half-court pace** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=4s)

Doug Christie: "Pace doesn't necessarily mean pivot, tear. It doesn't mean that as my college coach would say. Pace means how quickly you're moving in the half court. That's a huge one. You have to make sure that you're cutting, you're moving, you're reading. If players are getting above you on splits, you're making quick cuts. That's pace also. So there's the full court pace, but then there's the half court pace, which probably is even more important because when guys are trying to grab hold and control, you got to knock their hands off and run through them."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=4s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=4s)

**2. Unidentified speaker — "The league has gotten a lot more steals over the last three years." — The league-wide increase in steals over the last three years** [00:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=52s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "I watch you play and you got a lot of steals and that was cool to see. The league has gotten a lot more steals over the last three years than most of this 21st century. So I wanted to know from your perspective what you're noticing, what you're seeing, why that might be."

**Doug Christie:** "Maybe a lot of gambling, but also maybe guys aren't as good as passing. So guys are just shooting the gaps and different things. That one is hard to say. But I also say the skill level of the player today, guys are very skillful. I look at a guy like Matisse and his reading is like a defensive back, like a safety. He is just back there because as you put a player in a position, if you study, which I think there's a lot of film and a lot of guys do, once you come off of an action, it starts to limit your choices. And not everybody can make every pass."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=52s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=52s)

**3. Doug Christie — "Once you come off of an action, it starts to limit your choices." — How film study limits passing choices for players** [01:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=118s)

Doug Christie: "If you come off a step up and all of a sudden there's someone in the corner, that's a really hard pass to make. So now I'm gambling that I'm going to cut off a different pass. And I think that guys with their video and their studying are doing a better job."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=118s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=118s)

**4. Doug Christie — "Now that they've added Walker Kessler, they're more in the drop." — How Walker Kessler impacts defensive schemes** [02:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=170s)

Doug Christie: "The Lakers the last couple years were one of the more frequent teams to switch on defensively. And now that they've added **Walker Kessler**, they're more in the drop. As a perimeter defender, when I know that there's somebody back there that's in that key and they're protecting the rim, it's like having a big brother. All of a sudden I'm bolstered. I feel super confident."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=170s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=170s)

**5. Doug Christie — "More than anything, it's probably above the break contested three." — Defensive strategy for guarding Luka Doncic** [03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=232s)

Doug Christie: "More than anything, it's probably above the break contested three. But once he gets a rhythm, the way that he calculates the game is just super impressive because if he doesn't hit a shot, then he gets to the free throw line to see it go through. He's looking for a layup, he's reading the defense. So there's a lot of different aspects of it. You just have to try to give him as many looks as you possibly can. For players of that type of level, you can't show them the same thing consistently because he just processes the information at such a high level."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=232s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=232s)

**6. Unidentified speaker — "How good of an opportunity is it for a guy like Darius to kind of watch?" — Darius Acuff Jr. learning from matching up against Luka Doncic** [04:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=282s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Seeing Luka this many times in the same division, how good of an opportunity is it for a guy like Darius to kind of watch and kind of learn matching up with guys so often?"

**Doug Christie:** "Definitely. I think watching how he processes the game is huge for a young player. Darius, last time we played them, we saw tilts, we saw blitzes. They read it. He has seen pretty much every defense for a young player. I haven't seen that as much, but I think that shows what the league thinks of him as a young player. He can really score the basketball and play. So he's now looking at Luka, how does he process that information? How does he go about seeing what the different defenses are and then manipulating them to the benefit of his team?"

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=282s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwjbf47H0&t=282s)
