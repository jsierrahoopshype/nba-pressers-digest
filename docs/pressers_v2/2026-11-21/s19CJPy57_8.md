# Hawks vs. Hornets Postgame Press Conference: Head Coach Quin Snyder — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19CJPy57_8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19CJPy57_8)

_Speakers identified: Quin Snyder_

**1. Quin Snyder — "when we turn the ball over, it's hard to find any aggression" — Quin Snyder on how turnovers impact the team's defensive aggression** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19CJPy57_8&t=16s)

Quin Snyder: "When we turn the ball over, it's hard to find any aggression because usually it's a number break, three-on-two, two-on-one, whatever it is, and we're taking it out of the net. So I think when we started taking better care of the basketball, and the other thing is defensive rebounding, when you get, you play good defense through a possession and you don't rebound the ball, it can be deflating. I think those two things, if we're taking care of that, we have a chance to run and we have more energy."

**3. Quin Snyder — "he's running without the promise of a reward" — Quin Snyder on the timely energy and impact of Zubac** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19CJPy57_8&t=114s)

Quin Snyder: "It's timely as well. There's plays, whether it's a block or, you see him dunk, but what you don't notice all the time is he sprinted to get to that position. Those are the things in my mind that are hard, because he's running without the promise of a reward. Sometimes, delayed gratification is hard. The things that our team needs to do to be good, that's consistent. There's not a payout in the moment. You have to run even when you don't get the ball, you have to get a hit even when you don't get the rebound, you have to space even if you don't get the shot. Those things are important to him, and as a result, he puts himself in position to make those kind of plays."

**5. Quin Snyder — "we have to be shifted to protect the paint" — Quin Snyder on the team's defensive strategy and ball movement** [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19CJPy57_8&t=243s)

Quin Snyder: "We've been able to generate some stuff on occasion in the backcourt and that wasn't as available tonight. There's a fine line. We want to really, we have to be shifted to protect the paint. That said, there's a fine line. If we can start being shifted, we have a chance to drift out and make some of those plays in passing lanes, particularly guys that have length. We've seen **Jalen** do that recently. If we can anticipate some of those situations, they do a good job of moving the ball. The thing about ball movement, and we feel this too, is the more passes you make, the greater the likelihood that you could turn it over. So that's something for us that's really important, being able to anticipate those situations."

**6. Quin Snyder — "the thing that he did tonight where he was really impactful was he played without the ball" — Quin Snyder on Jalen's perimeter shooting and impact without the ball** [05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19CJPy57_8&t=330s)

Quin Snyder: "He knows that we want him to keep shooting. The catch-and-shoots that he hit were just, you know, quick decision. I thought the thing that he did tonight where he was really impactful was he played without the ball. That's important for him and for us, particularly when people are game-planning for him or there's a matchup on the ball. His ability to do both, to get out in transition, to get to the foul line, offensive rebound, all those things add up and I think it calms your nervous system a little bit too. He knows how we feel about him and his shooting the ball is important for us, so it's good to see a couple go in. No question about it, but I don't want to obsess about it either because there's lots of ways he can help us."

**7. Quin Snyder — "Diabate was part of it" — Quin Snyder on the team's offensive rebounding success** [06:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19CJPy57_8&t=398s)

Quin Snyder: "**Diabate** was part of it. They not only do they have, and he and Calkrunner, two guys that it's very difficult to keep off the glass by yourself, and if you are able to put a second body on, which we didn't do enough, when we did, he may not get it, but then everybody's got to clean it up. The first part of that we need to do better, and then the second part of it, I'd like to get to the second part where he actually doesn't get it. But credit to him as far as how relentless he is on the glass. I thought as the game went on we had some possessions, and those will be guard rebounds. I think **RayJ** got five of them. He's a small guy on the court, but everybody we got to have everybody in the mix to be effective on the defensive glass."
