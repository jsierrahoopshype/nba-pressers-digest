# Hawks vs. Hornets Postgame Press Conference: Dyson Daniels — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DS4hICFyTQ8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DS4hICFyTQ8)

_Speakers identified: Dyson Daniels_

**1. Dyson Daniels — "everyone just stepped up. Obviously we don't have a true point guard out there, but we're just pushing the pace." — Dyson Daniels on team adjustments without Trae Young and Dejounte Murray** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DS4hICFyTQ8&t=9s)

Dyson Daniels: "Everyone stepped up. Obviously we don't have a true point guard out there, but we're just pushing the pace. Everyone is getting touches. We have guys that can play with the ball, guys that can play off the ball, so I think everyone just filled those voids. **Rayjon Tucker** has been really good coming in as well, playing some good minutes for us. **Kobe Bufkin** is coming in as well, playing some really good minutes. Everyone has stepped up, it's been good."

**2. Dyson Daniels — "Zubac coming in, not backing down. That's what we like, another big fella getting in the mix." — Dyson Daniels on Ivica Zubac's impact and recent scuffle** [01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DS4hICFyTQ8&t=66s)

**3. Dyson Daniels — "we kind of just took our foot off the gas and gave them too many good looks." — Dyson Daniels on team message during halftime deficit** [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DS4hICFyTQ8&t=101s)

Dyson Daniels: "Towards the end of the first quarter and the start of the second, we kind of just took our foot off the gas and gave them too many good looks, and we didn't push the pace enough. Going into halftime, we got some momentum, and we knew we just had to stick with it. We played these guys the second game of the season and blew them out, so we knew they were going to come out hungry and ready to put their best foot forward. We just had to weather that storm, and then once we got on top, we were able to turn them over a little bit, get out and run, and get our game going. The second half was a good half for us."

**4. Dyson Daniels — "I kind of like to sense when the team needs a little lift or a little momentum." — Dyson Daniels on his role in shifting team momentum** [02:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DS4hICFyTQ8&t=151s)

Dyson Daniels: "I kind of like to sense when the team needs a little lift or a little momentum and try and create a play on the defensive end, whether it be a steal or get a big rebound and get out and push, get someone a good look. I kind of sensed that we were a little down after that first quarter and we needed some energy. Sometimes all it takes is one guy to pick up the team's energy and other guys follow. Being able to try and get the guys going at the start of the second was what I came out with the mindset of."
