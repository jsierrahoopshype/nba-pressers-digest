# Which players made Kings rookie Darius Acuff Jr. starstruck | NBC Sports California — *Sacramento Kings*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c)

_Speakers identified: Morgan Ragan, Darius Acuff Jr., Deuce Mason_

**1. Darius Acuff Jr. — "I understand how he was. He is strong. He is strong. But Giannis for sure, Luka, Luka be doing some stuff that's different." — Darius Acuff Jr. on facing Giannis Antetokounmpo, Luka Doncic, and Damian Lillard** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c&t=4s)

**Morgan Ragan:** "Has anyone just blown you away in person, like sharing the floor with them and being like, oh god, this is actually different than what I actually have viewed on film?"

**Darius Acuff Jr.:** "**Giannis Antetokounmpo**. I understand how he was. He is strong. He is strong. But Giannis for sure, Luka, Luka be doing some stuff that's different. Especially this season right now. But if you ask me after the season, I give you more. So yeah, but definitely them two so far. And then, of course, **Damian Lillard**."

**Deuce Mason:** "Yeah. Shot making for sure. His shot making is I mean, it's insane."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c&t=4s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c&t=4s)

**2. Darius Acuff Jr. — "I almost got it with Damian Lillard last night. If he would have hit probably like two more threes on me, he probably would have had it." — Darius Acuff Jr. on his defensive matchup against Damian Lillard** [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c&t=57s)

**Deuce Mason:** "It was I almost got it with **Damian Lillard** last night. If he would have hit probably like two more threes on me, he probably would have had it. But he I think he hit two on me and then I fouled him on two, so it was it didn't look as crazy. But when he hit them two step backs, it was starting to look a little crazy."

**Darius Acuff Jr.:** "So if he would have hit like two more, that probably would have been it."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c&t=57s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c&t=57s)

**3. Darius Acuff Jr. — "He is one of the guys that I watch because I see a lot of similarities in our game, especially as far as scoring as point guards." — Darius Acuff Jr. on learning from Damian Lillard's game** [01:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJiKV6-ME0c&t=88s)

**Deuce Mason:** "When you hear someone like **Damian Lillard** talk about you like that, the last time he was in town, I have to imagine you grew up watching him. What is going through your mind when one of the legends of the game is kind of giving you some love for how you go about your business?"

**Darius Acuff Jr.:** "It is great because that is one of the guys and I am pretty sure he has seen it on social media of how much I talk about him. He is one of the guys that I watch because I see a lot of similarities in our game, especially as far as scoring as point guards and how much gravity we create. So it is definitely great. We seen it last night how great of a player he still is and you know, I am still learning. I am still watching those guys no matter what."
