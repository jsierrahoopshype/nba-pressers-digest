**1. Maxime Aubin — "he wants to do less and do it better and to repeat the same movements again and again" — Victor Wembanyama's focus on simplifying his isolation game** [01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfLnhUdA98A&t=66s)

Maxime Aubin: "I was asking **Victor Wembanyama** about his isolation game. He said during media day that he wanted to really improve his isolation game. I asked him after five games how he feels about it, is he improving, in what area? He was basically trying to say that he is trying to simplify his game. This is still a work in progress as we can see. The whole point was for him to say that he wants to do less and do it better and to repeat the same movements again and again. Once again, he is not there yet. I think that is the craziest part about Wembanyama, he can average 35, 10 rebounds and four blocks, and you watch his offensive game and you are like, it is a mess, we do not understand what is going on."

**3. Maxime Aubin — "he feels a lot about what is happening to the young generation in France" — Victor Wembanyama's desire to represent the youth of France** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfLnhUdA98A&t=195s)

Maxime Aubin: "He had the short summer, the NBA finals went all the way to mid-June, then he played for the French national team this summer. What he told me recently is that he can have the FOMO a little bit of France. He wished the summer could be longer, just to spend more time there, but also to prepare better for the NBA season. Also, the whole situation in France, we have a lot of protests right now and he feels a lot about what is happening to the young generation in France. He wants to have a voice, so I think he kind of wished he was there just to be a voice for the young people of France."

**4. Maxime Aubin — "maybe I am the villain but I think it is a pretty good news" — Victor Wembanyama's perspective on being labeled a villain** [05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfLnhUdA98A&t=309s)

Maxime Aubin: "It is a little crazy to think that he is a villain, to be honest with you. I am really trying to be objective and I have watched the finals again and again. They all play hard, they all play tough, they are rough sometimes. It went both ways for me with many players and I think Wembanyama said it for himself. He said, maybe I am the villain but I think it is a pretty good news, that means they care about me or something like that. That is how I feel about it. It is funny actually, because two days ago I have a friend who lives in New York, I used to live in New York, and every time I post something about Wembanyama and the Spurs, I get a lot of shit from Knicks fans saying Wembanyama is the worst."

**5. Maxime Aubin — "he is still trying to adjust to the FIBA basketball, so there is way less space" — Victor Wembanyama's adjustment to FIBA basketball** [06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfLnhUdA98A&t=403s)

Maxime Aubin: "He played four games this summer. Two were just friendly games, and they were basically preparing for the World Cup qualifiers. I went to the one they played in Paris against Slovenia. **Luka Doncic** was not playing, unfortunately. They won against Slovenia, he had a great game. I think he is still trying to adjust to the FIBA basketball, so there is way less space. It is very interesting for him, and especially to prepare him for the NBA season because he gets beat up every time, he does not have access to the paint at all. So things that he can experience in the NBA, I think it is good for him."
