**1. Charles Lee — "You had 11 returning guys and nine new guys. I want to build on where we started" — Charles Lee on balancing returning players and new roster additions** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t69VSaN4YE0&t=69s)

Charles Lee: "The little bit of a difference was you had 11 returning guys and nine new guys. The older guys, I want to build on where we started from last year, but you are also just all on the same page from a fundamental standpoint. That is where it all came back to. The new guys, you have to reintroduce a lot of your core principles so that they understand where you are. You have one group that can go a little accelerated, one group that is a little slower, but because they are working so well together, and because our coaches do such a phenomenal job of getting these guys up to speed, we are able to move at a pretty good clip."

**2. Charles Lee — "He has got an edge. He has got a hunger to him. He is a really smart basketball player" — Charles Lee on the value of veteran leadership and work ethic** [02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t69VSaN4YE0&t=128s)

Charles Lee: "It is huge. It is why he has lasted so long in the league because he has got an edge. He has got a hunger to him. He is a really smart basketball player too, and I think that is what these guys pick up on. The fact that every day he comes in here, he works his tail off. He is listening to every drill, he is asking questions during drills, he is pulling guys to the side and giving them valuable information. When the ball is tipped up, he is in the right spots and he is diving on the floor. Between him and I think **Dennis** and **Naz** and these guys, the fact that they come into a practice day like today and they do not care about being tired, they care about the everyday process, and that is what we love about them."

**3. Charles Lee — "We are just going to continue to try to build on our identity, all the habits" — Charles Lee on goals for the upcoming preseason games** [03:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t69VSaN4YE0&t=196s)

Charles Lee: "Number one, we are just going to continue to try to build on our identity, all the habits that we have. It is really good to go through practice situations and drills, you slow it down, you teach it, and then during these preseason games you get a chance to go out and do it versus another unscripted opponent that is going to try to test you in a lot of different ways. I look forward to seeing that. I also just look forward to seeing our group, like you said, play with a bunch of different lineups and let us see what combinations work really well together."

**4. Charles Lee — "Naz has been really exciting. The whole group is really exciting because we have a very deep team" — Charles Lee on the versatility and offensive potential of Naz Reid** [04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t69VSaN4YE0&t=240s)

Charles Lee: "**Naz** has been really exciting. The whole group is really exciting because we have a very deep team, a very versatile team. **Naz** in particular, like you said, to see what he is able to do offensively, using him as a pick-and-roll ball-handler, pick-and-roll screener, trying to get him to crash the offensive glass a little bit more, obviously his ability to shoot from three and potentially play versus some mismatches down in the post early in the clock and late in the clock, it is really exciting. It gives the coaching staff and teammates the ability to really find advantages and punish advantages in a lot of different ways. It is huge. And then defensively, we added another guy that is long and athletic, and the ability on the perimeter to scramble and cover as much ground as he does has been really impressive."
