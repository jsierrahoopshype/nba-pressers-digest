**3. Grayson Allen — "everything has to be perfect coverage-wise defensively, offensively, spacing, matchups, getting to spots that you want" — Grayson Allen on the team's defensive execution and rebounding** [01:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7z5xKTrMLg0&t=110s)

Grayson Allen: "we executed our coverages. On their side out-of-bounds play, we had a couple guys make good plays. Naz got two deflections on the ball just to throw them off a little bit. The margins are really small in the games, so everything has to be perfect coverage-wise defensively, offensively, spacing, matchups, getting to spots that you want. So I felt like we executed really well. Guys made some shots offensively, we made some free throws, but then really executing defensively, our switching and out-of-bounds plays and getting some stops too to keep them out of it."
