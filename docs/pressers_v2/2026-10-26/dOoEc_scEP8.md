**1. Sam Presti — "He's got a fractured fibula, dislocated ankle on the left side." — Jalen Williams' injury update and outlook** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOoEc_scEP8&t=13s)

Sam Presti: "I want to talk about Jalen. I was with him last night and this morning. He's in great spirits and is extremely optimistic about the situation. What he's dealing with is he's got a fractured fibula, dislocated ankle on the left side. This is an injury that generally we see more in football players. I've spent a lot of time speaking to different doctors, his agents, us, his family, and it's a surgical situation. That's the way you deal with this type of injury, and we'll have a timeline for everybody once we get through the surgery. I can't really give you that right now because it would be inappropriate. We've got to see where we are after that's done."
