**2. Pete Nance — "I think it just helps us think about the game more" — Pete Nance explains how new, constrained practice drills help players think better during games.** [01:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FDELf-YtVVE&t=115s)

Pete Nance: "Putting us in different situations. A lot of the times we do just, you know, growing up or whatever we do a lot of the same drills, but changing the drills, taking one person out of those drills, having us do three on four stuff or whatever we are having to guard in tougher situations or having to do offense in stressful situations with constraints and we cannot do this or cannot do that and I think it just helps us think about the game more instead of just kind of the same drills we have been doing all our lives that are not necessarily conducive to the game."
