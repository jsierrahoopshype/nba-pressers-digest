# Denver Nuggets Training Camp Day Two | Practice Media Availability — *Denver Nuggets*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U)

_Speakers identified: Coach Adelman, Jamal Murray, Marvin Bagley III_

**1. Coach Adelman — "Instead of trying to survive in your career, you're actually out here trying to fit into a good team." — Coach Adelman praises Marvin Bagley III for his adjustment to a winning team environment.** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=69s)

Coach Adelman: "Marvin has special skills. I think for him, he hasn't played meaningful basketball in a long time. People forget what that means to players. Instead of trying to survive in your career, you're actually out here trying to fit into a good team. And I think he's really enjoying it and playing with Tyus, the second unit is fun. You get rewarded for running hard."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=69s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=69s)

**2. Coach Adelman — "I want to keep him there and get him comfortable with that spot." — Coach Adelman clarifies his plan to keep Thomas Bryant at the five position.** [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=152s)

Coach Adelman: "As a five right now. I was really impressed with Spencer as a four last year. I think the four spot, TB is really talented, he's got a chance. I want to keep him there and get him comfortable with that spot. In a pinch obviously he could do it, but he's a traditional five to me."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=152s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=152s)

**3. Coach Adelman — Coach Adelman highlights Alpha Diallo's defensive potential and basketball IQ.** [04:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=299s)

Coach Adelman: "He can guard. He's got a special skill. He doesn't get screened. You can tell he played overseas, the physicality of what that is. Honestly, I think offensively he's been impressive in the sense of kind of like Spencer, he knows where to be at the right time and that's big for him. If he can be a guy that guards and knows how to cut and move, playing in space, he has a chance to play."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=299s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=299s)

**4. Jamal Murray — "Me and Tyus have a better relationship than we did five summers ago" — Jamal Murray notes his improving chemistry with teammate Tyus Jones.** [08:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=525s)

Jamal Murray: "Me and Tyus have a better relationship than we did five summers ago or five months ago, so it's just gradual."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=525s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=525s)

**5. Marvin Bagley III — "I think today was better than yesterday overall, just as a team." — Marvin Bagley III sees improvement in the team's transition defense on day two of training camp.** [14:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=876s)

Marvin Bagley III: "Transition defense, positioning ourselves on when the shots go up, who crashes, trying to get our rules together and execute them better than we did yesterday. I think today was better than yesterday overall, just as a team. I think we were a little bit more sharper, obviously it's day two, but we just got to keep continuing on the stack days and get better every day."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=876s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=876s)

**6. Marvin Bagley III — "I can play both. My game is I can shoot the three, make quick decisions in the pick and roll" — Marvin Bagley III expresses comfort playing both the four and five positions.** [16:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=976s)

Marvin Bagley III: "I can play both. Obviously last few teams I've been on, I've been the five, playing a lot of five, back up five. But I feel like I can play both. My game is I can shoot the three, make quick decisions in the pick and roll, you know, just being able to protect the rim, move my feet. I could do both and like I said, whatever coach needs me to do, I'll be ready to do."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=976s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiW_DtkHs9U&t=976s)

Marvin Bagley III: "I'm getting it, I'm picking it up. You know, that was part of the reason I wanted to come back, to come to Denver a little earlier before camp, so I could you know get used to the plays, talk to the coaches about what we want to do, defensively, offensively.
