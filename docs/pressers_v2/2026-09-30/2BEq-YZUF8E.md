**4. Donovan Mitchell — "adding a guy like Peyton Watson changes us in a major way" — Donovan Mitchell on the defensive potential of the Cavaliers** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BEq-YZUF8E&t=346s)

Donovan Mitchell: "I think the biggest thing is our length, our versatility. Adding a guy like **Peyton Watson** changes us in a major way. Obviously you have **Jaylen Tyson** on the perimeter as well, even Mario. Just our size, our athleticism, being able to implement different coverages, different schemes, I think will definitely be able to help us come down the stretch, not be so one-track minded defensively. And obviously having Evan and Jaylen, they do their thing back there for us as always, and they lead us. But I think with the addition of Peyton and Jaylen, I think those two are going to elevate us from the perimeter spot, and then myself, **James**, **Sam** continue to do what we do on the ball, and then obviously having those two guys behind us to protect us, protect all the mistakes, always helps."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BEq-YZUF8E&t=346s

**5. Donovan Mitchell — "Na'Qwan and I talked a lot this summer. I have sent him a lot of graphs" — Donovan Mitchell on his mentorship of teammate Na'Qwan Tomlin** [08:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BEq-YZUF8E&t=536s)

Donovan Mitchell: "Na'Qwan and I talked a lot this summer. I have sent him a lot of graphs that kind of show where different things about where he was in his career compared to other guys in the league. There were some things that were motivating and some things that were like, you are doing a really good job here. Understanding you have to have that relationship and build on that. I think for him, he came into camp one of our best defenders naturally, obviously, but I think he is taking that to another level. His shooting, his confidence in his shooting is obviously huge, and I am not saying he is going to come out and shoot 40 percent, and I told him that, we are not expecting you to come out and shoot 40 percent this year, that takes time and progression. But just to see how much time and effort he has put into his jump shooting is definitely an improvement, and even his decision-making as well."
