**5. Tyus Jones — "Teams are picking us up full, trying to hound us, trying to not let us get into our sets." — Tyus Jones on the value of competitive training camp practices** [08:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VenZuHngv-Q&t=481s)

**6. Tyus Jones — "Nikola is obviously an incredible passer, probably the best passer in the league." — Tyus Jones on developing chemistry with Nikola Jokic** [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VenZuHngv-Q&t=606s)

Tyus Jones: "Nikola is obviously an incredible passer, probably the best passer in the league and sees plays two or three steps ahead. And as a point guard, someone who feels like I see plays two or three steps ahead as well, for me that is exciting and just trying to jump-start that chemistry in the two-man game. And then like I said with Marvin, we have played together as well as a lob threat, he is rolling a little more dynamically towards the rim. So just getting that timing down on, you know, kind of when to hit him, when not to, our screen angles and stuff like that. So that is what training camp is for, just trying to build that chemistry with both of those guys."

**7. Christian Braun — "I jump off my left leg my whole life, and it was my left ankle." — Christian Braun on his physical recovery and explosive movement** [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VenZuHngv-Q&t=852s)

Christian Braun: "For me, I jump off my left leg my whole life, and it was my left ankle. And I have jumped specifically off my left leg my whole life. So a lot of it was just kind of learning how to do different things, maybe jump off two feet, maybe play off your right leg. And you could see sometimes my timing was just way off. So I think I learned a lot about that. I learned a lot about, you know, different ways to play, different ways to get to, you know, different things. And it will be good for me, I think in the long run."

**8. Christian Braun — "I need to be a guy where Jamal is looking for me to bring it up." — Christian Braun on the team's offensive consistency and growth** [17:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VenZuHngv-Q&t=1072s)

Christian Braun: "Last year we needed to be better in the playoffs obviously, so you want to see more consistency. But like I said, me bringing the ball up and taking care of the ball and not making it just on Jamal or just on Nikola. You know, having more people do more things. So it is not the same thing all year. And I think having guys healthy really helps that too. And it gives us more options in those things. So doing it now will make it easier when we do it in the playoffs. I think especially for me in the playoffs it was weird because you are hurt, you come back in, you are trying to play, you are trying to make sure your ankle is good. And then you get in a playoff series with a really good team. And you have not brought the ball up, you know, all year. So you are just not doing it. And Jamal is looking for me to bring it up, you know, to help him out. And I know that will help him in his legs in the fourth quarter of a big playoff game. And it will help him, you know, not have to do so much."
