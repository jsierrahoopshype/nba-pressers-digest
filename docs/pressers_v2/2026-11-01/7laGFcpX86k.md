**1. Myles Turner — "The toughest part for all of us is mentally, shots aren't falling, and that in turn affects your defense." — Myles Turner on the team's mental struggle with shooting slumps** [00:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7laGFcpX86k&t=2s)

Myles Turner: "The toughest part for all of us is mentally, shots aren't falling, and that in turn affects your defense. Your effort on defense, now you're thinking about the last play, and the maturity of this group has to be getting past that. Sometimes you're not going to hit shots, but if you're able to hang your hat on your defense, it makes our lives a lot easier. Again, it's no knocking our players. I have confidence in our guys hitting shots, but when it's not happening, you can't let it trickle down to our effort on the other end of the floor."

**2. Myles Turner — "You have to trust the process. We've been playing this game our entire lives, it comes to a point where it gets mental." — Myles Turner on maintaining confidence during shooting slumps** [00:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7laGFcpX86k&t=47s)

Myles Turner: "You have to trust the process. We've been playing this game our entire lives, it comes to a point where it gets mental. It's not about ability. You have five, six-year-plus veterans in the league and there are going to be times where you're not hitting shots. It just is what it is, but you can't lose focus, lose confidence in who you are as a player because that's the first thing that happens. I'd like to call it the law of averages. A lot of us in here are 35, 40 percent shooters, so while maybe it's bad now, as the season goes along, you hit your averages. You just got to keep shooting shots."
