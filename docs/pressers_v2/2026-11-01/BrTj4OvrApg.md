**4. Nick Nurse — "we got to raise those levels up to kind of average and we wouldn't see a lot of those double-digit deficits" — Nick Nurse on the team's resilience in overcoming double-digit deficits** [01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BrTj4OvrApg&t=82s)

Nick Nurse: "I talked to Tyrese after the game and said, you had another comeback. He said, I don't want to keep doing this. As a coach, it's a double-edged sword, the resiliency of coming back from down, but how do you interpret the ability to come back from double-digit deficits like that? Well, I think there's a little bit going on, lineup changes and all those things. I think it gets a little clunky for us, etc. We just got to keep fighting through it and I think that's all we're really doing. We just continue to keep playing and there are stretches where, I keep talking about the stretches of play, the stretches when it gets kind of bad, we got to raise those levels up to kind of average and we wouldn't see a lot of those double-digit deficits. That's a big thing, but I don't really read that much into it now. I'm just glad we keep competing and find a way."
