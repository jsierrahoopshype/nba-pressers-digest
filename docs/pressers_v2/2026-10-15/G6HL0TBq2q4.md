**1. Jamahl Mosley — "They were communicating with one another, flying around, covering for one another, doing it best without fouling." — Jamahl Mosley on Pelicans' defensive connectivity and communication** [05:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G6HL0TBq2q4&t=353s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Let's start with defense in the first half. It seemed like that might have been the best defensive half that we've seen so far in preseason. What did you think of the way the guys were connected and even into the third quarter when you had the rotation guys?"

**Jamahl Mosley:** "I thought our guys were very connected. That's a great word. They were communicating with one another, flying around, covering for one another, doing it best without fouling. **Unidentified speaker:** "It seems like everybody, though, that that connection, that communication is is rising right now, kind of throughout the team, yeah?"

**Jamahl Mosley:** "Well, they've all taken the challenge on and I think they believe that that's what's going to get you over the top. **Unidentified speaker:** "Under 20 in the first and the third, is that the most this team looked like you want them to look like defensively tonight?"

**4. Jamahl Mosley — "End of games is going to be so important for us to continue to stay locked in." — Jamahl Mosley on end-of-game decision making and communication** [07:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G6HL0TBq2q4&t=457s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "The fourth quarter, how important is it for those guys to be in situations like that where you have to hold on to a lead, you have to have, you know, don't foul in the situation, you know, defend this three with point whatever left, just how important is it for those guys?"

**Jamahl Mosley:** "It's critical. You can watch it on film. We talk about end of game decision making, end of game communication, talked about it in the locker room. Yes, we did win the game, but there are so many things that we can clean up. I think the understand what we're doing in the huddle, coming out of the huddle with the right lines of communication, but again, the compete level of each one of these guys is at a high level and that's what we're asking them to do and I think we can take it to up another notch. But end of games is going to be so important for us to continue to stay locked in in what we're doing in these situations."
