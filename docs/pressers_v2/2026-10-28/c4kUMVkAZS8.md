**1. Ja Morant — "It came down to stops. I messaged to each other the whole game was the first team" — Ja Morant on the keys to Portland's clutch time victory** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c4kUMVkAZS8&t=12s)

**Ashley:** "In a game filled with buckets of every variety, what did this win come down to in clutch time?"

**Ja Morant:** "It came down to stops. I messaged to each other the whole game was the first team to lock in on defense to win the game. Obviously, coming out of the half down five, not playing how we want to, we had to take a step in that direction. Third quarter wasn't pretty, but we got it done in the fourth and that's all that matters."

**2. Ja Morant — "It's a blessing to be able to play around one of the best shooters of all time" — Ja Morant on the chemistry of the Portland roster** [00:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c4kUMVkAZS8&t=42s)

**Ashley:** "You, Damian, Denny, the list goes on and on. What excites you most about playing with this group?"

**Ja Morant:** "It's just so much fun. So much guys that can do a lot. Easier looks, easier coverages for me. It's a blessing to be able to play around one of the best shooters of all time and then the talent like Damian as well, and the rest of the guys. We just got to continue to gel together, keep the chemistry going, and keep going and doing what we're doing."
