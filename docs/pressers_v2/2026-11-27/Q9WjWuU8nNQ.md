**3. Joe Mazzulla — "The goal is to try to develop as many as you can because you don't know what you're going to be up against." — Developing roster depth and player opportunities** [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q9WjWuU8nNQ&t=126s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I think it's productive. I don't know if we've gotten to 15 yet, I think we've gotten to 12, but I do think again, one of the things we've tried to do is create a locker room where anybody can impact winning at any time. Regardless of who we put out there, I truly believe they give us a chance to impact winning, whether it's offensive rebounds, forcing turnovers, screening, executing. And so I think that's part of the regular season is developing those opportunities. You've seen some great moments from **Chris** and then he reminds you real fast that he's a rookie, 19-year-old. And then you've seen some, I think **Ron**'s play has been really good, the two first half that he's played in the last two games. You've seen what he's able to do there, and so that just adds more guys that just continue to get more comfortable and be able to impact winning. So I think the goal is to try to develop as many as you can because you don't know what you're going to be up against and what matchups are going to look like and what calls for offense and defense. So I hope it doesn't get down to nine, but the goal is to try to develop as many guys as we can."
