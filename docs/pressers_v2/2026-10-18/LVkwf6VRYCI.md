**5. Jared McCain — "reading the closeout is huge" — Jared McCain on attacking closeouts and playmaking** [03:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LVkwf6VRYCI&t=220s)

Jared McCain: "Reading the closeout is huge. This summer was a lot of just making sure somebody had one of the sticks and just trying to get it in my face because a lot of the time they are closing out really hard, but I still feel like I can make those shots.
