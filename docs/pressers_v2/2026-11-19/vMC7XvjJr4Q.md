# Cam Spencer Walk-Off Interview | Grizzlies vs. Cavaliers — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMC7XvjJr4Q](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMC7XvjJr4Q)

_Speakers identified: Rob Fischer, Cam Spencer_

**1. Cam Spencer — "I felt like we dictated a little bit more, even when they got paint touches" — Cam Spencer on Memphis Grizzlies' defensive improvement in the second half** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMC7XvjJr4Q&t=0s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Cam, it was a high-scoring first half, not a lot of defense. What did you guys do better defensively in that second half?"

**Cam Spencer:** "I felt like we dictated a little bit more, even when they got paint touches. I feel like we collapsed on them, made them kick out a little bit tougher, and made one more effort to get a contest on some of those outside shots. Credit to them, they are a well-coached team, but I am proud of our guys for finishing it out."

**2. Cam Spencer — "We have just been sharing the ball, making simple plays, on-time, on-target passes" — Cam Spencer on the Memphis Grizzlies' offensive ball movement** [00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMC7XvjJr4Q&t=24s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "The offense has been clicking these last four games. What is going so well, what feels so smooth?"

**Cam Spencer:** "I was proud of how we didn't let our defense control our offense, or vice versa. We have just been sharing the ball, making simple plays, on-time, on-target passes is something that we talk about a lot. Having a big fella get going too is a huge piece for us, so credit to everybody, it was a team effort tonight."

**3. Cam Spencer — "When everybody can shoot, pass, and dribble, I feel like everybody is a threat" — Cam Spencer on the value of having multiple playmakers on the floor** [00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMC7XvjJr4Q&t=49s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "How great is it to have so many facilitators on the floor at the same time?"

**Cam Spencer:** "It makes offense that much easier. When everybody can shoot, pass, and dribble, I feel like everybody is a threat to do all those three. It makes the game very simple on that end and a very unselfish group, so proud of the guys."
