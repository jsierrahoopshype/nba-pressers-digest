**5. Taylor Jenkins — "He is a playmaker for us. I think he is finding his rhythm on the offensive side." — Taylor Jenkins on Jaren Jackson Jr.'s adjustment to the system** [05:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PyamDCQ6Ap0&t=306s)

Taylor Jenkins: "He has nine rebounds, three offensive rebounds, four assists, four blocks. He is a playmaker for us. I know he has a couple turnovers sprinkled in there, but he is screening and he is rolling. We are hitting him at the top of the key, guys are cutting off, he is getting a dribble hand-off actions. I think he is finding his rhythm on the offensive side, how he runs the floor, and he can play in different spots on the wings and in the corners, still being an advantage creator in pick-and-roll actions or be a spacer for us."
