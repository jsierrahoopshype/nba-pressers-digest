**5. Kevin Durant — "we're doing a lot of screening actions, a lot of flaring actions, just playing random ball." — Kevin Durant on the team's off-ball movement and screening** [03:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbYpG0bw38s&t=219s)

Kevin Durant: "When you got guys that out there that can shoot the basketball, the defense is going to be on edge when you set a flare or you set a pin down. They're going to be into the ball because we got guys that can shoot and that can score. So sometimes guys screening is going to be the guy that's open. We're doing a lot of screening actions, a lot of flaring actions, just playing random ball sometimes. And that's always good too, to switch it up and not always running a set, but have a concept that you can play out of."

**6. Kevin Durant — "we know exactly how we're going to guard teams in each possession and then we all go." — Kevin Durant on the team's transition offense philosophy** [04:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbYpG0bw38s&t=282s)

Kevin Durant: "We got a good philosophy on the defensive side of the ball, whereas we know exactly how we're going to guard teams in each possession and then we all go in there and rebound. It's that simple. And then a lot of teams are also crashing as well. So when an offense crash, I think you get a little bit more opportunity to run out and get layups. So we got great passers, we got good runners, and last game against Atlanta was an emphasis that we didn't run out in transition as much as we could have, and this game we wanted to have a little bit more energy to get out on the break."

**7. Kevin Durant — "we don't have to call that play, we just kind of know that all right, if we're sitting." — Kevin Durant on balancing set plays with random basketball** [05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbYpG0bw38s&t=327s)

Kevin Durant: "We've been working on this since training camp to the point where it's like we don't have to call that play, we just kind of know that all right, if we're sitting up there and nobody's moving, then flare or pin down or dribble handoff. So instead of sitting around, I think we're just moving around a little bit more and randomly moving around and that's throwing the defense off a bit. I think a lot of times when you call sets and they scout too, they know exactly what we're calling, exactly what these plays do. So when you can play random but also out of a concept of a screen down or pin down or dribble handoff, I think that just makes the game easier for you."
