**4. Deni Avdija — "Today wasn't really a loss, it was more of a learning experience for us." — Deni Avdija views the loss as a learning experience for Portland** [02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mG4JUm2CpIg&t=123s)

Deni Avdija: "Today wasn't really a loss, it was more of a learning experience for us. I think we were very high, we won three great games in a row, and things were clicking. You know, that's all you can do is just look at this game, watch film, understand that everybody on the team, including myself, can be better. **5. Deni Avdija — "Sometimes it's kind of of your nature to take more responsibility and be more aggressive, but I trust my teammates." — Deni Avdija on trusting teammates over taking individual responsibility** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mG4JUm2CpIg&t=195s)

Deni Avdija: "Sometimes it's kind of of your nature to take more responsibility and be more aggressive, but you know what, I trust my teammates. I trust my teammates. I'm going to keep kicking it out, I'm going to keep let Dame shoot his shots, I'm going to keep let Jrue attack the rim, Drew, I go on and on. It doesn't matter the score, you know. When we need a bucket or, you know, coach or players could trust in me and I have the ball, sure, I'll be aggressive. But if the right play is to kick it out for Dame for a three, and he didn't have the greatest shooting night tonight, I believe in him that he's going to make the next one. And this, this is my mentality, this is how I'm going to keep playing. The same thing when I'm not going to have a great game, they're going to keep trusting in me. So I think this is the big part of how a team should act, and I trust all the guys to get their heads up, put the work in, and be better next game."
