**2. Aaron Wiggins — "it's awesome just being able to come in here and have an opportunity to contribute" — Aaron Wiggins on his increased opportunities with the Atlanta Hawks** [00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IP9YRjMz0w0&t=29s)

**3. Aaron Wiggins — "I think the style of play speaks for itself, especially with our team" — Aaron Wiggins on his offensive role and similarities to playing with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** [01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IP9YRjMz0w0&t=79s)
