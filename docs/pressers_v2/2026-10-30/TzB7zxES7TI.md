**4. Luke Kennard — "he attracts a lot of attention on the offensive end and he's so skilled, he plays at his own pace." — Luke Kennard on Devin Booker's ability to handle defensive pressure** [02:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TzB7zxES7TI&t=159s)

Luke Kennard: "He attracts a lot of attention on the offensive end and he's so skilled, he plays at his own pace. He can get to his spots whenever he wants. But teams, they don't want him to feel comfortable. They want to put pressure on him and throw multiple bodies at him at all times, every second he's in the game. He's going to see a lot of different coverages this year. For us, playing off of him, we got to be in the right spot. He has to trust us to be in the right spot and make the right plays when he finds us if he does get double-teamed."
