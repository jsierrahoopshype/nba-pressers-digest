**5. Jamahl Mosley — "Offensively, I really think it's the pace in which you playing, get the ball over half court." — Jamahl Mosley defines what makes a good basketball possession** [04:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQvR9d3RU3w&t=267s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "How do you kind of define what a good possession is for you? What is what is it that makes a possession good on either end of the floor for you?"

**Jamahl Mosley:** "Offensively, I really think it's the pace in which you playing, get the ball over half court by 21. Then the ball gets from side to side of the floor, you make the defense have to shift. And then you're looking for the best available shot. You go from good to great. You love to get some uncontested shots, knock down three or get into the rim is beautiful. But if you get a mid-range shot 7-6-5 on the shot clock, that's a good shot. That's a good possession, especially if everybody's touched it and it's moved side to side. Defensively, do you make teams uncomfortable right away? And that's part of picking up full court, disrupting them. Now they're starting their offense and their first pass is maybe made at 15. And now from there, you've got a tight paint, you make them move the ball around the perimeter, not touching the paint, and then it's whatever shot they get up is contested and then the finishing the possession off with the rebound is what you like to have happen."
