**1. Charles Lee — "when things aren't going your way, what is your focus going to be?" — Charles Lee on Charlotte's learning experience after a tough loss** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nnI_6n3Q6t4&t=0s)

Charles Lee: "I think it was a good learning experience for our entire group tonight. When things aren't going your way, what is your focus going to be? Are we going to look for excuses, are we going to look for people to bail us out of those tough times, or are we going to band together and try to figure it out on our own? I actually thought that there were some quality looks that we got in there. There were some lapses defensively in terms of offensive rebounds potentially during that stretch, guys getting back cut and stuff like that. I felt like a lot of self-inflicted wounds, and then on top of that, a good learning opportunity for us to realize that there's things that we can control in those moments."

Charles Lee: "Number one is their physicality. They got a bunch of really pesky, physical, on-ball defenders and you got to be able to, I think, physically be able to to deal with it. But then also the mental aspect. I thought that versus the Nets, we were able to to wear down a couple of their creators. They're going to hit some shots, they're high-volume shooters, they're good players, and you got to have that kind of mental maturity to understand that all those punches, all those grabs and holds, it actually wears them down and at the end of the game, they're able to miss some shots."

**3. Charles Lee — "a lot of ball turnovers in particular tonight. What do you attribute that to?" — Charles Lee on Charlotte's high turnover rate** [01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nnI_6n3Q6t4&t=90s)

Charles Lee: "A lot of ball turnovers in particular tonight. What do you attribute that to? Some of it is us, not doing what we do or being in the spots that we're used to being in. A lot of times when you're doing things that you shouldn't be doing, at positions that either the ball handler is not familiar with or you as a screener, I think it creates some indecision. And then there was a couple that are just a little too casual. So, I think a lot of those two areas that I just talked about are a little bit just like us needing to look inward and figure out how we can be better."

**4. Charles Lee — "we want to create the best uncontested shot that we can get" — Charles Lee on shot selection and team confidence** [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nnI_6n3Q6t4&t=125s)

Charles Lee: "Our biggest thing is less about the volume of what our shot profile looks like and more so, we want to create the best uncontested shot that we can get. And so if it ends up being a catch and shoot three for **Brandon Miller** or **Naz Reid** or **Dennis Schroder** and it's a wide-open shot, you got to let it fly with confidence. If it's a layup under the basket, you got to take that. So a lot of it is just what is the shot quality that we're trying to create and we're looking for the best uncontested shot and sometimes it ends up being the three."
