# Dejounte Murray | Pelicans vs Jazz Postgame Interview 10/23/26 — *New Orleans Pelicans*

**1. Dejounte Murray — "It's great to compete. We preach being aggressive and guarding, being physical, the physicality of the game." — Dejounte Murray on the Pelicans' team resilience and defensive identity** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jcMvRWqpbU&t=15s)

Dejounte Murray: "It's great to compete. We preach being aggressive and guarding, being physical, the physicality of the game. But I think for us the players, you'll never see me say nothing about my coaches, like never. We are on the floor and I think it's just unacceptable, back-to-back games for teams to be in the bonus with almost nine minutes in the fourth quarter. Any team in the league, if they're watching this, that's what they're preaching to their teams when they got us on the schedule. So we got to be physical, we got to be smart, discipline, one through five, and whoever is on the floor, we got to be real disciplined."

**2. Dejounte Murray — "It's a challenge of balance, being that guy that try to get guys the ball." — Dejounte Murray on his role as a playmaker and team leader** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jcMvRWqpbU&t=98s)

Dejounte Murray: "It's a challenge of balance, being that guy that try to get guys the ball. I feel like I'm just trying to show guys the way when getting them open shots, just knowing that's the way that we want to play. If I come out just selfish, it's a way of the game, you just got to react to the game. Sometimes you're going to come out hot because the game gives you that, but I just try to come out getting guys involved. But I still got to be aggressive more, but at the end of the day, for me as an individual, I'll be better. I hit the shot, then we win, but it's the other things, the little things that we got to execute."
