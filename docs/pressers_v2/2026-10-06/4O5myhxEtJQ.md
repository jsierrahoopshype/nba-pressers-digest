# Taylor Jenkins Media Availability | 10.06.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4O5myhxEtJQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4O5myhxEtJQ)

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "I anticipate working in some of those guys, not sure if it will be all the guys." — Taylor Jenkins discusses integrating players and evaluating roster depth** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4O5myhxEtJQ&t=13s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I anticipate working in some of those guys, not sure if it will be all the guys. I will get with the staff on our flight to Oklahoma City, talk a little bit more about that. Just continue this ramp up for our guys, but also want to see the depth of our roster and see guys that are committing to what we are talking about. Obviously, first exposure yesterday, there were some good moments, but definitely a lot of areas for improvement. We focused on that today, and regardless of who plays tomorrow, we expect them to hopefully take strides in the positive direction."

**2. Taylor Jenkins — "We just got a little pre-exposed to giving up transition too much when we overran our route." — Taylor Jenkins on team rebounding and transition defense improvements** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4O5myhxEtJQ&t=71s)

Taylor Jenkins: "That is the strength of those guys for sure, in **Jae Crowder** and J-Rock. I thought our first unit, when we did do it, we maybe just overran our routes a little bit. We were getting to the low side a little bit too much. We were not getting to the high side. We had our hands on some balls, just did not bounce our way. Credit Minnesota for snatching those up. We just got a little pre-exposed to giving up transition too much when we overran our route or did not have pressure when they did get the rebound. This is something we are trying to ingrain, it is a new habit for a lot of these guys, making sure we are picking the right spots when to crash and when not to crash."

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "I wanted to get the ball in Tyler Herro's hands, see him handle pressure." — Taylor Jenkins evaluates Tyler Herro's performance and role** [02:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4O5myhxEtJQ&t=158s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I wanted to watch him in this system a little bit more. **Ryan**, with the ding and some of the fouls, maybe just did not get the rhythm that we were hoping. Hopefully the next opportunity we get more of what we are hoping to see. Scoot as well, willing moving the ball, but also can be great with the ball in his hands. When there were opportunities in dead-ball situations, I wanted to get the ball in **Tyler Herro**'s hands, see him handle pressure, how we are going to have to calibrate when teams pick him up full court and are trying to pressure him even in the half court to deny his catch. That was something I wanted to lean in, knowing that **Ryan**, Scoot, even challenging Brain to be guys that bring the ball up the floor, but I do not want Tyler just to be a guy that is playing off the ball."

**4. Taylor Jenkins — "We have got a lot of guards, trying to figure out where our wings fit in here." — Taylor Jenkins on roster versatility and three-guard lineups** [03:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4O5myhxEtJQ&t=229s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I just think when you look at our roster, we have got a lot of guards, trying to figure out where our wings fit in here. Uste played the first game, I would anticipate him getting an opportunity next game, and then getting **Nate** some opportunity but later in the game, not so much in the first half. It is something that I want to see, knowing that is kind of what is on our roster and see if those guys can find chemistry success. They have obviously in previous years played that way. I want to see the different varieties that we can go with our depth. Can we go big? Can we go smaller guard lines because we have got a lot of shooting as well, but what is that going to do on the defensive side?"
