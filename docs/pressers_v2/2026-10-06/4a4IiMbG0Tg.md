Going up against ourselves has been really good, but everyone knows the plays and they're grabbing and holding and fighting for positioning and stuff like that and I know we're going to get a very physical tough minded Brooklyn team as well, but I look forward to seeing those guys with some different lineups, putting them in some situations to be creators, to be screeners, to have to guard certain people."

**6. Charles Lee — "definitely want our guys continuing to be relentless on the glass" — Charles Lee on team pace and glass dominance** [05:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4a4IiMbG0Tg&t=324s)

Charles Lee: "Definitely want our guys continuing to be relentless on the glass. A lot of the pace for us in terms of quick inbound passes, I love to see our guys having that next play mentality, closest man to the ball, being able to get it out quickly and how can we get it high and wide up the court. I also think that part of that pace thing for me, we love to gauge how many pass aheads, pass acrosses are we getting? How are we creating some early advantages in the clock with our passing?"
