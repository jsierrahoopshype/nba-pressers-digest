**3. Tuomas Iisalo — "We just play better when he's out there, which is what ultimately playing basketball is about" — Chris Murray's role and trust as a fifth starter** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWk-7Zb1M94&t=102s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "We just play better when he's out there, which is what ultimately playing basketball is about. You're one of five guys who's out there and you try to help the team win as much as possible. He's really doing that through excellent point-of-attack defense. In Charlotte, it was Brandon Miller; today, it was Ingram, really limiting their touches, limiting their efficiency, helping with the rebounding, creating disruption defensively, so contributing to the possession game.
