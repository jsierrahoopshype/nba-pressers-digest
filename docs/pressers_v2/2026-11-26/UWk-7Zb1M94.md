**3. Tuomas Iisalo — "We just play better when he's out there which is what ultimately playing basketball is about" — Chris Murray's role and growth as a starter** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWk-7Zb1M94&t=102s)

You're one of five guys who's out there and you try to help the team win as much as possible and he's really doing that. He's doing it through excellent point-of-attack defense. **5. Tuomas Iisalo — "His turnovers are up this year and I'm super excited about that because last year I thought" — Cam Spencer's improved decision-making and risk-taking** [04:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWk-7Zb1M94&t=286s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "His turnovers are up this year and I'm super excited about that because last year I thought he was a bit risk-averse and he had extremely high assist-to-turnover ratio. We talked about this during the off-season and heading into this season that that's one of the things where he can improve. The best creators have higher turnover numbers because they also look for the higher leverage, the higher value passes and he's had multiple of those where he skips it to the other side instead of making the safest pass."
