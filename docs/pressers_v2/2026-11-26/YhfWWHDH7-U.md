Steve Kerr: "We've been growing offensively since the trip. We've tweaked a few things, and that lineup change obviously changes some things as well. **7. Steve Kerr — "it's particularly hard when you've been a great player as Draymond Green has" — Steve Kerr on Draymond Green's adjustment to aging** [08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhfWWHDH7-U&t=508s)

Steve Kerr: "It's not easy for any player when they get older, but it's particularly hard when you've been a great player as **Draymond Green** has.
