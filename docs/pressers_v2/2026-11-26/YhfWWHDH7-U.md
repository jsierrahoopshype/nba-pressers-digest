**1. Steve Kerr — "It always helps to build rhythm and we did that the last couple of days in practice." — Steve Kerr credits recent practices for improved offensive rhythm** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhfWWHDH7-U&t=12s)

Steve Kerr: "We had two really good practices in a row the last two days. We are just building rhythm, getting better with our recognition of what we are trying to do offensively. It has been a tough five, six-game stretch shooting the ball, but it is just a matter of time with the quality shooters we have. It always helps to build rhythm and we did that the last couple of days in practice."

**2. Steve Kerr — "Having Looney next to him kind of frees him up to roam a little bit and disrupt." — Steve Kerr explains the tactical value of Draymond Green and Kevon Looney** [00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhfWWHDH7-U&t=55s)

Steve Kerr: "Looney gives us the shot-blocking and paint presence and then Draymond, he can guard anybody. We can put him on **Jason Tatum** like we did the other night, or he can plug off of a player and play center field. We can use him in a lot of different ways, but having Looney next to him kind of frees him up to roam a little bit and disrupt the offense."

**3. Steve Kerr — "Draymond has always been a great passer and a point forward." — Steve Kerr discusses Draymond Green's evolution as a playmaker** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhfWWHDH7-U&t=95s)

Steve Kerr: "We have been growing offensively since the trip. We have tweaked a few things and that lineup change obviously changes some things as well. Draymond has always been a great passer and a point forward. I just feel like he is in a great place. He is doing such a great job leading our team, showing patience offensively, and now recognizing the change. He is taking four or five threes a game next to Looney and he is in a good spot here the last couple games. I am really pleased with his play, but most of all just the way he is leading us. He has been incredible."

**4. Steve Kerr — "He just gives us that element of speed that can break down the defense." — Steve Kerr highlights Brandon Podziemski's impact on team speed** [02:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhfWWHDH7-U&t=160s)

Steve Kerr: "We really missed **Brandon Podziemski** while he was out. He just gives us that element of speed that can break down the defense. That was a big key tonight as well."

**6. Steve Kerr — "I have always loved George. He is just really, really smart and he is physical." — Steve Kerr reflects on George Niang's skill set and personality** [05:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhfWWHDH7-U&t=307s)

Steve Kerr: "I have always loved George. We had him here nine years ago in our G League program and he has always been one of my favorite players as an opponent. Just watching his brain at work, he is just really, really smart and he is physical and he is competitive and he is really skilled. **7. Steve Kerr — "It is not easy for any player when they get older, but it is particularly hard." — Steve Kerr on Draymond Green's adjustment to aging** [08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YhfWWHDH7-U&t=508s)

Steve Kerr: "It is not easy for any player when they get older, but it is particularly hard when you have been a great player as Draymond has. He is a Hall of Fame player, first-ballot Hall of Famer. You get older and you have to adapt and you have to reimagine yourself and that is not an easy thing to do, especially for somebody as competitive and passionate as Draymond. I think he is really finding his way. Those first few games without Steph were pretty rough, but he is understanding what he has to do. I think we are understanding as a staff what we have to do too. It is really Draymond putting himself in this position of understanding that he can still lead in a really productive manner even through some of his frustrations and that is exactly what he has done. He has been brilliant in the locker room and on the court."
