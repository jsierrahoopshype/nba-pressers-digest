**2. Ime Udoka — "He got us settled. Didn't love the way we lacked organization at the start of the fourth." — Ime Udoka on Fred VanVleet's performance down the stretch** [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=73s)

It's not just about making shots, it's about getting organized and getting the right look and we didn't have that for a stretch there. **3. Ime Udoka — "Don't always look at the end number, you look at the effectiveness." — Ime Udoka on the defensive game plan against Booker and Green** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=112s)

Ime Udoka: "Anytime **Booker** got 25, but 11 on raw free throws, you're shooting 6 for 18 and **Jalen** 8 for 21, so don't always look at the end number, you look at the effectiveness to your point and holding them to low field goals. Like the attention to detail on those guys for the most part. They got some things, **Jalen** getting downhill, reaching the screens and **Booker** off the kick-outs getting some of those threes as well, but overall made them work through a lot of bodies at them."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=112s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=112s)

**4. Ime Udoka — "It kind of snowballed tonight, we were 16 for 30, so missed quite a few." — Ime Udoka on the team's struggles with free throws** [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=156s)

Ime Udoka: "It kind of snowballed tonight, we were 16 for 30, so missed quite a few. To go 14 for 26 in the first half and have a 10-point lead with the way we shot the three is a good sign that our defense was guarding well, but like to knock those ones down, extend the lead there and just didn't get the reps in the second half, only four."

**5. Ime Udoka — "It's almost a second nature thing now. Guys don't really celebrate it too much." — Ime Udoka on Kevin Durant's scoring milestones** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=194s)

Ime Udoka: "It's almost a second nature thing now. Guys don't really celebrate it too much because they just joked in there he's passing so many that he's getting a ball every few games or whatever. While you're in it playing with someone, that's great, that is that great, and as he's passing all these milestones you're kind of just taking it for granted at times. But you look back on it and the accomplishments are things that can't be duplicated. Guys recognize it, I'm sure he will at the end of his career, but he's surpassing so many things it's almost becoming second nature now."

He's grown a ton, he's taking a huge step this year as well and we love the progress he's making."
