**2. Ime Udoka — "Don't always look at the end number, you look at the effectiveness to your point and holding them." — Ime Udoka on the defensive strategy against Devin Booker and Kevin Durant** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=112s)

Ime Udoka: "Anytime, you know, Booker got 25 but 11 on raw free throws. You're shooting 6 for 18 and Jalen 8 for 21. Don't always look at the end number, you look at the effectiveness to your point and holding them to low field goals. Like the attention to detail on those guys for the most part. They got some things, you know, Jalen getting downhill, rejecting the screens and Booker off the kickouts against the low threes as well. But overall made them work through a lot of bodies at them."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=112s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=112s)

**3. Ime Udoka — "He's surpassing so many things that it's almost becoming second nature now." — Ime Udoka on Kevin Durant's career scoring milestones** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=194s)

Ime Udoka: "It's almost a second nature thing now. Guys don't really celebrate it too much because they just joked in there he's passing so many that he's getting a ball every few games or whatever. While you're in it playing with someone, that's great. That is that great and as passing all these milestones you're just taking it for granted at times. But you look back on it and the accomplishments are things that can't be duplicated. Guys recognize it, I'm sure he will at the end of his career. But he's surpassing so many things that it's almost becoming second nature now."

**4. Ime Udoka — "He's flirting with them every night now so it's almost becoming commonplace." — Ime Udoka on Alperen Sengun's development and triple-double performance** [04:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=247s)

Ime Udoka: "It's been great. We recognized him as well tonight for that, for another one and he's flirting with them every night now so it's almost becoming commonplace. It's very impressive the way he's grown and he dominates the game in all these different ways. Luxury for us to have a big that can do all those things, not just a low post scorer but the isolations, obviously the assist numbers are high and guys especially when they're making shots and he's still on the glass doing his thing there. He's grown a ton, he's taking a huge step this year as well and we love the progress he's making. Obviously a huge part of what we do."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=247s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=247s)

**5. Ime Udoka — "It's a him and Kevin thing always and you saw that tonight." — Ime Udoka on Dillon Brooks' defensive edge and team composure** [05:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIhRzm9VY7A&t=321s)

Ime Udoka: "It's a him and Kevin thing always and you saw that tonight. He was chirping with everybody to your point. I think it was a man and him early, Jabari and him at some point and even him and Alperen quite a bit late. You understand that we all know he is and love him for it. But on the other side, you have to keep composure, not get caught up in it and the team that's either crying or whining about certain things, you kind of get the edge on. Dillon that's a big part of what he does.
