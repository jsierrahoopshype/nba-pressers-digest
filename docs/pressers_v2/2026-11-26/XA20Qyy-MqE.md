# Jaylen Wells Walk-Off Interview | Grizzlies vs. Clippers — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA20Qyy-MqE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA20Qyy-MqE)

_Speakers identified: Rob Fischer, Jaylen Wells_

**1. Jaylen Wells — "we were very, very good defensive half, just had to play both sides of the ball." — Jaylen Wells on Memphis Grizzlies' second-half defensive intensity** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA20Qyy-MqE&t=3s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Jaylen, they climbed back there at the end of the second quarter, but you took it back to them again in the third and the fourth. What was the difference in the second half?"

**Jaylen Wells:** "I think we had to get our offense going. We were very, very good defensive half, just had to play both sides of the ball."

**2. Jaylen Wells — "a lot of it is just wearing out the other team. We play a very intense game." — Jaylen Wells on Grizzlies' ability to wear down opponents** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA20Qyy-MqE&t=16s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "As far as second halves, you guys have been very successful in second halves. What leads to that?"

**Jaylen Wells:** "I think a lot of it is just wearing out the other team. We play a very intense game, picking up full court, being disruptive, so I don't think a lot of teams can keep up with that."

**3. Jaylen Wells — "we have a lot of weapons. Even with guys out, I think it shows our depth." — Jaylen Wells on Grizzlies' ball movement and depth** [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA20Qyy-MqE&t=30s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "I know you guys talk so much about ball movement, and in that third quarter, nine assists on 12 made baskets. How important is that, especially the last couple of games the assist numbers weren't there?"

**Jaylen Wells:** "It's very important. We have a lot of weapons. Even with guys out, I think it shows our depth, shows that anyone can have the ball in their hands and make a play."

**4. Jaylen Wells — "we just got to play our game. That's it. I think if we play our game we'll win." — Jaylen Wells on upcoming rematch against Minnesota Timberwolves** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA20Qyy-MqE&t=50s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Minnesota ended up winning tonight against Oklahoma City. So Friday's game comes down to the winner wins the group. You played them less than a week ago. What did you learn from that game that needs to be better this time around?"

**Jaylen Wells:** "Defensively, I think we started off really good. We just got to play our game. That's it. I think if we play our game we'll win."
