**1. De’Anthony Melton — "He do so many great things on the court and especially in transition and his speed" — Brandon Williams' impact on the Warriors' bench rotation** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=38s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "**Brandon Williams** made his debut tonight, coming back from his injury. How good is it for your bench to have that boost coming in a game?"

**De’Anthony Melton:** "That's my dog right there. He do so many great things on the court and especially in transition and his speed and quickness to get to the rim. I don't think there's too many people really stopping him getting there. It's just about making layups and he made seven of them today, so he's just exceptional."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=38s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=38s)

**2. De’Anthony Melton — "We got to protect home court no matter what, so I think we're doing a great job" — Warriors' philosophy on defending home court after road trips** [01:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=64s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Two games back-to-back at home, two wins. What's been the philosophy coming back home after a tough road trip?"

**De’Anthony Melton:** "This little skid we had, we felt like we was getting punched in the mouth, so we wanted to come out here and just start punching first and get more discipline on both sides of the ball. We got to protect home court no matter what, so I think we're doing a great job of that. Now it's just about continuing this run and keep it going."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=64s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=64s)

**3. De’Anthony Melton — "We tweaked a couple things that people were kind of scouting on us" — Warriors' ball movement and high-quality shot selection** [01:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=88s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "You guys get 37 assists on 49 buckets and 20 made threes as a team. Talk about the catch and shoot threes, because that tells me the ball is moving."

**De’Anthony Melton:** "We tweaked a couple things that people were kind of scouting on us. We changed a couple things and also we were just sticking to our game plan and just making sure the ball was moving as long as our bodies. We trying to keep the defense honest and just keep them in misdirection."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=88s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=88s)

**4. De’Anthony Melton — "When he's off the court, we got to just make sure we maintain the lead" — Adjusting the Warriors' rotation during Stephen Curry's absence** [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=126s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "A big conversation has been the non-Steph minutes. How do you guys view this period right now that you're playing without Steph in the lineup?"

**De’Anthony Melton:** "When he's in the game, he's going to do so many great things for us that when he's off the court, we got to just make sure we maintain the lead and sometimes when we're winning, even grow the lead. So we're just trying to figure it out when he's not on the floor and I think we're coming around that corner with it."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=126s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=126s)

**5. De’Anthony Melton — "I think that's the beauty of this team, we got so much depth and so much confidence" — Depth and next-man-up mentality on the Warriors' roster** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=157s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Tonight the bench had 61 points to Sacramento's 19. Talk about the contribution from your bench tonight."

**De’Anthony Melton:** "I've been saying it since day one, how much depth we got. I think anybody on this team can really go. The dudes that's not even really playing can still go.
