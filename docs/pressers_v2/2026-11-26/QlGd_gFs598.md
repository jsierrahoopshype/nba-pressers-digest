**4. Doug Christie — "Darius is going to continue to see every defense that that you can create" — Doug Christie on defensive adjustments for Darius** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlGd_gFs598&t=95s)

Doug Christie: "I think you're going to see it more. I think **Darius** is going to continue to see every defense that that you can create. They were blitzing small-small, they were blitzing small-big, so it didn't matter. If it was a five-man they were blitzing, if it was a small they were blitzing. I think probably haven't seen the small-small blitz, but even that goes for not only **Darius**, but it goes for the screener. Where are you going once that happens? You got to release and find an area that he can pass you the basketball, coming up out of the corner. We got to make sure that we make ourselves available because once he gets off the basketball, you put two on the ball, now you have the advantage. But if you don't recognize it quickly enough or you don't make the pass, then you lose the advantage."

**6. Doug Christie — "making sure to Shon's point, making ourselves available when they begin to blitz us" — Doug Christie on maintaining urgency and pace** [03:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlGd_gFs598&t=191s)

Doug Christie: "More than anything, just talking about the things that that we can control. Not turning the basketball over, making sure to **Shon**'s point, making ourselves available when they begin to blitz us, playing with a more sense of urgency as far as pace, getting into our stuff quicker so that we're not deeper into the shot clock and all of a sudden you're finding yourself in butter clock, which is seven or under and you're rushing shots.
