**1. Micah Nori — "We don't need home runs, we just need more singles and just make the easy play." — Micah Nori on team's commitment to player development and simplicity** [01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GXXrpHsVKFM&t=66s)

Micah Nori: "I think that we always talk about, and Gill and his staff in player development do a really good job about talking about pictures, just keeping it simple and all the short-sided games and all the CLA stuff and putting them in position and then just seeing what you see. The thing we talked about with these guys tonight was, we don't need home runs, we just need more singles and just make the easy play. Just get yourself downhill. If **Victor Wembanyama** presents himself early, kick it to the corner, which we did a phenomenal job of. We get up 53s, we make 19 of them, and we end up with 28 assists all the while shooting 31 free throws."

**4. Micah Nori — "We weren't necessarily making it a point of emphasis to shoot up to shoot 53s." — Micah Nori on team's strategic approach to shooting against San Antonio** [03:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GXXrpHsVKFM&t=225s)

Micah Nori: "We weren't necessarily making it a point of emphasis to shoot up to shoot 53s. What we made a point of emphasis was with if you see **Victor Wembanyama** in the paint and you don't have a straight line to finish, to find the kickout and make the right play. They do a really good job because what they try to do, like a lot of teams, and we do the same things, try to play everything two-on-two, funnel it down to **Victor Wembanyama** and then stay home on shooters and then make you have to take mid-range and bad shots over top of **Victor Wembanyama**. But what happened was we were able to break down their initial pressure, not always with a pick-and-roll, and then **Victor Wembanyama** had to come over and help and then we were able and which chain reaction started opening up some things."

**6. Micah Nori — "When you have no fear that anyone can block your shot, that's a compliment to him." — Micah Nori on the challenge of defending Victor Wembanyama** [07:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GXXrpHsVKFM&t=435s)

Micah Nori: "44 points, but I guess if the man, oh man, he's a problem. You know, Dwayne Casey used to always talk about **Kevin Durant**, meaning the rim is always open because he's seven-foot and the way he shoots the ball. It's the same thing for **Victor Wembanyama**. Like when you have no fear that anyone can block your shot, and that's not a detriment to him, that's a compliment to him, and he just got himself to his spots, he found in rhythm. All we tried to do is make it difficult and I mean he goes 17 for 33, 9 for 9 from three. He made some tough ones.
