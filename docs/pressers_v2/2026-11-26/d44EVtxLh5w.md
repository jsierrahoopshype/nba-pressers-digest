**1. Charles Lee — "I got to be better for the guys, and then finishing possessions." — Charles Lee on Charlotte's late-game execution and defensive lapses** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d44EVtxLh5w&t=15s)

Charles Lee: "There are just a couple moments throughout the game where you have to be able to build upon your momentum really and not allow the other team to chip into your lead as much as we did, or find ways to extend it. The baseline out of bounds, sideline out of bounds, turnovers stick out in my mind. I got to be better for the guys, and then finishing possessions. At the end of the day in that fourth quarter, they get a 34-point quarter and it felt just a ton of guys getting driven. You got to be able to wall up, you got to be able to slide and absorb that contact and be able to stop them before they have two feet in the paint. Our guys then found a couple possessions where we did, and then we just didn't finish possessions, and that's what happened on that last play for them to take that lead."
