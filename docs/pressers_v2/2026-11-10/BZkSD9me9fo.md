**4. Jordan Ott — "We got to eat off the dribble threes. We got 26 overall, that's not going to be enough." — Jordan Ott on the Suns' struggles with mid-range shooting** [02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZkSD9me9fo&t=142s)

**5. Jordan Ott — "They just force your mental stamina to stay with it, knowing that it's going to feel a little different." — Jordan Ott on the difficulty of guarding Victor Wembanyama** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZkSD9me9fo&t=180s)

Jordan Ott: "They just force your mental stamina to stay with it, knowing that it's going to feel a little different than most nights. You would love to get some times where you collapse the D, attack, but they're able to stay home. He just sits in the rim. They have elite on-ball guys, so we tried to get that elite ball guy off of there every once in a while. But then once you get in the paint, they fan out. So they do a great job of putting that guy or both bigs really protecting the rim, and then stealing out or staying home off the ball."
