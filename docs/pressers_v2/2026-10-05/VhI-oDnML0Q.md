**1. Charles Lee — "It'll be nice to have somebody out there that doesn't know every action you're about to get to" — Charles Lee on the value of preseason games for team development** [00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VhI-oDnML0Q&t=1s)

Charles Lee: "We're excited to be able to get a preseason game under our belt. The environment that our fans helped create last year, we're looking forward to getting back in front of them. It'll be nice to have somebody out there that doesn't know every action you're about to get to. The physicality level will be there. That's what training camp brings out of you, is the high level of competition. You have a lot of guys trying to compete for minutes, for time, and so, looking forward to the opportunity of playing another opponent."

**2. Charles Lee — "Especially your main players have to realize you're going to get touched as part of the game" — Charles Lee on teaching players to handle increased physical defensive play** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VhI-oDnML0Q&t=50s)

Charles Lee: "Our guys are just way more aware of it now. Early on, when you're not aware of it, when you're not expecting it, it can mentally fry your brain a little bit where you're just like, every time I'm trying to get a catch, I'm getting grabbed, I'm getting touched. It's a league-wide trend. Especially your main players have to realize you're going to get touched as part of the game. Now you got to figure out, how am I getting open? How am I going to execute a little bit better? Screening has been a big emphasis for us as well, but then also there's been the off-ball weight room piece too. Our guys have taken a holistic view on how we can combat the physicality a lot better, mentally and physically."

**3. Charles Lee — "It's all an opportunity to keep learning about our group, keep learning combinations" — Charles Lee on the importance of preseason for learning team combinations** [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VhI-oDnML0Q&t=100s)

Charles Lee: "Every day is important. Practice days are extremely important. The preseason games are very important because it's all an opportunity to keep learning about our group, keep learning combinations, keep learning what the game is going to take out of you to win games. Our personnel is different, and we got to get used to what that looks like on the court offensively and defensively."
