# Steve Kerr discusses Warriors’ struggles trying to replace injured Steph Curry | NBC Sports Bay Area — *Golden State Warriors*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4)

_Speakers identified: Steve Kerr_

**1. Steve Kerr — "My coaching has suffered without Steph as well. Let's be honest, Raymond is not nearly as good." — Steve Kerr on the difficulty of coaching without Steph Curry** [04:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=280s)

Steve Kerr: "My coaching has suffered without Steph as well. Let's be honest, Raymond is not nearly as good of a PR guy without Steph. I listened to a tape of **Tim Roy** the other night. No excitement, no energy. So we all miss Steph. The game is harder without him. Take away any team's superstar and all of a sudden the defense is not nearly as compromised and the game just gets more difficult."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=280s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=280s)

**2. Steve Kerr — "We have to make adjustments and find ways to attack. When you do that as a group." — Steve Kerr on the team's need to adapt without Steph Curry** [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=305s)

Steve Kerr: "We have to make adjustments and find ways to attack. When you do that as a group, then individual guys can shine. But when individuals try to take it upon themselves to replace Steph, it will be a struggle."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=305s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=305s)

**3. Steve Kerr — "Guys like that, like Mikal Bridges and Kyrie Irving, can play with anybody." — Steve Kerr on the unique skill sets of Mikal Bridges and Kyrie Irving** [07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=442s)

Steve Kerr: "Guys like that, like **Mikal Bridges** and **Kyrie Irving**, can play with anybody. That's one of the things that makes them both special is you don't have to build lineups around them because they're so skilled and smart. So when you put two of them together, it becomes really difficult to defend and to try to defeat. The skill set, the intelligence, the competitiveness, they fit beautifully."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=442s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=442s)

**4. Steve Kerr — "He's just an incredible human being. He's incredibly competitive and hilarious at the same time." — Steve Kerr on George Niang's impact as a teammate** [09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syzlCKK5OU4&t=540s)

Steve Kerr: "He's just an incredible human being. He's incredibly competitive and hilarious at the same time. There's probably no better combination if you want a teammate who's going to help you win and keep things loose and connect the guys. Everybody loves George and he's also just a winning player. He's smart, tough, the ball moves when he's out there. He either shoots it or moves it, the ball never sticks."
