**2. Devin Carter — "I still feel fatigue, more so energy low. Only you play two minutes and everything shuts down." — Devin Carter describes the physical impact of his illness** [00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gRx8c_CLmKI&t=25s)

Devin Carter: "I still feel fatigue, more so energy low. Only you play two minutes and everything shuts down. And then just building that back up. Obviously the way I play is tough to not be tired, so on top of the way I play, dealing with the sickness is just tough. But I am not in a rush, but I definitely feel myself coming back into shape and playing a lot better."

**3. Devin Carter — "I definitely was upset the timing of that happened because I had a good game in Cleveland." — Devin Carter on the frustration of his illness timing** [01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gRx8c_CLmKI&t=82s)

Devin Carter: "Everybody wants to get signed to a deal, you feel more secure. But I definitely was upset the timing of that happened because I had a good game in Cleveland. I was looking forward to playing the rest of the preseason and showing what I worked on all summer and then everything gets stripped quick. But I am working back into it, so feeling good."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gRx8c_CLmKI&t=82s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gRx8c_CLmKI&t=82s)

**4. Devin Carter — "I think G League is actually been very helpful so far, getting my wind back." — Devin Carter on using G League games for conditioning** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gRx8c_CLmKI&t=133s)

Devin Carter: "I am watching the games and then I am getting edits of the games. Even the G League games I am playing, Coach **Craig** is clipping stuff and sending it, and we are watching them together. I think G League is actually been very helpful so far, getting my wind back and getting reps in that I missed out on for four weeks."
