**5. Erik Spoelstra — "It's been way more about this collective intensity and effort and a commitment to doing the tough things." — Prioritizing collective defensive effort over specific schemes** [03:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8eLGGOTmyO4&t=218s)

Erik Spoelstra: "It was collaborative, but I was trying to get our guys on the road trip to stop, or not, because they were great about it. It's not about the schemes. We can figure out the schemes; there's a bunch of schemes that work. And ultimately, yes, I do want their feedback to see if we do this scheme, will it result in this kind of effort that I'm looking for.
