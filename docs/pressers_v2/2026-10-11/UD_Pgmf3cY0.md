**3. Luke Kennard — "the way that we're able to play off of each other, but with that, there's got to be a lot of trust" — Luke Kennard on team chemistry and offensive ball movement** [02:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UD_Pgmf3cY0&t=139s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "How important is it for an offense in the halfcourt to have so many different ways that they can go about scoring, and a lot of it being rooted in just like the two and three pass principles?"

**Luke Kennard:** "I think just the way that we're able to play off of each other, but with that, there's got to be a lot of trust. Guys being in the right spots, guys moving to create more space for each other, and it's stuff that we work on every single day. We throw different groups out there in practices, just different combinations, because we have a deep team. We have a lot of weapons, guys that can have a big night any night, and we know that. I think for us, just trusting in each other, the way that we want to play, being in the right spots, and being ready to shoot our shots when we can. We don't want to turn down any shots."

**4. Luke Kennard — "we have to be smart about when we do tag, when we do crash the boards" — Luke Kennard on adjusting to team defensive rebounding habits** [03:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UD_Pgmf3cY0&t=222s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "How are you adjusting to the habit of just getting back versus getting in the mix, and how are you seeing that weigh on an opponent in terms of taking transition opportunities away?"

**Luke Kennard:** "It kind of slows them down in transition, but at the same time, we have to be smart about when we do tag, when we do crash the boards. We want to be aggressive, we want to slow them down, we want to try to get more possessions. We want to win the possession game as much as we can every single night. But I think for us, like tonight, when we got a couple in transition, kind of not leaking out but just kind of getting behind our defense, and they were able to play fast with some throw-aheads. It's stuff that we're still trying to figure out, but our habits have been really good. We talk about things and we translate them to games."

**5. Luke Kennard — "the first guys out there, you want to set the tone for the entire night" — Luke Kennard on the impact of preseason starting lineups** [05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UD_Pgmf3cY0&t=301s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "When you are starting, obviously this is just preseason, does that help your mental game at all when you go into the game and you're in the starting lineup?"

**Luke Kennard:** "When you start a game, you're the first guys out there, you want to set the tone for the entire night. For me, it's being aggressive, competing, playing hard, being all over the place, taking shots on the offensive end, being aggressive in that sense, but defensively just competing and talking and being aggressive. So yeah, I think for me, it definitely gets you going a little bit. That adrenaline at the start of games definitely raises the level a little bit, but either way for me, I'm excited just to be a part of this group and help any way that I can."

**6. Luke Kennard — "it's about being professionals and coming in here and finding that role and fitting in" — Luke Kennard on building professional trust with new teammates** [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UD_Pgmf3cY0&t=357s)
