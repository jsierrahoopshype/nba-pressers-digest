**1. Charles Lee — "He's a very deserving coach to have another head coaching position." — Charles Lee on Taylor Jenkins' coaching opportunity in Milwaukee** [00:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5FVDKuu2iVk&t=14s)

Charles Lee: "I am very happy for **Taylor Jenkins** and his opportunity, not only for him, but also for his family. He is a very deserving coach to have another head coaching position. I think that he has earned that opportunity from all the hard work that he has put in. I believe he is the leading wins head coach in Memphis Grizzlies history. He has obviously helped rebuild that program and led them to the playoffs and he has shown the player development mindset that he has to help guys get better and be able to sign longer-term contracts. He has impacted a ton of people, he has impacted a whole organization."

**4. Charles Lee — "I see the person, the professional, the way that he takes care of his body." — Charles Lee on Dennis Schroder's professional growth and impact** [04:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5FVDKuu2iVk&t=291s)

Charles Lee: "It is a holistic view of how much he has grown. I see the person, the professional, the way that he takes care of his body, to be working with the German national team most of the summer and then taking a couple weeks to go back and spend some time with his family and then come to training camp ready to go and tip-top shape. I am so impressed with the professionalism that he has, the communication level. He and I are able to talk about what is going on right now, what is new, what has he done before. I always believe as a coach we can learn from some of the players, especially the really good players and he has done it a long time. So, I trust his input and thoughts and opinions. And then I think that he has just shown to the young guys what it is like to work every day. A teammate has something going on, **Kobe White** in particular has an event going on and he and his wife get a babysitter, their first couple nights in Charlotte, but it meant that much to him. I see a ton of growth from him as a person, but a professional, and then on the court, I think that his competitive spirit shines through."
