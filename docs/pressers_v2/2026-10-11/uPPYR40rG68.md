**1. Jordan Ott — "We wanted to see what fit with that lineup." — Evaluating player performance in preseason lineup experiments** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPPYR40rG68&t=21s)

Jordan Ott: "It is good reps. I did not know exactly who they would play, but we wanted to see what fit with that lineup. Obviously, our lineup is not complete there either, but just giving them the reps, get out, start, see what it looks like. Also, back to that second unit, we will take a look at the film. A lot of times, the impact of that position is not always the box score stats. 10 to 15 is a heck of a night, but it does not matter who plays the five. We need 48 minutes of great five-man play."
