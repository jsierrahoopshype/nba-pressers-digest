**3. Nate Ament — "it's public knowledge at this point that Kevin Durant is going to try to get to his" — Nate Ament on the difficulty of guarding elite scorers like Kevin Durant** [02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n_MlmBEX6A0&t=146s)

Nate Ament: "It's public knowledge at this point that **Kevin Durant** is going to try to get to his pull-up. He's going to try to shoot over top of you. As a kid, I remember thinking I just want to guard him. I think I could do a good job on him. Then you get all excited when you get out there, you're jumping at every pump fake, you're trying to push him and shove him. It's got to be super specific because the margin for error, the margin for winning and losing is very thin. It's almost what is this guy like doing versus what he's not like doing and what are you showing him on defense."
