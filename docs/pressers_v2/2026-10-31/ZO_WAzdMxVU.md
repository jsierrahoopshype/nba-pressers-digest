**Unidentified speaker:** "What was it like for you guys just to see the performance that **Ron** had today, especially knowing how hard he works on his game, especially on offensive end and his shots?"

**Duncan Robinson:** "It was awesome. I think you saw just the reaction from the group on the bench, how much we all love seeing it. He deserves it, he earns it. **3. "I think it's starting to slow down for him, for sure. I think anytime you start the season, there's all these narratives." — Duncan Robinson on the team's offensive growth and aggressiveness** [01:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZO_WAzdMxVU&t=115s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "We saw **AT** was everywhere on both ends, especially in the first half. Just how have you seen him kind of grow into his game and understand how much work that meant to keep you guys on offense with his being aggressive?"

**Duncan Robinson:** "I think it's starting to slow down for him, for sure. I think anytime you start the season, there's all these narratives and expectations and what not. Just getting the opportunity to try to settle, a couple games in, we start to get back to our identity as a group. And then for him, I mean, he's so versatile, he's so dynamic. He's just fun to play with. The rim pressure that he's able to provide is really unique. For us, it's really on us to allow him and unlock the best version of himself. Obviously, he's going to will it himself, but it's a group and collective effort."
