# Damian Lillard Postgame Interview | Portland Trail Blazers 112, Utah Jazz 90 | Oct. 31, 2026 — *Portland Trail Blazers*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3N5Dxj8LDVM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3N5Dxj8LDVM)

_Speakers identified: Brooke Olzendam, Damian Lillard_

**1. Damian Lillard — "We knew we were going to get in that altitude, it was going to have us a little bit winded." — Damian Lillard on the Trail Blazers' preparation for high-altitude games** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3N5Dxj8LDVM&t=11s)

Damian Lillard: "We came out here, we knew we were going to get in that altitude, it was going to have us a little bit winded, but I think we watched that film, paid attention to the tendencies, and we were on top of it. We turned the ball over a little bit, got sped up a little bit, but ultimately we stuck to the scouting report. We were sharp, we defended, we rebounded the ball, and I think that allowed us to get off to a good start and we just held it down from there."

**2. Damian Lillard — "We switched up into the ball aggressively to where they weren't coming off comfortable to pull up threes." — Damian Lillard on the Trail Blazers' defensive strategy against the Jazz** [00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3N5Dxj8LDVM&t=46s)

Damian Lillard: "We wanted to keep them in the paint when they were trying to run guys up in the pick and roll. We communicated well, we switched up into the ball aggressively to where they weren't coming off comfortable to pull up threes, and even with that, we didn't let them turn the corner and get into the paint. We had some resistance on the ball, we got deflections, we were in the passing lanes, we were on a string, and we did it consistently and it led to a pretty good win for us."

**3. Damian Lillard — "I just told them if they run up there, I'm going to just switch and jump into the ball." — Damian Lillard on his defensive adjustments in the third quarter** [01:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3N5Dxj8LDVM&t=78s)

Damian Lillard: "To start the quarter, I kept hearing their coach yelling out for my guy to go set ball screens to try to put me in action. We got coverages where they try to show and all of those things, but at some point, you're just going to have to switch and you're going to have to sit down and guard. So in that third quarter, I just told them if they run up there, I'm going to just switch and jump into the ball and I'm going to have to guard and get some stops, and that's what I did."
