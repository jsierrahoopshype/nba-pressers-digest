# Houston Rockets Postgame: Ime Udoka talks win over Clippers — *Houston Rockets*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4)

_Speakers identified: Ime Udoka_

**1. Ime Udoka — "when they did switch, I think him and Alperen had a good chemistry on that two-man game" — Ime Udoka on the chemistry between Kevin Porter Jr. and Alperen Sengun** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=6s)

Ime Udoka: "He was going well in the first half and when they did switch, I think him and Alperen had a good chemistry on that two-man game. They were going to have their advantage and the doubles came and they moved it. Trying to get him the ball all the time and so some nights they double, some nights they let him go one-on-one and he exploits it. So, taking what the game gives him and like the matchups he had tonight."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=6s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=6s)

**2. Ime Udoka — "seems a little bit loud when Garland hits three in a row, but for the most part" — Ime Udoka on the team's defensive effort against the Clippers** [00:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=56s)

Ime Udoka: "Attention to detail was great. They are a heavy three-point shooting team. To hold them to 28 percent, I think eight percent, only 11 makes. Seems a little bit loud when Garland hits three in a row, but for the most part kept those guys in check, got them off the three-point line, which they heavily rely on and so I like our attention to detail on certain personnel. Stayed down on some Ingram pump fakes and gave them some fouls and buckets there, but overall 104 is 104."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=56s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=56s)

**3. Ime Udoka — "I love the fact that we got our ISO guys, we got one of the best post players" — Ime Udoka on the Rockets' offensive balance and versatility** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=96s)

Ime Udoka: "It's been solid overall. We're improved offensively and everybody has individually, but collectively I feel like we have good balance and a variety of ways to get it done. We want to obviously play with pace and cause some turnovers to get out and I think we're really good when we do that, but also half-court execution is a big part of it. I love the fact that we got our ISO guys, we got one of the best post players in the league as well as Kevin on the on the block and several different pick-and-roll handlers, several different shooters, so it's not relying on one style of play."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=96s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=96s)

**4. Ime Udoka — "his energy and the way he kind of wreaks havoc off the bench is great for us" — Ime Udoka on Tari Eason's impact off the bench** [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=148s)

Ime Udoka: "For the most part, that's what he's done here. He started some games obviously with the injuries in and out, but his energy and the way he kind of wreaks havoc off the bench is great for us. It's a change of pace when he comes in, you're going out with our man and bringing him and Marcus, so the physicality and defensive intensity is still there. And so, yeah, he's shooting the ball well, knowing when to slash, mix it up, get out in transition and doing what he does usually on defense. So, I love the boost he gives us."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=148s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=148s)

**5. Ime Udoka — "being more aggressive, trying to get downhill, touch the paint and really attack the basket" — Ime Udoka on Amen Thompson's increased aggression** [03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=192s)

Ime Udoka: "Being more aggressive, trying to get downhill, touch the paint and really attack the basket. He's missed some early on in the season that, you know, we said we love those looks and I think just a concentrated effort to get to his spots and be more aggressive as far as finishing. And so, I'm not really worried about that. It's some nights like I said it's different guys and when he's aggressive we're that much better."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=192s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=192s)

**6. Ime Udoka — "when the ball is moving and we're taking care of it and it's popping around the gym" — Ime Udoka on late-game execution and team versatility** [04:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmYnUII1ti4&t=259s)

Ime Udoka: "Yeah, I mean a lot's due to matchup and what's going well. We always feel like Alperen and Kevin have an advantage most nights and depending on how the defense plays it, other guys will be recipients of that. But yes, you know, I think we still don't want to just rely on those two. We got other guys that can score. Obviously Fred is going to shoot the ball well, Amen is going to get downhill, Jabari will mix it all in and when the ball is moving and we're taking care of it and it's popping around the gym like tonight, 35 assists on 46 buckets, it's kind of equal opportunity and harder to guard."
