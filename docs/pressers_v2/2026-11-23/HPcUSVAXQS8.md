# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 11.23.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcUSVAXQS8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcUSVAXQS8)

_Speakers identified: Taylor Jenkins_

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "It was great to see our guys put 48 minutes together." — Taylor Jenkins on the Bucks' resilience and physical play** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcUSVAXQS8&t=15s)

Taylor Jenkins: "It was great to see our guys put 48 minutes together. I mean, you look at the start of the first quarter and the start of the third quarter, we didn't get off to the right starts both quarters there, but our guys just were resilient all night. Obviously, we had a tough one against them in our first game back in Milwaukee, and this was going to be a tough one again. The physicality, obviously **Barnes**, heck of a player. I thought we did a great job throughout the game just trying to make him take tough shots, but he's hard to shut down. I thought our rotations for the most part were really good, and then just trying to keep them off the boards. I mean, they had 31 second-chance points in the first game. We talked about it at halftime, 14 for the game, that's a big deciding factor."

**5. Taylor Jenkins — "Cas, he's shown a lot of good growth this season." — Cas's growth and two-way ability** [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcUSVAXQS8&t=186s)

Taylor Jenkins: "**Cas**, he's shown a lot of good growth this season. Sometimes the way the game shakes out, I know he didn't play there in the second half, but he's got a great ability to get into the paint, how he's navigating screens, attacking space, getting the three assists. **6. Taylor Jenkins — "You see Tyler and Braden being primary creators for us." — Execution and trust in the closing stretch** [04:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcUSVAXQS8&t=245s)

Taylor Jenkins: "You see **Tyler** and **Braden** being primary creators for us, and you got **Shed** and **Barnes** out there, and **Murray** **Boyle**, they're really hard to go against. I thought we got the right matchups, we got the ball into the right spots, and the unselfishness for **Tyler** to be like, 'Hey, **Braden**, you got this this time,' or **Braden** seeing something, willing to get it to another guy. **Kuz** coming out being a facilitator for us. Guys made big-time shots, Miles with a big three, **Kuz** with a big three, getting two on the ball, but then also **Braden**'s ability to attack and get into the teeth of the defense and go finish, had a big finish there. But then we knew they were going to start hitting, that's what Darko does, starts trapping. Thought our guys displayed great poise, we let the clock go down, **Tyler** obviously gets a big-time shot, and then the execution, they're going to be switching, they're going to be physical, thought for the most part we did a good job down the stretch."
