# Houston Rockets Postgame: Kevin Durant talks big fourth quarter and win — *Houston Rockets*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ)

**1. Kevin Durant — "Usually when you get more shots up than your opponent, you have a good chance of winning." — Kevin Durant on his process for analyzing game statistics** [00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=7s)

Kevin Durant: "I'm looking at field goal percentages of both teams. Then I look at how many shots both teams took. Then I look at the turnovers. Usually when you get more shots up than your opponent, you have a good chance of winning. And then I look at my stats."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=7s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=7s)

**2. Kevin Durant — "You see other teams give up easy shots to try to stop guys in the paint." — Kevin Durant on the Rockets' defensive strategy and scouting** [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=30s)

Kevin Durant: "You look at our team. Our scouting this was the 18th game of the year. They are looking at our stats and seeing guys, looking at our film and seeing guys stepping up, knocking down shots. You see other teams give up easy shots to try to stop guys in the paint or stop me on a pick and roll. So I felt like they just tried to play solid defense tonight. They did a good job of switching up coverages throughout the possession, which threw us off a bit. But I think we got our rhythm. Outside of **Darius Garland** going crazy in the start of the third quarter, I feel like we had the game under control."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=30s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=30s)

**3. Kevin Durant — "I feel like we had to make multiple passes tonight." — Kevin Durant on team ball movement and offensive flow** [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=73s)

Kevin Durant: "I just think it was a flowing game. I don't think coach we haven't talked about it at all. I know that's been a topic of discussion a lot throughout the season thus far, but I feel like we had to make multiple passes tonight. They did a good job on our initial actions and this season our initial actions have been getting us some good looks. So sometimes the ball is not going to find guys on that backside when we just pick and roll, dunk or pick and roll, lob or catch and shoot three. So tonight they made us work a little bit harder and the ball found me a little bit more."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=73s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=73s)

**4. Kevin Durant — "I love that we got more shooting on the floor." — Kevin Durant on the importance of perimeter shooting for the Rockets** [01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=116s)

Kevin Durant: "I love that we got more shooting on the floor. I think that was the main problem for us last year. The lack of shooting that we had on the floor and the lack of three-point attempts. I'm not saying we're going crazy and shooting 40 or 53s now, but we are more efficient because we got more shooters out there and that's that simple. I think a lot of that stuff that why people hated last year so much was because we couldn't, we didn't knock down a lot of shots from the perimeter. I feel like we got guys that are shooting with confidence and we just getting them up too."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=116s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=116s)

**5. Kevin Durant — "I just think overall we just don't like losing." — Kevin Durant on team accountability after defensive struggles** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEWB4xc9VeQ&t=153s)

Kevin Durant: "I just think overall we just don't like losing. I just think that we know that we had that game, we could have won easily. So yeah, I mean each individual knows exactly what happened on the defensive side of the ball and you dig down deep, hold yourself accountable internally and come back just with a better effort. It's a long season. We played against a championship-level organization. Offense is a part of our league now, so it's going to be times where we can't get a stop. It's that simple. They hit tough shots on us, but that's the name of the game sometimes. So we can't get discouraged, we got to come back to the next game and play with that same energy and effort."
