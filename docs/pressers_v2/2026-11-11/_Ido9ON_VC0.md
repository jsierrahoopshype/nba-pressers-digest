**5. Jordan Ott — "We're bottom 10 in three-point rate, I don't think that's where we want to live." — Addressing the team's three-point shooting and shot selection** [02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Ido9ON_VC0&t=134s)

Jordan Ott: "We got 43 of them up tonight, I think it was 26 last night. We're bottom 10 in three-point rate, I don't think that's where we want to live. I think we want to live a little bit above that, taking high-quality shots. Part of that's catch-and-shoot, but those guys have the ability to shoot off the dribble, especially in drop coverage once we get a good screen, let it go. I thought **Luke** took a couple good ones last night too, when he had a small window he was able to let it go, and keep encouraging them to let it go. And then obviously, **Collin** took the message well, and he's know anytime we get him up there 8, 9, 10 threes, we feel really good about it."
