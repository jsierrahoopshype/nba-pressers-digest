**4. Alvan Adams — "I worked here from 75 to 2025 in one capacity or another." — Alvan Adams on his 50-year tenure with the Phoenix Suns organization** [05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRYzxN4-XT0&t=310s)

**6. Alvan Adams — "These new units sat about two feet further back. So I figured it was a safer distance." — Alvan Adams on selecting new basketball standards for the arena** [08:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRYzxN4-XT0&t=520s)

Alvan Adams: "One of my jobs was to be the purchasing agent for all FF&E, all furnishings, fixtures and equipment. One of the things I investigated real closely were basketball standards. There was a company out of Oregon and they had a new goal that they were selling in Europe and the Blazers used at their training camp. In the old days, the support, because I was watching some old video of **Dick Van Arsdale** getting smashed by **Wilton Chamberlain** into the old support of just some metal poles crisscrossed and it's only four feet beyond the end the baseline. These new units sat about two feet further back. So I figured it was a safer distance. But I was worried about the longer lever arm. So when **Will** goes up and dunks it or **Mark West** goes up and dunks it, how long does it do that before it restores to its quiet position? I decided to go ahead and buy those."

**7. Alvan Adams — "Our first nationally televised game in this building, us playing Orlando and this new big Shaquille O'Neal." — Shaquille O'Neal breaks the basketball goal during a televised game** [08:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRYzxN4-XT0&t=520s)

Alvan Adams: "Our first nationally televised game in this building, us playing Orlando and this new big **Shaquille O'Neal**, and he didn't even swing on or do a chin up. It was late in the second quarter, he grabbed it and let go and the whole thing quietly went back to its restored position. The NBA people were here, the commissioner was here, the guy I called about the goals, I saw him at halftime. He looked at me and he goes, Alvan, I said, I'm fired. I'm fired. Luckily, at the time the league only required you have a spare backboard because of **Darryl Dawkins** and those guys used to shout about. We had luckily the old two units from the Coliseum. So we were able to get them out, wipe them down with some paper towels and get it out there and finish the game."
