# Postgame Media Availability with Coach Daigneault, JMac, and Shai | OKC Thunder | November 10, 2026 — *Oklahoma City Thunder*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3CLul78Qpu0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3CLul78Qpu0)

_Speakers identified: Mark Daigneault, Jalen Williams, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander_

**1. Mark Daigneault — "He doesn't know anything. He just got here yesterday, so it's a hard position to put him in." — Mark Daigneault on Nikola Topic's debut and development** [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3CLul78Qpu0&t=152s)

Mark Daigneault: "He doesn't know anything. He just got here yesterday, so it's a hard position to put him in. He doesn't know the playbook, he's learning everybody's name. He's calling me coach, I think it's because he doesn't know my name is Mark yet, but he'll get there. I thought he played good minutes, he was functional out there. We were positive in his minutes in the first half, so that's our philosophy. If there's an opportunity, get him out there and let him get started, and I thought it was good for him to even get the minutes down the stretch tonight."
