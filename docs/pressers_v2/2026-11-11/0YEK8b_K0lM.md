**3. Will Hardy — "I think it's just the types of turnovers. Not all turnovers are the same." — Will Hardy on diagnosing turnovers and team rotation adjustments** [05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0YEK8b_K0lM&t=302s)

Will Hardy: "I think it's just the types of turnovers. Not all turnovers are the same. We have 19 of them, only eight are live ball, so you're looking at things like moving screens or the ball going out of bounds, whether that's off the dribble, a pass. Like Simone through that rocket there towards the end, that was a great look, just missed Josh Green. So I think we always go back and watch and we have to evaluate, is this a decision-making turnover, like we made a bad choice, or is it a physical error, like I tried to pass to you, you were the right person to pass to, and I just threw it too high."

**4. Will Hardy — "I think he's handling ball pressure really well. That's the biggest thing that I'm seeing." — Will Hardy on Kris Dunn's development as a primary ball-handler** [07:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0YEK8b_K0lM&t=471s)

Will Hardy: "I think he's handling ball pressure really well. That's the biggest thing that I'm seeing. In back-to-back games, like when he's the primary handler, last game with **Jaden McDaniels** picking him up, and then a guy like **Davion Mitchell**, who has a big-time reputation in our league for ball pressure, I think **Kris Dunn** is handling himself pretty well. Using his burst, better at setting up screens, being more patient, and then he's starting to really see the moments when the ball has to be kicked to his teammates to allow the game to open up for him as it goes along. Being a primary decision-maker is hard, but **Kris Dunn**'s studying it a lot, he's working on it a ton, and I think we're seeing a ton of growth."
