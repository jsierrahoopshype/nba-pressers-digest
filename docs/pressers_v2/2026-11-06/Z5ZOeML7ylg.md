# Devin Booker Postgame On-Court Interview after Suns win against Orlando Magic | 11-5-26 — *Phoenix Suns*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z5ZOeML7ylg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z5ZOeML7ylg)

_Speakers identified: Devin Booker, Unidentified speaker, Miles Bridges_

**1. Devin Booker — "We came in with the right mindset. We've been turning in the right way." — Devin Booker on the Suns' recent performance and team mindset** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z5ZOeML7ylg&t=0s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Back-to-back 30 pieces for you. You have the hot hand against tonight's win in the first half. How were you able to get it going early?"

**Devin Booker:** "Just feeding off the energy from yesterday, trying to get a much-needed win. We came in with the right mindset. We've been turning in the right way. They haven't converted into wins yet, but getting a win today was special."

**3. Devin Booker — "Taking on the challenge of guarding Franz, hitting stepback threes, getting boards." — Devin Booker praises Miles Bridges' versatility and rebounding** [00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z5ZOeML7ylg&t=48s)
