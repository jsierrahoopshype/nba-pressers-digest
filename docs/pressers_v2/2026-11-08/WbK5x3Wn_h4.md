# Jared McCain on Teammates and Enjoying the Work | Postgame Media | November 7, 2026 — *Oklahoma City Thunder*

**1. Jared McCain — "It is super impressive to see. He worked on a lot of the new stuff." — Jared McCain on his teammate's improved offensive versatility and work ethic** [00:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbK5x3Wn_h4&t=26s)

Jared McCain: "It is super impressive to see. When I went out there and was working out with him, we worked on a lot of the new stuff that I did not expect. I thought we would be doing more of the mid-range stuff and stuff that he did last year. To see it now, shooting more threes and catch-and-shoot and backdoors, it is pretty cool to see that translate. It just speaks to his work and how he is able to use exactly what he works on and put it right into the game."

**3. Jared McCain — "There is going to be times where you miss shots. It is something that you have to work through." — Jared McCain on learning to stay confident through shooting slumps** [01:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbK5x3Wn_h4&t=119s)

Jared McCain: "**Isaiah Joe** helped me a lot. Any questions I had defensively, offensively, we talked a lot about just being a shooter and how that works in this league. There is going to be times where you miss shots. It happens, it might happen again. It is something that you have to work through and I think staying confident is huge. Being able to affect the game defensively, rebounding, something, you have to find something to affect the game and that usually gets yourself going on the other end anyways. For me, I just try to rebound and play super hard on defense and now I can continue that and use both."
