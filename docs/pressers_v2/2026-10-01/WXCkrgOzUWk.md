**1. Micah Nori — "the thing that we're trying to do with Scoot is continue to play with pace" — Micah Nori on Scoot Henderson's development and playing with pace** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXCkrgOzUWk&t=50s)

Micah Nori: "The thing that we're trying to do with Scoot is continue to play with pace. The things I just tell him is just go as fast as you can. If somebody's in front of you, pass it. If they don't, just lay the ball in. Just simplify his game. I think that Scoot to me is more of a combo type guy than just a true point guard, and I think he would agree with that. So I told him I don't need you to worry about everybody else as far as facilitating and orchestrating. We will get stuff off of you just pushing pace and being who you are."

**2. Micah Nori — "I kind of look at him as a Russell Westbrook type player" — Micah Nori compares Scoot Henderson's playstyle to Russell Westbrook** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXCkrgOzUWk&t=86s)

Micah Nori: "I kind of look at him as a **Russell Westbrook** type player where he can be very dominant on both sides of the floor. He can also impact the glass. He's a strong, athletic guy and just that's how you want him to play when he's on the ball. Just play with pace, attack gaps, attack the paint, kick out from there. Make sure you get your finishing, and I think that's what we're looking for."

**5. Micah Nori — "our coaches will coach and I'm not running every single drill" — Micah Nori on his coaching philosophy and staff involvement** [05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXCkrgOzUWk&t=308s)

Micah Nori: "In our practices, our coaches will coach and I'm not running every single drill. I'm not the only voice they hear because I think that there is a shelf life to your voice and just constantly, you end up, I always joke, you sound like Charlie Brown's teacher after a while. That's what we don't want. And then you just try and pick and choose your spots, and I think that hopefully by again, letting them know what's expected, holding them to that standard and holding them accountable to it, so when you do get on them, then they are like, oh yeah, that makes sense."

**6. Micah Nori — "Quinton Crawford is running the defense and then Jay is doing the offense" — Micah Nori outlines the coaching staff responsibilities** [07:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXCkrgOzUWk&t=448s)

Micah Nori: "**Quinton Crawford** is running the defense and then **Jay** is doing the offense. **Mike Williams**, who is my number two, is doing all special situations and late game stuff and helping like rotations and all that. And then what Mike is doing, Mike is still a young coach as is Q, but Mike is great on both sides of the ball. And so what I have him doing is not to necessarily pigeonhole him, but he's helping **Jay** when we split up stuff and we do two offensive stations, he'll take one while Q's got a defense and then vice versa. So it's a nice mix and a nice flow."
