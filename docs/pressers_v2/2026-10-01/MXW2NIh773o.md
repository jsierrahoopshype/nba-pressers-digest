**3. Taylor Jenkins — "It becomes infectious the rest of the play and then that translates to a little rhythm." — Taylor Jenkins on improving team communication and defensive intensity** [03:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MXW2NIh773o&t=198s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I'd say just level of communication is slowly getting a little bit better, learning our terminology, learn the cadence of communication. It's been something I've been really focusing on. It's like a chain reaction, start of a possession to the end of the possession. We're trying to train at a very high velocity and speed. It's constant chaos in the half court and the full court. The shot clock's low, guys have to process the advantage that's there on offense, or then how we're going to start our defense and pressure as much as we can and not just rely on a 24-second shot clock and have rest time built in. So we're trying to stress the mind just as much as we are physically. I identified why, and it started with our defensive intensity, making sure we're starting every play 94 feet. It becomes infectious the rest of the play and then that translates to a little rhythm on the offensive side when we start hitting that lull."
