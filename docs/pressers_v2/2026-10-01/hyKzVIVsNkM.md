Maybe we move OG to the five a little bit more than what we did last year. There are a lot of different ways we can go with the team on both ends of the floor. The biggest thing is knowing that nothing's set in stone."

**10. Coach Brown — "we won in 22, nobody did anything extra special. We had a young player and Jordan Poole step up." — Coach Brown on the team's resilience and player development** [16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hyKzVIVsNkM&t=975s)

Coach Brown: "I experienced it when I was in Golden State. We won in 17 and 18. We lost **Kevin Durant** after that and it was funny because the outside world was like, oh my gosh, the world's going to crash now because KD is that special of a player. When we won in 22, nobody did anything extra special. We had a young player and **Jordan Poole** step up. We had **Andrew Wiggins** probably have his best year since being drafted, come in and step up. And a guy that not a lot of people remember, **Otto Porter**, stepped up. So everybody else just did a little bit more to try to balance out what we had missing from KD. It's no different here. We're not expecting Andre to come in and be Mitch or impact the game in a lot of different ways that Mitch did. Hey, just be yourself, but everybody else has to step up and box out a little bit harder and come across earlier to help stop the penetration sooner."
