**3. Jamahl Mosley — "they understand exactly what it takes to win in this league" — Jamahl Mosley on the sustained high-level play of Steph, Draymond, and Al Horford** [13:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ywtsVHfdgc&t=804s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Can you explain how **Steph** and **Draymon** and **Al Horford** are playing still at such a high level at their age?"

**4. Jamahl Mosley — "anywhere he steps on the court is a challenge" — Jamahl Mosley on the challenge of defending Steph Curry** [14:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ywtsVHfdgc&t=851s)

**Jamahl Mosley:** "Anywhere he steps on the court is a challenge.
