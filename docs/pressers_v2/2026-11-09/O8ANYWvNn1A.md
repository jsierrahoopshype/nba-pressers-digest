# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 11.8.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "we just got to find a way to channel that even more." — Taylor Jenkins on team's performance and need for consistency** [00:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O8ANYWvNn1A&t=26s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I am proud of how we started, outside of that stretch at the end of the first and the majority of the second. We played really good basketball. Those guys had a lot of pop to them. We just got to find a way to channel that even more. Obviously, they went on a little run there to build their lead, but then we kept fighting and only lost the quarter by four. Outside of that stretch there, I thought our guys played really good basketball today. So give **Tatum** and **George** credit, they are heck of a players. We just got to find a way to continue to stay the course, get three stops in a row, things that we have been talking about."

**4. Taylor Jenkins — "the impact that they can make, I need more consistency out of that." — Taylor Jenkins on recent decline in bench minutes** [03:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O8ANYWvNn1A&t=188s)

**5. Taylor Jenkins — "we are not even talking about the future with these guys. We talked about the game today." — Taylor Jenkins on team focus ahead of upcoming schedule** [03:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O8ANYWvNn1A&t=239s)
