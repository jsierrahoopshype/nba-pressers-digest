**6. Collin Gillespie — "You want to be next to your guy. If they get the rebound then you can pick up full." — Collin Gillespie on the importance of tagging and rebounding** [04:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nkg52cGUhgg&t=249s)

Collin Gillespie: "I think it's something that we definitely have talked about. That's the whole principle or the rules of tagging. For us, it's like you want to be next to your guy. If they get the rebound then you can pick up full, you could pressure. We do have to be smart about just running in aimlessly and letting teams get leak outs. I think Portland got a bunch of them in that game where we kind of found ourselves in no man's land and they just got a bunch of leak out layups, easy transition points. I think you kind of have to just read it and be smart about how you do it, but it's definitely impactful when you can get it right and you tag and if there's a miss you're there and able to pick up full, put pressure on them, not just let them be comfortable bringing the ball back up the floor."
