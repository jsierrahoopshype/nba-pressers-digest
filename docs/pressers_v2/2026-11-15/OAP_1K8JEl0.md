# Cedric Coward Walk-Off Interview | Nets vs. Grizzlies — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAP_1K8JEl0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAP_1K8JEl0)

_Speakers identified: Rob Fischer, Cedric Coward_

**1. Cedric Coward — "That's part of our culture, that's part of who we are. Coach preaches it, players preach it." — Cedric Coward on the Grizzlies' collective energy and team culture** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAP_1K8JEl0&t=0s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Cedric, first of all, it was obviously a long day for everybody. How were you guys able to bring energy for four quarters tonight?"

**Cedric Coward:** "That's what we need to be. That's part of our culture, that's part of who we are. Coach preaches it, players preach it, staff was preaching it. So before this game, we knew we were going to have to bring a little extra ump, and we did that today."

**2. Cedric Coward — "I know without Zach today, without QP, without a couple guys, JG, Ty, we needed that rebounding." — Cedric Coward on his rebounding approach and missing teammates** [00:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAP_1K8JEl0&t=19s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Another double-double for you. Rebounding has been huge for you all season. How much of an emphasis have you put on that to try and do even more on that end?"

**Cedric Coward:** "That's always been who I am since I was little. Part of rebounding is see ball, get ball. You want to touch the ball. Plus, I know without **Zach** today, without **QP**, without a couple guys, **JG**, **Ty**, we needed that rebounding."

**3. Cedric Coward — "I got a clip from my personal coach, Coach Thorp. He showed me an opportunity I had." — Cedric Coward on his putback dunk and coaching feedback** [00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAP_1K8JEl0&t=43s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Two plays I want to ask you about. One, the putback dunk on **Jaylen**'s miss. You go over **Deron Sharp**, who was getting rebounds all night tonight. What made you think you could get over him and throw it down?"

**Cedric Coward:** "The previous game, I got a clip from my personal coach, Coach **Thorp**. He showed me an opportunity I had over a putback dunk and I didn't get it and I got boxed out. So he said, 'be more athletic.' So I made sure to go in this game making sure I was more athletic. Plus, Sharp was killing us on the boards all night. So at least get one of them out of his hands."

**4. Cedric Coward — "I see it every day in practice, and I was the only one that threw one to him." — Cedric Coward on the alley-oop pass to Javon Small** [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAP_1K8JEl0&t=73s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Then the other one, the alley-oop pass to **Javon Small**. What made you think that he could get up there and throw that one down like he did?"

**Cedric Coward:** "I see it every day in practice, and I was the only one that threw one to him in Summer League. So I was like, let's get it now in the real season. I knew he was going to go get it. I thought it should have been an and-one, me personally, but he went and got it and shout out to him."

**5. Cedric Coward — "Watch the film, dissect what we can do better, and just take that road dog approach." — Cedric Coward on the Grizzlies' road dog mentality** [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAP_1K8JEl0&t=91s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Back on the road this week. What do you guys want to take from these last couple of games and keep moving forward?"

**Cedric Coward:** "Watch the film, dissect what we can do better, because there is a lot of things we could have done better, not only in this game but in the New Orleans game as well. And just take that road dog approach. We have to. Bring the energy, bring that grit, and bring our own playstyle to wherever we play at."
