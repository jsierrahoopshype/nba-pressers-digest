**4. Erik Spoelstra — "I don't want to give up 20 made threes. That's not going to form enough for us" — Erik Spoelstra on the importance of defensive activity and playmaking** [06:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AzsktxF9Gmg&t=387s)

Erik Spoelstra: "Definitely want the activity level. Definitely want playmakers out there defensively. Make a play. It's not all going to go according to plan, but make a play. It might be verticality, it might be a block, it might be a strip, it might be a deflection. I do know this, I don't want to give up 20 made threes. That's not going to form enough for us. So we'll find out what this balance is, but I also like the efforts that we are getting to shooters and making them do other things."
