**3. Jordan Ott — "Toby's a tank. He absolutely just goes through you, which we know of him for sure." — Jordan Ott on the performance of the second unit** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lpWsgZEs2H8&t=131s)

Jordan Ott: "Tonight it was just those two fives. It was just the five men, so we did a good job of hitting those other guys. **Toby**'s a tank. He absolutely just goes through you, which we know of him for sure. He was so strong. I think those are good reps. We'll take them as good reps. A lot of times we try to get a second guy down there, but when you're going to hit other guys that are coming in to crash, that's why teams are doing it. Wings come in, you can't double team the five, he has one-on-one versus man-man, he got the better of man-man. So I think those reps are great for man-man to get right now early in the season or in the preseason just to see exactly what it takes at this level, it's every possession and it's only going to increase with more teams coming for the offensive glass."

**4. Jordan Ott — "I think there's a lot of bright spots, especially again in Detroit that first half was fantastic." — Jordan Ott's overall assessment of the team's preseason performance** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lpWsgZEs2H8&t=215s)

Jordan Ott: "I think there's a lot of bright spots, especially again in Detroit that first half was fantastic. Going on the road, play like that in the first night. I thought our defensive intensity wasn't good enough to start the game tonight, that's why it was even. But our second unit, that's what they do. They change the game. And then we we really had control and I thought we got some good looks. We'll take a look in the second quarter, we became a little bit more perimeter-oriented because they were switching, but we got to maintain it no matter who's on the court. And then the third quarter just got so choppy. I don't know what is a foul, what is not. I think everyone's kind of guessing at this point, so we'll figure more of that out as the preseason goes along."
