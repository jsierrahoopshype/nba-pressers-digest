**3. Micah Nori — "we don't want to deter that mentality" — Micah Nori on Ja Morant's aggressive paint-attacking mentality** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xOrHP41v1rg&t=120s)

**4. Micah Nori — "if the five-man gets it, they're looking for Ja" — Micah Nori explains the decision-making process for point guard duties** [04:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xOrHP41v1rg&t=289s)

Micah Nori: "Some of it depends on if it's off of a rebound. If it's a rebound, we've told our guys basically one through four, if any of those guys get it to push. If the five-man gets it, they're looking for Ja. Now that also means that if the five is looking for Ja, but **Dame** is open or Danny is open on a throw-ahead, kick it up to them of course. On a make, the clock is moving, it's the same way. If Ja or **Dame** are right there, they can grab it, they both know what we're trying to do."
