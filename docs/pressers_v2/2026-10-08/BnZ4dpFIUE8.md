**1. Taylor Jenkins — "It's really encouraging for a young guard to just kind of play with that poise." — Taylor Jenkins praises the poise of his young guard** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BnZ4dpFIUE8&t=69s)

Taylor Jenkins: "He's just shown an act in the brief time that we've been with him that he relishes those moments right there. I don't think he gets sped up. He's finding his endurance, he's finding the energy store that he's got to have, especially in those fourth-quarter moments to get downhill, to beat pressure. In the first two games we've seen a lot of pressure, but in the fourth quarter, being able to get by it, being able to score, playmake at times, making the hockey assists, it's really encouraging for a young guard to just kind of play with that poise and to be able to face a lot of different coverages and still be able to beat it and make the correct play more often than not."
