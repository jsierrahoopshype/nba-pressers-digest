**3. Micah Nori — "I think the one thing that helps Dame is he doesn't have to bring it up and play pick and roll." — Micah Nori on team playmaking and reducing reliance on one player** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-_xxJL3h-1M&t=102s)

Micah Nori: "I think the one thing that helps **Dame** is he doesn't have to bring it up and play pick and roll. And very much so, I mean this like same as **Denny** as well last year. A lot of times **Denny** had to bring it up and had to carry a lot, especially when the injuries bug struck. So I think with **Ja** and with **Denny** and obviously we know what **Dame** can do with the basketball, but just being able to not have one guy just bring it up and play pick and roll, but to be able to get those guys off the ball, get them in a closeout situation where they can attack downhill. I mean, **Denny** is as good as anybody in the league at getting to the free throw line and getting downhill as we saw. And then anytime you give **Dame** any bit of air space, he's going to knock one down."
