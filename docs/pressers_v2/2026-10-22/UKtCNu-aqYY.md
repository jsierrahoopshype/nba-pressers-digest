# Myles Turner Postgame Press Conference | 10.21.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKtCNu-aqYY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKtCNu-aqYY)

_Speakers identified: Myles Turner_

**1. Myles Turner — "you can't win any basketball game if the other team is living at the bonus" — Myles Turner on the team's defensive performance and foul trouble** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKtCNu-aqYY&t=0s)

Myles Turner: "Overall, I think our biggest problem tonight on the defensive side of the board was fouls. I really think we played a pretty solid defensive game for the most part. I like the way our guys are rotating, I like the fighting competitiveness that we had. But you can't win any basketball game if the other team is living at the bonus for four quarters in a row. And you have two of their better players getting to the line, you're going to play. It's hard. It kills our momentum, we can't really get out and run like we'd like to, and you have to tip your cap to them. They found ways to manipulate the game."

**3. Myles Turner — "we're a younger group, we're a newer group that's trying to learn the cadence of the NBA" — Myles Turner on the team's progress and long-term development** [02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKtCNu-aqYY&t=123s)

Myles Turner: "My message to the team after the game was, fellas, from the first preseason game to where we're at now, we've gotten a lot better. And one of the things I hate that people say, but it's true, is oh, it's a long season, it's a long season. Yes, it's the first game, but if you keep looking at it, hey, it's a long season. You feel like, okay, we'll get there eventually. But that's not really our mindset. Obviously, we're building, and we want to get better every single night. But I think we took a step in the right direction tonight. Obviously, we would have liked to win, and the scoreboard will reflect that, but as a group, I think we played good basketball tonight.
