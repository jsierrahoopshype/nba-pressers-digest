# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 10.21.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1fTJ8ldNXc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1fTJ8ldNXc)

_Speakers identified: Taylor Jenkins_

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "we gave up 40 free throws, go back and watch the tape, see how many were, weren't" — Taylor Jenkins on defensive struggles and free throw disparity** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1fTJ8ldNXc&t=12s)

Taylor Jenkins: "Inability to keep them out of the paint, obviously physicality. They went on a run there, **AD** was playing great in the third quarter. We didn't have an ability to extend the lead. We weathered some storms, but we were only up three. So, we got to do a much better job there. We gave up 40 free throws, go back and watch the tape, see how many were, weren't, but there was some that are going to just be the nature of playing physical in the paint, but there was also ones that we could clean up earlier in possessions. They got in the bonus too quick, but I think that just kind of put us on our heels a little bit."

**2. Taylor Jenkins — "giving up 70 points is going to be a really tough recipe, we just got to get better" — Taylor Jenkins on defensive breakdowns and paint points** [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1fTJ8ldNXc&t=70s)

Taylor Jenkins: "They were getting downhill quite a bit. Obviously, I thought we did a pretty good job on **Trey Young** and his pick and rolls. Obviously, I know he knocked down some big threes, he's an elite playmaker. Try to have a good level of physicality out there, but those guys make great closeout reads when the ball was moving around. They were getting downhill and we didn't have the full commitment of just that backside rotation. Sometimes we were there, sometimes we weren't, but when we were there, they slip behind us on a cut or they got the lobs to the rim. So, we'll go watch the tape. That was a big emphasis is our shell defense. Giving up 70 points is going to be a really tough recipe, and we just got to get better in that area."

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "his two-way impact is something we're just going to continue stressing for him" — Taylor Jenkins on Braden's NBA debut and two-way impact** [01:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1fTJ8ldNXc&t=115s)

Taylor Jenkins: "A lot of good things. The shots were great, but I thought he was making good reads. He was one of our guys that was able to get downhill, rejecting ball screens, open space reads, and just drive situations. But you said, looked very comfortable. I thought he did some good things defensively, just his on-ball defense when he can play physical, keep a guy in front. A lot of learning. This is great for him to get this exposure right out of the gate. Things to build on moving forward, but his two-way impact is something we're just going to continue stressing for him, guarding, being a point of attack guy for us and a guy that can get downhill and obviously a guy that can knock down threes from the outside too."
