# Tuomas Iisalo Press Conference | Grizzlies vs. Jazz — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SiyhPWjgWYM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SiyhPWjgWYM)

_Speakers identified: Tuomas Iisalo_

**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "Ty Jerome is obviously one of those focal points that we go through." — Ty Jerome's impact on the Grizzlies' offensive flow and spacing** [02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SiyhPWjgWYM&t=142s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "The way the offense is built right now is we have a few focal points that we go through and **Ty Jerome** is obviously one of those. I thought he did a good job today of being aggressive initially then getting off of it if he didn't have the chance and trusting that the ball will find him and come back. I think he did a great job with that. Every game we are going against a thinking opponent so I think the logical consequence of this is teams will put more resources into stopping him and that will then in turn open up opportunities for the other players."

**2. Tuomas Iisalo — "They bring a very different type of style into it. You have five shooters out there." — The Grizzlies' bench unit provides a unique, high-pressure offensive style** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SiyhPWjgWYM&t=248s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "They bring a very different type of style into it. You have five shooters out there, you have a ton of downhill pressure. Everybody can shoot, but also we have **Jeremy**, **GG**, **Scotty** able to put tremendous downhill pressure and then you got **QP** at the five stretching the floor and **Cam Spencer**, one of the best shooters in the league. It is really difficult decisions for the other team what they need to do in that situation."

**3. Tuomas Iisalo — "I thought he did a great job with the rebounding. He had 11 rebounds, he had five assists." — Cam Spencer's debut performance and his value to the team** [05:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SiyhPWjgWYM&t=353s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "Typical **Cam Spencer** night. He shot two for 15 and plus 32. I thought he did a great job with the rebounding. He had 11 rebounds, he had five assists. His defense on Markkanen was very impressive. These are the things that I talk about with him that it is easy to forget the kid is 19. The kid is 19 and he is making big-time plays offensively and defensively that takes other guys 10 years in the league to figure those out."

**4. Tuomas Iisalo — "Maybe the best off-ball mover and movement shooter out of all the four men in Markkanen." — Lauri Markkanen's unique skill set as an elite offensive player** [06:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SiyhPWjgWYM&t=397s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "Going against one of the best four men in the world, maybe the best off-ball mover and movement shooter out of all the four men in Markkanen. Did a great job with him playing two positions, playing both the four and five, handling, screening. He just does it all and that is why it is a great performance to build on because we want to be a team that doesn't need to make shots to win and he is a player that epitomizes that."
