**5. Ja Morant — "It's a release. Obviously one guard out there, you want him to bring the ball up." — Ja Morant on the team's guard-heavy rotation** [03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s4u3frksBs4&t=221s)

Ja Morant: "It's a release. Obviously one guard out there, you want him to bring the ball up, put guys in position, run a play. We have multiple guys that can do that for us. So off the rebound, off the outlet, whoever got it can push. The way our offense is built, the system works for everybody, no matter what spot you are on the floor. We always can get into something for that guy. I think it's a plus. It's obviously tougher for defenses to guard."
