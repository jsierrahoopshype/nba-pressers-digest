**5. Jalen Williams — "my role doesn't really change, I kind of do a little bit of everything." — Jalen Williams on his role and playing off Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** [02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ejXanIBlO0s&t=171s)

Jalen Williams: "For me it's not really an adjustment. I grew up playing the one, I was at college playing the one. When I got here everything else was new, so I'm just back to doing what I normally do. But my role doesn't really change, I kind of do a little bit of everything. It makes his job easier. I think what we learned in the Indiana series was just him having to bring the ball up the whole game is exhausting. He exerts himself on defense too, he's in the right spots, he's playing defense, so with him bringing up the ball and fighting pressure and then having to try and score off the first action, it's just a lot. So having him off the ball, I think it opens up a lot of gaps because teams don't want to leave him, and at the same time he's good at reading it."
