**8. Jordan Ott — "Man-Man's ability to space the floor is there." — Man-Man's role in spacing the floor** [06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9IZnZu_oM48&t=365s)

Jordan Ott: "**Man-Man**'s ability to space the floor is there. When he gets the opportunity and he feels good, he can let it go. But he has added a game to where we can pitch the ball to him and play, especially with how teams are pressuring in today's league. You have to play through the five, similar to their split game. That's just another thing that we need to quickly add to our group. **Man-Man**'s been on it for months, and I think you can see it. Even the play with him and CG is similar to the play that Oso and CG. So, that's going to need to continue to be what we do with our fives, play through them and then also space the floor because of our bigger wings and how we can screen, take advantage of some of our size of just trying to get into the paint just with some of that split action."
