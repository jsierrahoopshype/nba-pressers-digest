# Hawks vs. Rockets Postgame Press Conference: Quin Snyder after 121-114 Loss — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUBiuPjsyA8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUBiuPjsyA8)

_Speakers identified: Quin Snyder_

**1. Quin Snyder — "we know how important the defensive boards are for us to get out and go." — Quin Snyder on the importance of offensive rebounding against the Rockets** [01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUBiuPjsyA8&t=82s)

Quin Snyder: "We know how important the defensive boards are for us to get out and go. The best way to attack them is in space. They are so big and they are spread out, and when you get in the half court, it is harder. You have to take those shots. We can look at them and see how contested they were, but a lot of times that is the best shot you are going to get in a possession. When we got on the offensive glass, we were able to get another one of those shots that we can rebound."

**2. Quin Snyder — "Shepherd and Bogie's threes, those were some breakdowns we got to clean up." — Quin Snyder on defensive breakdowns against Jabari Smith Jr. and Dillon Brooks** [02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUBiuPjsyA8&t=130s)

Quin Snyder: "Shepherd and Bogie's threes, those were some breakdowns we got to clean up and those guys are really good shooters, we know that. Someone might have a hot night, but we got to limit their attempts. We are more solid there and rebound, I think the offense becomes easier. I thought we got some good stuff at the rim too, we just were not able to finish."

**3. Quin Snyder — "He was having a tough shooting night and he stayed with it and eventually shots fall." — Quin Snyder on Aaron Holiday's impact off the bench** [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUBiuPjsyA8&t=186s)

Quin Snyder: "We knew him as a player, that is one of the reasons that we were pleased to be able to have him join our team. I thought the thing tonight that we had not seen was that he was having a tough shooting night and he stayed with it and eventually shots fall. And really that is the challenge mentally, when you are not making shots, what else can you do? Can you get stops? Get some stuff easier? Sometimes when you do that, you kind of get thrown into the game."

**4. Quin Snyder — "I thought Dyson did a good job. That is always the question, do you have him on the ball?" — Quin Snyder on Dyson Daniels' defensive performance against Kevin Durant** [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUBiuPjsyA8&t=270s)

Quin Snyder: "I thought Dyson did a good job. That is always the question, do you have him on the ball? Do you have him on **Munir Shepherd**? He is handling. You cannot put him on everybody. Those matchup questions, you have got other guys in the league too that can guard the ball. We are not always going to have them in at the same time and we got Lou as well. So some of those matchups for us, I thought we did a pretty good job. We were solid in the situations."
