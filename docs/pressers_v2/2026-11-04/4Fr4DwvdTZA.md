**3. Tyler Herro — "You got to continue to find ways to be able to sustain a game for 48 minutes" — Tyler Herro on the challenge of playing full 48-minute games** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Fr4DwvdTZA&t=50s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Early in the season it was the fourth quarter, then a little stretch it was the second quarter, then it was the fourth quarter the other night in Minnesota. What do you feel is holding this group back from playing that full 48 minutes?"

**Tyler Herro:** "I think we got some young guys, a new group. I think you got to continue to find ways to be able to sustain a game for 48 minutes, play winning basketball for 48 minutes. This league is tough, things can change fast, whether it's one quarter, three minutes, a couple possessions can swing a game. So you just got to be able to sustain a winnable game for 48 minutes."

**4. Tyler Herro — "I've obviously been fans of like Stephen Curry growing up" — Tyler Herro on his shooting inspirations and competitive drive** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Fr4DwvdTZA&t=84s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "You're one of the best shooters in this league. You're going up against a Boston team that's been long known as a great shooting team with **Paul George**, **Jayson Tatum**. Who's a guy in this league that you look up to as a shooter?"

**Tyler Herro:** "Right now, no one really. I just try to be able to compete with some of the best, honestly. I've obviously been fans of like **Stephen Curry** growing up, seeing the way he's changed the game and how he's shot the three and how it's evolutionized the game. He's still doing it at the age he's at. So you get inspiration from guys like him, but at the same time you got to compete against them guys as well."
