Bryson Graham says the team will give rookie Caleb Wilson grace during his development year.

“Internally, we know that it is a season of growth and development and we are going to give him we have to be okay with giving him grace throughout this year because he is he is going to have some ups and downs. There are going to be there is going to be some progress and then there is going to be some regression. But are we going to be like, Caleb, get out the game, you turn the ball over. It is like, no, that is where we are at. That is the building phase and and it is exciting because Caleb has all the potential in the world and we just kind of like have to water that seed correctly and in time it will sprout into something really, really beautiful.”
