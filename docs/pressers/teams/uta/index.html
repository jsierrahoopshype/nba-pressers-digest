"They're forming as a team" | Coach Hardy speaks to media after preseason win against Phoenix 🎥

Utah Jazz · Oct 17, 2026
WH
Will HardyJazz coach

Will Hardy on Utah Jazz's preseason progress and rebounding struggles.

“A lot to takeaway from a preseason game. I think our team is continuing to show that they're understanding each other and how to play together. Obviously the biggest concern coming out of tonight is the possession battle. They had 20 offensive rebounds. Credit to Phoenix, their effort and intensity was tremendous throughout the game. I thought our defense was pretty good and our rebounding was poor, and it hurt us a lot tonight. That coupled with 18 turnovers, the math is against you most nights. We happened to have a very efficient night offensively, shooting almost 50% from the floor, over 40 from three, and 37 free throw attempts. So we got bailed out by playing a solid game offensively from a shooting standpoint.”

Will Hardy on Keyonte George's playmaking and calm demeanor.

“I thought his playmaking was pretty good tonight. He's such a calm person that he doesn't get rattled, at least outwardly. He's processing the game pretty quickly for a rookie, understanding how to find his teammates. I think there's a lot there that is very positive to work with. He's still fighting some of his good nature in moments. I felt like there were some pockets of the game where he didn't even look at the basket, and sometimes the reality is you're the one that's open. That's why nobody else is open. But again, it's so much easier to work with somebody whose mindset is about the team. From day one, Keyonte has done nothing but give himself to the group. He wants to win, he wants to be a part of the team, and so a lot of positives to take away.”

On Keyonte George

Will Hardy on defensive growth and scheme variety.

“I think we're getting better. We mixed in a couple of different looks throughout preseason, you know, we have to get to know our team and try to understand some capabilities, also introduce some things to them that they can do live against opponents. In practice, it's a little bit trickier because we know what we're doing and so sometimes you can exploit it. Also there's just a different level of familiarity, but doing it against different opponents, you know, through four preseason games we tried to mix some of the defensive looks so that we can continue to work on these things the first part of the season. We do think that to get through 82 games, we're going to need a variety of coverages and schemes defensively, and so this was an opportunity throughout camp to introduce those things, get a good amount of film on them, and I thought the team handled it all pretty well.”

Will Hardy praises Keyonte George and Isaiah Collier's offensive impact.

“I thought they both used their burst to get in the paint. They do it differently. Keyonte George in transition, one-on-one, has the ability to put a lot of pressure on the defense. Not that Isaiah Collier doesn't, but Isaiah uses some off-ball action to get some of those back cuts, turned the corner a few times really nicely off handoffs and pick-and-roll. Those guys both know they have a ton of responsibility to create offense for our group. Sometimes that means that they are just the beginning phase of building the advantage.”

On Keyonte George, Isaiah Collier

Will Hardy discusses his approach to providing feedback to rookies.

“My job is to give Darren a lot of feedback. I need to pick the moments appropriately and I need to prioritize what he needs to hear now versus what he may need to hear later. I have preached to our young players for years now that there is a difference between playing poorly and shooting poorly. I thought that Darren a couple of times made really good choices and just missed. If those shots go in, maybe he feels differently. Darren has been a great learner and he is willing to receive feedback. My responsibility, not just with him but with our whole team, is prioritizing what is important for them to hear tomorrow versus what can wait.”
