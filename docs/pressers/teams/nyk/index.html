Coach Brown on the team's approach to the regular season.

“We're not playing at that level for 82 games. We played at a pretty high level and a lot of people when they talk about it, they talk about Game 4 and Game 6 in Atlanta and so on. Coach BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown on the team's resilience and player development.

“I experienced it when I was in Golden State. We won in 17 and 18. We lost Kevin Durant after that and it was funny because the outside world was like, oh my gosh, the world's going to crash now because KD is that special of a player. When we won in 22, nobody did anything extra special. We had a young player and Jordan Poole step up. We had Andrew Wiggins probably have his best year since being drafted, come in and step up. And a guy that not a lot of people remember, Otto Porter, stepped up. So everybody else just did a little bit more to try to balance out what we had missing from KD. It's no different here. We're not expecting Andre to come in and be Mitch or impact the game in a lot of different ways that Mitch did. Hey, just be yourself, but everybody else has to step up and box out a little bit harder and come across earlier to help stop the penetration sooner.”

On Kevin Durant, Jordan Poole, Andrew Wiggins, Otto Porter Jr.
