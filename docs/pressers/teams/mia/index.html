Head coach Jamahl Mosley discusses the team's selfish play and lack of ball movement during the first half of the game against the Miami Heat.

“Selfish play, which led to them running out and getting easy transition baskets. I think we have to continue to understand as a unit and as a group that we have to do it together. That's the style of play we need to have on both sides of the ball. And I think when we got down, each one of us wanted to chase it, to think that we could get it on our own. But in situations like that, you have to have a level of poise about you to know that that ball has got to move from side to side and find the right matchup and then share it and trust the pass.”
