Khris Middleton describes his approach to mentoring younger teammates on the Washington Wizards roster.

“I think it is just being myself. I think the guys know behind closed doors a lot with the workouts and practices I talk a lot of junk. Just just competitive. I want to win whether it is a shooting game, basketball game, one on one game, anything. So they know I am competitive, they know I want to beat them, but they know I am on their same side too. So there is times where I might say, hey, try this instead of doing what you did, but it is all about motivating. It is all about helping. It is not demoralizing or making seem like I have done more than them since I have been here longer. It is more of just helping. We are all teammates. I still have a lot to learn and hopefully I can help them learn a couple things also.”
