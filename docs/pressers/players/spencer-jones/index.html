Spencer JonesNuggets player

Spencer Jones compares the Nuggets offensive focus from last season to the current team structure.

“Last year the offensive focus was definitely pick and roll with Jamal, getting him in the post, and then Tim Hardaway trying to get him as many shots, and then playing off of that, how the defense reacts to that. This year, it's definitely still that pick and roll concept with Trevon Brazile really being a great passer and facilitator, which gets everyone involved, which I think that's amazing. And having a mid-post hub like Nikola Jokic, at least for me especially, keeps it pretty consistent with how I play with Nikola, in terms of who we're looking for in the end of the possession.”

On Jamal Murray, Tim Hardaway Jr., Trevon Brazile, Nikola Jokić

Spencer JonesNuggets player

Spencer Jones explains how Trevon Brazile's passing ability will impact his own role on the floor.

“Trevon Brazile in terms of he's looking at me in the corners a lot, and so I know I'll be getting a bit more volume for threes and just knowing that he's constantly looking for us on cuts as he's coming off screens and everything like that. You know he's more of a pass-first guy, that'll be that'll be amazing. Like I said, Jamal Murray and Nikola Jokic being able to play out of a hub out of him, and then just like how how much we're going to move the ball, maybe a bit more than the first unit just because like look, you got Jamal and Nikola, you want the ball in their hands.”

On Trevon Brazile, Jamal Murray, Nikola Jokić

On Spencer Jones

Coach Adelman discusses the difficulty of asking Spencer Jones to play a new position after his return to the court.

I mean he's such a, he's a connector when he plays, he just does the right thing, so I'm just glad he's back out on the court.”

On Spencer Jones
