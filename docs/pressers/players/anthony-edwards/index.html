Anthony EdwardsTimberwolves player

Anthony Edwards discusses Jaden McDaniels' aggressive play and his potential to increase his scoring average this season.

“Just being aggressive, exploiting the mismatch. They can't really guard him. They try to put the worst defenders on him. Just trying to give it to him and let him play in space. Jaden is one of the best two-way players in the NBA if not the best. Just him getting more touches and ball screens actions, clear sides, if they put a small guy on him, give it to him in the post. Just him getting more touches, being more involved, I think he going to average 20-25 points easy because he just know how to play the game.”

On Jaden McDaniels

Anthony Edwards describes how his role is changing this season due to the addition of a new point guard.
