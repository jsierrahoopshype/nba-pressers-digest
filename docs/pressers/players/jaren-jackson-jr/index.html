Will HardyJazz coach

Will Hardy discusses the chemistry and matchup advantages provided by Jaren Jackson Jr. and Lauri Markkanen playing together.

“They are both picking their spots pretty well. It is only one game, but we have seen it in practice also. They are both recognizing when their opportunities are to attack and when they need to move it. I thought Lauri Markkanen did not play great in the first half and he ends up with 17. I thought he missed a couple bunnies. Jaren Jackson Jr. had some nice moments where he punished the mismatch with his size. I think when those two are on the floor together, they are a little bit of a matchup problem in terms of their ability to play with some physicality.”

On Jaren Jackson Jr., Lauri Markkanen
