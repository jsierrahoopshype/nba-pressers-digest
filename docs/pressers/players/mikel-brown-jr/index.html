Mike BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown draws parallels between past championship experiences and the team's need for collective improvement after roster changes.

“I experienced it when I was in Golden State. We won in 17 and 18. We lost Kevin Durant after that and it was funny because the outside world was like, oh my gosh, the world's going to crash now because KD is that special of a player. When we won in 22, nobody did anything extra special. We had a young player and Jordan Poole step up. We had Andrew Wiggins probably have his best year since being drafted, come in and step up. And a guy that not a lot of people remember, Otto Porter, stepped up. So everybody else just did a little bit more to try to balance out what we had missing from KD. It's no different here. We're not expecting Andre to come in and be Mitch or impact the game in a lot of different ways that Mitch did. Hey, just be yourself, but everybody else has to step up and box out a little bit harder and come across earlier to help stop the penetration sooner.”

On Kevin Durant, Jordan Poole, Andrew Wiggins, Otto Porter Jr., Andre Miller, Mitchell Robinson

Mike BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown evaluates the physical attributes and veteran experience that Andre Miller brings to the team.

“He's a veteran, he's been there, done that with a few different teams. He's big body, he's got great feet. It seems like his instincts are pretty good on both ends of the floor. The thing that stands out the most, and it's early, we're just getting started, but his feet are quicker than what you could imagine. He's really light on his feet, which is a good thing. Having a guy that size with length and athleticism that understands the league can be beneficial long-term for us.”

On Andre Miller

Mike BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown discusses the challenges of maintaining high-level play throughout an 82-game season compared to the playoffs.

“We're not playing at that level for 82 games. We played at a pretty high level and a lot of people when they talk about it, they talk about Game 4 and Game 6 in Atlanta and so on. That's not going to happen over 82 games. For me, the 82-game season is about trying to find your way. There's a lot that will carry over for this year. We'll also experiment with some new things here and there, but the stuff that we had in the playoffs may not work during the regular season and so we have to be ready to evolve or pivot as the year goes along.”

Mike BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown discusses potential lineup versatility and ways to integrate key players into the offensive scheme.

“Obviously, playing with Cat a little bit more than we did. Being with this group for a year now, you get a better feel of what players can do what. I didn't know if OG could be as effective as he was in the pick-and-roll game. Same with Mikhail. To try to continue to find ways to get those guys involved at the top of the floor is something that will take a look at. We have great cutters that have tremendous size. We'll continue to mix that in. And we all know that Cat is a really good facilitator at the elbow. Maybe Andre is too, I don't know. Maybe we move OG to the five a little bit more than what we did last year. There are a lot of different ways we can go with the team on both ends of the floor. The biggest thing is knowing that nothing's set in stone.”

On Karl-Anthony Towns, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, Andre Miller

Coaching decisions
Oct 1, 2026 · Jalen Brunson and Coach Brown Hold First Media Availability of Training Camp 2026▶ Watch at 10:27
Mike BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown comments on the team's conditioning and the players' physical readiness at the start of training camp.

“I didn't put a ton of pressure on them to get back in the gym in August. We put a little bit more pressure on our younger guys and our older guys. We wanted everybody to start coming back after Labor Day and for the most part, most of the guys did. But again, if they didn't and they needed more time, I didn't pressure them because we'll have our fair share of practices to get back into it. After today's practice, and it's early, all the guys look like they're in pretty good shape, especially for a short summer. We have a conditioning test that we did last year and this year that the guys have to pass to get into camp and they all passed it.”

Mike BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown shares his perspective on his relationship with James Wiseman and his potential impact on the team.

“We didn't stay in touch where we call each other or anything like that, but whenever we saw each other, we obviously acknowledged one another. He's a fantastic human being, fantastic young man. It was one of the things where I'm sure he wanted the best for me and I definitely wanted the best for him. It's good to see him in the gym and when you're that long and that athletic with skill, there's a place for you in this league and sometimes it takes the right moment, the right team, the right situation to be able to fit some place and grow and we have a lot of great guys in camp that have the potential of impacting us in a lot of different ways throughout the course of the year and he's definitely one of the many guys that we have that we're excited about.”

On James Wiseman
