Jalen Green discusses working with Steve Nash during the offseason to improve his game.

“I was shooting with Steve Nash. I guess the beginning of it, me and Steve Nash would just have little shooting conversations and then Steve sat there and watched one day and then after that it was every day, every other day, whatever the case may be. But you know, just to learn the game from a mind like that who understands and done it before at the highest level was one all the accolades you could think of. I mean, it's special to understand and for him to teach at the same time, so I thought it was super helpful, especially for the season that I'm trying to have, and yeah, I think it's going to play a major part for sure.”

On Steve Nash
