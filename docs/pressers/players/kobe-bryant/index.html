Damian Lillard discusses the medical research he conducted regarding Achilles injury recovery timelines for older players.

“When I saw him go down and I saw him walk off the court by himself, Kobe was, I mean his reputation was as a warrior, that's just how he was. So I was kind of like more impressed with how fast he came back. But then when I tore mine and I started to do my homework and do my research and a lot of the, I guess medical people that I I knew I used every resource I had to just get information and I saw that guys who, you know, tear their Achilles in their mid to late 30s when they come back, they take that full 15 to 18 months. They're able to make a full recovery and the people who who don't, they don't make a full recovery.”

On Kobe Bryant
