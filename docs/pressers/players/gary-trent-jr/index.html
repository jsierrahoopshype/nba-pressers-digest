Taylor Jenkins discusses the team's offensive development and how players are learning to handle defensive pressure.

“We're going to face pressure regardless of who it's going to be. All of our guys have to develop against that style of defense. Learning their tendencies, if it's their ability to break down the defense one-on-one, it's playing off of get actions, which we call snap, it's single pick-and-rolls, double pick-and-rolls, it's north-south, east-west, trying to get a feel for one or running habits in our initial shape, which is still a work in progress.”
