Evan Mobley describes his plan to simplify his offensive game by modeling it after Kevin Garnett and Tim Duncan.

“I think offensively, I got to simplify my game just like how I was just talking about with Kevin Garnett and Tim Duncan. They had a very simple package and they got to their spots and just elevated very simply. So, that's what I'm trying to do as well. And defensively, just probably being more physical in certain areas on the floor and just asserting my dominance.”

On Kevin Garnett, Tim Duncan
