Meleek Thomas expresses appreciation for Jose Calderon and his role in organizing the team's training camp.

“Jose Calderon, I was very familiar with his game and now to be in the same organization as him is pretty special. I told him today that I am very appreciative for him having me here specifically and having my teammates here and all of us as one organization just because it takes a lot to infiltrate like a whole camp like this in a different country. You have to price everything out. You have to know everybody stands. So for him to orchestrate that and get that all together with the organization is big time in my opinion.”
