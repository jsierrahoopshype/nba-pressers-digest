Georges Niang discusses his intention to play for the Senegal national team during the upcoming summer if he remains healthy.

“So I have my Senegalese passport. I was planning on playing for them this summer. Unfortunately I had to get a job before I committed to doing that. Previously I committed to them and thought I was, but I am looking forward to playing for them this summer. I know I have said that for the past five summers, but this upcoming summer, as long as health isn't a problem, I will be playing for Senegal. My dad was born and raised in Senegal and I have a good relationship with Ghana, the head coach and Mack Tar, the GM. So I am super excited about that opportunity.”

Georges Niang shares his admiration for Stephen Curry after observing his work ethic and overall basketball skills during workouts.

“Have you ever watched him workout? It is like real discouraging. I thought I was really good and then I watch him and I am like there is a lot of work to do. I think watching someone like that, you will never or I don't think I will ever see someone on my own two eyes that is going to be that special. Shooting three pointers is one aspect of his game that he is extremely elite at, probably the best ever to do it, but there is so many other things that he does on the basketball court.”
