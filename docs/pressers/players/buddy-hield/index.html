Nick Nurse discusses Buddy Hield's potential role as an off-the-bench scorer during training camp media availability.

“I think Buddy Hield is maybe a prototypical off-the-bench scoring guy. As soon as he comes in, if for whatever reason the shots aren't going in or the offense is out of rhythm, he might be a guy to come in and spark that. He's got a very similar, the deep threes, the off the pin-downs, doesn't need much time. He's been great, it's been interesting to have him. I always say that you never really know these guys until you have them and he's really a good role player. You can tell he's smart, he knows what he's doing, he plays both ends. He'll make the open shot, that's been great to see.”
