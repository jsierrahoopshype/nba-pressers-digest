CJ Huntley describes his experience navigating between two-way contracts and the G-League last season.

“I feel it was a learning experience for me. Starting on a two-way and then being with the Valley Suns for a little bit and then being back on a two-way. I really just put my head down and work once that happened. I did not try to make excuses or doubt my ability or what not. I just went the next day, worked, and I decided to stay with the Valley Suns. I actually flew out the next day the next G-League game to Sacramento to play with them. So, I just really took it as a learning opportunity and just put my head down and worked and they congratulated me late March with another two-way, so it was great.”
