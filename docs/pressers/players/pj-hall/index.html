PJ Hall discusses his approach to training camp and his efforts to build team culture by being present and engaged with his teammates.

“I would just say being around. When we are playing, I make sure I go in there. I'm not lollygagging around in the weight room, I make sure I get through my stuff with intensity so that I can get up there and be around guys, watch them play, actually digest the basketball, and be around and support and continue to grow culture with them personally. And also that's also just my personality. I am easy to get along with. I like to think so. And so all these guys are so easy to get along with as well and so everybody kind of meshes together easily.”

PJ Hall describes his connection to the Charlotte area and his excitement about joining the team despite being sidelined for the first two days of camp.

“A lot of the guys keep on saying, 'Yeah man, we are basically home for you.' And I'm like, I mean I'm about three and a half hours away, but my mom grew up here and so I still have family down here as well and so I love Charleston. It's awesome. Aside from that, obviously it's great to be here with the team and incredible opportunity. Even though I haven't been playing these first two days, I'm looking forward to getting after it hard, but it's been incredible to be here, digesting the basketball, learning our system, and getting to know the guys.”

PJ Hall explains how he spent his rehab time at Clemson and leaned on his former coach and teammate Hunter Tyson for support.

“I haven't talked to him in a few weeks, but I talk to Hunter Tyson a good bit. We talk all the time. We've gone through some similar situations in our professional career, but he's been great to lean on. But I spent a lot of time with coach this summer because my first six weeks, you can't really do anything after your surgery. So they were like, 'Go somewhere that you're comfortable,' before you come back to Charlotte. And so I was also in Clemson doing my rehab there and I was spending a lot of time with coach and a couple of the guys that were still around that I played with. And so it was awesome to be able to, when I heard, I called coach and told him what to happen. And I might have messed his golf round up, I probably caught him in the middle of a round, but he was like, 'Dude, come on up, get here tomorrow.' And so it's so nice to have a school that's welcoming back with open arms, taking care of me.”
