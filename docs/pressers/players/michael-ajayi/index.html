Charles LeeHornets coach

Charles Lee praises the defensive energy and rebounding progress of Michael Ajayi during the preseason.

“Really just excited that he was able to earn an opportunity to play tonight. I think that he's just shown us who he can be, who he is as a defender, guarding multiple positions with his size and his length. And I think he'll figure out the physicality part of the game. Coming in and rebounding, I think it's been a really big thing for him as well. And then offensively, just finding his niche, finding where his spots to be able to slash and how can he drive it a little quicker and make quick decisions. But I thought he had some really big offensive rebounds, some extra passes here and there and I know some of those finishes that he missed, I'm confident that we've been watching him all off-season make those. And so really proud of the progress he's making and look forward to just continue to watch it. He's going to have opportunity with the Hornets but also in Greensboro too. The more reps he gets, the better he gets.”
