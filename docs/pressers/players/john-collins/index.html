John CollinsPistons player

John Collins discusses the importance of separating personal feelings from the business side of the NBA while mentoring younger players.

“One thing that I continually understand more and more is to just not take it personal. You know it is a business at the end of the day and trying to just separate what is business and what is real. And you know that is also what I try to lead to our young guys, is to just try to separate the two. But when you come into the gym man, you lock all the way in and you work and you try to just like I said, use that to build a stronger mind. There is going to be a lot of stuff that you cannot control in this league and you know it starts early if you can block out the noise.”

J.B. BickerstaffPistons coach

J.B. Bickerstaff evaluates the versatility and defensive impact of John Collins within the team's current rotation.

“We're working through some of these things and just trying to figure out lineups positionally. But his ability to play the four or the five, his ability to stretch the floor, he's been an elite roller through his career also, so he knows how to play the pick-and-roll if he's at the five with those guards. Defensively, his ability to switch on the perimeter, all good things for us. I love his toughness to get into it with seven and a half minutes to go in the first quarter of a preseason game is right up the Pistons' alley.”

On John Collins
