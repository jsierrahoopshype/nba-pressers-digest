Charles LeeHornets coach

Charles Lee on Dennis Schroder's professional growth and impact.

“It is a holistic view of how much he has grown. I see the person, the professional, the way that he takes care of his body, to be working with the German national team most of the summer and then taking a couple weeks to go back and spend some time with his family and then come to training camp ready to go and tip-top shape. I am so impressed with the professionalism that he has, the communication level. He and I are able to talk about what is going on right now, what is new, what has he done before. I always believe as a coach we can learn from some of the players, especially the really good players and he has done it a long time. So, I trust his input and thoughts and opinions. And then I think that he has just shown to the young guys what it is like to work every day. A teammate has something going on, Kobe White in particular has an event going on and he and his wife get a babysitter, their first couple nights in Charlotte, but it meant that much to him. I see a ton of growth from him as a person, but a professional, and then on the court, I think that his competitive spirit shines through.”

On Dennis Schröder, Kobe White
