Zuby EjioforHawks player

Zuby Ejiofor identifies Aaron Gordon and OG Anunoby as the players he models his game after.

“A great person that I would like to model myself as would be Aaron Gordon or OG Anunoby, top of player, two-way capable guys. Don't need the ball in their hands necessarily to be able to impact the game, but just specializing in their roles. They know exactly what they bring to the table each and every night, and those are guys that do it on both sides of the ball.”

On Aaron Gordon, OG Anunoby

Coach BrownKnicks coach

Coach Brown discusses potential lineup versatility and how to better utilize players like Karl-Anthony Towns and OG Anunoby.

“Obviously, playing with Cat a little bit more than we did. Being with this group for a year now, you get a better feel of what players can do what. I didn't know if OG could be as effective as he was in the pick-and-roll game. Same with Mikhail. To try to continue to find ways to get those guys involved at the top of the floor is something that will take a look at. We have great cutters that have tremendous size. We'll continue to mix that in. And we all know that Cat is a really good facilitator at the elbow. Maybe Andre is too, I don't know. Maybe we move OG to the five a little bit more than what we did last year. There are a lot of different ways we can go with the team on both ends of the floor. The biggest thing is knowing that nothing's set in stone.”

On Karl-Anthony Towns, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, Andre Miller

Nick Nurse76ers coach

Nick Nurse identifies limiting offensive rebounds from Knicks players as a key priority for the upcoming game.

“Josh Hart is not the only one. It's obviously one of their major strengths. It's at the top of the list of what needs to go well tomorrow. They cannot just be collecting a ton of offensive rebounds, kicking them out and hitting threes. It's Hart, it's OG, it's Bridges, it's Deuce, it's Clarkson. They've done a good job. I think their role guys have excelled. That's a major strength of theirs to win those extra possessions, and that makes a difference in a close game.”

On Josh Hart, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, Miles McBride, Jordan Clarkson
