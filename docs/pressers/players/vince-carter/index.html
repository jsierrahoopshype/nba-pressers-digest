Tyrese Maxey defines sacrifice as the effort players put into building camaraderie and working on their games together.

“Sacrifice doesn't always mean you got to give up something. Sacrifice could mean Joel Embiid working out with LeBron James. They're working out together before practice, or me working out with Vince Carter, or Vince Carter working out with Paul George, or me working out with Jalen Brunson. That's sacrifice. You kind of change your routine a little bit to try to get some camaraderie. That's the sacrifice we're talking about. Then getting on your teammates, being able to, hey man, LeBron, you supposed to crack them down right there, or Vince Carter with the X out. That's sacrifice as well.”

On Joel Embiid, LeBron James, Vince Carter, Paul George, Jalen Brunson
