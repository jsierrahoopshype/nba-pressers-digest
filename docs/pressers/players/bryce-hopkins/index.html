Cameron JohnsonNuggets player

Cam Johnson shares advice he gave to Bryce Hopkins regarding defensive closeouts.

“Bryce is a really talented player, and he, you know, he showed his stuff tonight and he's been showing his stuff throughout all of camp. A lot of the ways you can get on the court as a young guy in the NBA is just by doing the little simple things: running the court, getting to the corner, proper spacing, proper defensive coverages and like if the coaches trust you, that gives you a lot of opportunity to be on the court. So on that shot, I'm like when you have somebody that's kind of is a kind of like a wild three, if you close out too wild then you're going to foul him. I just told him like, hey, make sure you close out to the side of him. Go ahead, jump, attack the shooter, but you got like I've done it plenty of times myself. You want to get to the side of him because if he has a leg kick or jumps a little higher than you thought and your body like they're they're going to call it and it's it's a big thing in today's game. You will get called for that, more so than college. College, they're less likely to call fouls on threes for whatever reason. But he's very receptive. All the young guys we have on the team are very receptive.”

On Bryce Hopkins
