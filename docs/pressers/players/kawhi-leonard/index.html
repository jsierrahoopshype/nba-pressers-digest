Darko RajakovićRaptors coach

Darko Rajaković explains his philosophy on building a versatile team with interchangeable positions.

“The way we build teams in Europe, you sign point guard, then two guard, then three guard, then four, and five, and everybody knows their positions. And every time that you draw a play, you say one, two, three, four, five, and everybody knows who is who. And for us is Scotty starts at four, but then he moves to one, then he becomes a five. And Kawhi is three, but a different lineup, he becomes a four. It is very interchangeable. And what it does is the way we wanted to develop our system that is going to take a minute, it is not easy, is that all of those players that can make plays off of each other and understand each other's tendencies and what they do well.”

On Scottie Barnes, Kawhi Leonard
