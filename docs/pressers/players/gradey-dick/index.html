Gradey DickRaptors player

Gradey Dick discusses his focus on increasing his three-point shot volume and building confidence.

“I just build that confidence to shoot it more. I think that was my biggest focus, not really trying to fix my jumper or anything like that, just getting confident with getting shots up. Obviously, me reading the defense is the biggest part. If they're sagging off of me, then I have to shoot it. We want to shoot more threes, we want to get to that 35-ish per game number, so just helping the team out with that as much as possible.”

Gradey DickRaptors player

Gradey Dick reflects on the team's defensive potential and the importance of mental focus.

“I don't think we have a limit to how good we can be. If we're all dialed in, I feel like it's going to be a nightmare for any team if we're all on the same page. It's more mental than than our ability, I feel like. Even if we didn't have the best defenders, I think more just being on the same page when it comes to help and rotations and stuff like that, if a player gets beat, who can be there, step up for him. I think that's going to be our biggest thing to focus on when it comes to how good our defense can be.”

Gradey DickRaptors player

Gradey Dick discusses his defensive approach and his goal to be more aggressive on the ball.

“I just get back to what I am used to. I feel like those first two preseason games I wasn't really being as on-ball as I normally am.
