Peyton WatsonCavaliers player

Peyton Watson describes the tactical adjustments required to play on the perimeter alongside teammates LeBron James and Larry Nance Jr.

“It's a lot different playing from the outside in as opposed to the inside out. Obviously, the first four years of my career, I played with super talented offensive bigs, probably the most talented of all time, and it made things easy for me. But I also had adjustments to make there where it was being in the right place at the right time, being in the right spot, spacing the floor correctly so that he could have the space to operate in the interior. I think now it's a little bit different because it's more so moving with the ball on the perimeter. So, making sure that my spacing is correct depending on which way the ball is driven, and then also finding avenues to cut.”

On LeBron James, Larry Nance Jr.

Peyton WatsonCavaliers player

Peyton Watson discusses how the interior presence of Larry Nance Jr. and Jarrett Allen improves the team's defensive switchability.

“Having that back line there that helps me out and kind of deter shots at the rim or make that ball handler think twice before going in there. And even that little hesitation is enough for me to recover and keep the defense all balanced and keep them guessing. So, I think that having those two guys in their interior presence just helps us a lot. And then also just us, even though those guys are bigger than me, us having similar size, I think our switchability becomes better.”

On Larry Nance Jr., Jarrett Allen

Larry Nance Jr.Pacers player

Larry Nance Jr. discusses how his experience in the league has helped him adapt to the Pacers' offensive pace.

“This team is all about process and speed. I think that's something that really helped in my favor. Being in the league 12 years, playing with a bunch of different coaches, learning a bunch of different vocabularies, learning how to play. I think I've adjusted pretty well to the pace of the offense and I'm just excited to see it in action.”
Oct 19, 2026 · Rick Carlisle, Andrew Nembhard, Quenton Jackson & Larry Nance Jr. Media Availability | Oct. 19, 2026▶ Watch at 10:04
Larry Nance Jr.Pacers player

Larry Nance Jr. reflects on his career evolution and how playing multiple positions has improved his decision-making.

“I wouldn't say it's more so like specific team, but it's specific position. When I got in the league I was drafted as an undersized power forward. Then a couple years later I'm starting at the three in Cleveland and then back to some five and some four and some five again. I think being able to move down the positions early in my career allowed that really helps with the decision-making process.”
Oct 19, 2026 · Rick Carlisle, Andrew Nembhard, Quenton Jackson & Larry Nance Jr. Media Availability | Oct. 19, 2026▶ Watch at 10:32
Larry Nance Jr.Pacers player

Larry Nance Jr. praises the Pacers' organizational culture and how the team has welcomed new players.

“A lot of places talk about culture, but this is a culture. These guys work hard, they are in here early on off days. It's really good for me to see, really good for other guys to see as well that are just getting here. I feel welcome, but they've done an awesome job, the front office, the coaching staff, the players of opening their arms and me, Kelly, and some of the new guys like really doing a good job of ingratiating us.”
