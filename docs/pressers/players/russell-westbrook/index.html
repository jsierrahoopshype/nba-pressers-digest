Shai Gilgeous-AlexanderThunder player

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander shares his thoughts on the legacy and impact of Russell Westbrook.

“His dynamic is they come in terms of NBA player, personality, teammate, he's the whole package. The foundation for what we stand on now, part of the reason why this city is what it is is because of Russell Westbrook, and he deserves all those flowers for what he's done off the court and on the court. It's obviously a little shocking that it's this soon, I feel like he had a little bit more in the tank, but everyone has their own journey.”

On Russell Westbrook

Micah NoriTrail Blazers coach

Micah Nori compares the playstyle of Scoot Henderson to Russell Westbrook during training camp media availability.

“I kind of look at him as a Russell Westbrook type player where he can be very dominant on both sides of the floor. He can also impact the glass. He's a strong, athletic guy and just that's how you want him to play when he's on the ball. Just play with pace, attack gaps, attack the paint, kick out from there. Make sure you get your finishing, and I think that's what we're looking for.”

On Scoot Henderson, Russell Westbrook

Mark DaigneaultThunder coach

Mark Daigneault reflects on the career and impact of Russell Westbrook following his retirement announcement.

“Congrats to him on an unbelievable first ballot career. He's obviously one of the giants of the game not only in Oklahoma City but in the history of the league. He brought a particular spirit to the game and fearlessness and competitiveness that made it very easy to gravitate to him and obviously this city did and he reciprocated. He's an unbelievable member of the community even to this day, he's doing great work in the Oklahoma City community and so special player, special guy.”

On Russell Westbrook
