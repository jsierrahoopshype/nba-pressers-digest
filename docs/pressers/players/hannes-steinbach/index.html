Charles Lee on Hannes's rapid learning and defensive potential.

“He takes the coaching so quickly and he picks up things really fast. The only thing he didn't pick up fast today was that I wanted him to make that after-action a little bit quicker, but he finally got it. And that's who he is and that's who he is and that's like you get to coach him, he wants to be coached, which is really exciting. I think everyone got to see defensively there's still like how to figure out where to be in center field, how to read. We want to continue to push him and his voice and his communication in defensive coverages because he's capable. Like he's got the strength, he's got the foot speed to move laterally, it's just like the communication and how fast can he pick up on all these sets that are happening in the NBA. And then offensively, I think we got to see like he's got a great motor, he's rim running, he's able to seal, he's got really good hands, his touch around the basket, he came up with a couple offensive rebounds or got fouled on the glass too. So really excited about the versatility that Hannes brings.”

On Hannes Steinbach
