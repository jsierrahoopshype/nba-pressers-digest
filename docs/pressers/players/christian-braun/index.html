Christian BraunNuggets player

Christian Braun on the team's offensive consistency and growth.

“Last year we needed to be better in the playoffs obviously, so you want to see more consistency. But like I said, me bringing the ball up and taking care of the ball and not making it just on Jamal or just on Nikola. You know, having more people do more things. So it is not the same thing all year. And I think having guys healthy really helps that too. And it gives us more options in those things. So doing it now will make it easier when we do it in the playoffs. I think especially for me in the playoffs it was weird because you are hurt, you come back in, you are trying to play, you are trying to make sure your ankle is good. And then you get in a playoff series with a really good team. And you have not brought the ball up, you know, all year. So you are just not doing it. And Jamal is looking for me to bring it up, you know, to help him out. And I know that will help him in his legs in the fourth quarter of a big playoff game. And it will help him, you know, not have to do so much.”

On Jamal Murray, Nikola Jokić
