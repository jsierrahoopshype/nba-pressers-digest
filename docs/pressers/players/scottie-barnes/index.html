Darko RajakovićRaptors coach

Darko Rajaković explains his philosophy on building an interchangeable, versatile team system compared to traditional European models.

“The way we build teams in Europe, you sign point guard, then two guard, then three guard, then four, and five, and everybody knows their positions. And every time that you draw a play, you say one, two, three, four, five, and everybody knows who is who. And for us is Scotty starts at four, but then he moves to one, then he becomes a five. And Kawhi is three, but a different lineup, he becomes a four. It is very interchangeable. And what it does is the way we wanted to develop our system that is going to take a minute, it is not easy, is that all of those players that can make plays off of each other and understand each other's tendencies and what they do well.”

On Scottie Barnes, Kawhi Leonard

Coaching decisions
Oct 7, 2026 · Toronto Raptors Media Availability | Practice | October 7, 2026▶ Watch at 16:01
DR
Darko RajakovićRaptors coach

Darko Rajakovic evaluates Scottie Barnes' offensive decision-making and expresses a desire for him to play with more force.

“That's going to be definitely work in progress. We cannot take away from his identity and what he brings to our team, which is hustle plays, rebounding, playing in the dunkers. He just needs to do a good job of recognizing when he needs to go and break the dunker and when he needs to be more in a pick and roll up high. I wish that he played a little bit with more force on offensive end to be honest with you. I'm used to seeing him playing more forceful, going to the rim, finishing above the rim. So that's something that we got to continue working on. I like that he got nine rebounds and that he was active on the glass tonight.”

On Scottie Barnes
