Charles LeeHornets coach

Charles Lee on Naz Reid's versatility and offensive impact.

“Watching him from afar, I think that he hasn't been in a ton of those positions. He definitely has shown throughout his career that he is a very capable scorer and a ton of it I think has been done playing off of other guys and spacing the floor and shooting. But as you continue to dive into the film, there were some areas in which Minnesota did a good job of using him as a screener at times. We want to be able to leverage the versatility that he has and then we also want to be able to push him to another level with some of that maybe mid-post, post-up, versus advantage disadvantages. I think that he has done a really good job so far of just playing under control, letting the spacing around him work and then either figuring it out for himself or his teammates.”

On Naz Reid
