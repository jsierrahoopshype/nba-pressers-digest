Spencer JonesNuggets player

Spencer Jones compares the Nuggets offensive identity from last season to the current approach involving new facilitators.

“Last year the offensive focus was definitely pick and roll with Jamal, getting him in the post, and then Tim Hardaway trying to get him as many shots, and then playing off of that, how the defense reacts to that. This year, it's definitely still that pick and roll concept with Trevon Brazile really being a great passer and facilitator, which gets everyone involved, which I think that's amazing. And having a mid-post hub like Nikola Jokic, at least for me especially, keeps it pretty consistent with how I play with Nikola, in terms of who we're looking for in the end of the possession.”

On Jamal Murray, Tim Hardaway Jr., Trevon Brazile, Nikola Jokić

Julian StrawtherNuggets player

Julian Strawther discusses the challenge of replicating the offensive production left by Tim Hardaway Jr.

“That's all I've heard all summer is just finding ways to replicate Tim's production, the things that he was able to do for us. Obviously he had an amazing year, 6th man of the year finalist, so we're obviously missing that and we got to fill that void. But understanding that my skill set is the one that matches the most with his and my willingness to go out there and shoot. At the end of the day you need a guy that's a little bit unconscious, a little bit crazy in the head to go out there and launch threes as soon as they come off.”

On Tim Hardaway Jr.
