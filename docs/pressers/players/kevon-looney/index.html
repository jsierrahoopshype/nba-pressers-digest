Kevon Looney discusses the importance of team chemistry built during a summer trip to Slovenia with Luka Doncic.

“Being connected, having a great chemistry is a big step to being a special team. There's going to be a lot of ups and downs and you have to be able to lean on that chemistry of being friends, being a family, being a brotherhood to do something special. It's really important.”
