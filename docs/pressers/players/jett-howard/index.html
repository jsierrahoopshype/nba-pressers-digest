Jett Howard reflects on his early basketball memories and the impact of having women coaches.

“I think with my first memory of basketball, I always go back to my first game. It was at Montclair Rec Center. I was playing for the Montclair Mighty Midgets at four years old and that was my first ever basketball game and I got the video still, so it's super special. And then with the assistant coaches, my mom was my coach most of my life, so I've always been used to having women coaches in my life, so this is super special, super huge for the organization.”
