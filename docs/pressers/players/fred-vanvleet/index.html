Jalen GreenSuns player

Jalen Green describes his evolution as a playmaker and the process of learning to control the pace of the game.

“Obviously it's been different, you know. Coming to the league, all you are set out to do is just, you know, score. I'm mixing in here and there just setting up the offense, but I feel like I've had the ball in my hands my whole career, including high school and so forth and so on, all the way into the NBA. So it's not no different from that aspect. It's just the slowing down process, you know, not going one speed all the time and I feel like that's been a main thing of my game, so the slowing down process and not looking for my shot right away in the beginning of the offense, the flow.”
