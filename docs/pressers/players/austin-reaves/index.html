Austin ReavesLakers player

Austin Reaves describes his personal leadership style as one-on-one communication rather than public criticism.

“I think that people lead in different ways. I am not someone that is going to sit out here and scream at people. I like to talk to people one on one, have real conversation that way. People will never feel like I am trying to embarrass them in front of people. Whenever we have a down period of time, pulling people to the side, talking to them about what I see, what I think they can do better, and just trying to give everybody my input and how I see things to help them moving forward.”

Mark Daigneault on Cason Wallace's reliability and defensive versatility.

“Off the ball is where we first noticed his reliability on that end of the floor, which made it very easy to play him as a young player. He was in the right spots, he was executing nothing fancy, but just very, very reliable in all the facets of the game that really matter. And then once he was on the court, we started to realize that he could take on pretty significant matchups. As we saw in the playoffs with Austin Reaves and Booker at times, those are big-time players and he's able to take those matchups to some degree.”

On Cason Wallace, Chet Holmgren, Luguentz Dort, Austin Reaves, Devin Booker
