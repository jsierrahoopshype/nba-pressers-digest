Coby White says his Hornets teammates give him the confidence to take a leadership role.

“Most of the time when guys come in a situation like this, the coaches get them the confidence to go out there and lead and which they do, but I think the unique thing about my situation is my teammates give me the confidence to go out there and lead. Coby White says summer workouts helped the Hornets transition quickly into training camp.

“I think all that time invested allow us to kind of transition quickly in the training camp in terms of competing.
