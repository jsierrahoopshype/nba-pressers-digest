“I've loved Tre Mann's, especially last game, I thought that his ability to be a little bit more efficient with the dribble last game was really good. I think that we just have to continue to help him, give him more direction in some of the called plays, the stick-shift plays, what do we want to get to and how do we want to play. But overall, I love the impact that he's given us offensively. He's another ball handler, creator, and he's playing with a ton of confidence, which I love.”
