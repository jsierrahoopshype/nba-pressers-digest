Head coach Brian Keefe explains his offensive philosophy regarding the importance of having multiple playmakers on the floor.

“I think that's how we've always wanted to develop this as an organization is that we think for us to be consistent and play at the highest level basketball, you can't just have one decision maker. You need multiple. I think the additions of Trey and Anthony are giving us more of a focal point offensively at the playoff, but AJ is going to be part of that, KeyShawn is going to be part of that. We see the secondary playmaking of Bub and what he's brought. So I think there are multiple guys who can do those things.”

On Tre Mann, Anthony, AJ Dybantsa, KeyShawn, Bub Carrington

Charles LeeHornets coach

Charles Lee highlights the growth in decision-making and offensive efficiency shown by Tre Mann.

“I've loved Tre Mann's, especially last game, I thought that his ability to be a little bit more efficient with the dribble last game was really good. He was more decisive, making quick decisions and reads and playing with the pass. I think our team feels very comfortable passing the ball ahead to him. He's been great. I think his decision-making has been really good, he's shooting the ball well. I think that we just have to continue to help him, give him more direction in some of the called plays, the stick-shift plays, what do we want to get to and how do we want to play. But overall, I love the impact that he's given us offensively. He's another ball handler, creator, and he's playing with a ton of confidence, which I love.”

On Tre Mann
