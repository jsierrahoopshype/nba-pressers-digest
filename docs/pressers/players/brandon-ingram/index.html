Brandon IngramClippers player

Brandon Ingram discusses his recovery from surgery on a partial Achilles tear sustained while playing for the Toronto Raptors.

“I am doing well in my recovery. I am coming along. I have been in the facility for a little over a month now, just recovering, getting on the court a little bit, getting stronger, getting stronger in my legs so I can return back safely. I had been dealing with some pain throughout the year with the Raptors and especially coming down crunch time with the Raptors. I did not quite know what it was, but near the end of the season we figured out what it was and we thought that surgery was the best thing to do so I can recover well and be ready for this season.”
