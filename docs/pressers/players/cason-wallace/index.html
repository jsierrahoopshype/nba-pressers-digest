Mark DaigneaultThunder coach

Mark Daigneault reflects on Cason Wallace's early reliability and his ability to defend high-level players in the playoffs.

“Off the ball is where we first noticed his reliability on that end of the floor, which made it very easy to play him as a young player. He was in the right spots, he was executing nothing fancy, but just very, very reliable in all the facets of the game that really matter. And then once he was on the court, we started to realize that he could take on pretty significant matchups. As we saw in the playoffs with Austin Reaves and Booker at times, those are big-time players and he's able to take those matchups to some degree.”

On Cason Wallace, Austin Reaves, Devin Booker

Mark DaigneaultThunder coach

Mark Daigneault explains why execution is a premium requirement for undersized players like Cason Wallace.

“When you're undersized, and he's undersized, McClain's undersized, Cason can be undersized depending on who he's guarding, when you're undersized, that's okay. Not everybody needs to be 6'7", but that's where execution becomes premium. Your margin for error with execution is very low and so you've got to be an execution monster. He's off to a good start with that. He's done a good job there and that's what we've tried to do with players that maybe give something up physically.”

On Cason Wallace
