“I feel like it'll be beneficial for guys. Craig Porter Jr.Cavaliers player

Craig Porter Jr. on finding his path through defensive effort.

“When I came out of college and I was doing a lot of my pre-draft workouts, coaches told me straight up, everybody in the NBA is getting looked at because they can score or their offense. So the only way you're going to get on the court is defense. I understood that a lot of the things that would get me on the court are the little things: hustling, playing defense, and rebounding too. That's one of the things I'm good at at my size, and just understanding that plays a big role into it and it'll allow you to pick your spots.”
