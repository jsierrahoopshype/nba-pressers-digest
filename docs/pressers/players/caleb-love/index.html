Cam Johnson recalls playing with Caleb Love at a summer alumni game and his early impressions of him.

“I like to play in that. A Carolina has an alumni game every summer. And I like to go back because when I was a player, it was really cool seeing the NBA guys come and play in front of the campers. It's in front of kids' campers. And so I try to make a point to get back there and play with my old teammates and stuff and you know, just spend some time with the guys that are there. So this was two summers ago that I was playing. And a lot of the times the freshmen will be put with the veterans, right? So Caleb was on my team. And I knew him, I knew he was a highly touted guy, I knew, you know, he was he was a really good prospect, but I didn't know how ready he was till I got in that game. Like this dude's a freak. Dunking stuff, and then he's like you can see him starting to figure out the game. So I paid attention to Caleb, you know, all throughout that season and you know, I was high on him from day one. I'm like he's kind of like, you know, he's kind of playing down a little bit in terms of like he's not playing as much of a four as he was, but he reminded me of like a a younger, smaller Kevin Garnett, like a 2026 Kevin Garnett. Just talented, can athletically gifted, and so he has a really bright future ahead of him.”
