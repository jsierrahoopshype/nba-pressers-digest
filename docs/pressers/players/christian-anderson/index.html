Charles LeeHornets coach

Charles Lee praises Christian Anderson's growth and defensive impact.

“Love the growth that Christian made. I think just like the rest of our team, with every rep you got better and better. He finds his confidence. I think I love the the defensive impact he had. There are some possessions where he is picking up full court, refusing to be screened in pick and roll situations. I thought his hands were active, coming in and got a couple big rebounds. Like in Boston, he got fouled on one and hit the floor. Offensively, I think that he understands the pace at which we want to play and some of his outlets, he's getting up the court, playing early pick and roll. It's good to see the German connection in effect with him and Hannis and he's hitting them in the seam. I think he's finding where to shoot the ball and how to be more shot ready on the perimeter.”

On Christian Anderson
