Jarrett JackPistons coach

Jarrett Jack explains the team's depth and how players prepare for varying roles within the rotation.

“We're constantly saying everybody's showcasing their ability for their opportunity. Luckily for me, I don't have to make those decisions, but that's one of the strengths of our team, our depth, being able to rely and count on a number of guys given different circumstances. And I think our guys have a respect level for each other's talents in the locker room, and they understand that anyone could get called on. It's not because you're not playing well, it can just be a different look and different skill set. So for us, we just use it to our advantage, understanding that maybe some of the stints that the other teams play won't be as long, but we play ours long, fast, and fierce and physical.”

Jarrett Jack praises Abuka for his composure, basketball IQ, and ability to apply coaching instructions during games.

“He has a very even-keeled personality about him on his own, and that's kind of priceless when you translate it in the game situations. He doesn't get rattled, doesn't get too high or too low. So what he was able to map out tonight, being able to get into the paint, whether to score or be able to read for kick-outs, those are things that he naturally possesses, but with the short time we've been together, he's picked up things really quickly and been able to showcase a lot of the reason why he's a Detroit Piston.”

Jarrett Jack explains why Elijah is a good fit for the Pistons' team identity and culture.
