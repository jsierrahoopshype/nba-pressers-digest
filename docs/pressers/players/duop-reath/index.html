Duop Reath credits his former teammates Robert Williams III and Deandre Ayton for teaching him the importance of consistency.

“Most of those guys, there was some experience there. So just seeing those guys there, they've played in the league for for a while, especially guys like Rob and DA as well. Just learning from those guys, things that I've seen was just being consistent every night, just trying to just play hard every night. I definitely want to make sure that I just bring that here and also lead by example as well.”
