AJ Green discusses the benefits of having a deep roster and how internal competition pushes the team to improve.

“It's a great thing to have that depth and to have multiple guys on a night that can go out there and impact and help win a game. Because of that as well, it requires even more of the little things that we talk about, like the willingness to communicate, to come together, to get on the same page, because there is so many different lineups and things that could be out there. It's exciting and makes for great competition in practice, going at each other and pushing each other to get better, and that's only going to benefit all of us in the long run regardless of what happens.”

AJ GreenBucks player

AJ Green explains the importance of maintaining composure and execution during games while keeping the high intensity established in practice.

“It's so good to practice that way because it really stresses you, like you're processing, you're thinking physically, your communication, and that's a great thing that we need to work on. The more that we can take it from the practice floor and have that same organized chaos, but then in games, be very organized and intentional and execute well and not maybe get sped up or lack spacing or lack screening and the little things. We got to keep that pace, that fast go-go-go, but then also be slow within it to execute.”

AJ GreenBucks player

AJ Green describes the team's developmental philosophy of focusing on incremental improvement and reducing mistakes over time.

“I like to see it as how can we be less wrong? The goal is to be right, but it's super hard to be exactly right all the time. So the translation, it's like we're going to make mistakes, but how can we just over time be less wrong and do more of the right stuff and just stack that and stack that and learn throughout it. But it's tough, but like I said, the process, he does such a good job of practice film, this is what we're focused on, and let's just lock in on that today and get better at it.”
