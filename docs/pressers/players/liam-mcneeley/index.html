Charles Lee evaluates the impact of rookie Tidjane Salaün.

“You can feel his impact. You can feel him in the game, whether he's making shots or missing shots. I thought that he took the right shots, I thought that he helped our offense actually flow a lot better. His after-actions, he had a couple hit-unders and then played in the seam and had a great kick-out to somebody and they missed a three. He also had a slip that put two people on the ball and then created a lob opportunity for Liam to Ryan. I think that he's really starting to realize how he can impact the offensive game without even having to score all the time. And then defensively, I think his continued physicality and strength and he's moving really good laterally, he's affecting the defensive side of the floor.”

On Tidjane Salaün, Liam McNeeley, Ryan Kalkbrenner

Charles Lee on the development of Liam McNeeley and Tidjane Salaun.

“I've loved their off seasons. There's been a serious commitment from those guys in the weight room, on the court, their skill sets have grown, understanding what their roles are. There's been great acceptance of that and so I'm excited to watch them versus another team. Going up against ourselves has been really good, but everyone knows the plays and they're grabbing and holding and fighting for positioning and stuff like that and I know we're going to get a very physical tough minded Brooklyn team as well, but I look forward to seeing those guys with some different lineups, putting them in some situations to be creators, to be screeners, to have to guard certain people.”
