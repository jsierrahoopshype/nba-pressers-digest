Darko Rajaković discusses the leadership qualities and coachability of Kyle Filipowski during training camp.

“During open gym every time that we played live, he is not shying away from from, you know, talking to the guys. This is not his first rodeo, this is not his first team. He is trying to integrate it in a system and to understand his teammates and to see what are the biggest gaps that he can help in. But his voice is being heard right away with our team and our guys have a lot of respect for him.”

Darko RajakovićRaptors coach

Darko Rajaković shares an anecdote about his past interaction with Kyle Filipowski during a game while he was an assistant coach.

“I always like loved Kyle. I always like engaging with him. I was not as assistant coach, I was not responsible for daily work with him, so he was not my guy, call it that way. But we always had good conversations. I do remember when during COVID, fellow co-coach Taylor Jenkins was out and I was a substitute teacher for for a couple of games. And the first game that we played, we played against Golden State. It was a great game and end of the game, I drew up a play for us to inbound the ball because we needed to save it. And it was not a great play, but Kyle was smart enough to to flash into right position, catch the ball, we got a dunk on that play and everybody were saying, oh, look at coach, he has got a great play.”

On Kyle Filipowski, Taylor Jenkins
