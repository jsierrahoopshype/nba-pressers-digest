Duncan RobinsonPistons player

Duncan Robinson discusses the offensive benefits of having multiple three-point shooters on the Detroit Pistons roster during training camp.

“We got a lot of guys that can shoot, which is really exciting. I think anytime that you have multiple threats from behind the arc, it's just harder to defend the three-point line. You can't necessarily just help off of one guy, or not help off of one guy. So it's going to be a great addition and excited to do some different things offensively as a group. X's and O's, that's up to the coaches to come up with, but for us to execute, so it should be a fun challenge.”
