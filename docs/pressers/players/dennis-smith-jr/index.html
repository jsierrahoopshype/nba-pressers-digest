Charles LeeHornets coach

Charles Lee explains the defensive and leadership value Dennis Smith Jr. brings to the team.

“I expect number one, you see how defensively he's picking up guys full court. I think he's really impacting the other team's ability to get into their offense. Sometimes they're having to really get into their clock or they're running offense from a half court. And then I think offensively he does a great job of getting us set, putting us in the right positions, getting to the piece of the paint at times. You know, we can become a little bit of a jump-shooting team and I think he helps us break the paint and create some better looks. So I also will look to him off the court. I think that he has great relationships with our young players. I think that he's been in playoff situations, he's been in hostile environments, he's very comfortable and secure in who he is and he understands what's needed of you on every type of situation.”

On Dennis Smith Jr.

Charles LeeHornets coach

Charles Lee praises the work ethic and leadership of veteran players including Dennis Smith Jr. and Naz Reid during practice.

“It is huge. It is why he has lasted so long in the league because he has got an edge. He has got a hunger to him. He is a really smart basketball player too, and I think that is what these guys pick up on. The fact that every day he comes in here, he works his tail off. He is listening to every drill, he is asking questions during drills, he is pulling guys to the side and giving them valuable information. When the ball is tipped up, he is in the right spots and he is diving on the floor. Between him and I think Dennis and Naz and these guys, the fact that they come into a practice day like today and they do not care about being tired, they care about the everyday process, and that is what we love about them.”

On Dennis Smith Jr., Naz Reid
