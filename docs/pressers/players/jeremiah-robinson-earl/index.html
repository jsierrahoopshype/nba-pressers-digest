Jamahl MosleyPelicans coach

Jamahl Mosley elaborates on the balance between scoring and playmaking for Jeremiah Robinson-Earl.

“That's the conversation, that's the film we watch. He did a great job studying at the summer, knowing exactly what he needs to do, watching great players, and there is a balance. There is a balance for when you command the floor and have everybody in their places, and then there's when you get downhill and you can attack and find and make the right read. So he's done a heck of a job of finding that balance. There's a lull for a little bit, he comes in, gets it off, gets a rebound, pushes the break, gets downhill, makes a layup. Next time down, calls a play, gets everybody set, and then there's a lull for a moment, then he comes down the court, takes a midrange pull-up that gets the momentum going back. So he's starting to understand that balance is what we're going to continue to need from him.”

On Jeremiah Robinson-Earl
