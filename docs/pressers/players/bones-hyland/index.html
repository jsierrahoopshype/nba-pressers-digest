Bones HylandClippers player

Bones Hyland explains his mindset regarding team depth and his approach to defining his role.

“I think it's just knowing kind of a next-man-up mentality with whoever it is. Unfortunately, that's kind of what basketball can be like for different teams, and that's why you see the teams that kind of make it very far, kind of stay healthier, I guess. And then at the end of the day, playing hard solves a lot of that. My mindset is kind of the same coming into any team I'm on, and that's just figuring out my role right away and trying to be the best I can be personally and then ultimately helping the team in that way.”
