“There is a lot. The past few days, especially with Jaylen Brown and Tyrese Maxey, the way they think the game is similar to the way I think the game, especially on the defensive end. When I get out on the same team as those guys, it is always something I kind of like to pick their brain. They have a thought, have a half-thought, very similar to the way I thought the game. When I see early, the past couple weeks, with Tyrese Maxey and those guys, I love the way he plays. I really do. Any time I get out there on his team, I love it because he is flying around in transition, he is making the right cuts, he is sacrificing shots.”
