Caris LeVert shares his perspective on modern NBA player development trends and the importance of player buy-in.

“I think that's where the NBA is going nowadays. You kind of see a lot of teams doing it. I think it's very advantageous. It's obviously like any other new way of doing things, players have to buy in, and I think as a player if you buy into it, you reap the benefits of it.
