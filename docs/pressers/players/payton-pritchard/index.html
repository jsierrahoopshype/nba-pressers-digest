Sam MerrillCavaliers player

Sam Merrill reacts to the addition of Payton Pritchard to the roster and the departure of Max Strus.

Sam MerrillCavaliers player

Sam Merrill discusses how the team's increased length on the roster can improve their defensive scheme.

“Last year we really tried to force a lot of turnovers and deflections. I think we can still do that, but maybe by being a little more compact, maybe you feel like you don't have to get out and pressure as much. If you got Payton at the three and then Evan and J.A. or Payton and Donovan or Payton and Isaac, that's a lot of length and something we haven't totally had. So I think something that can really help us defensively.”

On Payton Pritchard, Evan Mobley, Jarrett Allen, Donovan Mitchell, Isaac Okoro
