Jaylon TysonCavaliers player

Jaylon Tyson on Kenny Atkinson's expectations for his development.

“He told me straight up, he told me I had a great year. Obviously playoffs didn't go how I wanted. And me and him kind of, he gave it to me to figure out what he wanted me to work on. He said, 'Man, I love everything about what you brought to the table.' I told him, this is like a couple days after the playoff series, I took a couple days to really evaluate my season, evaluate how I thought it went. And obviously, I thought the regular season went great, but in the playoffs, I think the mental aspect took a toll on me. Being able to handle the ups and downs and not playing as much as I was used to in the regular season, I think that's something that really took a toll on me and affected my performance and ultimately affected the team.”

On Kenny Atkinson

Jaylon Tyson on his physical development and diet changes.

“I feel stronger, but I also feel like I'm moving better. So it's kind of like I think it had to do with my diet. I changed up my diet, got a chef. So I think that has something to do with it. It helps a lot. It helps with being able to battle down there with bigs, helps when you're going for offensive rebounds. Obviously, I love to crash and rebound and it helps on the defensive side a lot because those little bumps, they mean something now. You bump people off their line. So strength part means a lot. I think a lot of young guys don't understand how important the weight room is.”
