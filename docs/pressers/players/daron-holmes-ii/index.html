Daron Holmes II discusses the conditioning and health of teammates Aaron Gordon and Christian Braun.

“I'll start with AG. He took the whole summer to really work on his body. He rehabbed mentally, physically, because that was his biggest thing last year is being injured. I've been able to see his workouts, so he's always locked in, he's always doing the right things, he eats the right way, he's going to Pilates. He's doing the stuff he needs to do to get ready.”

On Aaron Gordon, Christian Braun

Daron Holmes II describes the responsibilities of playing the center position during a media availability.

“For the five, on offense you're the vocal point, so you have to be able to get into action a lot, come up and set a lot of screens. On defense you have to be the anchor. It's very important. It's very challenging, but it's fun and that's why we're here.”

DaRon Holmes IINuggets player

Daron Holmes II explains the importance of setting screens to help teammates like Jamal Murray score.

“We got guards like Jamal, we have guys that can score the ball and they're very good at what they do. Our job is to get them open, so setting a great hard screen for them and getting out quick puts a lot of pressure on that five man and the defense to be able to guard them as well.”

On Jamal Murray
