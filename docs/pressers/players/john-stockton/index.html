Micah NoriTrail Blazers coach

Micah Nori analyzes the shooting mechanics of Scoot Henderson and his work with John Stockton.

“I think he can get better shooting. A lot of his stuff is that he doesn't miss left or right. You have to worry about guys that are missing left and right. When you miss long and short, you can fix it because that means everything is pretty much online. And luckily for Scoot, if you watch him, he usually just misses short. So that is why I think that he does have a chance. And then obviously see him out here with JT, John Stockton, who is as good as anybody as far as shooting mechanics go, and cleaning that up and consistently working on so he can get better.”

On Scoot Henderson, John Stockton
