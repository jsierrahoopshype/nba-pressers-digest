Taylor JenkinsBucks coach

Taylor Jenkins analyzes the team's rebounding and transition defense performance following a game against Minnesota.

“That is the strength of those guys for sure, in Jae Crowder and J-Rock. I thought our first unit, when we did do it, we maybe just overran our routes a little bit. We were getting to the low side a little bit too much. We were not getting to the high side. We had our hands on some balls, just did not bounce our way. Credit Minnesota for snatching those up. We just got a little pre-exposed to giving up transition too much when we overran our route or did not have pressure when they did get the rebound. This is something we are trying to ingrain, it is a new habit for a lot of these guys, making sure we are picking the right spots when to crash and when not to crash.”

On Jae Crowder
