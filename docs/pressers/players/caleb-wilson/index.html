General manager Bryson Graham discusses the team's developmental approach for rookie Caleb Wilson during media day.

There are going to be there is going to be some progress and then there is going to be some regression. But are we going to be like, Caleb, get out the game, you turn the ball over. It is like, no, that is where we are at. That is the building phase and and it is exciting because Caleb has all the potential in the world and we just kind of like have to water that seed correctly and in time it will sprout into something really, really beautiful.”
