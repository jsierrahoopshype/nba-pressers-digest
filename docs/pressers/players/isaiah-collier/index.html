Will Hardy evaluates the offensive performance and playmaking roles of Keyonte George and Isaiah Collier.

“I thought they both used their burst to get in the paint. They do it differently. Keyonte George in transition, one-on-one, has the ability to put a lot of pressure on the defense. Not that Isaiah Collier doesn't, but Isaiah uses some off-ball action to get some of those back cuts, turned the corner a few times really nicely off handoffs and pick-and-roll. Those guys both know they have a ton of responsibility to create offense for our group. Sometimes that means that they are just the beginning phase of building the advantage.”

On Keyonte George, Isaiah Collier
