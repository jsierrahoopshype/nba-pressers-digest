Jordan Ott on the team's approach to the starting center position.

“We will mess around a little bit in the preseason. I do not want to, it is not like a day-of game, I do not think. I think you want to at least have a little bit rolling in. It is not a secret. There is no secrets, but we do have to see how it plays out. So a little bit of both. I think you want to let the preseason play out, but the couple days going into it, we will know. We will get a good feel. Everyone can see when a starting center goes out, there is an opportunity and a gap there, just like it would be in the season. You are trying to fight for a rotation spot. Something happens, you want to keep the rotation spot. We have two soldiers that they are either going to come in, compete, do what is best for the team. Who starts, who finishes, it is going to change.”
