Jalen WilliamsThunder player

Jalen Williams explains how having secondary ball-handlers helps reduce the physical burden on Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.

“For him, he's always guarded by the best player and so when he has to take the ball up, he's getting tired. He's playing a lot of minutes. So having someone to relieve that pressure off of him, bringing the ball up, it allows him to use that quickness and use the stamina he has to make the move stronger, whatever it is, and get to our spots and get to our play quicker for him in the half-court.”

On Shai Gilgeous-Alexander
