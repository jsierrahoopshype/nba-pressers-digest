Cade CunninghamPistons player

Cade Cunningham explains how improved floor spacing will benefit his game and the games of teammates Ausar Thompson and Jaden Ivey.

“More space allows you to go one-on-one, a lot easier, not as much help. I mean, that's the name of the game. You want to space the floor out, create advantages, and play the numbers game—three-on-two, two-on-ones, all those things. So, having space allows you to do that and allows you to make them pay with more threes. It's only going to help my game, it'll only help AT, it'll only help JD.”

On Ausar Thompson, Jaden Ivey
