Khris MiddletonWizards player

Khris Middleton emphasizes that defensive performance is the primary focus for the Washington Wizards during training camp.

“Defense is always the number one thing that gets the coaches going during training camp, get the conditioning, get the legs going. But also, any great team that I have been on, any good team that I have been on, we have been great on the defensive side of the ball. So that is where it starts, so that is where we have to put all our focus and energy on in order to let that translate, it will be a downhill effect onto the offense.”

Khris MiddletonWizards player

Khris Middleton describes his approach to mentoring younger teammates on the Washington Wizards roster.

“I think it is just being myself. I think the guys know behind closed doors a lot with the workouts and practices I talk a lot of junk. Just just competitive. I want to win whether it is a shooting game, basketball game, one on one game, anything. So they know I am competitive, they know I want to beat them, but they know I am on their same side too. So there is times where I might say, hey, try this instead of doing what you did, but it is all about motivating. It is all about helping. It is not demoralizing or making seem like I have done more than them since I have been here longer. It is more of just helping. We are all teammates. I still have a lot to learn and hopefully I can help them learn a couple things also.”

Khris MiddletonWizards player

Khris Middleton explains the factors that influenced his decision to return to the Washington Wizards.

“It was a lot of the stuff I saw last year. Just the daily habits, the way they the organization was ran from top to bottom. Then once I got in the locker room being around those guys, even though they are young and some of them may be inexperienced, but knowing that they want to reach the top at some point, so hopefully I can help them get there. And then when you look around and you see the roster, you see the additions that they added.”
