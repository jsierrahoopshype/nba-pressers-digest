Meleek Thomas on the increased pace and punctuality of NBA training camp.

“The biggest adjustment for me from playing in college to now being in training camp has been being more punctual. In college, you have a little bit more time to think. In the league, especially in training camp right now when everything is going so fast, everything has to be very punctual. You have to get to the next action quick. You have to make a decision quick. I am seeing it before we get to the games, so I say punctuality and just being precise with what I do.”

Meleek Thomas on the potential benefits of an NBA-backed league in Europe.

“I feel like that would give players coming out of college different experiences. From overseas players to college players, players are just looking for different experiences. I think that would be a great thing because if it is taken as seriously as the NBA is, just a Europe league, then I feel it benefits them, benefits their family, benefits their future, allowed them to compete in a different league without being in the direct NBA. But if it has NBA attached to it and it is very competitive and it is taken as serious, I feel it can be a beneficial for some guys.”

Meleek Thomas on his development with Cavs coaching staff.

“My time in college and who I was working with was Kenny Payne and Chin Coleman, Tyler Ulis at times. I had a lot of basketball knowledge around me. Meleek Thomas on Jared Allen's veteran presence.

“He is not flashy. He is not loud. Very quiet, but when it comes to doing his job, playing his role, he does it to the T. He does it like a vet. He does it like he has been doing it for many years. He knows where to be at as cat and mouse game when it comes to ball screens. It is very grateful for his position to be able to play that in-between game and make the guards have tougher decisions. He cleans up the rim. His putbacks are elite. Where he is at around the basket is elite. His hands are elite when it comes to catching the basketball and dunking the basketball.”

On Jared Allen

Meleek Thomas on learning to impact the game defensively.

“In this training camp, what I am learning about how to impact the game in many different ways is crashing more, crashing offensive glass, defensive glass more. I can impact defense by getting more deflections, being where I am supposed to be at. Because if I am where I am supposed to be at, then they know they can rely on me on defense. If I am where I am supposed to be at on offense, they know I am a reliable screen setter. I am in more actions. I can come off of flares. Just me being in the right spots and being a willing learner, learning to expand my knowing and my knowledge of where to be and when to be there gets me more looks offense and defense.”
