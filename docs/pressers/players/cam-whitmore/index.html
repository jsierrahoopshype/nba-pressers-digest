Coach Adelman discusses his uncertainty regarding the team's rotation plans for the upcoming season.

“We haven't decided that yet. We have the week to go and I think the first two games will keep the group together, as a, you know, because I think we're still looking at it. Are we playing a nine-man rotation, 10-man rotation, and then like I said, kind of a fake 11-man rotation where if the first half doesn't work out, is there somebody that you say, why don't we change it up and play them in the third quarter? A small amount of minutes, but just rotate them in. I think that's where we're at. All those guys, Trevon Brazile's obviously been more of a highlight thing, he's played really well. He also has played the most minutes with the best players, but I think Bryce has shown signs, Cam Whitmore shown signs, so you can't be scared to throw those guys out there as we're looking for a rotation.”

On Trevon Brazile, Bryce Hopkins, Cam Whitmore
