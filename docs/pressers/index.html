James HardenCavaliers player

James Harden discusses the technical aspects of screen setting and his chemistry with Evan Mobley during practice.

“There's so much that goes into being an elite screener. Knowing who has the basketball, knowing who is guarding the ball, and basically trying to create an advantage. There's going to be times where Evan comes up and hits the bottom of the screen and so he wants to get out a little faster so he can be able to get to the rim. And there's going to be times where he actually has to instead of physical screening, hold the screen. So just communicating with him on those type of situations so he can see and understand them when they appear. I think that helps our offense a lot just because I'm able to get downhill, he's able to get the ball in the pocket, or we got lob or we got kickout opportunities.”

On Evan Mobley
