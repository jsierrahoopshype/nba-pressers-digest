**1. Jordan Goodwin — "that mindset of feeling like it's going in every time it leaves your hands" — Jordan Goodwin on his shooting confidence and development** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8jgOI6izgwU&t=71s)

Jordan Goodwin: "That mindset of feeling like it's going in every time it leaves your hands started feeling a little bit last year. During the summer, that's all we did was just shoot the ball again. I know that's a key role that I need to do with this team, especially losing the shooters that we lost. I just want to take that next step, but all summer it's just been getting threes up everywhere on the court, off the dribble, catch and shoot, no matter what."
