# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 11.10.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp-UFbnebsg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp-UFbnebsg)

_Speakers identified: Taylor Jenkins_

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "we've had this struggle the last couple of games. It's really hurt us, 11 second-chance points." — Taylor Jenkins addresses the team's struggles with rebounding and foul disparity** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp-UFbnebsg&t=16s)

Taylor Jenkins: "There's a lot of factors involved in that. Give them a lot of credit. They've got a lot of elite offensive rebounders, bigs, guards, wings. We just got to do better. We've had this struggle the last couple of games. It's really hurt us, 11 second-chance points, but it just beats you down and you can't play your style of basketball on the offensive side. It leads to fouls and foul disparity too, which are concerning. So, an area we got to get a lot better in."

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "it's our opportunity. Not going to call it a challenge, it's an opportunity." — Taylor Jenkins on the challenge of developing chemistry with a diverse roster** [01:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp-UFbnebsg&t=92s)

Taylor Jenkins: "It's part of our journey, it's our opportunity. Not going to call it a challenge, it's an opportunity. I think we got a lot of guys that have got to continue trying different things. What we're doing right now is not working. I got to find something that's going to build consistency for us, starters, off the bench, sub patterns, reading the game. We got to continue to establish what we identify as our pillars offensively and defensively and get the group that's going to do that more often than not and not put us in holes and be able to sustain runs by the other team. **4. Taylor Jenkins — "just continue to give him the guidance on what to do defensively, points of emphasis on the offensive side." — Taylor Jenkins explains his guidance for Bogie during the game** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp-UFbnebsg&t=144s)
