**5. Micah Nori — "Tumani just really competed with him." — Micah Nori on defensive effort and rebounding against OKC** [03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ifxr-ockxF4&t=228s)

Micah Nori: "It was very good. One thing that Oklahoma City does is they take a lot of mid-range shots. It's funny because you don't want to give up rim and you don't want to give up threes, but then Shai comes in and he probably shoots the mid-range 52, 54 percent.
