**1. Tyronn Lue — "we got to bring that competitive fight every single night. And tonight just thought we didn't have that" — Tyronn Lue on the Clippers' lack of defensive toughness** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vWv4Y-M964I&t=5s)

Tyronn Lue: "Defensive toughness, defensive physicality. They got whatever they wanted tonight. We didn't do a good job of just taking care of the defensive end. We know at times it's going to be hard for us to score the basketball, but we got to bring that competitive fight every single night. And tonight just thought we didn't have that. We looked tired and exhausted, maybe because of their pace too, and we're not used to that. I don't know what it was, but we just didn't have that defensive mindset to start the game."
