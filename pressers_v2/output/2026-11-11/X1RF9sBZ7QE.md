**1. Damian Lillard — "He's the most unique player on our team to me, just that combination of size and aggressiveness." — Damian Lillard on City's unique impact and aggressive playstyle** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X1RF9sBZ7QE&t=8s)

Damian Lillard: "I thought **City** was **City**. He's the most unique player on our team to me, just that combination of size and aggressiveness, and almost recklessness a little bit. But he impacts the game in so many ways. He's chasing guys around defensively, he's offensive rebounding and defensive rebounding, diving for loose balls. Sometimes that emotion that you got to put into the game that might get you a tech or might get you a flagrant foul, every team needs that. And he brings that, even in practice, that type of emotion and that type of care."

**2. Damian Lillard — "I was always kind of talking him down about keeping his composure and understanding." — Damian Lillard on mentoring teammates from the bench** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X1RF9sBZ7QE&t=94s)

**3. Damian Lillard — "I felt super fresh with those short stints and I think this change was good." — Damian Lillard on the benefits of shorter rotation stints** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X1RF9sBZ7QE&t=194s)

Damian Lillard: "I think before the stretches were pretty quick and sometimes it can be difficult to get in a real rhythm when you are in and out the game so fast. But I felt super fresh with those short stints and I think this change was good because I'm able to rest for a decent period throughout the game, but I'm playing longer stretches so it allows me to kind of set up the game the way that I typically do, you know, with patience and getting off the ball and moving and picking my spots a little bit better."

**4. Damian Lillard — "I think with Denny, our games just complement each other so well." — Damian Lillard on developing chemistry with Denny** [06:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X1RF9sBZ7QE&t=364s)

Damian Lillard: "I think with **Denny**, our games just complement each other so well. He wants the space to get into the paint and my ability to shoot and the attention that I'm going to get from guys allows him to have that space. And me being a shooter, I know that guys are going to have to sometimes collapse and when they do that, he's a good playmaker, he's a good passer, so I'll get opportunities from that. A lot of times when I'm maybe attacking on one side of the floor and I notice that there's not a lot of space or they're in the gaps, I'm always aware that if they're in the gaps and I advance this ball to **Denny**, they're going to be playing catch-up."
