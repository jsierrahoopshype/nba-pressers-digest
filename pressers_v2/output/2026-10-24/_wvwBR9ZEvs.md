# “The Ball Movement Has Been Contagious.” | Chris Finch Postgame Sound | 10.23.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_wvwBR9ZEvs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_wvwBR9ZEvs)

_Speakers identified: Chris Finch_

**1. Chris Finch — "it starts with Jaden, Anthony, and Nickeil, those guys just getting back in the play and rebounding" — Jaden McDaniels, Anthony Edwards, and Nickeil Alexander-Walker's impact on rebounding** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_wvwBR9ZEvs&t=11s)

Chris Finch: "It starts with Jaden, Anthony, and Nickeil, those guys just getting back in the play and rebounding the ball in the air. We have been harping on it. Obviously, it is easier to rebound when you play really good first-shot defense and you can force them into shots where you are not in rotations and stuff like that. That has been key to it as well, being able to be in good help spots and then go pursue the ball. It is the pursuit of the ball on both ends of the floor. We were one of the worst teams in terms of watching rebounds last year and not actually pursuing the ball, and this year we have been able to switch our mentality to be more of a pursuit team."
