**4. Quin Snyder — "to be an official in the NBA may be the hardest job" — Quin Snyder on the difficulty of officiating in the NBA** [02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FHVBZeEDLzs&t=142s)

Quin Snyder: "To be an official in the NBA may be the hardest job. Some people say it's coaching, but those guys, understanding the level of detail that goes into it, and the way the game is right now, there's a few calls that are hard, whether it's the new points of emphasis. So you try to speak to them in a way that you can actually describe what you're seeing on the court, and sometimes you're more demonstrative than others, and that's part of the job too."
