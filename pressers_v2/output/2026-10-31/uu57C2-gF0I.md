**1. Lloyd Pierce — "that's the separator for that team. They're a team that's going to put a lot of pressure" — Lloyd Pierce on the Pacers' defensive struggles against the Heat** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uu57C2-gF0I&t=5s)

Lloyd Pierce: "Obviously when you're shooting well, you understand that trying to sustain that level of shooting is going to be tough, so you got to stick to the basics. Can we defend without fouling? Can we come up with some rebounds? They had seven offensive rebounds at the half and 15 free throws, and that's the separator for that team. They're a team that's going to put a lot of pressure on you in the paint, 64 points in the paint. They're a team that's going to put a lot of pressure on your defense if you don't have a tight paint defense. When they can do that, the game slows down for us because we're having to chase and chase and then we've got to take it out."

**2. Lloyd Pierce — "They make it difficult with Adebayo and Giannis, and then that sets up the Klay Thompson, Mitchell" — Lloyd Pierce on defending Bam Adebayo and Giannis Antetokounmpo** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uu57C2-gF0I&t=50s)

Lloyd Pierce: "They make it difficult with **Adebayo** and **Giannis**, and then that sets up the **Klay Thompson**, **Mitchell**, doesn't it, with open threes because you've got to pay so much attention to the big guys. That's going to be the strength of their team and that's going to be the challenge we have playing them. If you try and put any of your defenders on an island, one-on-one, obviously they shot it well, they scored it well, and you're going to feel that. You're trying to disrupt it a little bit.
