# Taylor Jenkins Media Availability | 10.30.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "the integrity of our defense fell with our offense. I'm always going to be the first one" — Taylor Jenkins on team's defensive intensity and offensive rhythm** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UhDM_LYyiyM&t=27s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I think a combination of a couple things. I feel like we didn't have the right morning setting, so that starts with me and the preparedness. When the game got going, the integrity of our defense fell with our offense. I'm always going to be the first one to stand out front and go, how can I help these guys continue to get better offensively, find our rhythm? Certain guys are struggling right now making shots, or we're not touching the paint as frequent as we need to night to night, and that can linger and impact our defensive intensity. So, start with some focus on the defense in the morning that I needed to tighten up a little bit better, knowing that's got to be a calling card for us every single day."

**5. Taylor Jenkins — "just our discipline with them, how we're navigating their isos and their pick and rolls" — Taylor Jenkins on team discipline and defensive pressure against Cleveland** [05:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UhDM_LYyiyM&t=344s)

Taylor Jenkins: "Always game-to-game, game plan discipline. Every individual team is going to have their star players that are going to be featured on the offensive side. They've got a lot of collection of great players up and down that lineup. Obviously in their backcourt, just our discipline with them, how we're navigating their isos and their pick and rolls and their ability to create contact and get to the free throw line. We've got to get better in playing aggressive without fouling, but then also we've got to be able to still be relentless with our pressure. So where is our point of attack defense, whether that's one through five? It's a big that's handling as a passer, it's one of our four men guarding in isolation, it's constant pick and rolls and we're switching or not switching, how we're just constantly putting their initial creators under duress as much as possible."
