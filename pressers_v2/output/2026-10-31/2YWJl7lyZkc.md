**1. Coach Adelman — "I thought the second half our pick-and-roll defense was much better, very aggressive, high hands." — Coach Adelman on Nuggets' second-half defensive adjustments** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2YWJl7lyZkc&t=3s)

Coach Adelman: "The shot making is going to happen in the NBA. It was just the little things, miscommunications and switches. I thought the second half our pick-and-roll defense was much better, very aggressive, high hands, at least to deter their ball movement. I thought in the first half they moved the thing side to side, they were really impressive, and they have very impressive offensive players. They really do. So when they get you on the run, it's driving and kicking and all those things, it leads to good looks. So they're a very good offensive team, but I did think we were better in the second half."
