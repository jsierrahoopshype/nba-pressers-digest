**1. Collin Gillespie — "this loss feels different than Portland obviously and OKC because it's more of the way that we" — Collin Gillespie on the team's evolving defensive and offensive chemistry** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r-ssy-urdls&t=131s)

Collin Gillespie: "This loss feels different than Portland obviously and OKC because it's more of the way that we were doing some of the stuff that we pride ourselves on, the stuff that we talk about. It's definitely like there's moving parts. Obviously last year guys were in and out of the lineup so offensively we're still figuring out all being on the floor together. Defensively we added some new guys, Mamadi Diakite didn't really play a ton last year so he's doing a great job. We're going to continue to grow, we're going to continue to learn from the stuff and I said in the locker room the world's not ending."

**2. Collin Gillespie — "everybody has unbelievable confidence in Jalen Green and we support him just tell him to go out there" — Collin Gillespie on the team's unwavering confidence in Jalen Green** [06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r-ssy-urdls&t=406s)

Collin Gillespie: "There's 82 games in a season. Sometimes you're going to have quiet nights, sometimes you're going to have off nights. Everybody has unbelievable confidence in **Jalen Green** and we support him just tell him to go out there, continue to play, continue to take his shots, continue to play hard. Obviously when Dillon Brooks went out I was like, yo, this is going to flip for you at some point really soon, continue to put pressure on the defense.
