**1. Charles Lee — "17 turnovers tonight, 36 points off of our turnovers and a lot of them seem like they were" — Charles Lee on Charlotte's offensive struggles and turnovers against Atlanta** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2eSbTSOdeAU&t=12s)

Charles Lee: "I thought the break offensively was probably the biggest one. Let's not shy away from it. 17 turnovers tonight, 36 points off of our turnovers and a lot of them seem like they were pick-sixes going the other way. I thought when we played with purpose offensively, we got to our angle spacing. We trusted the seam pass, seam pass kick-outs for easy threes, playing versus closeouts, we were really dangerous. At times, credit Atlanta a little bit, their pressure and physicality took us out of an offensive rhythm."
