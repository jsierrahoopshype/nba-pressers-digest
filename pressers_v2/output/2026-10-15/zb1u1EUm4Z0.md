**1. Jordan Ott — "He's had a great preseason. His impact defensively, just his activity, length, athleticism, lateral quickness defensively can really impact" — Ryan Dunn's impressive defensive impact during the preseason** [00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zb1u1EUm4Z0&t=7s)

Jordan Ott: "He's had a great preseason. It starts with the summer, but you don't know how it's going to translate and he's had a great preseason. I feel like all four games, his impact defensively, just his activity, length, athleticism, lateral quickness defensively can really impact the opposing team's ball handlers. Now he's figuring out, you know, cut, when to cut. He's improved his shooting. He's been able to make an impact on both ends. So he's had a great camp so far and I'm sure it'll continue because of the way he works. He's an incredible worker and it's great to see it pay off."

**2. Jordan Ott — "Especially at a game like this where they're ultra aggressive, pressure the basketball 94 feet, he had to" — Bol Bol's role and defensive responsibilities in an aggressive system** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zb1u1EUm4Z0&t=97s)

Jordan Ott: "Especially at a game like this where they're ultra aggressive, pressure the basketball 94 feet, knowing that they have an elite rim protector in the back behind him, he had to handle a lot of those duties tonight. I thought overall our first 21 minutes of that first half, we were right there. We went toe-to-toe with them. Our defensive intent from the jump was high level, but their ball pressure bothered us. Their ball pressure bothered us there in the end of the second, and for him to only end up with two turnovers, eight assists, two turnovers, we know what he brings: speed, downhill ability. He's got enough feistiness in him that we need to survive here in the NBA and again, he's been a guy that's been in the gym all summer, so it's good to see it pay off."
