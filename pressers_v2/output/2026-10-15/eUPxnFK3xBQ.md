**2. Taylor Jenkins — "I want to give guys ample sample size to try to build up to a workload" — Taylor Jenkins on balancing player workloads during the preseason** [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUPxnFK3xBQ&t=57s)

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "we're going to face pressure regardless of who it's going to be" — Taylor Jenkins on developing players against defensive pressure** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUPxnFK3xBQ&t=169s)

Taylor Jenkins: "We're going to face pressure regardless of who it's going to be. All of our guys have to develop against that style of defense. **4. Taylor Jenkins — "they actually have a cognitive kind of synergy with their teammates" — Taylor Jenkins on building team chemistry and cognitive synergy** [04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUPxnFK3xBQ&t=293s)

Taylor Jenkins: "That's the depths of the layers when you talk about chemistry. We can just say chemistry, but what does that mean? Guys vibe with each other and they actually have a cognitive kind of synergy with their teammates. The guy with the ball knows where his teammates are and they know how they're going to be moving or that's maybe that's not the space to go capture based off of how the defense is being deployed. But then vice versa, if I'm off the ball, I know I'm coming into a screen, I know I'm cutting off the ball. That's the stuff that we're trying to wire right now. We are making progress."
