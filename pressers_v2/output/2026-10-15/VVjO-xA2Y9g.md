# “[Jaden] Is Unlocking The Offense This Season.” | Anthony Edwards Postgame Sound | 10.14.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VVjO-xA2Y9g](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VVjO-xA2Y9g)

_Speakers identified: Anthony Edwards_

**1. Anthony Edwards — "Jaden is one of the best two-way players in the NBA if not the best." — Anthony Edwards on Jaden McDaniels' aggressive play and versatility** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VVjO-xA2Y9g&t=21s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "With **Jaden McDaniels** coming in tonight, what did you like about what he was doing for you guys in that starting group?"

**Anthony Edwards:** "Just being aggressive, exploiting the mismatch. They can't really guard him. They try to put the worst defenders on him. Just trying to give it to him and let him play in space. Jaden is one of the best two-way players in the NBA if not the best. Just him getting more touches and ball screens actions, clear sides, if they put a small guy on him, give it to him in the post. Just him getting more touches, being more involved, I think he going to average 20-25 points easy because he just know how to play the game."

**2. Anthony Edwards — "I feel like maybe Jaden got to bring it up more when they are pressuring me and Rudy." — Anthony Edwards on Jaden McDaniels' role in initiating offense** [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VVjO-xA2Y9g&t=103s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "How do you make sure he gets more touches like when you are physical or the game slows down?"

**Anthony Edwards:** "We just can't forget he out there. It is just that simple. They pressuring me and Rudy so it is kind of hard to get into actions. I feel like maybe Jaden got to bring it up more when they are pressuring me and Rudy like that. Just because they try to hide the defenders on Jaden and Jaden, I think we got to let them bring the ball up and initiate our actions."

**3. Anthony Edwards — "I got a point guard now so just playing off of maybe playing off the catch." — Anthony Edwards on evolving his role with a new point guard** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VVjO-xA2Y9g&t=143s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Do you feel like that is still something that you can lean into with the same sort of volume you did last year?"

**Anthony Edwards:** "It may be a little bit tougher just because I got a point guard now so just playing off of maybe playing off the catch, getting to one-dribble pull-ups. Not hunting them as much. I feel like last year I was hunting them towards the fourth quarter because it was a lot easier, we had bigs on the floor, but I feel like now it is more of a four-out, one-in, so maybe more catching and shoots."
