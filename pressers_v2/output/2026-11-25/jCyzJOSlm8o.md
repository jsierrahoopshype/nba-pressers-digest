**4. Davion Mitchell — "Time is the biggest thing. It's just time." — Davion Mitchell on adjusting to moving screen calls** [07:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCyzJOSlm8o&t=435s)

Davion Mitchell: "Time is the biggest thing. It's just time. When the guards see us, obviously they have their own read in their head to evade the push and make the right read. But just being on the same page with the bigs, knowing that we are not in their mind, we don't know if they're going to race it or if they want to be patient this time. Just us being on the same page in terms of when we're coming up, just waiting on it, it ultimately lets us set really good screens."
