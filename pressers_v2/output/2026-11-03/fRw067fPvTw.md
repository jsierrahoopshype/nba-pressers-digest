**7. Tuomas Iisalo — "we obviously have our defensive principles that we've tried to design that are as flexible as possible" — Tuomas Iisalo on balancing perimeter defense and rim protection** [07:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRw067fPvTw&t=438s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "We obviously have our defensive principles that we've tried to design that are as flexible as possible so they can be used in as many situations. And then there is game-to-game adjustments. One of the things Pelicans do really well is they generate paint touches and they generate high-quality rim attempts. And then you have that the balance between how much you want to put resources on that and how much you want to take away the threes and maybe look to stay in front. Today, for example, if you look at **Herb Jones** as a career shooter, three for seven is maybe a little bit over his career average. Matkovic five for eight, I believe he's been in the last two years more or less like a 38% three-point shooter off the catch and shoot. So the looks were good and you have to make some decisions in the game plan."
