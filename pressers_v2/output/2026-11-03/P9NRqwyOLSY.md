**3. Ty Jerome — "It's a different feeling when you lose." — Ty Jerome on the difference between winning and losing** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P9NRqwyOLSY&t=120s)

Ty Jerome: "It's funny, right? Because you say you want to learn in wins and losses. It's a different feeling when you lose. Yes, when we won those games in Golden State, those close games that came down to the wire, it could have went either way. You look at the lessons still, but you don't feel it as much. We'll definitely feel this one more, definitely dig deeper more. It's a little bit of a wake-up call."
