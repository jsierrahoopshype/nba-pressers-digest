**5. Micah Nori — "We're not going to steal the ball from Jamal Murray or Nikola Jokic" — Coaching players to avoid gambling and reaching on defense** [03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIBQQWXCkp0&t=192s)

Micah Nori: "Basically stop gambling, stop reaching. We're not going to steal the ball from **Jamal Murray** or **Nikola Jokic** quite honestly. So it's almost just be there, be physical. If you do foul or be physical, be physical before they can get to that in the scoring area where it's one dribble and at the rim. But honestly, it's just continue to talk about moving your feet, catch the drives with your chest, and stop reaching and slapping down. Just don't bail them out."
