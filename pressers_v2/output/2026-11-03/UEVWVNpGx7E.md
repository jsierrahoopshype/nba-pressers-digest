# Jamahl Mosley | Pelicans vs Grizzlies Postgame Interview 11/2/26 — *New Orleans Pelicans*

**1. Jamahl Mosley — "The ability to find a way down the stretch to get the stops that we needed" — Jamahl Mosley on team resilience and execution in overtime** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UEVWVNpGx7E&t=27s)

Jamahl Mosley: "We talk about by committee and it definitely was by committee. These guys did an unbelievable job of finding a way to stay poised. Going into overtime, obviously we had the lead and gave it back to them, but the ability to find a way down the stretch to get the stops that we needed, to execute the things that we needed, that's what I'm most proud of these guys. The learning lessons that they're continuing to show and following the game plan. Our coaches did an unbelievable job of talking about the game plan and what we could give up, what we could take away. Obviously there's always going to be cleanup in these situations, but the resiliency and the toughness and the grit that these guys showed, that's what it's got to look like."

**6. Jamahl Mosley — "This is a by committee team and every night it's going to be somebody different." — Jamahl Mosley on the team's 'by committee' philosophy** [05:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UEVWVNpGx7E&t=324s)

Jamahl Mosley: "That's who **Herb** is. **Herb** is a big-time stopper and obviously he hit some big-time shots for us as well, had some great looks.
