# Erik Spoelstra Talks Miami Heat 7 Straight Wins, Bam Adebayo Triple-Double, Jović and Giannis Injury — *Miami Heat*

_Speakers identified: Erik Spoelstra_

**1. Erik Spoelstra — "It was almost like old school Bam where we played him at the hub." — Bam Adebayo's performance as a playmaker in the post** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCKON95a64k&t=5s)

Erik Spoelstra: "It was almost like old school Bam where we played him at the hub. A lot of it was in the post or in pick and rolls. But the way they are defending it, we were just letting him try to create an action, get to the paint, and then play out of whatever paint touch happened from there. He made some really good passes to set up the guys pretty consistently throughout the course of the game. I was glad to see him have the triple-double because he was playing the right way the entire game. Super efficient, and I think it just fed confidence into the other guys."

**2. Erik Spoelstra — "I liked the way he rebounded the basketball tonight. He really went after it." — Nikola Jović's rebounding and defensive versatility** [01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCKON95a64k&t=62s)

Erik Spoelstra: "I liked the way he rebounded the basketball tonight. He really went after it. It was one of his better rebounding games. It's not just that he rebounds, it's the optics of it. He was in traffic, going up there, and securing the rebounds. I like that offensive rebound there at the end. He's been working at it, he had a couple good practices to prepare, but he's been preparing before this. Our depth is really important. You see his versatility, how that always can help. They went to a really small lineup, and so Nikola's versatility defensively really helps you. His ability to switch, keep the ball in front of him, and get these weird lineups, you just need a lot of versatility defensively, and he was able to provide that."

**3. Erik Spoelstra — "It's not super high. He almost played tonight. Thought it was the right decision by everybody." — Status of Giannis Antetokounmpo's injury** [02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCKON95a64k&t=132s)

Erik Spoelstra: "It's not super high. He almost played tonight. Thought it was the right decision by everybody. We'll see how these two days go, but I'm optimistic."

**4. Erik Spoelstra — "He faces the defense a lot more now, so he's able to see what's happening." — Bam Adebayo's development in reading double-teams** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCKON95a64k&t=215s)

Erik Spoelstra: "He faces the defense a lot more now, so he's able to see what's happening and where the help is coming from. They're bringing it from the baseline, and then if he puts the ball on the floor, they would aggressively dig or trap from the top. I think the first step is facing it. If you're backing in, sometimes you can get surprised. I also think we've spent an inordinate amount of time this year on post spacing because of the dynamic between Giannis and Bam. If we're not right with our spacing when the two of them are on the court, we get jammed up. It's been a while since we've been jammed up, and I'm thinking about that Charlotte game, it was crazy. But that was part of the process we had to go through to work through this. We've spent a lot of time with it, we've watched a lot of film on it, they've worked on it, and then that helps you. Even in a game like this where Giannis is not playing, everybody's spacing was a lot more detailed and we were able to just generate offense from a post-up, and that's progress from where we were probably three weeks ago."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCKON95a64k&t=215s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCKON95a64k&t=215s)

**5. Erik Spoelstra — "With Giannis, it's the ground and pound mentality that both of those guys have." — Bam Adebayo's increased assertiveness in the paint** [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCKON95a64k&t=400s)

Erik Spoelstra: "With Giannis, it's the ground and pound mentality that both of those guys have. And I think some of how Giannis plays is rubbing off on Bam. I think he's being more assertive once he gets into the circle area, just being way more aggressive, even sometimes just trying to go up and dunk it, and that's when you get hammered instead of trying to finesse it. But all of it, and I think once you start seeing it happen more and more, you start going to it more often. We've been emphasizing, you know, with the two of them, it should feel like a frontcourt when they're leaving the game after the game. It should feel like there's been an element of ground and pound and physicality where they have to ice themselves. If we're not doing that, then we're not maximizing their brutality. And they can do it in different ways. It's not just putting your head down and just being brutal, it's playing with nuance to get to your brutality. And I think that those two are getting better with that."
