**3. Mark Daigneault — "When we are double big, there is something structural about you probably not going to get as many." — Mark Daigneault on team spacing and offensive strategy** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dVrGo4zdNI0&t=98s)

Mark Daigneault: "I thought we shot it well obviously and got some decent ones. I thought some of that was stylistic. I have mentioned when I have been asked about the threes offensively, when we are double big, there is something structural about you probably not going to get as many and when you are five smalls you are going to get more. The floor is just more spaced and that is just the nature of playing that way and there are trade-offs with both. I like the way we moved the ball, I like the shots we took. I thought we offensive rebounded pretty well given the size in the game. So like I said, we played a pretty clean game offensively and obviously they were just a little bit better tonight."

**4. Mark Daigneault — "You have to be a little bit of a punching bag. You have to be tough enough to mentally stay with it." — Mark Daigneault on developing mental toughness in young players** [02:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dVrGo4zdNI0&t=141s)

Mark Daigneault: "I will lump him in with **Mikael** and **Nick** here now and **Brooks**, really all those guys that are not in consistent Bennet, even at times that are not in consistent rotation minutes. You have to be a little bit of a punching bag. You have to be tough enough to mentally stay with it, understand it is going to come back around at some point and be able to play in character and hard and inside the team when your number gets called again. And the guys that have been here that have done that well from **K-Rich** to even **Wiggins**, guys like that, it makes them tougher and helps them improve and it allows us to go to them in a lot of different situations. So we are trying to build that muscle with some of the guys that are a little bit newer."

**5. Mark Daigneault — "We are not going to chase a game that the likelihood is low for the reasons that you mentioned." — Mark Daigneault on balancing minutes during a condensed schedule** [03:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dVrGo4zdNI0&t=201s)

Mark Daigneault: "Some of that is game context. It was 20 at some point in the fourth early. At that point, the chances of winning are low and so philosophically, especially in a condensed part of the schedule, we put the bench guys in there and we are still trying to win the game, but we try to give them the chance to cut into it and if they can get it in a strikable spot, then we will go back to those guys. I even went back to **Hart** and **Jared** there, I think it was 16 with nine to go. But at the same time, we are not going to chase a game that the likelihood is low for the reasons that you mentioned. So it is a balancing act, it is never perfect, but that is just philosophically how we choose to handle an 82-game season."
