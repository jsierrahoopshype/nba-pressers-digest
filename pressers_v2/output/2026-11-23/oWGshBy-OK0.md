**1. Erik Spoelstra — "some of that of how Giannis plays is rubbing off on Bam" — Erik Spoelstra on Giannis Antetokounmpo's influence on Bam Adebayo's play style** [00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWGshBy-OK0&t=7s)

Erik Spoelstra: "With Giannis, it's the ground and pound mentality that both of those guys have. And I think some of that of how Giannis plays is rubbing off on Bam. I think he's being more assertive once he gets into the circle area, just being much more aggressive, even sometimes just trying to go up and dunk it, and that's when you get hammered instead of trying to finesse it. Once you start seeing it happen more often, you start going to it more often."

**2. Erik Spoelstra — "it should feel like a front court when they're leaving the game after the game" — Erik Spoelstra on the necessary physicality of the Heat's frontcourt** [00:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWGshBy-OK0&t=52s)

Erik Spoelstra: "With the two of them, it should feel like a front court when they're leaving the game after the game. It should feel like there's been an element of ground and pound and physicality where they have to ice themselves. If we're not doing that, then we're not maximizing their brutality. And they can do it in different ways. It's not just putting your head down and just being brutal, it's playing with nuance."
