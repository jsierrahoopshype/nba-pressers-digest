# Coach Charles Lee Practice Media Availability | 10/12/26 — *Charlotte Hornets*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MKlPYtWCZrI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MKlPYtWCZrI)

_Speakers identified: Charles Lee_

**1. Charles Lee — "we just respected space a little bit better, our running habits were better" — Charles Lee on Hornets' improved offensive spacing and rim reads** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MKlPYtWCZrI&t=9s)

Charles Lee: "I thought we did a much better job of playing with the pass. In that first game, we played in crowds too much. We had a couple charges, we had a couple moving screens, guys playing too fast, whether it be the screener or the ball handler. In this game, we respected space a little bit better, our running habits were better, we weren't playing in crowds, we were more willing to make the better rim reads. I just love the activity offensively and the togetherness. To have 26 assists this game compared to 16 last game just shows that the ball was moving and we were creating really good quality looks and our process was much better offensively."

**2. Charles Lee — "you can feel him in the game, whether he's making shots or missing shots" — Charles Lee evaluates the impact of rookie Tidjane Salaün** [01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MKlPYtWCZrI&t=62s)

Charles Lee: "You can feel his impact. You can feel him in the game, whether he's making shots or missing shots. I thought that he took the right shots, I thought that he helped our offense actually flow a lot better. His after-actions, he had a couple hit-unders and then played in the seam and had a great kick-out to somebody and they missed a three. He also had a slip that put two people on the ball and then created a lob opportunity for **Liam** to **Ryan**. I think that he's really starting to realize how he can impact the offensive game without even having to score all the time. And then defensively, I think his continued physicality and strength and he's moving really good laterally, he's affecting the defensive side of the floor."

**3. Charles Lee — "the guy just has a knack for getting involved" — Charles Lee praises the versatility and impact of rookie Royce** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MKlPYtWCZrI&t=153s)

Charles Lee: "The guy just has a knack for getting involved. Love having him around and his presence and the way that he competes. I think you're seeing it defensively. We watched a clip today, one of his hot closeouts. He gets to **AJ Green**'s body, makes him put it down, he gets a low contest, a high contest, comes back in and helps rebound. I think he's had a couple really good passes and creations. I'm still a little bit questionable about that left-hand finish that he had, but it was pretty impressive. Love what **Royce** is giving to our team."

**4. Charles Lee — "we have a ton of returning guys, we have some new guys coming in" — Charles Lee on team progress and integrating new veteran players** [03:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MKlPYtWCZrI&t=196s)

Charles Lee: "We've made great progress from game one to game two, from day one of training camp to where we are now. We've added some layers, we've thrown some things in there pretty quickly because it's like a balance of we have a ton of returning guys, we have some new guys coming in, but some of those new guys are also veterans and so they understand the league and can pick up things really fast. I love where we are. I know that we are not a finished product by any stretch of the imagination and I'm excited to see how we can keep growing throughout the rest of the preseason."

**5. Charles Lee — "his ability to be a little bit more efficient with the dribble last game was really good" — Charles Lee on the efficiency and growth of Tre Mann** [03:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MKlPYtWCZrI&t=238s)

Charles Lee: "I've loved **Tre Mann**'s, especially last game, I thought that his ability to be a little bit more efficient with the dribble last game was really good. He was more decisive, making quick decisions and reads and playing with the pass. I think our team feels very comfortable passing the ball ahead to him. He's been great. I think his decision-making has been really good, he's shooting the ball well. I think that we just have to continue to help him, give him more direction in some of the called plays, the stick-shift plays, what do we want to get to and how do we want to play. But overall, I love the impact that he's given us offensively. He's another ball handler, creator, and he's playing with a ton of confidence, which I love."
