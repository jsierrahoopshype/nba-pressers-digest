**5. Mark Daigneault — "If you have to change your principles based on the circumstances then you have the wrong principles" — Mark Daigneault on team response to injuries** [04:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6jNgK7NB2M&t=289s)

Mark Daigneault: "Initially when it first happened, if I'm being completely honest, the team had an appropriate human response. It's a couple guys that have worked incredibly hard that are going to be knocked out for a long period of time, which has an impact not only on our team but on them. There was a level of empathy and it was certainly an unpredictable circumstance. So I'd be lying if I said we just flipped the switch and kept moving. There was an appropriate period of time that we allowed that processing to happen. But in terms of what we lean on, we lean on the same things. Your circumstances are always changing but our principles aren't. I've said this many times, but if you have to change your principles based on the circumstances then you have the wrong principles. We really don't change much in any circumstance, we lean on the things that we're about and trust those things and these guys do a great job of bringing that to life."
