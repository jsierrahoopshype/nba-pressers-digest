**5. Erik Spoelstra — "We want to play with great pace and spacing in the full court." — Balancing pick-and-roll success with broader offensive goals** [03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PuR2qcICNpE&t=212s)

Erik Spoelstra: "We want to play with great pace and spacing in the full court, but you also have to do that in the half court. Then your pace ends up becoming like this, not as much like that, but you have to bring energy to the offense regardless. It doesn't necessarily matter what the trigger is. Sometimes the trigger might just be a pitch-ahead drive, or it might be a pick-and-roll, it might be a post-up. I think we're developing more out of our menu, more diversity. If we just go to one thing all the time, then that efficiency will probably go down. Right now we're tapping into quite a few different things."

**6. Erik Spoelstra — "You always have to start with Kevin Durant, just the level of attention and awareness you have." — Houston's offensive threats and off-ball movement challenges** [04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PuR2qcICNpE&t=292s)

Erik Spoelstra: "You always have to start with **Kevin Durant**, just the level of attention and awareness you have with him. **Alperen Sengun** is having a terrific start to this season and he does so much that goes into that unpredictable bucket where you don't necessarily know if it's going to be a cut, you don't know where his pass is going to be. If he catches it in the short roll, he's as good as anybody when he catches it there. He's great in the post, now he's shooting the ball terrific from three, so that adds a lot of variability to what they do and they have tremendous off-ball cutters. So those cutters become crashers too."

**7. Erik Spoelstra — "You have to drill first what your base coverages are and your base responsibilities first." — Developing instinctual defensive reads in pick-and-roll coverage** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PuR2qcICNpE&t=352s)

Erik Spoelstra: "You have to drill first what your base coverages are and your base responsibilities first. And then you do that over and over and over enough, then there has to be instinctual reads. That takes the longest time, where it's non-verbal reads and communications and you just sense when somebody is pulling a trigger that you know what to do from there. But we've been working at it a lot with film sessions and in the practice sessions, particularly early in the year and then on the road trip. That'll take some time, but we're making progress."
