**3. Dusty May — "we got to focus on improvement getting better and shrinking the margins" — Dusty May on the team's need to improve and shrink margins** [02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5mO1aTv-56I&t=147s)

Dusty May: "We have a lot of work to do. With our lineup and getting guys back healthy, having guys in and out of the lineup, we just got to focus on improvement getting better and shrinking the margins. Right now, we don't have the margins to to give up the offensive rebounds or to have the unforced errors or the turnovers. So we just got to clean that up and really just focus on our processes."

**4. Dusty May — "success is never linear and never happens the way we want to" — Dusty May on the challenges of developing a rookie** [04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5mO1aTv-56I&t=269s)

Dusty May: "I don't think **Rez** is happy with the way it's going. He missed most of camp, missed all practice, now the ball's not going in, but success is never linear and never happens the way we want to. There's no one that wants it more for him or is putting in more to have it go well, and it just hasn't yet. And I think when you look across the NBA, the 82-game rookie season, very few guys just have that steady growth process, and there's going to be bumps in the road."
