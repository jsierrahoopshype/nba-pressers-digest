# Cooper Flagg Postgame Interview | Mavericks vs. Celtics | Nov. 15, 2026 — *Dallas Mavericks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0)

_Speakers identified: Cooper Flagg_

**1. Cooper Flagg — "I don't think we competed at a high enough level throughout the three games." — Cooper Flagg on the Mavericks' lack of competitive intensity** [01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0&t=62s)

Cooper Flagg: "I just think we competed. I don't think we competed at a high enough level throughout the three games. We have to be at something that is controllable for us. The shots, make or miss, is out of our control. I think just competing at a really, really high level is something that we can do a lot better, and something I thought we were doing before this road trip. I think we kind of got away from that identity throughout these three games."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0&t=62s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0&t=62s)

**2. Cooper Flagg — "The level of competitive nature that they showed, one through 10, one through 11, one through 12." — Cooper Flagg on the Celtics' high level of competition** [02:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0&t=166s)

Cooper Flagg: "They played, for the most part, 10 guys that were getting a lot of minutes. The level of competitive nature that they showed, one through 10, one through 11, one through 12, even at the end of the game when their bench was in. The level that they were competing at is something that we need to look at and look in the mirror and decide if we're competing at that same level. If we want it as bad as they did, I don't think we did from the start of the game."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0&t=166s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0&t=166s)

**3. Cooper Flagg — "It's going to start with our vets, our leaders. I need to step up." — Cooper Flagg on the need for veteran leadership** [03:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-yiRvfxUV0&t=217s)

Cooper Flagg: "It's going to start with our vets, our leaders. I need to step up and be a lot better. I need to reset, get my body back right, get to a good spot mentally. I think just starting with our older guys, we got to set the tone and show that we're not just going to fold, we're not going to flop over from a tough stretch. We need to come back swinging, and like I said, just get back to competing. I think that's where it starts."
