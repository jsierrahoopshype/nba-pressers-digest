**3. Quin Snyder — "we didn't play with enough force where their aggressiveness made it hard for us." — Quin Snyder on the team's lack of defensive force** [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hdpb2fZN8MQ&t=65s)

Quin Snyder: "We didn't play with enough force where their aggressiveness made it hard for us to have good possessions offensively. When you turn the ball over and you give up defensive rebounds, 25 of their 37 points were generated from our offense. We're not being able to rebound, and to me, we need to play with force. Running is forceful, and if we run, we have a chance to change sides of the floor, move the ball, get in the lane, and that's when good things happen."
