# Mo Bamba talks about his 26 point performance, what's working vs. Spurs — *Utah Jazz*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_xLTjdUhqpU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_xLTjdUhqpU)

_Speakers identified: Mo Bamba_

**1. Mo Bamba — "It's funny to me when people are surprised that I can step out and shoot" — Mo Bamba on his perimeter shooting development** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_xLTjdUhqpU&t=13s)

Mo Bamba: "I worked on my shot a lot. I worked on my shot all summer. It's funny to me when people are surprised that I can step out and shoot, but it's just something that I worked on, something that the coaches continuously encourage me to shoot in my warm-up. Today was just a matter of game plan, just going out there and trying to get Wembanyama to leave the paint."

**3. Mo Bamba — "The margin of error in the NBA, the difference between winning and losing is so small" — Mo Bamba on the small margins of error in the NBA** [01:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_xLTjdUhqpU&t=111s)

Mo Bamba: "The margin of error in the NBA, the difference between winning and losing is so small. It's the difference of missing a box out, the difference of turning the ball over in extra time, the difference of not whipping a screen and chasing instead. It sucks because we're on the losing end of having to learn that, but there's a lot of good things we do out there."

**4. Mo Bamba — "I've been on teams before where it was clearly a rebuilding and it just kind of felt hopeless" — Mo Bamba on the team's resilience during a rebuilding phase** [02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_xLTjdUhqpU&t=136s)

Mo Bamba: "I've been on teams before where it was clearly a rebuilding and it just kind of felt hopeless, but it doesn't feel like that in our locker room. It doesn't feel like that on the court. It doesn't feel like that in our huddles. The coaches care, the players care, everybody cares. It's just a matter of putting together one or two extra possessions of just solid, smart NBA basketball. No one's coming through the door—what did **Rick Pitino** say when he was with the Celtics? **Larry Bird** ain't coming through the door to help us. It's a matter of who's in that locker room, who's in film with us, and who's really with us."
