_Speakers identified: Tuomas Iisalo_

**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "It's a testament to their hard work. We have live groups even on game days." — Tuomas Iisalo on Jevon Carter and Taylor Hendricks' development** [00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQ47R8haVCw&t=58s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "It's a testament to their hard work. We have live groups even on game days that they've played. They're doing live play, and that's one of the things we want to keep a thread going with, that guys are still looking to improve during the season. Some of those gains that they have made during the summer, they don't lose it just by watching others play basketball. They've been working extremely hard and we have a ton of confidence in them and they have a ton of confidence in themselves that when they step in, they're going to be ready to do what when their number is called."

**2. Tuomas Iisalo — "He's just developed into a very balanced player on both ends." — Tuomas Iisalo on Cedric's defensive and offensive versatility** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQ47R8haVCw&t=120s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "It's been very apparent when you look at the on-offs in our team. I think he's leading the whole team and not just in the on-offs, but also the expected ones that adjust for his shot quality. He's just developed into a very balanced player on both ends. Sometimes the narrative there is that he could be more aggressive or take more shots, but you also have to look at in which lineups he plays. He plays in that starting unit. We could have the same discussion also with Zaki Edi that they play together with Tai Jerome, they play together with Booz. In that unit, a lot of the offense goes through the two decision-makers and then when they play in different units, now they absorb more of the scoring responsibility, more of the creation responsibility."

**4. Tuomas Iisalo — "You can try to emphasize a different type of pace, for example." — Tuomas Iisalo on balancing offensive strategy against similar opponents** [04:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQ47R8haVCw&t=273s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "There's a couple ways to do that. You can try to emphasize a different type of pace, for example. Try to play faster or try to slow it down and be more deliberate about how we attack. Then you can think about the half-court offense also as basically through three different components. We can try to stay as dynamic as possible, which we do through what we call our flow offense, which a lot of times the first screen is set at 21 on a shot clock with three seconds. Or then you can go to your best offensive players in that for those actions on the court, or then you can try to attack through scouting the other team's weaknesses."
