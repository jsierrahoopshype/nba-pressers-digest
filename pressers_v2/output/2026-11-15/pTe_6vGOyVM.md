# Jordi Fernández Postgame Press Conference vs. Memphis Grizzlies — *Brooklyn Nets*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM)

_Speakers identified: Jordi Fernández_

**1. Jordi Fernández — "30 points of 16 turnovers is pretty impressive to their 14 for 15 points" — Jordi Fernández on the team's inconsistency and turnover struggles** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM&t=16s)

Jordi Fernández: "I think the inconsistency and you see it on the possession game with points off turnovers. Every loose ball was for them. They fought it, they won it, and 30 points of 16 turnovers is pretty impressive to their 14 for 15 points. Then you go to the free throws, you go to the second chance points, all that adds up and that's where you lose the game. I think we cut it to seven at some point and you should have been in the game, but we are not mature enough. We played against a team that played in the second of a back-to-back with the same travel that we had and they played the right way, played really hard, and we are not there yet."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM&t=16s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM&t=16s)

**2. Jordi Fernández — "a lot of those 50-50 balls, we didn't come up with those balls" — Jordi Fernández on the team's failure to secure 50-50 balls** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM&t=98s)

Jordi Fernández: "That team pressures the ball, but once again I thought that a lot of those 50-50 balls, we didn't come up with those balls and they made more winning plays than us. I can give you a lot of them that I have in my mind from throwing, like inbounding the ball to the other team three times to outlet passes to the other team. Like those are very loud mistakes. Can it happen one time? Yes, but when it happens like six times, those are big swings and we just got to believe that we are better and work and do it consistently."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM&t=98s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTe_6vGOyVM&t=98s)
