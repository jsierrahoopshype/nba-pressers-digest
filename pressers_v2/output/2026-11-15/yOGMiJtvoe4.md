# "We Gave Them Too Much Momentum." | Ayo Dosunmu Post Game Sound | 11.14.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yOGMiJtvoe4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yOGMiJtvoe4)

_Speakers identified: Ayo Dosunmu_

**1. Ayo Dosunmu — "Attention to detail from the start, I think that has to be better." — Ayo Dosunmu on Minnesota Timberwolves' poor start and lack of focus** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yOGMiJtvoe4&t=0s)

Ayo Dosunmu: "Attention to detail from the start, I think that has to be better. Attention to game plan has to be better, and we have to be able to pivot when shots are not falling. I think we picked it up too late, but the NBA has the best players in the world. So, when you give a team momentum, they make their threes. They flew in today off a back-to-back, they had nothing to lose, and when you give them ammo, you give them momentum, that is when you are in trouble. So, I think that is what got us caught up tonight."
