**3. Caleb Martin — "obviously we're a lot of players are part of their toughest critic and obviously it's easy" — Caleb Martin on managing frustration when shots aren't falling** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GlSJJvfX6CM&t=71s)

Caleb Martin: "Obviously we're a lot of players are part of their toughest critic and obviously it's easy to get down on yourself, but that's part of the game. We're not going to make every shot and at the end of the day if you're playing hard, the things happen. It doesn't really matter if the shots will the shots will eventually fall. Just like you can start the game really hot and go cold. It can be the reverse of a team that's not making shots early. The game is a game of runs and I think that we all know that. We just have to continue to believe that and stay the course and that's why we got to hang our hat on the defensive end because that's the one thing you can control in the game."

**4. Caleb Martin — "that's where great teams are great, you know, that's why good teams are good" — Caleb Martin on maintaining focus despite opponent roster changes** [02:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GlSJJvfX6CM&t=127s)
