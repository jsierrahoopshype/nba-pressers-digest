**3. Bones Hyland — "Whatever the game needs, we come in and as a second unit we talk as a unit." — Bones Hyland on the second unit's consistency and impact** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LV1SmElzhD4&t=36s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "As a bench, you guys take a lot of pride in when you step onto the floor, you take it up a notch. What have you guys been able to do, the consistency to start this season, especially at home?"

**Bones Hyland:** "Honestly just giving the game a different look. Whether the game needs pace, scoring, playmaking, whatever the game needs, we come in and as a second unit we talk as a unit.
