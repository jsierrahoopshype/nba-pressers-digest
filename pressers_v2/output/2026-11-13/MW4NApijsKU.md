**1. Draymond Green — "An oblique abdominal injury is one of those things that it tells you when it's healed." — Draymond Green on Stephen Curry's oblique abdominal injury timeline** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MW4NApijsKU&t=21s)

Draymond Green: "The Rockets broke our eight-game winning streak. **Stephen Curry** left the game in the first quarter with right abdominal soreness, which ultimately turned out to be, after an MRI, an oblique abdominal tear. He will be re-evaluated in two weeks. Re-evaluated in two weeks, I would say there's probably no chance that he's back in two weeks. You're just re-evaluating in two weeks and seeing where you are. An oblique injury is one of those things that it tells you when it's healed. There's no set certain timeline. Some of them heal faster than others depending on the severity, depending on where it could be. Some of them heal faster than others, some of them take longer. So there's no certainty. But then you get to the point where you need your core strength, you need your rotational strength. You need all of those things. So as you progress, as the pain starts to subside, you start to push the boundaries more and more. But no one knows how long that will take. I think after a few days, you start to get a better picture of what it is that you're looking at."
