**1. Erik Spoelstra — "He can have that exponential impact on our offense just by being who he is" — Erik Spoelstra on Giannis Antetokounmpo's offensive impact and basketball IQ** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o5lrDJpEGuI&t=0s)

Erik Spoelstra: "We controlled the entire tenor of the game offensively. He was **Magic Johnson** in the first 14 minutes of the game, setting everybody up. I love the collaboration and the connection that he and **Clay** are getting and it's growing each and every game. They're finding new ways to amplify each other. And then when he needed to be aggressive, he found different ways to get downhill and do what he does. He wasn't a bull in a china shop to start the game, it was setting everybody up and that's what we're talking about, exponential impact. He can have that exponential impact on our offense just by being who he is, as arguably at any given night the best player in the league, getting downhill but also getting a flow, allowing our offense to get into a flow. And that's where his IQ is coming in, of reading the game in the context of when to do what and I thought he was brilliant with that not only tonight but the other night."
