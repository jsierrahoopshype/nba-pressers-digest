**4. Rudy Gobert — "When we all come in with the mindset of playing defense, good things happen." — Rudy Gobert on team-wide contributions and defensive mindset** [01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlGg43Au058&t=80s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "It's not going to be everybody contributing every night. **Anthony Edwards** and **LaMelo Ball** have been such a big part of your guys' success early on, and they both kind of had off nights by their standards. But yet, **Jaden McDaniels**, you, Jaden, Jonathan, how big is that when you guys are getting contributions from other guys?"

**Rudy Gobert:** "Strength of the team is the team. When we all come in with the mindset of playing defense, good things happen. **Anthony Edwards** did a great job defensively. His shots didn't go in, but he did a great job defensively tonight. And **LaMelo Ball** just brings so much attention, you know, that sometimes even when the shots don't go in, he still does a lot of good things for us. So that's the way we want to play. That's where we are the most dangerous team when we don't know who's going to score."
