# "The ball got moving pretty good" | Will Hardy speaks to the media following preseason win — *Utah Jazz*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SY9MU84M1ds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SY9MU84M1ds)

_Speakers identified: Will Hardy_

**1. Will Hardy — "It was a good first effort for our group, plenty of stuff to build on." — Will Hardy reflects on team performance in preseason opener** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SY9MU84M1ds&t=11s)

Will Hardy: "It was a good first effort for our group, plenty of stuff to build on. I thought there were a lot of things that we did pretty well in the game. Obviously, there is tons to clean up, which is to be expected at this point in camp, but I think it is a good one for the group to build on. I thought the ball move was great, 28 assists. The part I am happiest about on that end is only 10 turnovers. I thought we did a good job taking care of the ball."

**2. Will Hardy — "I thought they both used their burst to get in the paint." — Will Hardy praises Keyonte George and Isaiah Collier's offensive impact** [00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SY9MU84M1ds&t=58s)

Will Hardy: "I thought they both used their burst to get in the paint. They do it differently. **Keyonte George** in transition, one-on-one, has the ability to put a lot of pressure on the defense. Not that **Isaiah Collier** doesn't, but Isaiah uses some off-ball action to get some of those back cuts, turned the corner a few times really nicely off handoffs and pick-and-roll. Those guys both know they have a ton of responsibility to create offense for our group. Sometimes that means that they are just the beginning phase of building the advantage."

**3. Will Hardy — "They are both picking their spots pretty well." — Will Hardy evaluates the chemistry between Jaren Jackson Jr. and Lauri Markkanen** [01:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SY9MU84M1ds&t=110s)

Will Hardy: "They are both picking their spots pretty well. It is only one game, but we have seen it in practice also. They are both recognizing when their opportunities are to attack and when they need to move it. I thought **Lauri Markkanen** did not play great in the first half and he ends up with 17. I thought he missed a couple bunnies. **Jaren Jackson Jr.** had some nice moments where he punished the mismatch with his size. I think when those two are on the floor together, they are a little bit of a matchup problem in terms of their ability to play with some physicality."

**4. Will Hardy — "My job is to give Darren a lot of feedback." — Will Hardy discusses his approach to providing feedback to rookies** [03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SY9MU84M1ds&t=182s)

Will Hardy: "My job is to give **Darren** a lot of feedback. I need to pick the moments appropriately and I need to prioritize what he needs to hear now versus what he may need to hear later. I have preached to our young players for years now that there is a difference between playing poorly and shooting poorly. I thought that **Darren** a couple of times made really good choices and just missed. If those shots go in, maybe he feels differently. **Darren** has been a great learner and he is willing to receive feedback. My responsibility, not just with him but with our whole team, is prioritizing what is important for them to hear tomorrow versus what can wait."

**5. Will Hardy — "Darren has been really humble from the moment that he got to us." — Will Hardy on Darren's growth and poise as a rookie** [04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SY9MU84M1ds&t=271s)

Will Hardy: "**Darren** has been really humble from the moment that he got to us in terms of willingness to try to understand better what is happening around him. He asks a lot of questions and it is going to give him the opportunity to be a pretty good player because I think sensitivity to feedback is something you have to get rid of if you really want to become the best version of yourself. We are all trying to help him, just like we are trying to help all the guys on our team. To date, I think he is processing it pretty well. I understand that there is a lot happening for a rookie and you would have to ask him in terms of how it feels, but from my standpoint, I think he has played with a lot of poise."
