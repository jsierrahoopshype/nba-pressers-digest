**1. Khris Middleton (Washington Wizards) — "Defense is always the number one thing that gets the coaches going during training camp" — Khris Middleton emphasizes that defensive performance is the primary focus for the Washington Wizards during training camp.** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SsFWq9zt44&t=85s) (approx.)

Khris Middleton: "Defense is always the number one thing that gets the coaches going during training camp, get the conditioning, get the legs going. **3. Khris Middleton (Washington Wizards) — "I think it is just being myself" — Khris Middleton describes his approach to mentoring younger teammates on the Washington Wizards roster.** [03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SsFWq9zt44&t=192s) (approx.)

Khris Middleton: "I think it is just being myself. I think the guys know behind closed doors a lot with the workouts and practices I talk a lot of junk. Just just competitive. I want to win whether it is a shooting game, basketball game, one on one game, anything. So they know I am competitive, they know I want to beat them, but they know I am on their same side too. So there is times where I might say, hey, try this instead of doing what you did, but it is all about motivating. It is all about helping. It is not demoralizing or making seem like I have done more than them since I have been here longer. It is more of just helping. We are all teammates. I still have a lot to learn and hopefully I can help them learn a couple things also."

**4. Khris Middleton (Washington Wizards) — "It was a lot of the stuff I saw last year" — Khris Middleton explains the factors that influenced his decision to return to the Washington Wizards.** [04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SsFWq9zt44&t=293s) (approx.)

Khris Middleton: "It was a lot of the stuff I saw last year. Just the daily habits, the way they the organization was ran from top to bottom. Then once I got in the locker room being around those guys, even though they are young and some of them may be inexperienced, but knowing that they want to reach the top at some point, so hopefully I can help them get there. And then when you look around and you see the roster, you see the additions that they added."
