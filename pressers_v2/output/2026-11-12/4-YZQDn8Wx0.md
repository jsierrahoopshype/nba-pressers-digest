**6. Nate Ament — "I've seen a bunch of different players from Jalen Brunson to KD." — Nate Ament on learning from guarding elite offensive players** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4-YZQDn8Wx0&t=216s)

Nate Ament: "I've seen a bunch of different players from **Jalen Brunson** to **KD**. I think what's synonymous and similar with all of them is just the force they play with, the strength they play with on offense, so I've been trying to come with that same force and physicality. Also just their decision-making, how quick they make decisions, how well they know themselves, their team, and what they're trying to accomplish. I've been in enough games where I know where we're trying to accomplish as a team and where my shots are going to come from, so just those quick decisions. I'm still learning, I'm still trying to figure it out as well, where I can be more aggressive and where I need to make the right play."
