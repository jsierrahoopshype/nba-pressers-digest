# Steve Kerr addresses Warriors’ continued first-quarter struggles | NBC Sports Bay Area — *Golden State Warriors*

**1. Steve Kerr — "we're not bringing the necessary force and focus to start games" — Steve Kerr on Warriors' recurring first-quarter struggles** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hdR05Q_aX0c&t=8s)

Steve Kerr: "We're just, this has been a theme in the early part of the season here. We're not bringing the necessary force and focus to start games. We're getting killed in the first quarter over and over again. And so we've got to find the execution to deal with the pressure. So everything matters, especially when you're down the way we are with Steph and Jimmy. We have to execute, otherwise it's going to look like that at times. And so I don't think the execution is there yet."

**2. Steve Kerr — "it's 100% on the Golden State Warriors. We're a team, so we figure things out together" — Steve Kerr on collective responsibility for Warriors' performance** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hdR05Q_aX0c&t=77s)

Steve Kerr: "It's us. It's 100% on the Golden State Warriors. We're a team, so we figure things out together. We collaborate. So it's on all of us. And that's what practice is for, that's what game film is for. And so I've got to do better, they've got to do better, and we're going to get there. I'm very confident that we will, but we're not there yet, clearly."

**4. Steve Kerr — "the ball's going to find Trayce, it's going to find the guy who can attack" — Steve Kerr on team's increased reliance on Trayce Jackson-Davis** [02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hdR05Q_aX0c&t=122s)

Steve Kerr: "That's how we were cutting harder and playing faster and more efficiently after the first quarter. And so when that happens, then the ball's going to find Trayce, it's going to find the guy who can attack. But in the first quarter, we were standing still, lazy cuts, poor screens, and then they're a really athletic defensive-minded team that can get after you, and they forced a bunch of turnovers. I think we must have had nine or 10 in the first quarter, and then only six or seven the rest of the game. So we've got to be ready, got to be more prepared to start the game."
