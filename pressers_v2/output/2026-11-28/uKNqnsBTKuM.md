**1. JJ Redick — "to beat these teams, you can't have those momentary mental lapses" — JJ Redick on the Lakers' defensive lapses against the Spurs** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uKNqnsBTKuM&t=12s)

JJ Redick: "The difference in the third, to me, because part of the thing with them was we want to try to limit them in the paint. The paint is priority and getting back and getting our defense set. They had 10 transition points in the first half and 30 paint points in the first half, which is not great. They were two for 12 from three. **Kelton Johnson** hits a three, **Carter Bryan** hits an off the dribble. They made threes in that third quarter, we did not. And you can certainly point to certain plays. I told the guys after, I think of five or six dumb things that I did tonight. But to beat these teams, you can't have those momentary mental lapses. We had a few of those and then it turned into a one possession game."
