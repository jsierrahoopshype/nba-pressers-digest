# Will Hardy talks about having Jusuf Nurkic back, Kyle Filipowski vs. Denver — *Utah Jazz*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ajTb5c4BrIU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ajTb5c4BrIU)

_Speakers identified: Will Hardy_

**1. Will Hardy — "late game execution was poor and we gave into physicality" — Will Hardy on the team's late-game execution and physicality** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ajTb5c4BrIU&t=0s)

Will Hardy: "So many positives tonight. Obviously, having Nurk back was nice and I thought the guys competed. Late game execution was poor and we gave into physicality. We didn't change ends, gave up too many layups, too many lapses. Got to stay in the moment and compete. The game's 48 minutes long, takes a lot of mental conditioning to get to the end, and we have to step it up. Playing a lot of good basketball and the end of the game is certainly not up to our standard."

**4. Will Hardy — "we need to toughen up. We give into physicality at the end" — Will Hardy on the need for increased team toughness** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ajTb5c4BrIU&t=102s)
