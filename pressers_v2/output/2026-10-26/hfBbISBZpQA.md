**1. Charles Lee — "he earned the guys' trust around the basket with how great his hands were as well" — Charles Lee on Ryan Colkbrener's development and defensive impact** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfBbISBZpQA&t=13s)

Charles Lee: "It is funny, especially at the beginning of his rookie season last year, he showed glimpses of what he is doing now. There were some moments to start the season last year, he was one of the best bigs at protecting the rim. He had one of the best field goal percentages because everything was a dunk. I think that he earned the guys' trust around the basket with how great his hands were as well. Then he got himself back together towards the end of the season, and I think right now we are just seeing a little bit more sustained effort, sustained physicality from him. He has found his comfort level, he has found what works best for him and his routines and his process to properly mentally and physically prepare him for the games."

**4. Charles Lee — "mistakes are going to happen, we can coach those mistakes in the timeout" — Coaching Christian and managing early career mistakes** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfBbISBZpQA&t=215s)

Charles Lee: "**Christian** has been thrown into the deep end to begin the season due to the depleted backcourt. Mistakes are going to happen, we can coach those mistakes in the timeout, we can coach those mistakes at halftime, we can coach him the next day. I want him to set a standard for himself for how competitive he is and just really coming in and being a dog. He is a smart player, he is a very skilled player offensively. We have seen throughout training camp and practices and games how disruptive he can be defensively and like keep it simple. Do those things, do them at a high competitive level, and all the other stuff is going to come for him because he is so smart, he works so hard."

**5. Charles Lee — "the free throw line, I think that this team, they have great personnel that's able to be crafty" — Hornets' need for free-throw line discipline against Washington** [04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfBbISBZpQA&t=264s)

Charles Lee: "The free throw line. I think that this team, they have great personnel that is able to be crafty. Young being one of those guys, Davis being one of those guys. They have been around, they understand how to put you in compromising situations and so you have got to have great discipline and understand that they are going to get a couple calls that you might disagree with, but you only have so much energy during a game. I hope that we do not waste it on things like that. I hope we really just lock in and focus on the competitive spirit and each other during the game."

**6. Charles Lee — "both players are really going to rely on the team around them" — Team-oriented approach to the Alex Sarr and Moussa Diabate matchup** [05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfBbISBZpQA&t=318s)

Charles Lee: "I think that both players are really going to rely on the team around them. Obviously, they might draw the individual matchup, but it is a team game ultimately out there and so I think that they are familiar with each other's games. Moussa brings obviously great energy offensively and versatility defensively, so just mixing up our coverages on a guy like Sarr is going to be important, which will also then dictate a little bit of how their offense is able to run with him as a screener or him as a spacer."

**7. Charles Lee — "it helps me for sure get to learn them a little bit more" — Benefits of rotating younger players due to veteran injuries** [05:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfBbISBZpQA&t=358s)

Charles Lee: "It helps me for sure get to learn them a little bit more, put them in some positions like you said that they may not have been in or they were going to be in, they are just not doing it for larger stints of the game. And so I think it has been great. The other cool part about it too is their teammates get to see how good they are now that they are new players on our team. There is a trust level that is gained and earned, and then their interactions now the new guys they get to learn and now they get to come to the huddle and ask a question."
