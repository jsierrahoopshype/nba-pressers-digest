**Unidentified speaker:** "I enjoyed your press conference on Friday. You said it's not all sunshine and fairy tales and rainbows. How did you think this team responded to that disappointment from Friday here tonight?"

**Tyrese Haliburton:** "I thought the first half was a little rough for us. I think the story of our year so far has been turnovers and keeping teams off the free throw line. I thought of taking care of the ball, getting stops, finishing with a rebound, being able to run. You can see what our offense can do, but we have to stay at it for four quarters. There's lots to improve on, I think we're getting better."
