**2. Joe Mazzulla — "you have to defend him with your offense. You can't play inefficient offense." — Joe Mazzulla on the strategy for defending Nikola Jokic** [00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XNXIfdNSqA&t=37s)

Joe Mazzulla: "There's certain guys, Jokic, Embiid, two-man combinations. You have to defend him with your offense. You can't play inefficient offense. You can't allow them to get more shots than you. You can't turn the ball over. If you get into a close game, you have to pick your poison. He is a very good player and he has an ability to put pressure on every single coverage. He's seen every single coverage. So it's not about stopping him, it's about maintaining him and winning on the margins."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XNXIfdNSqA&t=37s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XNXIfdNSqA&t=37s)

**3. Joe Mazzulla — "you have to play two-way basketball. You have to have understanding of what the other team is great at." — Joe Mazzulla on the necessity of playing two-way basketball** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XNXIfdNSqA&t=120s)

Joe Mazzulla: "You have to play two-way basketball. You have to have understanding of what the other team is great at. You have to combat that, you have to take that away and you have to try to impose your will on it. Try to play connected basketball regardless. You can't win one without the other. If you just look at it from one perspective and say, 'Okay, how are we going to stop him?' that's not a right answer. You have to put pressure on them with the things that you do well."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XNXIfdNSqA&t=120s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XNXIfdNSqA&t=120s)
