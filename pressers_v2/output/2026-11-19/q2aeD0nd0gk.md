**1. Doug Christie — "Leaky is doing exactly what I like, down in the stance, arms out, just presenting a picture." — Doug Christie praises Leaky Black's defensive impact and fundamentals** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2aeD0nd0gk&t=36s)

Doug Christie: "Leaky is doing exactly what I like, down in the stance, arms out, just presenting a picture that gaps aren't there. Then he's extremely active with his hands. When someone leaves their feet, he leaves his feet, which puts them in a real tough position. Really good hands, especially on defense, runs the floor, shoots the shots when he's open. He's just a team player, but defensively solid. You can put him on just about anybody, and yeah, big fan of Leaky."

**2. Doug Christie — "What did you see? And then getting off the ball early when he comes off. If he sees that there's" — Doug Christie on coaching De'Aaron Fox to improve defensive decision-making** [01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2aeD0nd0gk&t=82s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "What I want him to do is digest the game early, understand, let's go off of a drag, let's go off of a mid pick-and-roll, let's go off of a double drag. What were those coverages?"

**Doug Christie:** "What did you see? And then getting off the ball early when he comes off. If he sees that there's a crowd, get off of it, calculate that, read it, make sure that you're making the notes and then let's go back to things that were working. Where did you see gaps at? Where did you see something? Also for your teammate, because ultimately, he's going to be able to find his rhythm. He can get his own shot, but sometimes he's like, 'Hey coach, I think we should run a stack.' There's something for Keegan on a hip down. Those are the things that I really want him to digest the game, not only for himself but for his teammates."

**3. Doug Christie — "Throw that out the window and just be aggressive." — Doug Christie emphasizes aggression over consistency for Keegan Murray** [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2aeD0nd0gk&t=148s)

Doug Christie: "Throw that out the window and just be aggressive. That's what run really hard, get to the corners, come off of pin-downs, looking, understanding when you're coming off the pin-down, make the reading the corner before you even go. You should already know, am I about to turn the corner? Am I open? Catch the ball ready to shoot it. Defensively active in gaps.
