**7. Jordan Ott — "Elite shooting teams don't necessarily need to touch the paint." — The discrepancy between paint touches and offensive analytics** [06:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHAAm9Wd7QA&t=413s)

Jordan Ott: "Some teams can get high quality shots and just number of paint touches, they may only need one paint touch, not multiple in a possession. That would be one. Elite shooting teams don't necessarily need to touch the paint. You can get the right outside the paint, still collapse the defense with a roller or some type of cut. It's something that's hard to wrap your head around because you want some type of paint pressure."
