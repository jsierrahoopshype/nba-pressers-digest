**2. Tyrese Haliburton — "our core is our core, same group. We have had the same coaching staff for a long time" — Tyrese Haliburton on team continuity and roster stability** [04:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41RkOr25bHE&t=282s)

Tyrese Haliburton: "Our core is our core, same group. We have had the same coaching staff for a long time now, so I think our principles are our principles and we are understanding of that and that has been really good. I think it is hard when you have a lot of roster turnover, when you have a lot of coaching turnover, it is hard to sustain success. Fortunately for us, we have kept the same group together for the most part. Obviously we are missing two key pieces from our championship run, but we are filling those in pretty well. I think **Pascal Siakam** has been really good for us and **Kelly Olynyk** has been really good for us."
