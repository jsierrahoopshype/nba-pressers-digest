# Head Coach Jordan Ott Training Camp Media Availability | Phoenix Suns | 10-1-26 — *Phoenix Suns*

**1. Jordan Ott — "If he gets it and he brings it, we will have options when he has the ball." — Jordan Ott on Miles Bridges' potential as a playmaker** [00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtxfbtiJy_g&t=24s)

Jordan Ott: "Miles starts with the defensive rebound. His size, he is a good defensive rebounder. When we put him in that spot where he gets it and we have such speed, then we can just run without setting up anything. He can bring it. That is the most ideal space. We will use him against pressure. Guys pressuring our guards, he will have a lesser matchup, so at least he can initiate offense and get into it. We are pretty conceptual. If he gets it and he brings it, we will have options when he has the ball. A lot of that is some inverted stuff because of his size. So if we can get a small screen and get a mismatch that way, we will have an advantage right away."

**2. Jordan Ott — "The more options you have, the harder it is for the defense just to lock in." — Jordan Ott on the importance of having multiple playmaking threats** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtxfbtiJy_g&t=84s)

Jordan Ott: "That is just how the game is going. The more options you have, the harder it is for the defense just to lock in to the guy with the most gravity. I remember **Devin Booker** saying that last year. I have heard it multiple times over the course of my NBA career: you want everyone to be a threat. Everyone to be a threat, everyone has their own gravity. Sometimes your gravity is that you are a good shooter, so you use that space. Sometimes it is that you are a great screener, sometimes it is that you are a great roller, so your space is above the vertical spacing of it all. Everyone has their own gravity. It just comes in different ways."

**3. Jordan Ott — "We want to force turnovers without fouling. We understand there is a little risk in it." — Jordan Ott on the team's aggressive defensive philosophy** [02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtxfbtiJy_g&t=132s)

Jordan Ott: "We want to force turnovers without fouling. We understand there is a little risk in it. We probably lean into some of the fouling is okay because we want you to be so aggressive because we want the ball. We want to steal it and take it from the other team, knowing that there is some risk. Just do not put yourself in a bad way, a long closeout where you get someone's feet or up top. Knowing where a lot of fouls happen is you are out of position, even on the ball, and they end up shooting it and you end up fouling. Some of it will be his experience, but it comes down to he has great size, great instincts, and his spirit and heart wants to defend."

**4. Jordan Ott — "A lot of our offensive rebounding came because we sent so many bodies." — Jordan Ott on the team's offensive rebounding strategy** [03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtxfbtiJy_g&t=207s)

Jordan Ott: "A lot of our offensive rebounding came because we sent so many bodies and a lot of guards were not able to finish. So then it led to your rim rate. We want to get to the free-throw line, but you have to get to the rim. A lot of our offensive rebounds we would have to pass it out or create another action because it was a 6-4, 6-5, 6-6. **Miles Bridges** will have the ability to go back up. We have to get him there. It is new for him. Historically, he has not got there. We have addressed it right away. We know we can coach that."

**5. Jordan Ott — "We will mess around a little bit in the preseason. We do have two soldiers." — Jordan Ott on the team's approach to the starting center position** [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtxfbtiJy_g&t=420s)

Jordan Ott: "We will mess around a little bit in the preseason. I do not want to, it is not like a day-of game, I do not think. I think you want to at least have a little bit rolling in. It is not a secret. There is no secrets, but we do have to see how it plays out. So a little bit of both. I think you want to let the preseason play out, but the couple days going into it, we will know. We will get a good feel. Everyone can see when a starting center goes out, there is an opportunity and a gap there, just like it would be in the season. You are trying to fight for a rotation spot. Something happens, you want to keep the rotation spot. We have two soldiers that they are either going to come in, compete, do what is best for the team. Who starts, who finishes, it is going to change."
