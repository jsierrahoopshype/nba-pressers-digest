**4. PJ Hall — "I talk to Hunter Tyson a good bit. We talk all the time. We've gone through some similar situations." — PJ Hall on his relationship with coach Brad Brownell and Hunter Tyson** [01:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1L7svvpfSko&t=118s)

We've gone through some similar situations in our professional career, but he's been great to lean on. And so it was awesome to be able to, when I heard, I called coach and told him what to happen. And I might have messed his golf round up, I probably caught him in the middle of a round, but he was like, 'Dude, come on up, get here tomorrow.'
