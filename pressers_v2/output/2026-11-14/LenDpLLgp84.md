**11. Darius Acuff Jr. — "I met AI for the first time in my senior year high school playing at the Iverson Classic." — Darius Acuff Jr. on his relationship with Allen Iverson** [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LenDpLLgp84&t=735s)

Darius Acuff Jr.: "I met AI for the first time in my senior year high school playing at the Iverson Classic. I had just signed with Reebok. And it's crazy, we, you know, they had a little one-on-one, a little stuff up there, and he just showed up in the middle of it. And I'm like, oh, I'm like, there go AI. I'm like, yeah, I'm going to win. So, yeah, we got the win, winning the tournament, and then that was the first time he stood up. I shook his hand and it was, I told him he was my favorite player. And, you know, ever since then it's been a great connection. The number one thing he installed in me, he said, you know, just always be a dog first. Like, don't worry about nothing, be a dog first, go out there and play hard, and everything else will come later. And it makes sense, coming from a guy like him, because he went out there and he hustled, no matter what. He was a hustler on the court, and he's going to get to it regardless, he's going to die for it. And that's what he was giving advice to me, and I appreciated that. And ever since then it's been a great relationship, and, you know, that's my dog."
