# Head Coach Jordan Ott Practice Media Availability | 11-14-26 — *Phoenix Suns*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp2JBkYnxxY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp2JBkYnxxY)

_Speakers identified: Jordan Ott_

**1. Jordan Ott — "When he's got everything going, that's what makes him the toughest." — Luka Doncic's evolving role and impact on the team** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp2JBkYnxxY&t=15s)

Jordan Ott: "I do think the shift in his role and responsibility even with some of the roster changes, not that he has the ball in his hands more, but he is in a place where it is his ball. When he has everything going, that's what makes him the toughest. It is the three-point line, it is the fouling, it is the rim, and then he is one of the elite passers in the league. He is one of those guys if you give him a consistent look, he is going to figure it out. He plays at his own pace."

**2. Jordan Ott — "How physical can you be, but also disciplined at the same time?" — Balancing physical defense with disciplined play to avoid fouls** [01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp2JBkYnxxY&t=82s)

Jordan Ott: "It does not matter who you play, it is how physical can you be, but also disciplined at the same time knowing that there is a higher likelihood of drawing a foul in certain areas. We have to take those out. Knowing that there is going to be some 50-50 calls not go your way, or the foul in the three-point shooter. Some of those things you just have to take out, knowing that Luka is going to get some and-ones. They are going to have some for sure. Can we be disciplined enough in certain areas not to put them to the free-throw line to just get a great impact?"

**4. Jordan Ott — "He applies his force on both ends." — Assessing the impact of new additions Miles Bridges and DeAndre Ayton** [06:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp2JBkYnxxY&t=407s)

Jordan Ott: "We can feel where **DeAndre Ayton** is heading. Every time he steps on the floor, he applies his force on both ends. Again, that is something that we are going to need. He is strong enough, his IQ is high enough, he is competitive enough, he is going to get it. When an opportunity comes, he will be ready. Miles, it is always about his physicality and size. We are learning his game. I think he is significantly more versatile than I thought. He can score in a bunch of different ways."

**5. Jordan Ott — "That is what superstars do, they find ways to be effective in a bunch of different areas." — Devin Booker's ability to maximize his playmaking without a traditional point guard** [08:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp2JBkYnxxY&t=492s)

Jordan Ott: "That is what superstars do, they find ways to be effective in a bunch of different areas. That is part of being a really high-level player, is always being effective and trying to get your team to win no matter what the circumstance is. It is not surprising. Every night is different. He is the one that is figuring out those things on the fly a lot of times, who to attack. He has seen so many different scenarios in his career. That is why we rely on him so much because he has been through it all."
