# Mahershala Ali Reveals Winning 2 Oscars Didn't Guarantee More Roles in Hollywood — *7PM in Brooklyn*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ko6ThLsvJNc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ko6ThLsvJNc)

_Speakers identified: Mahershala Ali, Carmelo Anthony_

**4. Carmelo Anthony — "When black actors get Oscars, the preeminent notion around fans is like, 'All right, you are now validated.'" — Carmelo Anthony and Mahershala Ali on the validation of winning an Oscar** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ko6ThLsvJNc&t=144s)

**Carmelo Anthony:** "Is it different being a black actor though? Because I feel like once, especially, I remember when Denzel got his, when Halle got hers, **Michael B. Jordan** just got his. Like when black actors get Oscars, the preeminent notion around fans is like, 'All right, you are now officially on this list of people. You are now validated. You are in the Denzel class. You are in the class of A-list tier dudes.' And you're saying that it's kind of give and take. It's good but it's not."

**Mahershala Ali:** "I just don't know what that means. I respectfully, meaning it's kind of just talk. You know, it's just it doesn't translate to anything real. It's just who's better, LeBron or Jordan? Who cares? They're great. There's no definitive answer. You know what I'm saying? So I think, and there's something to like, it's not the same world when Sydney Poitier won an Oscar as when **Halle Berry** or Viola Davis or, you know what I mean? It's different eras, different world."
