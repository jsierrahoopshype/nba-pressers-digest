# "They're forming as a team" | Coach Hardy speaks to media after preseason win against Phoenix 🎥 — *Utah Jazz*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkV51DIcs40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkV51DIcs40)

_Speakers identified: Will Hardy_

**1. Will Hardy — "Obviously the biggest concern coming out of tonight is the possession battle." — Will Hardy on Utah Jazz's preseason progress and rebounding struggles** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkV51DIcs40&t=5s)

Will Hardy: "A lot to takeaway from a preseason game. I think our team is continuing to show that they're understanding each other and how to play together. Obviously the biggest concern coming out of tonight is the possession battle. They had 20 offensive rebounds. Credit to Phoenix, their effort and intensity was tremendous throughout the game. I thought our defense was pretty good and our rebounding was poor, and it hurt us a lot tonight. That coupled with 18 turnovers, the math is against you most nights. We happened to have a very efficient night offensively, shooting almost 50% from the floor, over 40 from three, and 37 free throw attempts. So we got bailed out by playing a solid game offensively from a shooting standpoint."

**2. Will Hardy — "He's such a calm person that he doesn't get rattled, at least outwardly." — Will Hardy on Keyonte George's playmaking and calm demeanor** [01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkV51DIcs40&t=81s)

Will Hardy: "I thought his playmaking was pretty good tonight. He's such a calm person that he doesn't get rattled, at least outwardly. He's processing the game pretty quickly for a rookie, understanding how to find his teammates. I think there's a lot there that is very positive to work with. He's still fighting some of his good nature in moments. I felt like there were some pockets of the game where he didn't even look at the basket, and sometimes the reality is you're the one that's open. That's why nobody else is open. But again, it's so much easier to work with somebody whose mindset is about the team. From day one, Keyonte has done nothing but give himself to the group. He wants to win, he wants to be a part of the team, and so a lot of positives to take away."

**3. Will Hardy — "We mixed in a couple of different looks throughout preseason, you know, we have to get to know our team." — Will Hardy on defensive growth and scheme variety** [02:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkV51DIcs40&t=149s)

Will Hardy: "I think we're getting better. We mixed in a couple of different looks throughout preseason, you know, we have to get to know our team and try to understand some capabilities, also introduce some things to them that they can do live against opponents. In practice, it's a little bit trickier because we know what we're doing and so sometimes you can exploit it. Also there's just a different level of familiarity, but doing it against different opponents, you know, through four preseason games we tried to mix some of the defensive looks so that we can continue to work on these things the first part of the season. We do think that to get through 82 games, we're going to need a variety of coverages and schemes defensively, and so this was an opportunity throughout camp to introduce those things, get a good amount of film on them, and I thought the team handled it all pretty well."

**5. Will Hardy — "I think Ochai is continuing to show that he has the ability to impact the game on both ends." — Will Hardy on Ochai Agbaji's development and impact** [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkV51DIcs40&t=236s)

Will Hardy: "Free throws is big for anybody on the offensive side. I think last year we saw Keyonte take a big leap offensively and free throws was a big part of that. I think Ochai is continuing to show that he has the ability to impact the game on both ends, and that's what we need for us to to get to where we want to go. We need a lot of players who impact the game on both sides. It's just preseason, I understand that, but I think Ochai has shown a tremendous amount of growth over the course of one year. If you look at where Ochai is now compared to where he was this time last year, it's a testament to the work that he's done, the ability to learn. I think Ochai is a really fast learner, and the way that he's worked with our staff, our assistant coaches do a lot of hard work that that doesn't get talked about enough, but to have Ochai where he is at this point, you know, a year in, we're really excited."
