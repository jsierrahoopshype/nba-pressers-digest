**6. Nick Nurse — "it's still a defensive rebounding and it's finishing our defense by rebounding" — Nick Nurse on defensive rebounding and transition defense** [04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poUKGpx0jYs&t=281s)

Nick Nurse: "I certainly think that it's still a defensive rebounding and it's finishing our defense by rebounding. There's still some concerns there that I think we're doing a pretty good job of checking, but we're not in what I call make two moves. You got to check and now you know the ball is bouncing over the man, you got to throw it in gear and go chase it. A lot of those were coming across the free-throw line and I thought we'd made our move to block out, but the ball was now live and we got to run, we got to get more bodies in there to run those down or at least tip them. So again, I think it's a double reaction to finish off the defense that certainly going to be one of the focuses."
