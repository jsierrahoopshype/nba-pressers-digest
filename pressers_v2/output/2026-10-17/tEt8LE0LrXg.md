**1. Jalen Green — "I was trying to make the home run play." — Jalen Green on his decision-making process for attacking the basket** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEt8LE0LrXg&t=12s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Obviously you've been able to get to the basket, saw it even more again tonight. How are you reading situations where it's the time to attack, the time to try to make a play? Is it coming far more simplified for you, or do you still feel like there's some areas where you can put the turnovers in but you were trying to make a play on those?"

**Jalen Green:** "With the turnovers, I was trying to make the home run play. Overall, I feel like scoring is something that I've done so far my whole career, so I'm not really too much worried about that part. I'm worried about trying to make the right pass, getting two feet in the paint, and letting the play develop in front of me."

**8. Jalen Green — "I feel like I've had the ball in my hands my whole career." — Jalen Green on his evolution as a playmaker** [05:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEt8LE0LrXg&t=345s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Jalen, you've played alongside a lot of table-setting point guards, **VanVleet**, **Kevin Porter Jr.**, and now you're kind of turning into one, I want to say, with your ability to just sort of set up the offense. What's that evolution process been like for you?"

**Jalen Green:** "Obviously it's been different, you know. Coming to the league, all you are set out to do is just, you know, score. I'm mixing in here and there just setting up the offense, but I feel like I've had the ball in my hands my whole career, including high school and so forth and so on, all the way into the NBA. So it's not no different from that aspect. It's just the slowing down process, you know, not going one speed all the time and I feel like that's been a main thing of my game, so the slowing down process and not looking for my shot right away in the beginning of the offense, the flow."
