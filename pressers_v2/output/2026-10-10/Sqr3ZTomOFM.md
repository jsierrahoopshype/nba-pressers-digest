**1. Khaman Maluach — "It doesn't matter whether they got the offensive rebounds on me or also on the team." — Khaman Maluach on the need for Phoenix Suns bigs to improve rebounding** [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sqr3ZTomOFM&t=33s)

Khaman Maluach: "I think there is a lot of good stuff and there is a lot of stuff that I need to work on. Obviously we gave up a lot of offensive rebounds. We gave up 11 offensive rebounds, six to **Walker** and five to **Smith**. It is something as bigs we got to take pride in that. It doesn't matter whether they got the offensive rebounds on me or also on the team. It still comes back to us because that is our role as bigs to get the rebounds. So that is something we really need to work on and I am just watching film and just learn from each and every game and just continue getting better every game."

**3. Khaman Maluach — "There is more space in the NBA and the ball can go where you won't be able to get it." — Khaman Maluach on the differences between college and NBA rebounding** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sqr3ZTomOFM&t=150s)

Khaman Maluach: "There is a lot of differences. Pros crash, but college everybody crashes. Pros, some people run back, so it is kind of different. And there is more space in the NBA and the ball can go where you won't be able to get it. The ball can bounce back the other side or you can be in the right position and the ball still goes the wrong way, so I can't control that."
