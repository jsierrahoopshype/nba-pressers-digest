Deuce Mason: "As the Kings continue to search for an identity and some consistency, you are trying to find players who fit the mold for what you want to see around Kings rookie **Darius Acuff Jr.** I know the sample size is small, but one guy comes to mind: **Leaky Black**. The Kings are in desperate need of players who do the dirty work. Can you guard a couple of positions? Can you play defense with physicality? Can you communicate? Can you give that first, second, and third efforts? And oh, can you also hit the three? I think they have that on the roster in **Leaky Black**."

Deuce Mason: "**Leaky Black** is 27 years old and he's bounced around the league a little bit. He's been on Charlotte, Washington, and the G League, and right now on a two-way deal with Sacramento trying to find that long-term NBA home. But when he's gotten the chance, I think he's shown that he can really help this team. In the Kings' lone win this year against Dallas, he had four threes and four steals.
