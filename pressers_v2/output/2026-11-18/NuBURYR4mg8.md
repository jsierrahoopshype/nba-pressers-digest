# Deuce explains what Leaky Black adds to Kings to complement Darius Acuff Jr. | NBC Sports California — *Sacramento Kings*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuBURYR4mg8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuBURYR4mg8)

**1. Deuce Mason — "The Kings are in desperate need of players who do the dirty work." — Leaky Black provides necessary defensive versatility and spacing for the Kings** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuBURYR4mg8&t=0s)

Deuce Mason: "As the Kings continue to search for an identity and some consistency, you're trying to find players who fit the mold for what you want to see around Kings rookie **Darius Acuff Jr.** The Kings are in desperate need of players who do the dirty work. Can you guard a couple of positions? Can you play defense with physicality? Can you communicate? Can you give that first, second, and third efforts? And oh, can you also hit the three? I think they have that on the roster in **Leaky Black**. When he's gotten the chance, I think he's shown that he can really help this team."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuBURYR4mg8&t=0s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuBURYR4mg8&t=0s)

**2. Deuce Mason — "That shooting is the perfect complement to Darius Acuff Jr." — Leaky Black's perimeter shooting complements Darius Acuff Jr.'s playstyle** [00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuBURYR4mg8&t=55s)

Deuce Mason: "In the Kings' lone win this year against Dallas, he had four threes and four steals. He did that again against the Oklahoma City Thunder. He's also shooting the ball well from downtown, 14 of 30 from distance with three games where he's had at least four threes. That shooting is the perfect complement to **Darius Acuff Jr.** He's got great length, he's strong defensively, he could spread the floor with his three-point shooting, and he's unselfish. The best part, he knows who he is. I'd love to see **Leaky Black** get some more minutes for the Kings."
