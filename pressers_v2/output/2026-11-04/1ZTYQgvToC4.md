# Shootaround Media Availability with Micah Peavy | November 4, 2026 | OKC Thunder — *Oklahoma City Thunder*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ZTYQgvToC4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ZTYQgvToC4)

_Speakers identified: Nick Gallo, Micah Peavy_

**1. Micah Peavy — "I'm constantly in the gym just working and then coaches telling me I'm playing, so I'm going to get out there and play." — Micah Peavy on his transition to the OKC Thunder roster** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ZTYQgvToC4&t=0s)

**Nick Gallo:** "Micah, you are coming in, you are a two-way player, and suddenly you are figuring into massive parts of these games, including the win at Cleveland. Now you turn around and you play them again here at home. What is this like for you and how are you preparing to go after this same team that you just went and got a game from on their home court?"

**Micah Peavy:** "It is nothing new to me. I am always ready for when my name is called. I am constantly in the gym just working and then coaches telling me I'm playing, so I'm going to get out there and play. And preparing for a team we've just played, we just look at the mistakes we made and we fix those things and we try to come out here and get another win."

**4. Micah Peavy — "You want the vets to give you confidence to shoot the ball." — Micah Peavy on veteran encouragement to shoot the ball** [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ZTYQgvToC4&t=89s)

**Nick Gallo:** "**Isaiah Hartenstein** was telling us the other night the only time that he yells at a young guy like **Bennet Sturtz** or you is when you don't shoot the ball. What does that feel like when you come into a new team and you have guys that are like, 'Come on, hoist it up'?"

**Micah Peavy:** "That is what you want. You want the vets to give you confidence to shoot the ball because coming in on a new team, you don't know exactly where you are going to get your shot.
