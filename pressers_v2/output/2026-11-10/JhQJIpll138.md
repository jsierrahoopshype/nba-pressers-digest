**5. Alex Caruso — "it is about being aggressive and then executing when the read is there" — Alex Caruso on reading the game and being aggressive** [03:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhQJIpll138&t=203s)

Alex Caruso: "It is about reading the game. It is basketball at the end of the day. For 20 years of my life now, I have been practicing three-on-two transition drills and defensive rotation half-court drills. So at this point, it is pretty ingrained in me what the reads are and what it looks like. It is about being aggressive and then executing when the read is there, whether it is to shoot, pass, or dribble. I enjoy it because it gives me free advantages. It is a lot harder when someone is guarding you one-on-one and they are already in front of you to score in the NBA, but when guys are in rotations and you get to play angles, it makes it a lot easier."
