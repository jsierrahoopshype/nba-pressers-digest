# Joe Mazzulla on what Celtics need to IMPROVE on After 2 Losses | Celtics Practice Interview — *Boston Celtics*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I)

_Speakers identified: Joe Mazzulla_

**1. Joe Mazzulla — "I think the biggest trend is our rebounding. In critical moments, we haven't come up with the rebounds." — Joe Mazzulla identifies rebounding as the Celtics' biggest recent trend** [00:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=17s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I think the biggest trend is our rebounding. In critical moments, we haven't come up with the rebounds that we needed to. Looking at the Orlando game, the Celtics get two or three of them in the fourth quarter and they make you pay. Again, when you're in a close game, it's not just about the fourth quarter. I think the run and the momentum that we gave Denver was in the second quarter when they got those three or four offensive rebounds and they were able to cut into the lead a little bit."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=17s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=17s)

**2. Joe Mazzulla — "The screener's job is to be able to help create the two-on-one." — Joe Mazzulla explains the screener's role in creating two-on-one advantages** [01:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=88s)

Joe Mazzulla: "The screener's job is to be able to help create the two-on-one. There's got to be a good relationship between ball-handler and screener. Obviously, **Draymond Green** is one of the best screeners of all time, especially versus different coverages. He goes drop, he's going to stick a screen, he's going to switch, he's going to do a great job of hitting under and what not. So, it's the responsibility of the screener to help create the two-on-one based on how the coverage is coming."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=88s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=88s)

**3. Joe Mazzulla — "I'd say the one thing that has changed over the last few years is coverages are changing." — Joe Mazzulla on how NBA defensive coverages are constantly changing** [01:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=113s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I'd say the one thing that has changed over the last few years is coverages are changing every other possession. One possession, it might be they were maintaining off-ball for a little bit, and then in one of the last possessions of the fourth quarter, **Gordon** and **Brown** are in it, they switch, we don't slip to the screen and then we get an empty possession because we missed the advantage of slipping on an off-ball read. So, I'd say the harder thing with the screening now is coverages are changing and it's more players reading the game."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=113s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=113s)

**4. Joe Mazzulla — "The key is just having your two-on-one outlets. You have to be able to get out." — Joe Mazzulla on the importance of having multiple two-on-one outlets** [02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=164s)

Joe Mazzulla: "The key is just having your two-on-one outlets. You have to be able to get out of the screen to create the two-on-one. There's three two-on-ones because now there's two on the ball. So, it's just making the right play on that. We missed a couple of those reads. I think we fell in love with just the seam two-on-one and missed kind of the fill-behind one a couple of times. It's just recognizing the two-on-ones and then making plays out of it."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=164s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=164s)

**5. Joe Mazzulla — "Keeping his minutes right around that 36-minute range is something that we want to be able to do." — Joe Mazzulla on maintaining consistency with Jayson Tatum's playing time** [05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPNWyPcWR8I&t=327s)

Joe Mazzulla: "Keeping his minutes right around that 36-minute range is something that we want to be able to do. That gives us the best, as you get into like your playoff minutes later in the season, you get to a point with some things and as you get into a situation where you want to take advantage of playoff-like rotations, we might change a little bit. But just being consistent in keeping him right around that 36-minute range is something that we want to try and do as much as we can."
