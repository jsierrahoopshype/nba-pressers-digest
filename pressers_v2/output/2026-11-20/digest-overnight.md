Tuomas Iisalo: "I thought especially the first half I had a very divided opinion about the possessions we had because I thought we played some of the best disruption we have had in a long time. We were getting a lot of deflections, we were executing our pick-and-roll coverage. Guys looked very good, moving very well with their feet. And then there were a couple of things in the first half that we did a poor job of, so there was a huge contrast. We allowed guys to get to their strong hand all the way to the rim, we put them on the free-throw line, and we gave them second-shot opportunities. You can imagine what the outcome is going to be in NBA games if you allow guys right-hand layups, free throws, and then five to ten shots more. Usually the outcome is going to be very bad. So that was the main thing in the first half, and I think our guys did a tremendous job in the second half then doing much better job not fouling, keeping them off their strong hand, and the rebounding stayed as an issue, but we on the other hand were able to force some turnovers and negate that rebounding differential."
