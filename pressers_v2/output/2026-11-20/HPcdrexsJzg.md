# Tuomas Iisalo Press Conference | Grizzlies vs. Warriors — *Memphis Grizzlies*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcdrexsJzg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcdrexsJzg)

_Speakers identified: Tuomas Iisalo_

**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "our guys did a tremendous job in the second half then doing much better job not fouling" — Tuomas Iisalo on Grizzlies' defensive improvements in second half** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcdrexsJzg&t=0s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "I thought especially the first half I had a very divided opinion about the possessions we had because I thought we played some of the best disruption we have had in a long time. We were getting a lot of deflections, we were executing our pick-and-roll coverage. Guys looked very good, moving very well with their feet. And then there were a couple of things in the first half that we did a poor job of, so there was a huge contrast. We allowed guys to get to their strong hand all the way to the rim, we put them on the free-throw line, and we gave them second-shot opportunities. You can imagine what the outcome is going to be in NBA games if you allow guys right-hand layups, free throws, and then five to ten shots more. Usually the outcome is going to be very bad. So that was the main thing in the first half, and I think our guys did a tremendous job in the second half then doing much better job not fouling, keeping them off their strong hand, and the rebounding stayed as an issue, but we on the other hand were able to force some turnovers and negate that rebounding differential."

Tuomas Iisalo: "What we have also spoken about that eventually at some point this group hopefully will play in the playoffs. And you only get better at it if you are put in the situations where you actually get to practice that. And that's why it's such a luxury for young teams to get those get reps in in the tight games that we have had that we have spoken about also this season, different time and score scenarios. **5. Tuomas Iisalo — "both guys have high ambitions about what and who they want to be as basketball players" — Tuomas Iisalo on Cedric Coward and Jaylen Wells' complementary skills** [06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPcdrexsJzg&t=393s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "Both guys have high ambitions about what and who they want to be as basketball players, but they're not using the team as a platform for self-development in that regard that they would put themselves above the team. And I think there's been discussions about Cedric should do this or Cedric should do that. Cedric's leading our team in the on-offs exactly because he makes a lot of the right plays on the right time. He's aggressive in the right time, he's patient. And I think a lot of times these young players, the advice or the general narrative can be extremely misleading. And that's one of those things where then helps to have good relationships with the players and obviously helps to win because you see then everybody benefits from that."
