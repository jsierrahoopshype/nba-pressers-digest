**3. Micah Nori — "when you have the gravity of Dame and you have the multiple ball-handlers, it's difficult to guard" — Micah Nori on the impact of Dame and Drew Holiday** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRl-bPYpQzs&t=75s)

Micah Nori: "It just slowed things down. The one thing that we were able to do was to get into the paint. When you have the gravity of **Dame** and you have the multiple ball-handlers, which we had at the end of the floor, it's difficult to guard. When you get into the paint and we have willing passers to kick and then now all of a sudden the driving lanes open up and we were able to either get to the free-throw line, get to the rim, or find a kick-out and knock some of those down."
