# Neemias Queta Explains what he's learned from Draymond Green | Celtics Practice Interview — *Boston Celtics*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg)

_Speakers identified: Neemias Queta_

**1. Neemias Queta — "one of my favorite teams ever watched was the Warriors in the early days" — Neemias Queta on studying the Warriors' screen-setting and playmaking** [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=65s)

Neemias Queta: "One of my favorite teams ever watched was the Warriors in the early days when they had **David Lee**, and all those guys setting all those screens for **Draymond Green**. Those guys would create really good screens for them and they were able to get advantages, two-on-ones, short rolls, and creating downhill opportunities. People forget about that because they shot a lot of threes and made a lot of moves, but that was something that they did a really good job at and I think I learned a lot from that."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=65s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=65s)

**2. Neemias Queta — "the more I can help them relieve some of that pressure when it's through screens, that's my job" — Neemias Queta on his role as a big man against pressure** [01:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=113s)

Neemias Queta: "I think it's a connection piece. Whenever they're getting pressure, the majority of the times for us fives, we're not really getting that much pressure in comparison. We can be a pressure release for them. Like I said, those guys get a lot of pressure a lot of the time, they got to bring the ball up the floor, and for me, the more I can help them relieve some of that pressure when it's through screens, whether it's getting the ball and let them create off the ball, that's my job."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=113s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=113s)

**3. Neemias Queta — "those help me build a foundation to where I'm at right now" — Neemias Queta on how G League experience built his foundation** [03:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=185s)

Neemias Queta: "I think those help me build a foundation to where I'm at right now. Obviously over the years, I've been back and forth between the G League and the NBA, and it was a great experience for me because whether I was in the rotation, whether I was coming off an injury, coming back into the G League and getting consistent rotation minutes where you can develop on different things that you want to be able to showcase on the court, those are meaningful reps that will keep you in rhythm for your opportunity and give you a sense of what you can do."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=185s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=185s)

**4. Neemias Queta — "we can be better with the turnovers. A lot of that can be on us, being able to handle the ball" — Neemias Queta on areas for improvement including turnovers and rebounding** [05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=301s)

Neemias Queta: "I think we can be better at everything on the floor. There's a lot of aspects we can fix and be better at. I think we can be better with the turnovers. A lot of that can be on us, being able to handle the ball, catching, and then just rebounding as well, which is so crucial for us. If we want to be able to get more possessions and create more havoc, we got to be able to do those things at a high level."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=301s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo6FHjYU3gg&t=301s)
