**1. Dean Wade — "the energy he brings and the defensive force he plays with is so important to a team" — Dean Wade on the importance of VJ Edgecombe's energy and defense** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Op1SVAus7kA&t=6s)

Dean Wade: "He is so important to our team. Before the season, when everything happened, they were asking who is going to sacrifice. I think everyone was like, I think VJ is going to have to do it. But the energy he brings and the defensive force he plays with is so important to a team. I think he is probably one of the keys to our team and I do not even know if he realizes it, how important he is for us. He brings so much energy in offensive rebounding and defense. He is everywhere. I told him, your box score stats might not show up how important you really are to this team, but you are just, you cannot stay, the NBA is full of highs and lows and you just have to stay here."

**5. Dean Wade — "nobody cares who is scoring, we have so much firepower" — Dean Wade on the team's unselfish culture and character** [05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Op1SVAus7kA&t=325s)
