# Hawks vs. Rockets Postgame Press Conference: Aaron Wiggins and Jalen Johnson after 121-144 Loss — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jlz1EPvf24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jlz1EPvf24)

_Speakers identified: Jalen Johnson, Aaron Wiggins_

**1. Jalen Johnson — "the main thing was their second chance points. We gave up a lot of offensive rebounds" — Jalen Johnson on Rockets' offensive rebounding and transition dominance** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jlz1EPvf24&t=12s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "What was it that the Rockets were able to do, especially in that first half, that gave them the lead?"

**Jalen Johnson:** "The main thing was their second chance points. We gave up a lot of offensive rebounds and we felt that. Obviously, when we don't get those stops and get those rebounds and get out in transition, they were hurting us on the offensive glass. That was a big part of it."

**Unidentified speaker:** "What were you guys able to do to string together the points and stops?"

**3. Aaron Wiggins — "the way that our team is going to be good is moving the ball" — Aaron Wiggins on Coach Snyder's system and team ball movement** [01:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jlz1EPvf24&t=110s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "How do you think Coach **Snyder** and the coaching staff have put you in the best positions to succeed?"

**Aaron Wiggins:** "It's just the system. The way that we're going to be successful and the way that our team is going to be good is moving the ball. **4. Jalen Johnson — "we're not going to overreact to anything. There's a lot of things we don't do well" — Jalen Johnson on team progress and learning from game film** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jlz1EPvf24&t=143s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Coach **Snyder** was talking about the team being a work in progress. What do you need to do to get that progress going?"

**Jalen Johnson:** "It's game three. We're not going to overreact to anything. There's a lot of things we don't do well so far in these three games and there's a lot of things that we can improve on. Taking it game by game, continuing to try to get better, learn from the film that we watch. There's nothing to overreact over, but we still have a lot of room to grow and everybody's hungry to keep growing."

**5. Jalen Johnson — "we've just got to do a better job between our guys just playing the right way" — Jalen Johnson on the need for better execution and pace** [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jlz1EPvf24&t=186s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "What about the Rockets challenged you all offensively?"

**Jalen Johnson:** "I don't know if there was something that necessarily challenged us offensively. We've just got to do a better job between our guys just playing the right way and playing our style. That first half and even a little bit in that second half, we were just a little slow and not playing the pace that we want to play at.
