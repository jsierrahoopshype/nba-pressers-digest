# Taylor Jenkins Postgame Press Conference | 10.24.26 — *Milwaukee Bucks*

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "It was Joel Embiid or Tyrese Maxey or Jaylen Brown down the stretch where one of those guys" — Taylor Jenkins on the late-game execution of Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, and Jaylen Brown** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F_X8tee3KCY&t=0s)

Taylor Jenkins: "It just felt like it was one of those games where it was **Joel Embiid** or **Tyrese Maxey** or **Jaylen Brown** down the stretch where one of those guys or two of them together just hit a bucket, got a rebound, made a stop down the stretch. That's what those guys can do. How difficult it was though to just see them be able to do that time and again down the stretch. They're proving great players."

**2. Taylor Jenkins — "We had tremendous effort, 48 minutes, obviously didn't make the plays down the stretch." — Taylor Jenkins praises team's competitive effort despite the 16-point deficit** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F_X8tee3KCY&t=27s)

Taylor Jenkins: "We had tremendous effort, 48 minutes, obviously didn't make the plays down the stretch. They went on runs, we went on runs. I thought what we talked about this morning, what we needed to apply tonight, our guys did it beautifully. Obviously they made some big-time shots. A number of their guys made a bunch of threes. **3. Taylor Jenkins — "We prioritized getting to the paint, big stark difference when we touch the paint." — Taylor Jenkins discusses the team's offensive process and shot selection** [01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F_X8tee3KCY&t=87s)

Taylor Jenkins: "We prioritized getting to the paint, big stark difference when we touch the paint and when we don't touch the paint. I thought we were getting that. The playmaking was there, we could have racked up a whole lot more assists. First two games we were knocking down a lot of threes. I thought we had the rhythm early in the game, just those shots definitely weren't falling, but the style of play offensively and defensively, this was more a reflection of what we want to do."

**4. Taylor Jenkins — "Great environment for Nate to get his first opportunity. I thought he was having a heck of a summer." — Taylor Jenkins on Nate's debut and his development needs** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F_X8tee3KCY&t=135s)

Taylor Jenkins: "Great environment for **Nate** to get his first opportunity. I thought he was having a heck of a summer, heck of a preseason, injury got in the way a little bit. Had been playing in play groups, doing everything possible, playing well. I thought it was a great opportunity on the second night of a back-to-back with certain matchups and it was a great learning opportunity for him, how he can be better in some of those foul situations, rebounding in particular. I thought his off-ball defense was great. Just got to continue to play with confidence on the offensive side. We know what he's capable of and I just wanted to see it pretty early here in the season."
