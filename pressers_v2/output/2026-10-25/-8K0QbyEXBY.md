**4. Jamahl Mosley — "you're going to win some games, you're going to lose some games" — Jamahl Mosley on team mental resilience and focus on details** [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-8K0QbyEXBY&t=103s)

Jamahl Mosley: "It's the NBA. You're going to win some games, you're going to lose some games, and so you got to understand if you're focusing on the right things and if you're pulling yourself together as a unit. That's what these guys will continue to focus on doing, and can we find the details within these games? Because there's a common theme in these moments: how do we do better without fouling? How do we take care of the basketball? How do we finish out possessions with the rebound? I think those are big keys and the small details of a game that we can control, not necessarily what the opponent is doing."
