**Unidentified speaker:** "Looking at your game tonight, you set a franchise record for most points scored in a home opener. You passed **Charles Barkley**, also **Eddie Johnson**. I know we always talk about milestones with you, but what does that mean?"

**3. Devin Booker — "The first game of the season was embarrassing for us, and we wore that." — Devin Booker on building team identity and overcoming early season struggles** [00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_W9Z8WgWUI&t=54s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "We also talked about just this team's ability to respond, coming out, turning things around. Coach talks about building habits and creating more of those. What habits do you think that you guys built on tonight?"

**Devin Booker:** "Really what we built our identity on last year. To be completely honest, the first game of the season was embarrassing for us, and we wore that.
