**1. Jaden McDaniels — "I just out there flowing tonight, taking what they give me." — Jaden McDaniels on his career-high 34-point performance against Brooklyn** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fI6kwPgbuNo&t=0s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Jaden, coach has been on you to be aggressive. You take 16 shots tonight, you have a career-high 34 points. What was the defense giving you?"

**Jaden McDaniels:** "Really just I don't know, I just out there flowing tonight, taking what they give me. If they give me a lane to go right, go right, and if they close short, shoot the three. It's just all the work that I put in, to just keep trusting myself."

**2. Jaden McDaniels — "We all know we're super versatile as a team and we all know we could hoop." — Timberwolves' team versatility and performance without Anthony Edwards** [00:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fI6kwPgbuNo&t=18s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "You guys are now 10 and 4 since losing **Anthony Edwards** to injury. What's it been like to see some of these other guys, whether it's **Kyle Anderson**, whether it's **Terrence Shannon**, **Jonathan Kuminga** step up and play much bigger roles?"

**Jaden McDaniels:** "It's good to see. We all know we're super versatile as a team and we all know we could hoop. It could be anybody's night each game and that's how it's been since Ant's been out. We've been taking care of business and we're just ready for our brother to get back."

**3. Jaden McDaniels — "Really just staying on him no matter where he's at on the floor." — Defensive strategy to limit Michael Porter Jr.** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fI6kwPgbuNo&t=41s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "**Michael Porter Jr.** such a big part of what Brooklyn does. He only takes seven shots, you limit him to 11 points. How'd you guys slow him down?"

**Jaden McDaniels:** "Really just staying on him no matter where he's at on the floor. He's a lethal shooter, he shoots if you're riding his face, and then just denying him, making it tough for him, and that was the outcome."
