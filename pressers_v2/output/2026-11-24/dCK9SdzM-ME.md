# Coach Daigneault on OKC's Compete Level | Postgame Media | November 23, 2026 — *Oklahoma City Thunder*

_Speakers identified: Mark Daigneault_

**1. Mark Daigneault — "defensively we left something to be desired tonight. Obviously our margins are a little thin with our size" — Mark Daigneault on OKC's defensive shortcomings and thin margins** [00:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dCK9SdzM-ME&t=10s)

Mark Daigneault: "I thought obviously offensively we were pretty good tonight. 120 points is enough to win anywhere, home or road. Defensively we left something to be desired tonight. Obviously our margins are a little thin with our size, but that's a gift. If you want to get better, it really puts the spotlight on the need to be really tight on multiple possessions and they put a lot of pressure on you offensively obviously with the pace and the pace in the halfcourt. So we were behind a lot of plays where we could have been better, but I thought we really competed."

**2. Mark Daigneault — "we're playing small and the margins on that are thinner, especially when you're going against size" — Mark Daigneault on the challenges of playing small ball** [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dCK9SdzM-ME&t=70s)

Mark Daigneault: "We're playing small and the margins on that are thinner, especially when you're going against size. I thought that showed up last night and tonight. So some of it is structural despite the effort, but it just puts that much more of an emphasis on the need to execute. And like I said, it's a gift. These games are tough. We're giving ourselves a chance in some of these closer games where a couple plays away and that puts the spotlight on every single detail, every single possession and that's an opportunity for us to look in the mirror and improve because obviously it stings when you lose."

**4. Mark Daigneault — "I don't think any of these games have really been a lack of effort" — Mark Daigneault on the team's consistent competitive effort** [03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dCK9SdzM-ME&t=182s)

Mark Daigneault: "I don't think any of these games have really been a lack of effort. I don't think it's like we're not showing up or not wanting to win or laying it on the line. Guys are gutting it out and putting their best foot forward from a competitive standpoint and we never take that for granted. Win or lose, we want to be a team that competes together every night and I think we're doing that. We just have some guys out and we're having to scramble on that and it's putting a huge emphasis on our ability to execute. We're probably three or four plays away tonight from being able to come out of here with a win."
