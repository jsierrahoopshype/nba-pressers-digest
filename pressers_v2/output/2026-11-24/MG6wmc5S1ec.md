# Ayo Dosunmu Postgame Interview With Alan Horton | 11.23.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MG6wmc5S1ec](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MG6wmc5S1ec)

_Speakers identified: Alan Horton, Ayo Dosunmu_

**1. Ayo Dosunmu — "We have a bunch of different weapons, a bunch of different guys that can make plays." — Ayo Dosunmu on the Timberwolves' offensive aggression and team depth** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MG6wmc5S1ec&t=16s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "The defensive effort wasn't quite there, but it didn't need to be tonight because your offense was really flowing. What was the key to the offense tonight and guys just knocking down shots all over?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "Just being aggressive. I think that's the main thing. We have a bunch of different weapons, a bunch of different guys that can make plays, so we were able to do that. **Jaden McDaniels** was able to get to his spots. He led us tonight in scoring, and it was fascinating to watch."

**2. Ayo Dosunmu — "He's playing aggressive, he's playing efficient. He's seeing the ball go in." — Ayo Dosunmu on Jaden McDaniels' career-high performance and efficiency** [00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MG6wmc5S1ec&t=37s)

**Alan Horton:** "A career high for **Jaden McDaniels**, 34 points, 10 rebounds, 6 assists, 12 of 16 shooting. Is this a glimpse of what Jaden is? He's just gotten better and better every year he's been in the league. You joined him last season, but the world's at his fingertips, right?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "For sure. He's playing aggressive, he's playing efficient. He's seeing the ball go in, he knows he's getting his shots set throughout the offense, and he's an efficient basketball player. I always admire anybody who's efficient, anybody who can get their game off without taking a bunch of shots. So when his volume goes up, he's able to showcase what he did tonight."

**3. Ayo Dosunmu — "That was very important. That was something that we've been watching, something that we've been trying to continue." — Ayo Dosunmu on the importance of team ball movement** [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MG6wmc5S1ec&t=73s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "How do you feel about 35 assists as a team tonight moving the basketball?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "That was very important. That was something that we've been watching, something that we've been trying to continue to get better at, and we showcased it tonight. We finished the road trip off at 6-2, so that's encouraging, and we got to just keep going and get ready for OKC."

**4. Ayo Dosunmu — "Anytime you have someone come back to the team, you always show love and wish them the best." — Ayo Dosunmu on facing former teammate Julius Randle** [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MG6wmc5S1ec&t=91s)

**Alan Horton:** "How was it going against **Julius Randle**? You played with him obviously last season, not the full time he was here, but for a lot of the guys he was a real great teammate for two years."

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "It was important. We got the win, of course, but shout out to Julius. He battled last year with me and when I wasn't here, he had some great playoff performances here. Anytime you have someone come back to the team, you always show love and wish them the best and hope he continues to stay healthy and keep balling."
