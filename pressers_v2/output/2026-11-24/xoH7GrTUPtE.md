# Philadelphia 76ers Postgame Media Availability 🎙️ | Coach Nurse | 11.23.26 — *Philadelphia 76ers*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoH7GrTUPtE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoH7GrTUPtE)

_Speakers identified: Nick Nurse_

**1. Nick Nurse — "It's hard to lose by 10 on that particular stat. 10 makes, that's 30 points." — Coach Nurse on the 76ers' defensive struggles and three-point shooting** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoH7GrTUPtE&t=0s)

Nick Nurse: "First of all, they outplayed us. Their energy and hustle in general was excellent throughout the game. But the big thing is the three-point shooting. It's hard to lose by 10 on that particular stat. 10 makes, that's 30 points, that's hard to overcome unless you're going to beat them at the free-throw line extensively or win the turnover battle extensively. Possession battles were pretty even, and then they make 10 more threes than you. It's too bad, because I think we went 0-for-4 with **Jabari Smith Jr.**, Dean, and **Kentavious Caldwell-Pope**, and other than one of **Kentavious Caldwell-Pope**'s, they were all great shots. Maybe one of **Kentavious Caldwell-Pope**'s he had to arc it a little higher over Clingan coming out at him, but other than that, I think they were really good shots."

**3. Nick Nurse — "Your rim protection and inability to do that with some size is always a focal point." — Defensive rim protection and committee rebounding issues** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoH7GrTUPtE&t=143s)

Nick Nurse: "Your rim protection and inability to do that with some size is always a focal point of being really good defensively. Then you have to do it a little bit more by committee. I thought for the most part, I'm looking at possessions where I liked our help into the paint, they were doing a good job of zinging it out. I didn't particularly like our back out to get to them. There was a couple times they missed those and still got rebounds. We were compacted in the lane with everybody since we didn't get back out, and then giving up an offensive rebound as well. That's just playing through the possession, a little bit of energy and all that kind of stuff. I know it's a battle in there when you got a big guy in there to try to use a couple bodies on it, but we just didn't do enough pieces well."
