# Jonathan Kuminga Postgame Interview After Win Against Brooklyn Nets | 11.23.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

**1. "How can we come in and play as much defense as we can? That's going to help us" — Jonathan Kuminga on Minnesota's defensive intensity in the second half** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=adDmza75Rlc&t=0s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "JK, the defense was on display tonight. How powerful in the second half were you guys able to turn it up after kind of allowing the Nets to stick around in that first half?"

**Jonathan Kuminga:** "I think early we fouled a lot. We went back in the locker room, we all spoke, and I think our mindset just going forward is how can we come in and play as much defense as we can? That's going to help us translate in offense, and I think we were all able to talk to each other all the time about defense and defensive assignment, and I think that's been our goal every time."
