**5. Lionel Hollins — "I yell, but also hug. I also talk." — Lionel Hollins on his coaching style and communication** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rq-x5pt3Nbo&t=248s)

Lionel Hollins: "I yell, but also hug. I also talk. You and Zach were going through the jogging up and down at start of practice and I just said, yo, is that the fastest you can do that? Zach said, nah. And so y'all started running. I didn't yell at him and I didn't yell at you because if I yell, y'all would be all over me. But what I told Zach one time, he may not remember, I said, Zach, I know there's days you don't want to practice. I don't know why, but I know there's days that you don't want to practice. And I said, if you don't want to practice, just tell me. Because Z-Bo was way up here. I could give Z-Bo a day off and not feel bad about it. And so, I said, but if you practice, you got to practice hard. You don't mess up my practice."

**6. Lionel Hollins — "All the stars were tough. They were demanding of you and of themselves." — Lionel Hollins on coaching star players with high standards** [05:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rq-x5pt3Nbo&t=345s)

Lionel Hollins: "All the stars were tough. They were demanding of you and of themselves. So, you know, they're hard. They want something and they really believe in it. It's like now all the guys that want you to go and review something. You know blaten well that they nobody touched you. You just fell on the floor and rolled over. Now you want us to review. Man, get up off the floor. But you know, sometimes you got to be open and I learned to be, all my coaches, and Jack Ramsey had a lot of influence on me even after I quit playing. And he said, be on time, run practices and stop them on time, start them on time, end them on time. And he says, fundamentals first, and he says, demand high standards. And that's what it's about."
