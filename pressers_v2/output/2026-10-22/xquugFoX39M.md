**3. Isaiah Hartenstein — "It is not just finding a guy that can shoot. You have to be able to create." — Isaiah Hartenstein on the evolution of NBA frontcourt skill sets** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xquugFoX39M&t=96s)

Isaiah Hartenstein: "It is getting more skilled. Now it is getting back to where it is not just finding a guy that can shoot. You have to be able to play inside, you have to be able to create action. We are getting back to playing more double bigs. You have to be big, but you have to be skilled."

**4. Isaiah Hartenstein — "We were trying to copy the small ball from Golden State where it really wasn't small ball." — Isaiah Hartenstein on the reality of Golden State's small-ball era** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xquugFoX39M&t=120s)

Isaiah Hartenstein: "We were trying to copy the small ball from Golden State where it really wasn't small ball. You had **Kevin Durant** that was 6'11, you had **Draymond Green** that was 6'9, **Andre Iguodala**. Now we are just getting back to you have to be big, but you have to be skilled."

**5. Isaiah Hartenstein — "You cannot just play two big guys that cannot move at all or cannot create action." — Isaiah Hartenstein on the necessity of versatility in double-big lineups** [04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xquugFoX39M&t=269s)

Isaiah Hartenstein: "You have to be able to do multiple things. You cannot just play two big guys that cannot move at all or cannot create action. That is what makes the stuff look clunky, so you have to have a mixture. All of our bigs can do a lot of different things. If it goes from Chet, **Jalen Williams**, I, me, **Kevin Durant** was not big no more, he is a guard, but back in the day, **Kevin Durant** could do a lot of different things too."
