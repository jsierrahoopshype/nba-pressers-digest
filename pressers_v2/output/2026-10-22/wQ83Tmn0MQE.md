Rick Carlisle: "It is great to win. Our guys did a great job in the second half of being combative and fighting, and fighting a lot harder than we did in the first half, particularly the second quarter, which was well below standard from the standpoint of intensity and passion to compete. **5. "I didn't think I was going to cry, you know, I I really did it." — Tyrese Haliburton on the emotional significance of his return to play** [05:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wQ83Tmn0MQE&t=313s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Tyrese, we could see you almost on the verge of tears in your postgame chat with **Jeremiah**. What did this all mean to you and how did it affect everyone?"

I thought that this moment would just feel like super emotional for me and I didn't think I was going to cry, you know, I I really did it. But I think it all just hit me kind of there when talking to **Jeremiah** and, you know, seeing all those like Pacer fans in the arena. We haven't won here in five years or whatever. And to see all those Pacer fans here in New Orleans for this means a lot to me and this organization, this city means a lot. It just means a lot for me to, you know, be back doing what I love."

Tyrese Haliburton: "We got a lot to build on. We got a lot. I thought in the first half we were sloppy. I turned the ball over a lot.
