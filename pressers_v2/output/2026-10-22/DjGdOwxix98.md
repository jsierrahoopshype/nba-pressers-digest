**1. Charles Lee — "we need to be able to do this thing by committee. We need everybody." — Charles Lee on team performance without Nick Richards and DaQuan Jeffries** [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DjGdOwxix98&t=74s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "How do you feel about how your team is considering that you won this game without **Nick Richards** and **DaQuan Jeffries**?"

**Charles Lee:** "All training camp, all preseason, we've talked about just being able to do this thing by committee. We need everybody. We need all hands on deck and unfortunately, if someone is injured, the next guy has to step up and be ready to impact the game. Because these guys all work so hard and they're obsessed with their daily improvement, I know that they have earned the trust of myself and the coaching staff. So I feel very comfortable whenever we go into a game, healthy or injured, because I know we got a ton of really good bodies on this team to play."

**2. Charles Lee — "I was really impressed with Naz's activity. I also thought that he really tried to help us." — Charles Lee evaluates Naz Reid's performance against Julius Randle** [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DjGdOwxix98&t=113s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "And on that note, **Naz Reid** being on the team, how did you feel?"

**Charles Lee:** "I loved it. Number one, taking on a matchup versus **Julius Randle** to start the season, put him in a couple different coverages. I thought that he executed them well. I thought there were some really good possessions where his length, he's physical, he's showing his hands, he's making Randle have to hit a tough shot. The process was good, a couple times he made it, and a couple times he missed it. So overall, I was really impressed with Naz's activity. I also thought that he really tried to help us on the boards with nine defensive rebounds."

**3. Charles Lee — "he understands what the situation needs. He's not afraid to speak up and say what needs to be said." — Charles Lee on Dennis Smith Jr.'s leadership and communication** [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DjGdOwxix98&t=149s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "There were some kind of misunderstandings in first position and it felt like the guys didn't 100% know where to stand and then Dennis was coming and what he always does, bringing the guys together, very vocal. Is it something that you also communicated with him that he has like the allowance of me thinking of his leadership and seeing how he brings the guys already together and being so vocal here on the on the court?"

**Charles Lee:** "Some of it is definitely us empowering him to do it, but a lot of it, to be honest, is just that's just who Dennis is. I think that he understands what the situation needs. He's not afraid to speak up and say what needs to be said and I think that he's earned the respect of the coaching staff as well as his teammates. When he speaks, I think everyone's listening and you got to respect the guy's competitive spirit that he has, but also the basketball IQ that he has. So it's always from a really good place. It's trying to not only help his teammate be better, but the team be better and the team to win the game. He's an extension of the coaching staff, which I love because a lot of times he's saying the right messages as well."
