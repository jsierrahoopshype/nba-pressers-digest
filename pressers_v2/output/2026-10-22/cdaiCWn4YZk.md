# Anthony Davis on his DEBUT with the Washington Wizards | 10.21.26 Media Availability — *Washington Wizards*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdaiCWn4YZk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdaiCWn4YZk)

_Speakers identified: Anthony Davis_

**1. Anthony Davis — "having that dialogue through the course of the game is really good for us." — Anthony Davis on the team's chemistry and communication** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdaiCWn4YZk&t=94s)

Anthony Davis: "It was fun. But that's a good sign of a good team that no matter who the star players are, if you're number one on the team, you're number 15, we're able to go to each other and have those type of moments and that realness of, 'Hey, you're not doing this, you're not doing that,' and it kind of sparks us. Even before the third quarter, I think we came out and was a little flat, a lot of missed shots and called timeout and Bub came to me tonight and was like, 'You have one more gear, can I see it?' And then it just kind of happens. So, having that dialogue through the course of the game is really good for us."

**2. Anthony Davis — "as long as we can get it up the floor and get into our offense, doesn't matter." — Anthony Davis on the Wizards' committee approach to ball-handling** [03:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdaiCWn4YZk&t=237s)

Anthony Davis: "It doesn't matter. As long as we can get it up the floor and get into our offense, doesn't matter who we bring it up. It's obviously a luxury to have six, maybe seven guys on our team that can handle the ball and bring it up the floor. We know they're going to try to pressure **Trey**, and there's **Alex**, myself, **Keeshawn**, where he can get off the ball and kick it to us and we can kind of bring it up. It's a luxury to have that, but as long as we can get the ball up in a respectable amount of time and get into our offense, it doesn't matter who brings it up."
