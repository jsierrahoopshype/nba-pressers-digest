# Shooters Shoot: C.J. Miles at Jazz Media Day | ALL ACCESS — *Utah Jazz*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u3nNuDGedfQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u3nNuDGedfQ)

_Speakers identified: C.J. Miles, Keyonte George, Lauri Markkanen_

**1. C.J. Miles — "I bought my first camera while I was playing in Utah, and I was just playing around with it." — C.J. Miles on his transition from NBA player to photographer** [00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u3nNuDGedfQ&t=25s)

C.J. Miles: "I bought my first camera while I was playing in Utah, and I was just playing around with it. Like most young people, I fell off, and then I just slowly started coming back to it. Next thing you know, I was full in it, carrying around a bag with three cameras in it. My big love for it though is just naturally, I am a storyteller. That is everything I do from poetry, music, writing with that. So that is what brought me back."

**2. C.J. Miles — "It was almost like being drafted again by the Jazz, right?" — C.J. Miles on his return to the Utah Jazz organization** [01:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u3nNuDGedfQ&t=115s)

C.J. Miles: "After I retired and started doing some other things around basketball and just figuring out the route of that, being able to do some broadcast and some things of that nature, and them being one of the people that really took me in, it was almost like being drafted again by the Jazz, right? Being able to start over and start this second journey with the team that I started everything with. The guys in the broadcasting studio, they have taken me in and taught me a lot of things, and being able to do basketball stuff around, my relation to Keyonte being here, and being able to be around and be a resource has been really fun for me."
