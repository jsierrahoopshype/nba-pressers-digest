# Coach Charles Lee Practice Media Availability | 10/18/26 — *Charlotte Hornets*

_Speakers identified: Charles Lee_

**1. Charles Lee — "I think that team is better than where we started. I think we made some progress." — Charles Lee on Charlotte Hornets' defensive progress during preseason** [00:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrGExZ92Y24&t=10s)

Charles Lee: "I think that team is better than where we started. I think we made some progress in a lot of areas, starting defensively. I think that now we have a lot of guys that are in the right positions, and then now that we're in those right positions, our technique is getting better. How are we just more effective, how are we more impactful, either on the ball or off the ball. And so I think that our guys did a really good job throughout preseason, defensive rebounding. We finished in that category really high. We finished in free throw attempt rate really high. So some of the fundamentals of how we play defense, I thought were pretty good and we got better again."

**2. Charles Lee — "No matter who is in the lineup, we have a certain way that we want to play." — Charles Lee on improving team execution and reducing turnovers** [01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrGExZ92Y24&t=68s)

Charles Lee: "No matter who is in the lineup, we have a certain way that we want to play. And so I think that just from our running habit standpoint, some of our half-court execution, we got to clean that up. I think that if we do those things, we'll put ourselves in better position to create better shots for one another. I think that, you know, some of the turnovers will go down when we're not playing as slow, we're not playing in crowds, we don't have some of the casual turnovers."

**4. Charles Lee — "Love the growth that Christian made. I think just like the rest of our team." — Charles Lee praises Christian Anderson's growth and defensive impact** [02:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrGExZ92Y24&t=129s)

Charles Lee: "Love the growth that Christian made. I think just like the rest of our team, with every rep you got better and better. He finds his confidence. I think I love the the defensive impact he had. There are some possessions where he is picking up full court, refusing to be screened in pick and roll situations. I thought his hands were active, coming in and got a couple big rebounds. Like in Boston, he got fouled on one and hit the floor. Offensively, I think that he understands the pace at which we want to play and some of his outlets, he's getting up the court, playing early pick and roll. It's good to see the German connection in effect with him and **Hannis** and he's hitting them in the seam. I think he's finding where to shoot the ball and how to be more shot ready on the perimeter."
