**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "I never have that feeling that anybody is worried about their stats or who is scoring." — Tuomas Iisalo on Grizzlies' bench depth and team commitment** [00:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuppjPFzyOg&t=10s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "I think it speaks to the quality of our guys and their commitment to playing together. I never have that feeling that anybody is worried about their stats or who is scoring. **3. Tuomas Iisalo — "We made a commitment when the season ended that we are never going to be in that situation." — Tuomas Iisalo on the importance of the team's undefeated start** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuppjPFzyOg&t=216s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "It means everything. You know this, we have talked about it that nobody wants to be in that situation again. **5. Tuomas Iisalo — "He finds ways to impact the game without making shots. It is a very robust way." — Tuomas Iisalo on Chris Murray's impact beyond scoring** [07:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuppjPFzyOg&t=422s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "It is the second time already. I think we had this in the preseason also against Charlotte, similar type of game where he finds ways to impact the game without making shots. It is a very robust way of impacting the game when you know this guy does not need to hit one shot in order to help us win. In this type of situations, we wanted to stagger his minutes along with Jalen's, so we had all the time one taller point of attack defender on the floor, whether on Ayo or LaVine, and he did a great job on both guys."

**6. Tuomas Iisalo — "We know things can change in a hurry and the only thing we can affect is this moment." — Tuomas Iisalo on maintaining focus despite the 6-0 start** [08:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuppjPFzyOg&t=522s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "We got what is it, 76 games left? So we know things can change in a hurry and the only thing we can affect is this moment. It is a curious balance because at the same time we want to enjoy what we are doing. We have great chemistry I feel like top to bottom with the players and also within the staff and we also want to enjoy it in the moment, but at the same time keep our feet on the floor and make sure that we are fully locked in for each of the games."
