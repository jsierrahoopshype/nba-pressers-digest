**5. Taylor Jenkins — "we got to develop the chemistry with him as a roller and sometimes it's not to score" — Taylor Jenkins on Andre Jackson Jr.'s development and role** [04:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HRs9U_HwxYg&t=296s)

Taylor Jenkins: "He's still learning our system, a guy when he runs the floor, more consistency there. I think he can be a threat there. His pick-and-roll game, we got to develop the chemistry with him as a roller and sometimes it's not to score, it's just to open up the second-side opportunities on kick-outs for threes or second-side drives.
