**3. Brayden Burries — "The coaches do a great job of scout with the scouting report and then my own personal," — Brayden Burries on film study and defensive preparation** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUs8gsCiS9I&t=85s)

Brayden Burries: "The coaches do a great job of scout with the scouting report and then my own personal, I just watch them, see which way they like to go, is it right or left. On the offensive side for us and the defensive side for them, how are they guarding us, are they pushing on the sides with icing, going under the screens, over screens.
