# Zion Williamson | Pelicans vs Hawks Postgame Interview 10/31/26 — *New Orleans Pelicans*

_Speakers identified: Zion Williamson_

**1. Zion Williamson — "They just push me to be great on that end, so it doesn't feel like that." — Zion Williamson credits coaching staff for his defensive improvement** [00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8rpQlwLyPLU&t=54s)

Zion Williamson: "It doesn't even feel like that when I'm out there. I got to give credit to Coach Mose, Coach Eyes, and **Herb Jones**. We speak on **Herb Jones** a lot. Every game, they tell me what they kind of expect to see from me defensively and where they want me to be physical at. They just push me to be great on that end, so it doesn't feel like that when I'm out there, but I guess the results are starting to show a little bit."

**4. Zion Williamson — "CJ was a really great vet for me. From the moment me and him got to sit down." — Zion Williamson reflects on his relationship with CJ McCollum** [04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8rpQlwLyPLU&t=292s)

Zion Williamson: "CJ was a really great vet for me. From the moment me and him got to sit down, because when he came to the team, I was dealing with my broken foot and I was out that whole year. I was in Portland at the time and I was away from the team, but when I came back, him and Larry Nance, we would go out and get food and we would talk about what it looks like to be a pro, to be one of the greats. He would always kind of hold me to that standard. It was definitely good to see my brother."
