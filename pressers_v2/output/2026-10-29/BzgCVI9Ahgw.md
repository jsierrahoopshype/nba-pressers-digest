**5. Jamahl Mosley — "I loved the size, I loved the the versatility of both of them." — Jamahl Mosley on the versatility of playing two centers** [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BzgCVI9Ahgw&t=400s)

Jamahl Mosley: "I loved it. I loved the size, I loved the the versatility of both of them. Obviously **DQ** being a connector, a guy that can make plays, make passes, connect guys, **Carlo** being able to stretch the floor the way he does, but then there's a sense of physicality, finishing possessions out that you have with two bigs on the floor. And I think that's going to be big and we'll look at it throughout some games because you have again, you have two versatile bigs that can make sure they can make plays for one another, but then they stretch the floor so they give more space to get downhill and attack the basket without a crowd."
