**1. Nick Nurse — "I thought the last three minutes of the first half weren't a bunch of great possessions." — Nick Nurse on the Sixers' inconsistent first-half performance** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KT4XolYi_w0&t=9s)

Nick Nurse: "We did have a 10-point lead at halftime, but I thought the last three minutes of the first half weren't a bunch of great possessions. After we'd strung a bunch up and got a nice lead, I just thought we had three or four in a row that were just not solid enough, and that kind of carried its way into the second half. It gave them a little bit of momentum that they brought out of halftime with."

**2. Nick Nurse — "I don't think he was having much of a game, I don't think he was having much fun." — Nick Nurse on Tyrese Maxey's determination and late-game performance** [01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KT4XolYi_w0&t=80s)

Nick Nurse: "Just sheer determination. I don't think he was having much of a game, I don't think he was having much fun out there. He was getting knocked around a lot. I think he shot one free throw. He just caught fire, obviously. He got down the lane, made a couple, and I think that got him going. And then he got himself open and free for some looks. He didn't get very many good looks until the fourth. They were really picking him up, they were really physical, they were really putting two on him a lot on the screen and rolls and things. So, it was good to see him stay with it because it's not that easy sometimes when you think, well, maybe it just isn't my night, but he hung in there, I think both ends of the floor, and obviously hit some big shots down the stretch."
