# Hornets at Pistons: Coach Charles Lee Postgame Media Availability | 10/28/2026 — *Charlotte Hornets*

**1. Charles Lee — "they ratcheted up their defensive pressure a little bit, their physicality, and unfortunately had too many costly turnovers" — Charles Lee on Pistons' defensive pressure and Hornets' turnovers** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jb4E_7ranyU&t=0s)

**Shannon:** "Take me back to the start of the second half because that's really when the Pistons took control of this game. Where were some of the breakdowns that you saw?"

**Charles Lee:** "They ratcheted up their defensive pressure a little bit, their physicality, and unfortunately had too many costly turnovers or slow possessions. But I do love the overall fighting competitiveness of the group. To hold a good team like that to 97 points on the road shows the competitive spirit, the defensive intensity that we had. And then offensively, I thought that we just made one too many mistakes and they punished us for it."

**3. Charles Lee — "the pressure on Cunningham was really good. For us to hold him to 30 points" — Charles Lee on defensive effort against Cade Cunningham** [00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jb4E_7ranyU&t=49s)

**Shannon:** "When you look at it from start to finish, outside of just the fight, what else did you see?"

**Charles Lee:** "The pressure on Cunningham was really good. For us to hold him to 30 points, I thought that a lot of those buckets he had to work for and he was trying to dribble through our shifts.
