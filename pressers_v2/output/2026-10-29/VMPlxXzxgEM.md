# Hawks vs Heat Postgame Press Conference: Nickeil Alexander-Walker — *Atlanta Hawks*

**1. Nickeil Alexander-Walker — "we're showing that we can compete. Throughout the game, we have to do a better job of consistently competing." — Nickeil Alexander-Walker on Atlanta's second-half performance and energy** [00:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMPlxXzxgEM&t=14s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "I know you guys tried to make a few runs in the second half. What do you think was the biggest difference in this one?"

**Nickeil Alexander-Walker:** "I think it was just a little too late energy-wise. We're showing that we can compete. Throughout the game, we have to do a better job of consistently competing. There's times where our compete level drops, and that's what it was tonight."

**2. Nickeil Alexander-Walker — "It's just simple. Really simple. Not a lot of the actions and the O's. Just the small things." — Nickeil Alexander-Walker on the keys to bouncing back against Cleveland** [00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMPlxXzxgEM&t=48s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "You guys have a quick turnaround, of course, having the opportunity to pick up a win tomorrow night against Cleveland. What will be some things that will be essential to get back on the winning side of things?"

**Nickeil Alexander-Walker:** "It's just simple. Really simple. Not a lot of the actions and the O's. Just the small things. I think the first two games we really did that really well. Orlando made a run, we responded. How we acted in Charlotte shows the level of competition and the way that we competed at. We set a tone. Tonight, we set a tone throughout the game that gave us a fighting chance. We have to do that, and when we play to our strengths and play to ourselves, we end up giving ourselves a chance to win. I think we have a chance to win when we do those things."
