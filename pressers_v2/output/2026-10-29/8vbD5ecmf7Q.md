**2. Keyonte George — "we have to hang our hat on the defensive end" — Keyonte George on the Utah Jazz's defensive focus without key players** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8vbD5ecmf7Q&t=93s)

Keyonte George: "For us, we have to hang our hat on the defensive end. We have to. With the players that we have right now available, we can play fast, we can play hard, but we have to guard. So I think for us, we have to hang our hat on the defensive side. We have to play hard, we have to talk, communicate, deflections, defensive rebounding, crashing the glass on the offensive side. The small things we have to do for 48 minutes to put ourselves in the best position possible."
