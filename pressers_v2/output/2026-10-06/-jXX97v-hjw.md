# Hawks vs. Grizzlies Postgame Press Conference: Quin Snyder — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jXX97v-hjw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jXX97v-hjw)

_Speakers identified: Quin Snyder_

**1. Quin Snyder — "the possessions when we're collectively focused on passing the ball and playing together, we usually get good looks" — Quin Snyder on the importance of collective focus and ball movement** [00:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jXX97v-hjw&t=17s)

Quin Snyder: "Some of what we saw, we've learned before. We just have to have the right focus. The possessions when we're collectively focused on passing the ball and playing together, the results of that, while we don't always score, we usually get good looks. And when we don't do that, it becomes really hard to play defense. The inverse is also true. If we're not locked in mentally on the defensive end and we can't get stops, it becomes harder for us to score."

**3. Quin Snyder — "there's going to be challenges with rotations and coverages, but there's also things like communication" — Quin Snyder on the need for better communication and defensive focus** [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jXX97v-hjw&t=190s)

Quin Snyder: "Defensively, there's going to be challenges with rotations and coverages, but there's also things like communication. For us, collectively, I think we need to be focused on doing both well because of how intertwined they are. I don't want to give us a pass on either end of the floor. When we give up 49 points in a quarter, really, we have to know who we are. That's collectively defending and running, moving the ball, and scoring collectively as well."
