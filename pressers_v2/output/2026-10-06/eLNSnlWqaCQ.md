**2. Brandon Miller — "We feel like more teams are playing us more physical. When it comes down to the playoffs" — Brandon Miller on the importance of physicality in the NBA** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eLNSnlWqaCQ&t=32s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Another one of the focal points this season you guys have talked a lot about is physicality. How have you seen an increased attention to playing with more physicality so far in camp?"

**Brandon Miller:** "We feel like more teams are playing us more physical. When it comes down to the playoffs, play-in, the game is a lot more physical. So being prepared for physicality like that playoff environment is always good for us early on in the season. We still have to keep the mindset of being smart while we're playing physical. Physicality can go a long way. That's one thing that hurt us last year, but I think we'll be more prepared this year for a lot more physicality."

**Unidentified speaker:** "How much of playing with physicality is a mental approach?"

**Unidentified speaker:** "Preseason is obviously big for veterans to knock off some of the rust for the regular season starters, but it's a time for younger players to salvage themselves in the rotation. Is there anybody you're excited to watch tonight specifically?"

**5. Brandon Miller — "I think just leading more vocally. My voice is being heard around the building here." — Brandon Miller on his growing leadership role with the Hornets** [02:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eLNSnlWqaCQ&t=139s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Matt talked about veterans. You said during media day that you're kind of one of the older guys on the team now. How do you feel kind of taking that leadership step? What's that jump like going from year three to the fourth season?"

**Brandon Miller:** "I think just leading more vocally. My voice is being heard around the building here. Carrying that out as one of the leaders here is good for the team and this 82-game season. Just building bonds early. That's one thing that I've always preached on is bonds and trust. One thing that comes along with this 82-game season is building trust as the season goes on and I think it's going to get stronger as the day goes on."
