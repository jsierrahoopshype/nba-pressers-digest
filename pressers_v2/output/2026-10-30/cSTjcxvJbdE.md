# Hawks vs. Cavaliers Postgame Press Conference: Head Coach Quin Snyder — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSTjcxvJbdE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSTjcxvJbdE)

_Speakers identified: Quin Snyder_

**1. Quin Snyder — "Jalen Johnson is becoming more and more comfortable on the court." — Quin Snyder on Jalen Johnson's development and comfort on the court** [01:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSTjcxvJbdE&t=61s)

Quin Snyder: "One of the things, the first time you go through the league, you end up playing **James Harden** and **Donovan Mitchell**. Just the way he competes, I think that's something. He's got people around him, his teammates, that support him and have confidence in him, **CJ** in particular. You see those two talking, and I think Jalen is an intelligent player. He's an intelligent person and he's a really smart player too. So his learning curve, we're just going to keep having these experiences and it's good to see him. He's so fast, sometimes him learning to slow down a little bit because he's got the ability to make all kinds of passes and those reads, I think as he plays more, that's a gradual organic process that occurs."

**2. Quin Snyder — "He's letting the game come to him." — Quin Snyder on Aaron Holiday's impact and poise off the bench** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSTjcxvJbdE&t=135s)

**3. Quin Snyder — "We need those guys to keep shooting." — Quin Snyder on Onyeka Okongwu's offensive versatility and spacing** [02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSTjcxvJbdE&t=176s)

Quin Snyder: "It's a big force. The biggest thing, both he and Jalen, but obviously Onyeka, we need those guys to keep shooting. Sometimes you can be discouraged a little bit by a result, particularly if you're a good shooter and you're used to seeing the ball go in, and it affects other parts of your game. We had a few guys tonight I thought that really battled through some of that. It's been a big push to get him to feel comfortable shooting against closeouts, and when he does that, he also has the ability to put the ball on the floor."

**4. Quin Snyder — "It's good they're in there together, they really feed off each other." — Quin Snyder on Aaron Holiday and Onyeka Okongwu's chemistry** [04:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSTjcxvJbdE&t=295s)

Quin Snyder: "They're both capable coming off screening actions, playing off dribble handoffs, playing a little pick and roll, and it's been a real point of emphasis for both those guys when they're able to create advantages to have an awareness about what else is going on on the floor and to make those reads. Both of them tonight did a great job of making good decisions. Sometimes when you're in there, you don't want to be less aggressive. They were trapping Onyeka on handoffs and situations, and that happened the other night in pick and roll and we didn't read it as well as we needed to. To me, that's a big deal for Onyeka to be able to adjust to different coverages, whether they're top-locking a screen, and to your point, Aaron is able to do some of the same things. It's good they're in there together, they really feed off each other."
