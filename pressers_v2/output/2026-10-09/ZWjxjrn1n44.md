# Jamahl Mosley | Pelicans at Heat Postgame Interview 10/8/26 — *New Orleans Pelicans*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWjxjrn1n44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWjxjrn1n44)

_Speakers identified: Jamahl Mosley_

**1. Jamahl Mosley — "I think we have to continue to understand as a unit and as a group that we have to do it together." — Jamahl Mosley on the team's selfish play in the first half** [00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWjxjrn1n44&t=7s)

Jamahl Mosley: "Selfish play, which led to them running out and getting easy transition baskets. I think we have to continue to understand as a unit and as a group that we have to do it together. That's the style of play we need to have on both sides of the ball. And I think when we got down, each one of us wanted to chase it, to think that we could get it on our own. But in situations like that, you have to have a level of poise about you to know that that ball has got to move from side to side and find the right matchup and then share it and trust the pass."

**2. Jamahl Mosley — "Jordan Poole's been working his tail off defensively. He's doing everything that we've asked him to do." — Jamahl Mosley praises Jordan Poole's defensive development and effort** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWjxjrn1n44&t=97s)

Jamahl Mosley: "**Jordan Poole**'s been working his tail off defensively. He's doing everything that we've asked him to do, both offensively and defensively. Being a playmaker, being poised, being aggressive to score, which he knows he's capable of doing, but also being efficient in that. **3. Jamahl Mosley — "You have to give Miami a ton of credit. The way that they speed you up." — Jamahl Mosley on the team's perimeter shooting and Miami's defensive pressure** [02:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWjxjrn1n44&t=141s)

Jamahl Mosley: "You have to give Miami a ton of credit. The way that they speed you up, grabbing, holding, which that's their style of basketball. And so us to be able to get some shots up cleanly says something about our guys being able to do that. Now we just got to make sure we continue to hit home on the way we can get them, touching the paint to spray it. And that's how some of those turnovers happened. We got in the crowd and tried to make the play in the interior versus it was the perimeter pass that was available."
