# Hawks vs. Wizards Postgame Press Conference: Head Coach Quin Snyder — *Atlanta Hawks*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bi9Vo4OTgvw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bi9Vo4OTgvw)

_Speakers identified: Quin Snyder_

**1. Quin Snyder — "to be able to come into the game and play with that competitive fire" — Quin Snyder on the early impact of rookies Kobe Bufkin and Mouhamed Gueye** [02:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bi9Vo4OTgvw&t=179s)

Quin Snyder: "I have said from the beginning of the year, those guys and like our team, because we have got a fair amount of new players and some really young players and some players that are young, not really young. Those two guys are rookies. So, to be able to come into the game and play with that competitive fire that they have and just really impact the game in tangible ways, I think that is as much as anything a lift that they give the other guys."

**4. Quin Snyder — "we talked about finding a mismatch versus targeting a mismatch" — Quin Snyder on using motion to attack defensive mismatches** [07:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bi9Vo4OTgvw&t=475s)

Quin Snyder: "We talked about finding a mismatch versus targeting a mismatch, and it takes recognition during after timeout dead ball situations. I think those are times you can target, whether it is a back screen or pick and roll situations where you force a switch and then have an additional action. But it is something for us, we will play pick and roll, certainly within what we do as well, but being able to cut and attack closeouts and gain advantages that way, your style of play in my mind is a path to gaining advantages offensively."
