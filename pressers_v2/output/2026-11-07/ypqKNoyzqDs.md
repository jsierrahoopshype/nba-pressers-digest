**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "the energy levels in the team were lower today" — Tuomas Iisalo on Grizzlies' lack of energy and offensive rebounding** [00:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypqKNoyzqDs&t=14s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "In the first half, they had zero offensive rebounds. We just couldn't buy a bucket, and they shot really well. Overall, if I talk about that, the energy levels in the team were lower today. We could talk about many reasons for that. We got a tomorrow lockout day, I think it's very well deserved for the guys, they've been working their butts off, and then we have a practice before the Toronto game. I wouldn't draw too many conclusions about the effort of the guys. I didn't think that was the issue."

**4. Tuomas Iisalo — "they are good. They do the basics at a very high level" — Tuomas Iisalo explains why the Thunder are difficult to game plan** [01:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypqKNoyzqDs&t=119s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "It's not that they're hard to game plan against, it's like they are good. They do the basics at a very high level. They are very well coached, they have clear role definition. **5. Tuomas Iisalo — "the idea is to put the players in a position to succeed" — Tuomas Iisalo on developing rookie Cam Boozer** [03:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypqKNoyzqDs&t=188s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "The idea is to put the players in a position to succeed. It's not like we have a bag of tricks here that's 17 more things that we want to do. He's a 19-year-old rookie that's working extremely hard and absorbing a lot of those things. A lot of it comes down to the matchups, who does he have, does he have the advantage on the outside or on the inside without stewing the lineup. He plays a lot at the five, they play the five against him, that five sometimes is waiting at the free throw line. There are different ways and we'll keep adding those, but yeah, we are featuring him in a lot of different sets."

**6. Tuomas Iisalo — "we've tried to figure out the rotation pattern for our second units" — Tuomas Iisalo on rotation patterns and bench usage** [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypqKNoyzqDs&t=236s)
