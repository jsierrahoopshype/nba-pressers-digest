# Ayo Dosunmu Postgame Interview After Cup Win Vs The New Orleans Pelicans | 11.6.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DzYFgyU2LMY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DzYFgyU2LMY)

_Speakers identified: Ayo Dosunmu, Meghan McPeak_

**1. Ayo Dosunmu — "We want to do whatever it takes to win a game." — Ayo Dosunmu on the Bulls' emphasis on paint scoring** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DzYFgyU2LMY&t=0s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "The threes weren't falling as a team tonight. What was the emphasis to get into the paint to try to give you guys some kickout opportunities?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "We want to do whatever it takes to win a game. I got me harping on all season, each and every game is a tough game and that's a damn good basketball team. They won four straight. This is a cup game, everything is on the line and we want to give it our all. We threw punches, they threw punches, and it was all about who can withstand the punch."

**2. Ayo Dosunmu — "He didn't get it going early, he battled through adversity." — Ayo Dosunmu praises Trey Lyles' performance after injury** [00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DzYFgyU2LMY&t=31s)

**Meghan McPeak:** "**Trey Lyles** comes in, steps up. What did he mean? He may not have hit big shots, but..."

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "He played a hell of a game, man. He didn't get it going early, he battled through adversity. I was telling him like, 'Hey man, don't worry about the shots falling. Each and every possession we going to need you.' He got a big tip dunk and then he got another big dunk, so he played great down the stretch and we won the game, so nothing else matters."
