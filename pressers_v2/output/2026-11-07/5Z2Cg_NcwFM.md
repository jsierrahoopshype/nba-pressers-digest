**4. Charles Lee — "one possession at a time" — Charles Lee's halftime message to the Hornets** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Z2Cg_NcwFM&t=120s)

Charles Lee: "One possession at a time. There is a lot of game left. We said that our standard, no matter what the final score was, we wanted to make sure that there is still a mark of competitiveness and physicality that was going to be given, playing the right way offensively. The group at the end I thought really fought and competed and picked up full court and got some deflections and made some things happen.
