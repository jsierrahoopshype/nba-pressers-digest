**3. Micah Nori — "We have too many good ball-handlers, too many good decision-makers even with Ja out to have this many turnovers." — Micah Nori on the value of having high turnover games on film** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HNc7N4PQiBI&t=85s)

Micah Nori: "We have too many good ball-handlers, too many good decision-makers even with **Ja** out to have this many turnovers. Some of them are just the interior passing and some of them are just the carelessness against a team that yes, they apply pretty good pressure, but you can use their pressure against them. What I mean by that is you just throw it to the pocket, you turn, you are usually playing with a man advantage on the backside and it just they sped us up."

**4. Micah Nori — "He has gotten really good at the step-back to the left hand." — Micah Nori on the defensive strategy for guarding Luka Doncic** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HNc7N4PQiBI&t=143s)

Micah Nori: "He has gotten really good at the step-back to the left hand.
