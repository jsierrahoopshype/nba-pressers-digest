**1. Nick Nurse — "he seemed to take a level of confidence or maturity jump" — Tyrese Maxey's growth in confidence and maturity** [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g-iriObBTUk&t=65s)

Nick Nurse: "**Tyrese Maxey** has been very impressive. I think the big thing is he seemed to take a level of confidence or maturity jump. It just felt like he feels so sure of what he can do, which is a big step. The way he is approaching things and the way he feels out there on the court, and again, the biggest thing is the growth over the couple years has been really noticeable on the offensive end, but it is equally as much on the defensive end. He is out there battling, and especially when it is time to really knuckle down and get a stop, he is out there really playing."

**6. Nick Nurse — "we were having a lot of trouble with the mid pick-and-roll coverage" — Adjusting to mid pick-and-roll coverage** [09:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g-iriObBTUk&t=592s)

Nick Nurse: "We were having a lot of trouble with the mid pick-and-roll coverage. So we were trying to do whatever we had to do to make them do something other than that. It is tough.
