I just wanted to be ready for it, and obviously, we got to do some better things on both sides of the ball to make sure we can squeeze out wins like that at the end."

**3. Tyler Herro — "It's about the little things, the small details that are going to help us squeeze out wins." — Tyler Herro on the importance of small details in close games** [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tac_h3VHCFA&t=125s)

Tyler Herro: "I think we're going to be in a lot of games all season. It's about the little things, the small details that are going to help us squeeze out wins. Games are going to be back and forth, teams are going to go on runs, we're going to go on runs. It's about being able to do the little things in the moments of truth, whether it's 50-50 balls, being able to not foul on defense. Every time we foul, they shoot free throws, and then we got to set our offense, they set their defense, so it becomes harder to score and create advantages. The more we can get stops at the end of games, run out in transition, I think we'll be better."
