**1. Corey Kispert — "plays like that allow us more chances to score and then it deflates the other team." — Corey Kispert on the importance of rebounding against the Pistons** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CS3FlGY1-Aw&t=6s)

When you are on the other end and there is rebound after rebound after rebound and a big three, that flattens the other team. Credit to those guys for getting on the glass and cleaning it up. Seven offensive rebounds for **Dyson**, six for Jaylen, three for **Lou**, two for **Zubi**, that stuff goes a long way."

**3. Corey Kispert — "being able to read whether to drive it, whether to shoot it, or pass it and move it." — Corey Kispert on refining his driving and shooting skills** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CS3FlGY1-Aw&t=96s)

Corey Kispert: "For me, that is the skill. That is what has made me an NBA player. Either shoot it or drive it. Obviously, shooting it has been my main thing, that is my main skill, that is my NBA-level skill. But it is really easy to guard somebody that cannot drive as well. So, being able to read whether to drive it, whether to shoot it, or pass it and move it, that is the skill that I have had to get really good at over the course of my career to stick around. When you can do both and finish at the rim and also shoot threes at a really high level, you become really, really hard to guard."

**5. Corey Kispert — "the way that you get threes against a really good defensive team like Detroit is off offensive rebounds." — Corey Kispert on finding open shots off offensive rebounds** [03:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CS3FlGY1-Aw&t=217s)

Corey Kispert: "The way that you get threes against a really good defensive team like Detroit is off offensive rebounds. It is really hard for them to track the ball, track it off the rim, and then go find me, **Aaron**, whoever, to go guard them. That is where you can get your most open looks. It is something about the ball coming to you from the basket, it makes it easier to shoot it because it is like practice, versus having to catch it coming sideways off of a pick-and-roll or off the dribble. It feels more like practice when you get it off of an offensive rebound and shoot. Statistically, those are the ones that go in the most, those are the highest value threes that we can get, so you definitely want to hunt them and shoot them when you get those chances."
