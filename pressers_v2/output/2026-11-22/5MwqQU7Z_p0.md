# Steve Kerr harps on Warriors’ defense to step up vs. Celtics | NBC Sports Bay Area — *Golden State Warriors*

**1. Steve Kerr — "There's a difference between an open shot and an open shot with rhythm." — Steve Kerr on the lack of rhythm in Warriors' offense** [00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MwqQU7Z_p0&t=31s)

Steve Kerr: "There's a difference between an open shot and an open shot with rhythm. We're getting some open shots, but I don't think there's a rhythm to them. So they're less likely to go in. If you get a paint touch, kick out, open three, that's what you're looking for. It doesn't feel like we're getting a ton of those. So ball pressure in the last couple games hurt us, so we worked hard yesterday in practice on some things trying to generate better rhythm to our offense."

Steve Kerr: "Defense. The offense is what it is and we can tweak things and shots sometimes go in, sometimes they don't. But when you've lost five in a row, you dig it out with defense and get stops and run. We've got to grind out a win here to get out of the rut."

Steve Kerr: "We like him down on the low block. He's got a natural instinct down there to score, create an advantage and we definitely want to see more of that. We're just trying to get him to understand that it's going to take some time and he just has to focus on the detail. I'm way more interested in watching his defense tonight than his offense. **6. Steve Kerr — "You tend to find rhythm the harder you play and the better you play defensively." — Steve Kerr on the importance of ball movement and team rhythm** [05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MwqQU7Z_p0&t=311s)

Steve Kerr: "You tend to find rhythm the harder you play and the better you play defensively. Then you gain an advantage in transition and then the ball moves a couple times, somebody makes a three and everybody feels good and the crowd is into it. So that's really what I'm looking for. We always look at strategic tweaks, things we can run to get a guy the ball in a certain spot, but offense is all about trying to gain an advantage, get a defensive rotation and we weren't gaining many advantages on the trip so the ball wasn't moving quite as well."
