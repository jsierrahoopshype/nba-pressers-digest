**4. Micah Nori — "We weren't necessarily making it a point of emphasis to shoot up to shoot 53s." — Micah Nori on team's offensive strategy and defensive pressure** [03:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GXXrpHsVKFM&t=225s)

Micah Nori: "They do a really good job because what they try to do, like a lot of teams, and we do the same things, try to play everything two-on-two, funnel it down to **Wemby** and then stay home on shooters and then make you have to take mid-range and bad shots over top of **Wemby**. But we weren't necessarily making it a point of emphasis to shoot up to shoot 53s, but we made a point of emphasis was with if you see **Wemby** in the paint and you don't have a straight line to finish, to find the kickout and make the right play."
