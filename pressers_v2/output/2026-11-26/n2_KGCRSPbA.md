# Ayo Dosunmu Postgame Interview With Alan Horton | 11.25.26 — *Minnesota Timberwolves*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n2_KGCRSPbA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n2_KGCRSPbA)

_Speakers identified: Alan Horton, Ayo Dosunmu_

**1. Ayo Dosunmu — "they are a championship team, so they have championship DNA and we just wanted to fight" — Ayo Dosunmu on the Timberwolves' championship DNA and competitive resilience** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n2_KGCRSPbA&t=15s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "This one was hard fought, was it not? What a wild, how do you describe the swings in this game?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "It was a tough game. We knew that they were going to make runs. They are a championship team, so they have championship DNA and we just wanted to fight through their runs and just keep competing and keep staying together."

**2. Ayo Dosunmu — "we let their non-shamen, if they won their non-shamen in the first half, we were up 10" — Ayo Dosunmu on the Timberwolves' second unit impact and team response** [00:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n2_KGCRSPbA&t=35s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "It felt like you guys were in a really good rhythm in that first quarter, and then it all kind of went away. How did you guys end up getting it back, like midway through the third?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "We let their non-shamen, if they won their non-shamen in the first half, we were up 10 and their second unit really changed the game for them. So we wanted to harp on that for our team in the second half and do the same thing and we made a great run in the third quarter, we were able to finish the game out in the fourth."

**3. Ayo Dosunmu — "I always been ready for anything and you know that's my mindset just being able to adapt" — Ayo Dosunmu on stepping up as primary ball-handler after LaMelo Ball's injury** [01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n2_KGCRSPbA&t=90s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Were you ready when LaMelo went down with the ankle to be the primary ball-handler?"

**Ayo Dosunmu:** "For sure. I mean, you know, I always been ready for anything and you know that's my mindset just being able to adapt to any situation. I'm praying for LaMelo, hopefully it's nothing too serious and we got the win for him."
