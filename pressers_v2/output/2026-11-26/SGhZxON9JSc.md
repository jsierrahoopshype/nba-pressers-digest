# De’Anthony Melton recaps big night in Warriors’ win vs. Kings | NBC Sports BA — *Golden State Warriors*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc)

**1. De’Anthony Melton — "He do so many great things on the court and especially in transition and his speed" — Brandon Williams' impact on the Warriors' bench rotation** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=38s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "**Brandon Williams** made his debut tonight, coming back from his injury. How good is it for your bench to have that boost coming in a game?"

**De’Anthony Melton:** "He is my dog right there. He do so many great things on the court and especially in transition and his speed and quickness to get to the rim. I don't think there is too many people really stopping him getting there. It is just about making layups and he made seven of them today, so he is just exceptional."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=38s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=38s)

**2. De’Anthony Melton — "We got to protect home court no matter what, so I think we are doing a great job" — Warriors' philosophy on protecting home court after road trips** [01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=63s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "Two games back-to-back at home, two wins. What has been the philosophy coming back home after a tough road trip?"

**De’Anthony Melton:** "This little skid we had, we felt like we were getting punched in the mouth, so we wanted to come out here and start punching first and get more discipline on both sides of the ball. We got to protect home court no matter what, so I think we are doing a great job of that. Now it is just about continuing this run and keeping it going."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=63s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=63s)

**3. De’Anthony Melton — "It seems like much better shot, high-quality shots you guys are getting" — Warriors' focus on ball movement and high-quality shot selection** [01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=87s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "You guys get 37 assists on 49 buckets and 20 made threes as a team. Talk about how many catch-and-shoot threes you guys get up, because that tells me the ball is moving, they are in rhythm, and it seems like much better shot, high-quality shots you guys are getting."

**De’Anthony Melton:** "We tweaked a couple things that people were kind of scouting on us, so we changed a couple things and also we were just sticking to our game plan and just making sure the ball was moving as long as our bodies. We are trying to keep the defense honest and just keep them in misdirection."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=87s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=87s)

**4. De’Anthony Melton — "When he is in the game, he is going to do so many great things for us" — Adjusting to non-Stephen Curry minutes in the rotation** [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=126s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "A big conversation has been the non-**Stephen Curry** minutes. I think personally that is a chance for you guys to work on those non-**Stephen Curry** minutes. How do you guys view this period right now that you are playing without Stephen in the lineup?"

**De’Anthony Melton:** "Just like you said, non-Stephen minutes, we got to win those minutes because when he is in the game, he is going to do so many great things for us. When he is off the court, we got to just make sure we maintain the lead and sometimes when we are winning, even grow the lead. We are trying to figure it out when he is not on the floor and I think we are coming around that corner with it."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=126s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=126s)

**5. De’Anthony Melton — "I think the beauty of this team, we got so much depth and we got so much confidence" — Depth and next-man-up mentality on the Warriors' roster** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGhZxON9JSc&t=157s)

**Unidentified speaker:** "Tonight the bench had 61 points to Sacramento's 19. Talk about the contribution from your bench tonight."

**De’Anthony Melton:** "I have been saying this since day one, how much depth we got. I think anybody on this team could really go. The dudes that are not even really playing can still go. I think the beauty of this team, we got so much depth and we got so much confidence that anybody that steps up, it is always next man up for sure."
