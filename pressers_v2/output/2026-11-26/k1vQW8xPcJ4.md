# Brandon Williams notes shift in Warriors' confidence on offense | NBC Sports Bay Area — *Golden State Warriors*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4)

_Speakers identified: Brandon Williams, Kerith Burke_

**1. Brandon Williams — "Basketball is my safe space where I get to be me" — Brandon Williams on returning to the court after injury** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=8s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "When you checked in in the second quarter, two immediate buckets right there. You were like a racehorse coming out of the gates. How did it feel to you?"

**Brandon Williams:** "It felt great. I was just so excited to be back playing, to be around the team again, just to be on the court. Basketball is my safe space where I get to be me, and to be able to do that tonight with the teammate, it's in our coaches, it was it was one of the world."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=8s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=8s)

**2. Brandon Williams — "As long as we're finding guys, finding the open guys, making the right play, I think that's the most important thing." — Brandon Williams on the Warriors' offensive flow and ball movement** [00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=31s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "You had to watch that Celtics game. This one had a different flavor, okay? 37 assists, the balls hopping, the threes are falling. What's the flow of the offense feel like tonight?"

**Brandon Williams:** "I don't know, the ball was just moving. They were finding open guys. We didn't have a lid over the rim today, so that's always a plus. But as long as we're finding guys, finding the open guys, making the right play, I think that's the most important thing. We can live with the results, make or miss."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=31s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=31s)

**3. Brandon Williams — "It builds great confidence, especially in these cup games." — Brandon Williams on building team confidence through balanced scoring** [00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=55s)

**Unidentified reporter:** "You faced an opponent that does have a struggling defense. So lots of guys can get involved. You've got seven players in double-digit scoring. What does that do for the confidence as you guys try to build?"

**Brandon Williams:** "It builds great confidence, especially in these cup games. We're going to go up to Portland and try to get a win there in advance. But like you said, it just builds confidence as a group. The last week or two has been pretty rough for us as a whole. So games like this should just help us carry momentum to the next game."

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=55s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=55s)

**4. Brandon Williams — "He was finding the ball, the ball was going in, he was finding other guys" — Brandon Williams praises De'Anthony Melton's complete performance** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1vQW8xPcJ4&t=85s)

**Kerith Burke:** "Tell me about **De'Anthony Melton**'s role in this win, eight three-pointers, seven assists."

**Brandon Williams:** "Yeah, I mean he did a little bit of everything. He was hooping. He was finding the ball, the ball was going in, he was finding other guys when he when he didn't have it. A real complete game from him, I'm happy."
