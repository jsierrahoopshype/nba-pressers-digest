**3. Micah Nori — "I think it is a really good combination of we do have selfless guys." — Team chemistry and the impact of new roster additions** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=weWq2s7LipE&t=215s)

Micah Nori: "I think it is a really good combination of we do have selfless guys. But we also have guys that their skill sets match. It is not like we have three or four of the same. The gravity that Dame forces, we have really good guys that can screen, meaning Donovan and **Rob** that put pressure on the rim. You have **Scoot** who can get downhill. Obviously **Denny** can get downhill. **Drew Holidays** is like a Swiss Army knife, he can do everything. And **City** and **Tumani**, I feel bad, I apologize to **Tumani**, because quite honestly, he goes out and guards everybody every single night. I tell him I am going to run a play for him and I did not even run one. Then I said, hey, I am going to run the first play second half for you and he goes down and gets fouled and makes two free throws. We have a lot of really good offensive players, we have a lot of really good defensive players, and I am getting way too much credit for putting really good players in good positions."

**4. Micah Nori — "I think when you get worried about it is when Denny and Tumani or whoever it is misses multiple." — Addressing the team's high volume of missed free throws** [04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=weWq2s7LipE&t=274s)

Micah Nori: "I think it is different. The other night it was **Dame** that missed the free throws. Now **Dame** goes five for five. Now **Tumani** has missed four, **Denny** has missed three tonight. So I think when you get worried about it is when **Denny** and **Tumani** or whoever it is misses multiple in multiple games in a row. Does that make sense? Because I was really frustrated when I looked 30 for 41. I am like, damn we missed 11, but **Tumani** missed four and **Denny** missed three. That is seven of the 11. I know the one thing about **Denny**, when you look down at the end of the year, he is going to be over 80. But **Tumani**, that is probably on me as well, because I did not get him, he did not feel the ball, he did not get any touches, so then all of a sudden you get fouled and you go to the free throw line, it is tough."
