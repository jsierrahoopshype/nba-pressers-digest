**4. Jordan Ott — "I think we started off not great last year defensively a little bit." — Jordan Ott evaluates the team's defensive regression since last season** [06:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DntBlPIQ5Gw&t=396s)

Jordan Ott: "I think we started off not great last year defensively a little bit. I think we're missing Mark. I think that's a piece of it. Mark's overall size deters rim attempts, his IQ and ability to communicate on the floor. I think we knew when the injury happened that that would hurt us. We just thought we had three really good bigs that we can mix and match. So, defense is a lot predicated on the five-man. Look at those guys, how they play defense. So a lot of that is predicated on your five-man and then now we're scrambling. So now we're switching, now we're a little bit more aggressive to where our base isn't probably as convicted in our base right now defensively."

**5. Jordan Ott — "The one constant right now is what I'll continue to say is the defensive rebounding piece." — Jordan Ott emphasizes defensive rebounding as the key to consistency** [07:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DntBlPIQ5Gw&t=479s)

Jordan Ott: "The one constant right now is what I'll continue to say is the defensive rebounding piece. You got to get it right, especially with the way the league is crashing right now. It feels like everyone is moving more bodies in there. We understand that we're undersized. We understand it, but it's that gets compounded then when you start switching to slow down your drive and help your drive defense. So then you start switching, putting your fives on the perimeter and then your size is away from the rim. So those are the two areas we got to get right first before we start mixing and matching anything else."
