**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "We were out of timeouts in that situation. Three possible scenarios there: we can foul, put them on the free throw line." — Tuomas Iisalo explains the late-game strategy against the Pacers** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4okg1b1F7KY&t=32s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "The possession started with four-second differential. We were out of timeouts in that situation. Three possible scenarios there: we can foul, put them on the free throw line. We can let them bleed out the clock, which means if they put the shot up with let's say five seconds, we have to get the ball up in three seconds. Or we could try to get them to take an early shot by trapping the ball, which they did. Unfortunately, they made the shot."

**5. Tuomas Iisalo — "They put two on the ball and then they have the low man slide over, take the roll man." — Tuomas Iisalo on the team's ball movement and spacing** [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4okg1b1F7KY&t=243s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "Anytime we move the ball, they were very aggressive in their protection. So they put two on the ball and then they have the low man slide over, take the roll man, which basically leaves one player, call him X, out for the two players on the weak side. I didn't think we moved the ball quite fast enough to those guys. And then when we did, we did get quality looks, whether from the three or from closeouts. And those are the team reactions we keep talking about."
