**2. Luke Kennard — "finding a spot within this offense and playing off of guys, playing with the ball" — Luke Kennard on finding his role within the Suns' offense** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ma-V7bn66Hs&t=84s)

**3. Luke Kennard — "spacing is very, it obviously doesn't show up on like a box score, but being able to space" — Luke Kennard on the importance of spacing and screening** [02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ma-V7bn66Hs&t=132s)

Luke Kennard: "Spacing is very, it obviously doesn't show up on like a box score, but being able to space the floor and allowing guys just an extra, like this much more space, it can change everything in an offense. So I think for me, number one is being a good screener. I learned from some of the greats in the game, like Steph, for example. He's a great screener, and you don't see that a lot, but it's reasons why he gets open because he sets good screens and guys got to help. So I've been working a lot with Book and CG of, you know, when I'm with them, setting good screens, getting to space, getting open shots, and I think for me, just continuing to be that floor spacer for some of those guys to get to their spots and make plays, it can be really big for this offense."
