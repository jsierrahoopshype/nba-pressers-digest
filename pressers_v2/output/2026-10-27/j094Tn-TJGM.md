**1. Tuomas Iisalo — "very satisfied with our again, the word I keep using is poise, in the fourth quarter" — Tuomas Iisalo on Grizzlies' bench performance and late-game poise** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j094Tn-TJGM&t=0s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "I felt that things in the third and the fourth, we had good control of the game. I thought our guys played really well, especially the guys off the bench, the amount of energy they brought and pace on a second night of a back-to-back. We saw two teams that were really struggling to generate something and it came down to big plays down the stretch. But I am very satisfied with our again, the word I keep using is poise, in the fourth quarter of just staying with the program, getting to the situations, working the margins and then coming up with the crucial defensive stops at the right time."

**3. Tuomas Iisalo — "I personally don't consider it a luxury. I consider it a necessity in this schedule." — Iisalo views roster depth as a necessity for 82-game schedule** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j094Tn-TJGM&t=71s)

Tuomas Iisalo: "I personally don't consider it a luxury. I consider it a necessity in this schedule. We have an 82-game schedule. We got to find on a nightly basis, got to find those guys who are able to bring the juice and at the same time bring the necessary focus and be in that attack mode from the first moment on. And I think we definitely have that type of team, and I think we made a huge run in the first half building that lead, especially with our bench guys, and that's great to see."
