**1. Jordan Ott — "the start of it we just weren't quite able to get the ball under control" — Jordan Ott on the Suns' defensive struggles and lack of control** [00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8oG3D7Mei44&t=7s)

Jordan Ott: "Our group, we talk a lot about our defensive intensity, our defensive intent. The start of it we just weren't quite able to get the ball under control. So, guard the basketball, pick-and-roll defense, we tried a couple different things, tried to keep it under control. That's where it always starts with us. If we can't get the ball under control and they get into our paint, they get a little rhythm. That's what it felt like. We tried to get them out of it, I thought we did a good job on the glass at times, we kind of fixed that for a certain period of time, and then they get you to the free-throw line. There's just multiple ways that they can really attack your defense, we weren't quite able to get it under control tonight."

**3. Jordan Ott — "we got some high-quality looks, so that's what we're always searching" — Jordan Ott on the Suns' offensive intent and shooting strategy** [02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8oG3D7Mei44&t=122s)

Jordan Ott: "I thought we got some high-quality looks, so that's what we're always searching. Obviously, we want our best player to be in attack mode. Sometimes when they're showing you bodies like that, the best read is just to make the next one. Our quality of looks were pretty good. We got 53s up against this group, that's kind of what you want to do. You want to shoot as many 3s as you can get, a bunch of catch-and-shoot, I think it was 35 or 37 catch-and-shoot. So, I think overall, the intent of what we're trying to do offensively is there."
