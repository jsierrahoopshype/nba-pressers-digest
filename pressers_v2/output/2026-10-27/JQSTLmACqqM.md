**4. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander — "He has had a tough year and a half with injuries." — Shai Gilgeous-Alexander on supporting teammate Isaiah Hartenstein** [02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JQSTLmACqqM&t=147s)

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander: "It is pretty crazy. Obviously, more than anything, I just feel gutted for the guy. He will be a better basketball player than he ever was at the end of this, and I am fully confident in that."
