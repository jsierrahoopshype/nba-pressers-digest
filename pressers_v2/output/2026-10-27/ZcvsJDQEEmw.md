**1. Charles Lee — "I would love to see our habits be sustainable until that last buzzer." — Charles Lee on the Hornets' need for consistent defensive habits** [00:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZcvsJDQEEmw&t=17s)

Charles Lee: "I love that the group was able to respond after the Wizards went on a run. It is a really good team, but I think in all games you have to realize the game is far from over. I would love to see our habits be sustainable until that last buzzer. I thought we had a little bit of a lapse in transition defense and not protecting the paint in some key moments. After the timeout, we came out and **Brandon Miller** gets a big-time block on Anthony Davis's attempt at the rim. I thought our shift activity improved, some of the contests were great, and they banged a couple of threes that were heavily contested. But I thought for the most part, once we locked back in on our habits and our techniques, we got a little bit better."

**3. Charles Lee — "I see Dennis talking to him, I see Naz talking to him, I hear Musa, Grayson, whoever it is." — Charles Lee on Brandon Miller's maturity and team support** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZcvsJDQEEmw&t=98s)

Charles Lee: "As you get deep into games, you need to know where the ball is going, what your spacing is going to look like. There has got to be some preparation but also a confidence and a willingness to want to be in those moments. I think Brandon and the whole team are prepared for them and they want them. Part of Brandon's maturation is he himself, but then also I see Dennis talking to him, I see Naz talking to him, I hear **Musa**, Grayson, whoever it is down the line, Khan, **Kobe**, they are like, 'You are that guy, we believe in you.' And so when we put the ball in your hand, go make a play for yourself or your teammates. I think the fact that not only is Brandon prepared for it, but his teammates want to see him in those moments and they encourage him in those moments is really what is helping him as well."

**4. Charles Lee — "The thing that I have loved about Tidjane is his mindset and his approach coming into the game." — Charles Lee on Tidjane Salaun's defensive development** [02:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZcvsJDQEEmw&t=155s)

Charles Lee: "The thing that I have loved about Tidjane is his mindset and his approach coming into the game. Most young players when you come in, you think the best way that you can impact the game is by scoring. I think he has realized that the role that we need of him consistently every night is to be defensively focused, understand what the personnel tendencies are. Obviously, he was a young player last year trying to learn a new basketball game and because he works so hard, he cares so much, we knew that he was going to make progress. Glad to see that it is translating to the court because I agree with you, his defensive activity and rebounding has been really good to watch him want the matchups. He is like, 'Coach, put me on **Giannis Antetokounmpo** or put me on Davis,' and his willingness and readiness to like, if it is one possession, if it is ten possessions, he is down for it."
