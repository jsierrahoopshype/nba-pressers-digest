# Full Denver Nuggets Postgame Media Availability vs. GSW 🎙 — *Denver Nuggets*

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZHRZpi9Z8Qw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZHRZpi9Z8Qw)

_Speakers identified: Coach Adelman, Jamal Murray, Bryce Hopkins_

**1. Coach Adelman — "The physicality, I don't think we took a foul when we were switching up on anybody." — Coach Adelman on defensive struggles and lack of physicality** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZHRZpi9Z8Qw&t=8s)

Coach Adelman: "Tonight was very different. I just actually thought we handled the big-small pick-and-roll stuff okay. It was the small-small pick-and-roll stuff. The coverage wasn't good enough. We talked about in the locker room after the game, just the physicality. I don't think we took a foul when we were switching up on anybody, and that leads to slipping. It leads to 2-on-1s, 3-on-2s in the backside. So it was a bad night in that way, in every way. I thought they shot the ball really well when we shrunk, but that was the issue. It was all that small-small stuff."

**2. Coach Adelman — "If you let guys run untouched, it leads to breakdowns. It leads to people having to be." — Coach Adelman on defensive switching and rotation issues** [01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZHRZpi9Z8Qw&t=79s)

Coach Adelman: "If you're going to switch 1-through-4, like pretty much every team in our league does, the physicality part of it is huge. If you let guys run untouched, it leads to breakdowns. It leads to people having to be the low man for no reason, and that leads to extra rotations. If I have a lineup out there with **TB** and **Aaron**, that's not a lineup that scrambles well. That's not a small lineup. So you put yourself in a really tough spot. You have to win the first battle, and we lost it throughout the night."
